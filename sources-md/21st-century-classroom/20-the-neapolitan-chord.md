# The Neapolitan Chord

## <span id="page-279-1"></span>20.1 The Neapolitan Chord

The label "Neapolitan" is used in classical music for the  $\flat II$  chord and is labeled as "N" instead of  $\flat II$ . For example, when the Neapolitan chord occurs in first inversion—its most common inversion—it is labeled as N<sup>6</sup>.

Being a chromatically altered ii chord, the Neapolitan has pre-dominant harmonic function.

Notice the special character of the Neapolitan and how composers sometimes alter texture, register, and dynamics when the Neapolitan occurs, as in the first movement of Beethoven's Symphony No. 7, Op. 92.
