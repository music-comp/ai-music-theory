## <span id="page-411-0"></span>28.2 Avoiding Objectionable Parallels

When adding non-chord tones, one must avoid creating objectionable parallels. To avoid creating parallel 5ths and 8ves when adding non-chord tones, first identify intervals of 5ths and 8ves between voices in your original voice leading.

![](./images/_page_411_Picture_6.jpeg)

Figure 28.2.1 Progression with 8ves and 5ths identified

Avoid adding non-chord tones that move by step into notes separated by a  $5 \mathrm{th}$  or  $8 \mathrm{ve}$ .

![](./images/_page_411_Picture_9.jpeg)

Figure 28.2.2 Parallel 5ths and 8ves created by non-chord tones

It is best to avoid adding non-chord tones in two voices on the same beat unless they are moving in parallel 3rds or 6ths.
