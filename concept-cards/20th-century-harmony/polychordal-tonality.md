# Quick Definition

The establishment of tonal center in polychordal music through melodic predominance or harmonic gravitation to a dominating chord, since traditional harmonic function cannot secure tonality when progressions touch many key areas.

# Core Definition

"Polyharmonic progressions touch so many key areas that tonality cannot be secured through the harmonic personality of a prevailing scale. Tonality is established by the tonal implications of a predominating melodic line or by harmonic gravitation to a dominating chord. Resonant polychords play the tonic role most convincingly; a wealth of invention must be saved for the cadence if a firm tonal center is desired" (Persichetti, p. 145).

# Prerequisites

- **Polychord** — Tonality issues arise from polychordal practice

# Key Properties

1. Traditional harmonic tonality fails in polyharmony (too many key areas touched)
2. **Method 1**: Tonal implications of predominating melodic line
3. **Method 2**: Harmonic gravitation to dominating chord
4. Resonant polychords best suit the tonic role
5. Cadential invention must be saved for tonal establishment
6. Prolonged pure polychords produce tiring mass resonance
7. Relief techniques: ornamentation, unison/two-voice interruptions, note omission, polychords as architectural pillars

# Construction / Recognition

## To Establish Tonality in Polychordal Music
1. Choose a predominating melody that implies a tonal center, OR
2. Establish a dominating polychord through return and emphasis
3. Use resonant polychords for tonic function
4. Save inventive resources for cadential moments
5. Relieve extended polychordal passages with ornamentation, unison interruptions, or note omission

# Context & Application

Polychordal music requires alternative approaches to tonal organization. Traditional harmonic functions (V-I etc.) cannot serve since polychordal progressions touch many key areas. Use of polychords as architectural harmonic pillars with other harmony between creates dynamic contrast.

# Common Errors

- **Error**: Writing extended passages of pure polychords without relief
  **Correction**: Prolonged pure polychords result in tiring mass resonance; use ornamentation, unison interruptions, and note omission
- **Error**: Relying on harmonic function for tonal establishment
  **Correction**: Use melodic predominance or characteristic chord gravitation instead

# Common Confusions

- **Confusion**: Polychordal tonality works like traditional functional tonality
  **Clarification**: Traditional functions are inoperative; melody or chord dominance establishes center
- **Confusion**: Polytonality and polychordal tonality are the same
  **Clarification**: Polytonality maintains multiple keys; polychordal tonality establishes a single center through non-functional means
