# Quick Definition
A tone or tones sustained, repeated, or ornamented while other voices move through a succession of chords, some of which may be foreign to the pedal.

# Core Definition
A **pedal** is a tone or tones sustained, repeated, or ornamented while other voices move through a succession of chords, some of which may be foreign to the pedal. Tonic pedals have repose and non-tonic pedals have restlessness. Final pedals reinforce tonality or allow time for figuration to conclude its ornamental design. When pedals are placed a fifth or ninth below the main body of harmony, sheen and resonance are added. Polytonality is often suggested by triple pedal point. If three or more tones are held, a pedal chord evolves (Ch. 11, pp. 240-242).

# Prerequisites
- **Harmonic progression** -- understanding the moving harmonies against which the pedal holds

# Key Properties
1. Sustained, repeated, or ornamented (not only sustained)
2. Single pedal: one tone; double: two tones; triple: three tones (suggests polytonality)
3. Three+ tones = pedal chord
4. Tonic pedals: repose; non-tonic pedals: restlessness
5. Final pedals: reinforce tonality or allow figuration to conclude
6. Fifth or ninth below harmony: adds sheen and resonance
7. Upper voice: melodically obstinate; middle voice: obtrusive

# Construction / Recognition
- Identify sustained or repeated tones against moving harmony
- Classify as single, double, triple, or pedal chord
- Determine the tonal function (tonic, dominant, non-tonic)
- Note register placement and its effect on character

# Context & Application
Pedal point establishes and maintains tonal centers in twentieth-century music. Triple pedal often suggests polytonality. The relationship between pedal and moving harmonies creates varying degrees of consonance and dissonance as the progression unfolds.

# Common Errors
- Assuming pedal must be only sustained (it can be repeated or ornamented)
- Using triple pedal without considering polytonal implications

# Common Confusions
- Pedal can be sustained, repeated, or ornamented -- not only sustained
- Triple pedal may suggest polytonality rather than simple tonal reinforcement
- Register affects the character: sheen below, obstinacy above
