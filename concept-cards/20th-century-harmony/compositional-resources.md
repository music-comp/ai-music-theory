# Quick Definition
The complete range of harmonic, melodic, rhythmic, and timbral materials available to the twentieth-century composer, encompassing all historical and contemporary techniques.

# Formal Definition
A synthesis of harmonic potentialities includes chord materials governed by root progressions, intervallic texture tensions, and serial devices. The harmonic stock includes endless possible harmonic combinations. Any tone can succeed any other tone, any tone can sound simultaneously with any other tone or tones, and any group of tones can be followed by any other group of tones, just as any degree of tension or nuance can occur in any medium under any kind of stress or duration.

# Musical/Mathematical Formulation
Resource categories:
- **Chord construction**: Tertian, quartal, secundal, polychordal, compound
- **Harmonic organization**: Root progression, intervallic tension, serial
- **Textural types**: Homophonic, polyphonic, mixed
- **Tonal frameworks**: Tonal, modal, polytonal, atonal
- **Rhythmic resources**: Regular, asymmetrical, complex meters
- **Timbral resources**: All instrumental and electronic combinations

Freedom principle:
- Any succession of tones is possible
- Any simultaneity is possible
- Any juxtaposition is possible
- Any tension level is possible
- Any medium is possible
- Success depends on context and skill

# Musical Context/Application
The twentieth-century composer has access to the complete range of harmonic resources developed throughout music history, plus contemporary innovations. The techniques and materials of atonal music are often amalgamated with those of tonal idioms. Successful projection depends upon contextual and formal conditions that prevail, and upon the skill and soul of the composer. The choice of resources is a creative decision, not a technical limitation.
