# Quick Definition
A chord whose motivating power derives from the augmented sixth interval, with five basic types in twentieth-century practice, ideal for added-note treatment because its strong directional pull is unimpaired by color-tone additions.

# Core Definition
The augmented sixth is a likely chord to add notes to because its directional pull is sufficiently strong to be unimpaired by the addition of color tones. The augmented sixth interval gives the chord its motivating power; the interval a third from the bottom gives it its fundamental flavor. Twentieth-century practice employs five basic types: Italian with major third, minor third variant, augmented octave with major third, doubly augmented octave with major third, and augmented third. The French augmented sixth = Italian + M2 added above middle tone (a prime added-note example). Voices spanning the augmented sixth tend to expand, descend chromatically, or move obliquely.

# Prerequisites
- **Added-note chord**: The technique applied to augmented sixth formations

# Key Properties
1. Five basic types (not just the traditional Italian/French/German)
2. Augmented sixth interval = motivating power
3. Third from bottom = fundamental flavor
4. French sixth = Italian + added M2 (prime added-note example)
5. Resolution: expansion, chromatic descent, or oblique motion
6. Minor third variant = color change, not functional change
7. Augmented octave/doubly augmented octave types = ninth textures
8. Augmented third type = useful in quartal context (often enharmonically respelled)

# Construction / Recognition
- Identify the augmented sixth interval as the defining feature
- Five types from the bass: Aug6+M3, Aug6+m3, Aug6+A8+M3, Aug6+AA8+M3, Aug6+A3
- Resolution tendency of the spanning voices determines motion

# Context & Application
The augmented sixth chord is the paradigmatic added-note-friendly formation. Its directional pull is so strong that any number of seconds can be attached without changing its function. Types with augmented/doubly augmented octave are useful in ninth/eleventh/thirteenth textures. The augmented third type suits quartal contexts.
