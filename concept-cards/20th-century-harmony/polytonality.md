# Quick Definition
A compositional procedure in which two or more keys are combined simultaneously, creating multiple tonal planes operating together.

# Formal Definition
Polytonal writing is a procedure in which two or more keys are combined simultaneously. If only two keys are sounded, the specific term bitonality may be used, but polytonality has come generally to imply the use of more than one tonal plane at the same time. The scales forming different tonic centers may be intervallically identical or contrasting, traditional or synthetic. Polytonality is a specific kind of tonal organization, a means of moving groups of voices within confined harmonic register areas.

# Musical/Mathematical Formulation
Polytonal construction principles:
- **Vertical factor**: Must be carefully considered; arbitrary combination yields haphazard results
- **Tonal planes**: Each melodic line retains own individuality
- **Harmonic total**: Must have direction of flow, tension design, textural consistency
- **Over-all tonic**: Usually felt from the bass

Key relationship resonance (major keys):
- Most consonant to most dissonant: P5, M9, M6, M3, M7, etc. (cycle of fifths order)
- Tritone forms prime polytonal relationship (most resonant of dissonant combinations)
- Keys not closely related by fifths more easily set apart

Optimal conditions:
- Each tonal plane kept within clear scale formation
- Tritone-spanning scale steps brought into play quickly
- Keys kept far enough apart for voice activity
- Larger intervals between bottom keys in mixed combinations

# Musical Context/Application
Although each tonal plane has its own organizational center, a single over-all tonic structure is usually felt from the bass. From this basic polychordal tonic stems the polytonal texture. The tonal simplicity of ostinato helps clarify polytonal texture. Polychordal writing is often tonal, and when each group of voices functions within stable scale zone confines, polytonality occurs.

# Common Confusions
- Polytonality requires careful vertical construction, not arbitrary combination
- The bass usually determines the over-all tonic
- Shifting modality within polytonality clouds the texture
