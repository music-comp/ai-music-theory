# Quick Definition

Consecutive perfect fifth intervals between voices, an important harmonic ingredient in contemporary composition that requires creative handling to prevent the intervallic uniformity from becoming tiresome.

# Formal Definition

**Successive perfect fifths** are consecutive perfect fifth intervals moving in parallel between two voices. While traditional practice avoided parallel fifths, they form an important harmonic ingredient in twentieth-century composition. Fifths are emotionally immense, vague, and distant, or bare and dominating. Unless used imaginatively, their intervallic uniformity becomes tiresome, challenging a composer's inventiveness and facility.

# Musical/Mathematical Formulation

Contexts for parallel fifths:
- Two-part writing, prying loose oblique motion
- Coupled two-part writing (octave-doubled lines)
- Parallel harmony (as part of larger chord structures)
- Sequential pattern joints (end of one, beginning of next)
- Phrase joints

Challenge: Perfect fifths make voice independence difficult due to their acoustic prominence and uniformity.

Background techniques (pushing fifths to background):
- Place conjunct fifths in lower voices with contrary/oblique upper parts
- Use rhythmically agile accompanying parts to divert attention
- Insert other intervals between some fifths
- Use contrary outer parts with fifths in inner voices
- Soften upper fifths by placing third below
- Subdue skipping fifths by leaping to same chord members
- Use florid inner voices against outer-voice fifths

# Musical Context/Application

Effective use of parallel fifths requires:
- Awareness of their acoustic prominence
- Techniques to prevent dominance or monotony
- Integration with contrasting motion in other voices
- Exploitation of their unique emotional quality (immensity, vagueness, distance)

Fifths contribute significantly when:
- Folk or archaic character is desired
- Open, spacious sonority is needed
- Quartal textures are employed
- Coupled melodic lines require reinforcement

# Common Confusions

- **Forbidden vs. resource**: Traditional prohibition stems from voice independence concerns; modern practice uses fifths as distinctive resource.
- **Prominent vs. background**: The same interval can dominate texture or recede depending on treatment.
- **Consecutive vs. simultaneous**: Successive fifths are parallel motion; simultaneous fifth is simply an interval within a chord.
