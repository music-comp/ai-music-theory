# Quick Definition

The manner in which successive chords are linked through voice leading, involving both what chord follows what chord and how the voices move between them.

# Formal Definition

**Chord connection** refers to two inseparable factors in harmonic progression: what chord follows what chord, and how they are connected. When melodies sound together chords are formed, and when chords follow each other melodic motion is involved. Music can be primarily harmonic, melodic, or rhythmic, but there is seldom pure harmony or counterpoint as they are deeply involved with each other. Separate chord tones of any progression have melodic tendencies; even the most isolated chord is full of melodic potential.

# Musical/Mathematical Formulation

Connection principles:
- **Outer voices**: Govern harmonic direction
- **Inner voices**: Secure the relationship of chords
- **Common tones**: Held for smooth motion
- **Minimal motion**: Inner voices move as little as possible for unruffled progression

Motion types and effects:
- **Parallel intervals**: Lessen voice individuality
- **Contrary motion**: Gives voices independence
- **Oblique motion**: Gives voices independence
- **Strong linear urge**: Can overpower harmonic impulse

# Musical Context/Application

Techniques for fresh harmonic writing:
- Invert or transpose common-tone intervals to expand register
- Move voices past neighboring voices for harmonic lift or drop
- Animate voices by deflecting melodic line (octave displacement)
- Shift entire harmonic mass to another register suddenly
- Use successive octaves, fifths, and parallel harmony for reinforcement
- Vary number of voices for density changes
- Leave climactic point vacant by rests
- Allow voices to share melody for thematic harmony

The danger of overly smooth progressions: They may become stale; ingenuity required through leaping voices, straying dissonances, escaping common tones, modulatory twists, and chromatic daring.

# Common Confusions

- **Smooth vs. fresh**: Smooth connections are one facet; ingenuity is required to prevent staleness through varied techniques.
- **Harmony vs. counterpoint**: These are not separate domains; chord connection inherently involves both vertical and horizontal thinking.
- **Inner vs. outer voices**: Outer voices determine direction; inner voices determine relationship. Both are essential but serve different functions.
