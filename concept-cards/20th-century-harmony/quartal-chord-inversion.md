# Quick Definition
The rearrangement of quartal chord members to place different notes in the bass, providing color variety through the prominence of the perfect fifth, preventing harmonic monotony, and creating real harmonic movement without root change.

# Core Definition
In all types of three-note chords by fourths, two inversions are possible. Inverting chords having two perfect fourths helps prevent the harmonic monotony of uniform intervals. Either inversion can be used as a fundamental structure because of the strong perfect fifth. If the resonant perfect fifth dominates the texture, the second (created by the inverted seventh) often sounds like a note added to a simple formation. For four-note quartal chords, three inverted forms are extremely useful because of the variety of intervals they contain. Moving through inversions of the same chord produces real harmonic movement without root change. Open spacing increases harmonic expressiveness.

# Prerequisites
- **Three-note quartal chords**: The formations being inverted

# Key Properties
1. Three-note: two inversions; four-note: three inversions
2. Inversions introduce the perfect fifth prominently
3. Fifth prominence can make the chord sound like a simple formation with added note
4. Moving through inversions = harmonic movement without root change
5. Open spacing multiplies available colors
6. Inversions break the monotony of stacked fourths
7. Inversions in quartal harmony provide color variety, not the hierarchical bass relationships of tertian inversions

# Construction / Recognition
- Root position: fourths dominate
- Inversions: fifth appears between different voice pairs
- Four-note inversions contain mixtures of fourths, fifths, and seconds
- Open spacing combined with inversion multiplies colors

# Context & Application
Quartal inversions serve primarily as color and motion resources. Unlike tertian inversions which create hierarchical bass-note effects, quartal inversions mainly provide textural variety and introduce fifths into the predominantly fourth-based texture. Four-note quartal chords resolve easily to tertian chords through conjunct voice movement in inversions.
