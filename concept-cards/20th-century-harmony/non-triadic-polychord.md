# Quick Definition

Polychords with seventh-chord, quartal, or secundal units rather than triads, typically used in brief chordal groups or sforzando chords rather than extended passages, with all-secundal units creating polyclusters.

# Core Definition

"Polychords consisting of seventh chord units are seldom used in extended passages but more often in brief chordal groups that intensify a single line or a two-part statement, or as a sforzando chord" (Persichetti, p. 153). "When chords other than chords by thirds are used as building units in polychords, textural clarity becomes more of a problem. Polychordal formations of chords by fourths are, in effect, enlarged versions of three-note chords by fourths. And if all the units are secundal, a polycluster results" (p. 154).

# Key Properties

1. **Seventh-chord units**: Brief use (chordal groups, sforzando); may share common tones (homogeneous) or not
2. **Quartal units**: Become enlarged quartal chords; textural clarity is challenging
3. **Secundal units**: All-secundal = polycluster; better as uppermost unit in mixed construction
4. Seventh-chord polychords with common tones = homogeneous sound
5. Seventh-chord polychords without common tones = more dissonant
6. Chords by seconds function sonorously as uppermost unit of mixed polychord

# Construction / Recognition

## To Use Non-Triadic Units
1. For seventh-chord units: plan for brief use (groups, sforzando, intensification)
2. For quartal units: combine with triadic units for clarity
3. For secundal units: place as uppermost unit in mixed-unit polychord
4. Check for common tones between units to control blend

# Context & Application

Non-triadic polychords expand the polychordal palette beyond triadic sonorities. Seventh-chord units are particularly useful for dramatic punctuation, intensification of melodic statements, and brief harmonic color. Quartal and secundal units present clarity challenges but offer distinctive colors in mixed construction.

# Common Errors

- **Error**: Writing extended passages of seventh-chord polychords
  **Correction**: These are most effective in brief groups or as sforzando chords
- **Error**: Using all-quartal units expecting polychordal clarity
  **Correction**: All-quartal polychords become enlarged quartal chords; mix with triadic units for clarity

# Common Confusions

- **Confusion**: All-secundal polychords are a distinct category from polyclusters
  **Clarification**: They are the same thing; if all units are secundal, a polycluster results
- **Confusion**: Brief use indicates weakness
  **Clarification**: It reflects the optimal dramatic function of these dense structures
