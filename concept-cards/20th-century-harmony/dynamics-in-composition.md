# Quick Definition

The compositional use of dynamic nuance as an essential element affecting harmonic progression, creating its own rhythm, and interacting with natural musical rhythm to generate varied climactic conditions.

# Formal Definition

**Dynamics** are an essential element in composition that affect harmonic progression by the degree of dynamic nuance in which harmony is conceived. Harmonic progression is influenced by its dynamic context: a dissonant and restless progression set in pianissimo may explode into subito forte of violent polychords, while the same progression in forte context might find satisfaction in its overbearing tension and remain in the same harmonic sphere. Highly chromatic chords blend with more harmonic ease in soft passages than in loud.

# Musical/Mathematical Formulation

Dynamic rhythm:
- Projected through: piano, forte, crescendo, diminuendo, sforzando, subito directions
- **Reinforcement**: Dynamic rhythm coincides with natural rhythm
- **Contradiction**: Dynamic rhythm opposes natural rhythm, creating tension line

Climactic interactions:
- Crescendo through growing harmonic tension: overwhelming cumulative power
- Diminuendo through growing harmonic tension: different kind of tension
- Rhythm of melody, harmony, and dynamic nuance generate forces juxtaposed for varied climactic conditions

Dynamic effects on harmony:
- Soft passages: Chromatic chords blend more easily
- Loud passages: Harmonic tensions more exposed
- Context affects resolution expectations

# Musical Context/Application

Dynamics serve:
- Shaping harmonic progression and its perception
- Creating rhythmic counterpoint with melody and harmony
- Building and releasing tension through nuance
- Articulating formal structure through dynamic contrast

The interaction of:
- Melodic rhythm
- Harmonic rhythm
- Dynamic nuance
generates multiple possible climactic conditions through varied combinations.

# Common Confusions

- **Dynamics vs. volume**: Dynamics are compositional elements; volume is a performance parameter. Dynamics indicate more than just loud/soft.
- **Fixed vs. contextual effect**: The same dynamic marking has different effects depending on harmonic and formal context.
- **Dynamic rhythm**: Dynamics create their own rhythmic layer that interacts with melodic and harmonic rhythm.
