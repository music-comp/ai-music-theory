# Phrase Expansion

## Quick Definition

The compositional technique of extending the eight-measure periodic prototype through inserted material, repeated functions, or delayed cadential arrivals, creating dramatic effect through the amplification of closural energy while maintaining the underlying periodic logic.

## Formal Definition

**Phrase expansion** in Riemann's analytical framework refers to techniques by which composers extend beyond the eight-measure periodic norm while preserving its structural functions.

**Types of Expansion**:
1. **Inserted repetitions**: Measures labeled 3a-4a, 5a-6a, etc., repeat earlier functions
2. **Multiple cadential arrivals**: Multiple "8" functions (8, 8a, 8b, 8c) prolong closure
3. **Iterated antepenultimate**: Repeated "6" functions delay resolution
4. **Back-relating functions**: A "9" that echoes "8" rather than beginning a new period
5. **Takttriole**: Three measures substituted for two between strong downbeats

**Analytical Distinction** (from *Grosse Kompositionslehre*):
> "The most important distinction is between *actually constitutive, developing passages* and *insertions*... the *essential musical action* and the *moments of idle lingering* inserted between the main phases of that action."

**Burnham's Formulation**: This distinction creates "the most palpable bridge between background prototype and foreground realization."

## Musical Context

Phrase expansion is central to understanding Beethoven's development:

- Riemann sees Beethoven's maturation as "ever firmer positioning of the actual structure"
- Expansions amplify the closural energy of the period
- The stations of the prototype (especially 6 and 8) carry "the weight of ever larger spans of music"

**Dramatic Function**: Expansion creates musical drama through:
- Delay of expected cadential arrival
- Intensification through iterated approach to closure
- Multiplication of closural effects (comic or dramatic)

**The "6" Function Special Status**: The antepenultimate (often cadential 6/4) offers "an unequivocal sense that resolution is right around the corner"—making its iteration "highly dramatic."

## Examples

### Basic

**Simple register-based expansion** (Op. 31, No. 3, Period IV):
```
1-2, 3-4, (3a-4a), 5-6, (5a-6a), 7-8, (8a)
      [higher register] [higher register] [lower]
```
The repeated pairs extend without advancing the harmonic argument.

**Multiple "8" functions**:
```
Period: ...6  7  8  8a  8b  8c
            D  T   T   T   T
Each tonic arrival creates another cadential confirmation.
```

### From Repertoire

**Beethoven, Op. 31, No. 1, Period III**: Sixteen measures containing five "8" functions in the drive to the dominant. An "about-face" (*Umwenden*) at m. 36 retrospectively reinterprets an "8" as a "7," showing the flexibility of functional assignment.

**Beethoven, Op. 31, No. 1, Period IX (end of exposition)**: Nine "8" functions—the movement's "comic tic" of closural proliferation. The phrase feels like "a door that slams shut, only to pop back open... and so on."

**Beethoven, Op. 31, No. 3, Period VI**: 33 measures expanding from the second theme through the end of the exposition. The analysis shows: "7-8, 7-8, 6-7-8, 5-6, 5-6, 5-6, 4-5-6-7-8, (6-7-8)"—a "telescoping elongation, always moving a step further from the 8."

**Beethoven, Op. 31, No. 2 ("Tempest"), Period II**: The "6" function (cadential 6/4) "churns away" with repeated iterations, "building up to a tremendous discharge of energy." The capacitor/circuit metaphor: "6" builds current until "7" leads to grounding "8."

## Common Confusions

- Expansion does NOT break the periodic structure—it elaborates within it
- Multiple "8" functions can serve different expressive purposes (comic vs. dramatic)
- "Inserted" material is not inferior—it creates the dramatic effect
- The distinction between "essential" and "accessory" is analytical, not evaluative
- Expansion techniques differ between movements and composers—context matters
- Riemann's function numbers during expansion do not correspond to measure numbers
- Some theorists criticize the essential/accessory distinction as theoretically problematic
