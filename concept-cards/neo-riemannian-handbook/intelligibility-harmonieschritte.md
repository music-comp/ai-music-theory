# Intelligibility of Harmonieschritte

## Quick Definition

Riemann's graduated ranking of harmonic progressions by their directness of comprehension, distinguishing between relationships that are immediately understood versus those requiring mental mediation through intermediate chords.

## Formal Definition

**Intelligibility** (*Verständlichkeit*) in Riemann's Harmonieschritte system refers to:
- The degree to which a harmonic progression is directly comprehensible
- Whether a relationship requires "skipped-over" mediating chords
- The hierarchy from "directly intelligible" to "scarcely comprehensible"

### Riemann's Classification

**Directly Intelligible** (16 relationships):
- Understood without reference to intermediate chords
- Have "cadential force" in retrograde
- Form the core of harmonic practice

**Requiring Mediation** (9 relationships):
- Understood through reference to a "skipped-over" Klang
- Lead to expectation of the mediating chord
- More suited for modulation than local harmony

## The Sixteen Directly Intelligible Schritte/Wechsel

From the *Systematik der Harmonieschritte*:

### Category I: Seitenwechsel
- **Seitenwechsel**: Mode change on same root (always direct)

### Category II: Quintschritte
- **Schlichte Quintschritt**: Up fifth (dominant direction)
- **Gegenquintschritt**: Down fifth (subdominant direction)
- **Quintwechsel**: Fifth + mode change

### Category III: Terzschritte
- **Schlichte Terzschritt**: Up major third
- **Gegenterzschritt**: Down major third (up minor sixth)
- **Terzwechsel**: Third + mode change (relative relationship)
- **Gegenterzwechsel**: "Scarcely comprehensible" but admitted

### Category IV: Kleinterzschritte
- **Schlichte Kleinterzschritt**: Up major sixth / down minor third
- **Gegenkleinterzschritt**: Up minor third
- **Kleinterzwechsel**: Minor third relation + mode
- **Gegenkleinterzwechsel**: "Scarcely comprehensible" but admitted

### Category V: Halbtonschritte
- **Schlichte Leittonschritt**: Up semitone (leading tone)
- **Gegenleittonschritt**: Down semitone
- **Leittonwechsel**: L in PLR notation

## The Nine Requiring Mediation

### Ganztonschritte (Whole-tone)
All four require mediation through the Quintklang:
- **Schlichte Ganztonschritt**: Up whole tone
- **Gegenganztonschritt**: Down whole tone
- **Ganztonwechsel**: Whole tone + mode
- **Gegenganztonwechsel**: Inverse of above

Reason: The whole-tone interval is dissonant; understood as two fifths.

### Tritonusschritte (Tritone)
Most require mediation:
- **Schlichte Tritonusschritt**: Up tritone
- **Gegentritonusschritt**: Down tritone
- **Tritonuswechsel**: Tritone + mode (within scale, so more direct)
- **Doppelterzwechsel**: Common tone makes it somewhat direct

### The Gegenquintwechsel
Requires mediation through either:
- The Gegenquintklang
- The Seitenwechselklang

## Criteria for Intelligibility

### Factors Favoring Directness
1. **Acoustic interval**: Fifths and thirds more immediate than seconds or tritones
2. **Common tones**: More shared tones = more direct
3. **Diatonic membership**: Scale-based relationships more familiar
4. **Derivational simplicity**: Fewer generator steps = more direct

### The Correlation is Imperfect
Riemann's intelligibility judgments don't perfectly align with:
- Word length in generators
- Common-tone count
- Any single metric

He acknowledged his classifications included subjective judgment.

## Analytical Implications

### Expectations and Continuation
When a "requiring mediation" Schritt occurs:
- The skipped-over chord is "expected"
- Good compositional practice provides it
- The mediation restores intelligibility

### Modulation vs. Prolongation
- Directly intelligible: Can occur within a key
- Requiring mediation: Better suited for modulation

### Cadential Force
Directly intelligible relationships in retrograde:
- "Have cadential force" returning to tonic
- Function as authentic or plagal cadential motion
- This is a test of intelligibility

## Neo-Riemannian Departure

### All Operations Equal
Neo-Riemannian theory abandons intelligibility hierarchy:
- All 24 operations are structurally equivalent
- Coherence comes from group properties, not acoustic immediacy
- No relationships are privileged

### Embracing the "Chaos"
What Riemann called "scarcely comprehensible":
- Neo-Riemannians analyze without stigma
- Late Romantic music often uses these relationships
- The transformation is valid regardless of "difficulty"

### Engebretsen's Observation
Neo-Riemannian theory represents:
- Riemann's "horror vision" realized
- Any chord can follow any other
- But structure emerges from transformational logic

## Common Confusions

- **Intelligibility ≠ difficulty of performance**: This is conceptual, not technical
- **Not purely acoustic**: Later Riemann grounded this in psychology
- **Context matters**: The same Schritt may be more or less intelligible depending on surroundings
