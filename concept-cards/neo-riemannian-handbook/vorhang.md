# Quick Definition

A *Vorhang* ("curtain") is Riemann's designation for introductory measures at the opening of a period that function as a kind of preface or frame, fulfilling periodic functions (typically 6-7-8 or 3-4) but standing apart from the main thematic material that follows.

# Core Definition

*Vorhang* (German: "curtain") is Riemann's analytical device for handling contrasting introductory material within the periodic framework. When a movement begins with material distinct from the main theme, Riemann designates it as a Vorhang that fulfills specific periodic functions before the main period continues with function 1. Burnham notes Riemann's use in Op. 31, No. 2 ("Tempest") where the Largo is designated as "a 3-4 pair, and also as a *Vorhang*, or curtain" (p. 447). "The notion of a *Vorhang* that is then swept into the first full period of the movement both honors the extreme contrast but also encourages us to hear the entire opening as a single harmonic process" (p. 447). In Op. 31, No. 1, "Riemann again figures the contrasting opening measures as a *Vorhang*, though this *Vorhang* fulfills periodic functions 6 through 8" (p. 447).

# Prerequisites

- **Periodic function** — The numbered functions (1-8) that a Vorhang occupies within the period

# Key Properties

1. Designates introductory material as a preface or frame to the main period
2. The Vorhang fulfills periodic functions, typically higher-numbered ones (6-7-8 or 3-4)
3. After the Vorhang, the main period "continues afresh" with functions 1 through 8
4. Honors extreme contrasts within a theme while encouraging hearing the whole as a single harmonic process
5. A Vorhang can generate additional "8" functions, contributing to closural proliferation
6. The combination of Vorhang + full period can be repeated, multiplying cadential arrivals

# Construction / Recognition

## To Identify a Vorhang

1. Look for contrasting introductory material at the start of a movement or theme
2. The material is distinct in character from what follows (e.g., Largo vs. Allegro, running figure vs. chordal theme)
3. Riemann assigns periodic functions to this material, integrating it into the period structure
4. The main theme then begins "afresh" with function 1
5. The Vorhang creates additional cadential arrivals (8 functions) before the main period

## Typical Structures

```
Vorhang:    [6  7  8]  | Period I: [1  2  3  4  5  6  7  8]
            curtain      main theme

Or:

Vorhang:    [3  4]     | Period continues: [5  6  7  8]
            curtain      main continuation
```

# Context & Application

The Vorhang device allows Riemann to preserve his eight-measure periodic model while accounting for the dramatic contrasts characteristic of Beethoven's mature style. Without it, the Largo/Allegro juxtaposition in the Tempest sonata or the running figure/chordal theme contrast in Op. 31, No. 1 would resist periodic parsing. The concept connects to Riemann's historical observation that the "modern theme" (Mannheim style onward) is characterized by "the union of a greater number of different motives into a greater unified configuration" (Riemann, *Grosse Kompositionslehre*, cited p. 446).

# Examples

**Example 1** (p. 447, Ex. 16.5): Beethoven, Op. 31, No. 2 ("Tempest"), opening. The Largo (mm. 1-6) is designated as functions 3-4 and as Vorhang. The extreme contrast between Largo and Allegro is "stitched together" into a single period.

**Example 2** (pp. 447-448, Ex. 16.6): Beethoven, Op. 31, No. 1, opening. The two-measure running figure functions as Vorhang (functions 6-7-8). Period I continues with functions 1-8. "Because of Riemann's reading of the *Vorhang* measures, by the time we get to the end of Period II, we have heard no less than seven 8 functions" (p. 448).

# Common Errors

- **Error**: Treating Vorhang material as outside the periodic structure
  **Correction**: The Vorhang receives periodic function assignments and is analytically integrated

# Common Confusions

- **Confusion**: Thinking a Vorhang is the same as a slow introduction
  **Clarification**: While it may correspond to introductory material, Vorhang is specifically an analytical designation within Riemann's periodic framework — it must fulfill periodic functions

- **Confusion**: Believing all introductory passages qualify as Vorhang
  **Clarification**: Only material that can be assigned periodic functions and integrated into the period structure qualifies as Vorhang
