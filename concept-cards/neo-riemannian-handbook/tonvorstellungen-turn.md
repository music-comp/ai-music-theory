# Quick Definition

Riemann's decisive shift from grounding harmonic dualism in acoustic phenomena (overtones, undertones) to grounding it in psychological processes of active musical hearing -- the mind's capacity to organize, select, and complete tone relationships into Klang-representations.

# Core Definition

The *Tonvorstellungen* turn refers to Riemann's reconception of musical hearing as an active mental process rather than passive acoustic reception. In "Das Problem" (1905), Riemann declares: "The objectively produced Klange are ultimately only a sort of raw material for musical hearing" (p. 188). The mind **selects** which acoustic phenomena to attend to, **orders** tones into relationships of "greatest simplicity," and **completes** partial information into full Klang-representations.

This shift allowed Riemann to maintain dualism without defending acoustic undertones: if neither major nor minor consonance derives from physical phenomena, both can be grounded equally in the mind's organizing activity.

- **Harmonic dualism** -- The theoretical framework whose foundations are being relocated

# Key Properties

1. Musical hearing is active, selective, and interpretive -- not merely receptive
2. The mind organizes tones into relationships of "greatest simplicity"
3. Even major consonance cannot be fully justified acoustically
4. Both major and minor consonance derive from the same mental organizing principle
5. *Tonvorstellungen* (tone imaginations/representations) are the proper object of music theory

# Construction / Recognition

## The Argument Structure
1. **Negative claim**: Acoustic phenomena cannot explain consonance (even major)
2. **Positive claim**: The mind actively organizes sounds into Klang-representations
3. **Consequence**: Both major and minor are grounded equally in psychological processes
4. **Result**: Dualism is freed from defending physical undertones

## Intellectual Context
- Carl Stumpf's *Tonpsychologie* (1883-1890): Moved toward psychology of tone
- Rise of Gestalt psychology (von Ehrenfels): Perception as active organization
- Hermann Lotze's aesthetics: Distinguished intensity (frequency) from mass (wavelength)
- Music psychology emerging as academic discipline

# Context & Application

The Tonvorstellungen turn was long in development. Riemann had adumbrated psychological themes since "Die Natur der Harmonik" (1882). But the 1905 article marks the explicit, public abandonment of acoustic foundations.

The shift had profound implications: it anticipated 20th-century psychoacoustic approaches and established that musical hearing is interpretive. As Klumpenhouwer notes (Ch. 6), entering a dualist "experiential world" is a legitimate choice of analytical perspective -- not a claim about physical reality.

# Examples

**Riemann's key declaration** (p. 188): "Once one has truly divested oneself of the idea of wanting to derive the principles of consonance and dissonance from [acoustical] phenomena, then there is no longer in fact anything problematic about the logical opposition of major and minor."

**The practical consequence** (p. 190): "A truly elementary book of harmony along dualistic lines will eschew all speculative theoretical questions as to prime causes and completely avoid aggressive or defensive polemics." Theory shifts from physics to pedagogy.

**Stumpf's influence** (p. 188): Riemann acknowledges that Stumpf "was on the right track" in seeking psychological explanations, though Stumpf "covered himself with more and more debris the longer he labored."

# Common Errors

- **Error**: Treating the psychological turn as a retreat or concession
  **Correction**: Riemann framed it as an advance -- recognizing that even major consonance needed psychological grounding freed both modes equally

# Common Confusions

- **Confusion**: Tonvorstellungen means merely "hearing" or "listening"
  **Clarification**: It refers specifically to the mind's active organizing, selecting, and completing of tone relationships -- an interpretive process, not passive reception

- **Confusion**: The psychological turn made dualism subjective and unfalsifiable
  **Clarification**: While shifting from physics to psychology, Riemann maintained that the mathematical relationships (same number series, different domains) provide objective structure
