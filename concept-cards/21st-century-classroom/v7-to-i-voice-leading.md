# Quick Definition

The V7 to I progression can be voice led using either "strict" resolution (all tendency tones resolve normally, producing an incomplete I) or "free" resolution (leading tone in inner voice may descend to ^5, producing a complete I).

# Core Definition

Voice leading V7 to I requires choosing between two methods (Hutchinson, Ch. 27, pp. 406-407):

**Strict resolution**: (a) Complete V7 to incomplete I -- resolve ^7 up, ^4 and ^2 down; results in tripled root and one 3rd in I. (b) Incomplete V7 (no 5th) to complete I.

**Free resolution**: If ^7 is NOT in the soprano, it may resolve down a 3rd to ^5, resulting in a complete I chord.

# Prerequisites

- **Seventh chord resolution** -- V7-I is the most important application of seventh resolution
- **Rules of melody** -- Tendency tone resolution governs the procedure

# Key Properties

1. Strict: complete V7 --> incomplete I (tripled root + one 3rd)
2. Strict: incomplete V7 (no 5th) --> complete I
3. Free: ^7 in inner voice may drop to ^5 (produces complete I)
4. Free resolution only when leading tone is NOT in soprano
5. ^4 (7th of V7) always resolves down to ^3
6. ^2 (5th of V7) resolves down to ^1

# Construction / Recognition

## Strict Resolution (Complete V7 to Incomplete I):
1. ^7 (3rd of V7) goes UP to ^8
2. ^4 (7th of V7) goes DOWN to ^3
3. ^2 (5th of V7) goes DOWN to ^1
4. Result: I with tripled root and one 3rd

## Strict Resolution (Incomplete V7 to Complete I):
1. Omit ^2 (5th) from V7, double the root
2. ^7 goes UP to ^8
3. ^4 goes DOWN to ^3
4. Result: complete I chord

## Free Resolution:
1. Only use when ^7 is in an INNER voice (not soprano)
2. ^7 drops DOWN a 3rd to ^5
3. Result: complete I chord

# Context & Application

- **Typical contexts**: The most important harmonic motion in tonal music
- **Historical/stylistic notes**: Both strict and free resolutions are musically acceptable; in piano music, "improper" resolution of the 7th sometimes occurs for hand comfort

# Common Errors

- **Error**: Using free resolution when the leading tone is in the soprano
  **Correction**: Free resolution is only for inner-voice leading tones

- **Error**: Thinking an incomplete I chord is wrong
  **Correction**: Strict resolution producing a tripled root is perfectly acceptable

# Common Confusions

- **Confusion**: "Free" resolution means "wrong" resolution
  **Clarification**: Free resolution is a legitimate technique, not an error

- **Confusion**: There is only one way to voice lead V7-I
  **Clarification**: Three options exist: strict complete-to-incomplete, strict incomplete-to-complete, and free
