# Quick Definition
The V7 to I progression can be voice led using either "strict" resolution (all tendency tones resolve, producing an incomplete I) or "free" resolution (leading tone in inner voice may descend to the 5th, producing a complete I).

# Formal Definition
Voice leading V7 to I requires careful handling of tendency tones (scale degrees 7, 4, and 2). Two approaches exist:

**Strict resolution**:
- Leading tone (3rd of V7 = scale degree 7) resolves up to tonic
- The 7th of the chord (scale degree 4) resolves down to scale degree 3
- Scale degree 2 resolves down to tonic
- Results in incomplete I chord (tripled root, one 3rd)
- Alternative: Start with incomplete V7 (no 5th) to get complete I

**Free resolution**:
- If the leading tone is NOT in the soprano, it may resolve down a 3rd to scale degree 5
- Results in a complete I chord

# Rules/Procedures
1. **Strict - Complete V7 to incomplete I**:
   - Scale degree 7 (3rd of V7) goes UP to 8
   - Scale degree 4 (7th of V7) goes DOWN to 3
   - Scale degree 2 (5th of V7) goes DOWN to 1
   - Result: I chord with tripled root and one 3rd
2. **Strict - Incomplete V7 (no 5th) to complete I**:
   - Omit scale degree 2 from V7
   - Results in complete I chord
3. **Free resolution**:
   - Only use when leading tone is in an INNER voice
   - Leading tone (7) drops down a 3rd to scale degree 5
   - Results in complete I chord

# Musical Context
The V7-I progression is the most important harmonic motion in tonal music. The choice between strict and free resolution affects whether the tonic chord is complete or incomplete. Both are musically acceptable, but strict resolution is often preferred in academic exercises.

# Examples
- Strict: V7 (G-B-D-F in C major) to I with B going to C, F going to E, D going to C
- Free: B (in alto) drops to G instead of rising to C

# Common Confusions
- Using free resolution when the leading tone is in the soprano (only use in inner voices)
- Forgetting that strict resolution yields an incomplete I chord (this is acceptable)
- Not considering the incomplete V7 option for achieving a complete I chord
- Confusing "free" resolution with "wrong" - free resolution is correct in the right context
