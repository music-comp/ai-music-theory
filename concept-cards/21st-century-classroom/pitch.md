# Quick Definition
Pitch refers to the highness or lowness of a musical note, determined by the frequency of sound vibrations.

# Formal Definition
Pitch is the perceptual property of sound that allows notes to be ordered on a frequency-related scale from low to high. On a piano keyboard, pitches sound higher as you move to the right and lower as you move to the left.

# Construction/Recognition
- Higher frequency = higher pitch
- Lower frequency = lower pitch
- The piano has 88 distinct pitches
- Pitch is represented visually by placement on a staff (higher = higher pitch)

# Musical Context
Pitch is the fundamental building block of melody and harmony. Every note in music has a specific pitch, and the relationship between pitches creates the intervals, scales, and chords that form the basis of music theory.

# Examples
- Moving right on a piano keyboard produces higher pitches
- Moving left on a piano keyboard produces lower pitches
- The musical alphabet A-B-C-D-E-F-G represents seven basic pitch classes

# Common Confusions
- Pitch vs. volume (loudness): Pitch refers to high/low, not soft/loud
- Pitch class vs. specific pitch: C is a pitch class; C4 is a specific pitch
