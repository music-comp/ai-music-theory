# Quick Definition
A double neighbor (or "neighbor group") occurs when both the upper and lower neighbor tones appear before returning to the original chord tone.

# Core Definition
The double neighbor (sometimes called a "neighbor group") occurs when both the upper and lower neighbor occur before the return to the starting tone. The order can be upper-lower-return or lower-upper-return. The double neighbor is sometimes confused with escape tones and appoggiaturas -- always keep track of chord tones when analyzing.

# Prerequisites
- **Neighbor tone**: Understanding of single neighbor tones

# Key Properties
1. Both upper and lower neighbors appear before returning
2. Order: up-down-return or down-up-return
3. Starting and ending notes are the same chord tone
4. Middle two notes are non-chord tones
5. Sometimes confused with escape tones and appoggiaturas

# Construction / Recognition
1. Look for a chord tone that moves to both upper and lower neighbor before returning
2. The pattern visits both sides of the chord tone
3. All notes except the outer chord tones are NCTs
4. Keep track of the underlying chord to distinguish from other NCT types
5. Label as DN or D.N.

# Context & Application
Double neighbors create more elaborate melodic embellishment than single neighbor tones. They are common in Classical and Romantic music as ornamental figures, and appear in arpeggio embellishment patterns.

# Examples
- C major arpeggio embellished with double neighbors (illustrative)
- Kuhlau, Sonatina in C Major, Op. 55, No. 1, I (1823)
- Mozart, Piano Sonata K. 333, I (1784) -- double neighbor on beat 4 of first measure

# Common Errors
- Confusing with appoggiaturas or escape tones (always check the chord)
- Misidentifying the chord tone anchor

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion with other NCTs**: Without knowing the chord, a double neighbor can look like an escape tone or appoggiatura
- **Always identify the chord first**: The outer notes must be the same chord tone
- **Incomplete double neighbor**: Exists in late Romantic and jazz music
