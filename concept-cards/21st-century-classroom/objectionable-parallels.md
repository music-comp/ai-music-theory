# Quick Definition

Objectionable parallels are parallel fifths and parallel octaves (including their compound equivalents -- twelfths and unisons) that destroy voice independence and must be avoided in traditional voice leading.

# Core Definition

When writing in three or four parts, avoid having any two voices move in parallel fifths or parallel octaves (and their octave equivalents -- parallel twelfths and parallel unisons). In the Renaissance, Baroque, Classical, and Romantic eras, these objectionable parallels were thought to destroy the independence of the voices. Parallel 3rds, 4ths, and 6ths were considered acceptable. Parallel 2nds and 7ths generally were not used (Hutchinson, Ch. 26, p. 390).

# Prerequisites

- **Types of motion** -- Parallel motion is a specific type of similar motion
- **Voice leading** -- Objectionable parallels are a fundamental voice leading concern

# Key Properties

1. Parallel 5ths: two voices in parallel motion at the interval of a perfect 5th
2. Parallel 8ves: two voices in parallel motion at the interval of a perfect 8ve
3. Compound equivalents: parallel 12ths (= 5ths), parallel unisons (= 8ves)
4. Acceptable parallels: 3rds, 4ths, 6ths
5. Not used: parallel 2nds and 7ths
6. Check ALL six voice pairs in SATB writing

# Construction / Recognition

## To Check for Objectionable Parallels:
1. Check every pair of voices between successive chords
2. Identify intervals of 5th, 8ve, 12th, or unison in consecutive chords
3. If both voices move in the SAME direction between these intervals: objectionable parallel
4. Six pairs to check: S-A, S-T, S-B, A-T, A-B, T-B

# Context & Application

- **Typical contexts**: All traditional voice leading from Renaissance through Romantic periods
- **Historical/stylistic notes**: Modern and popular music often breaks this rule deliberately; in academic exercises, avoiding parallels is fundamental

# Common Errors

- **Error**: Only checking soprano-bass and forgetting inner voice pairs
  **Correction**: Check ALL six voice pairs in four-part writing

- **Error**: Not recognizing compound intervals (12ths, unisons)
  **Correction**: 12ths = 5ths and unisons = 8ves for parallel-checking purposes

# Common Confusions

- **Confusion**: All parallel motion is forbidden
  **Clarification**: Only parallel 5ths and 8ves (and their compounds); parallel 3rds and 6ths are fine

- **Confusion**: Direct (hidden) fifths are the same as parallel fifths
  **Clarification**: Parallel fifths require the same interval (P5) in BOTH chords; direct fifths only require arriving at a P5 by similar motion
