# Quick Definition
To invert an interval, move the lower note up an octave (or the upper note down); numeric sizes sum to 9 and qualities reverse (M becomes m, + becomes o, P stays P).

# Core Definition
There are only two notes in an interval, one lower and one higher. To invert an interval, change the position of the notes so the note that was lower is now higher. The original and inverted intervals always sum to 9 for the numeric size. Qualities invert as follows: major inverts to minor (and vice versa), augmented inverts to diminished (and vice versa), and perfect inverts to perfect.

- **Intervals**: The concept being inverted
- **Interval quality**: Understanding the quality categories that interchange

# Key Properties
1. Numeric sizes sum to 9: unison+8ve, 2nd+7th, 3rd+6th, 4th+5th
2. Quality inversions: P stays P, M becomes m, m becomes M, + becomes o, o becomes +
3. M3 inverts to m6; m6 inverts to M3
4. P4 inverts to P5; P5 inverts to P4
5. +4 inverts to o5 (both = 6 half steps, the tritone)
6. Useful for writing intervals below a given note

# Construction / Recognition
- Subtract the interval number from 9 to get the inverted number
- Flip the quality: P->P, M->m, m->M, +->o, o->+
- The inverted interval is the "complement" to complete an octave

# Context & Application
Interval inversion is useful for writing intervals below a given note: invert the interval, find the note above, then write it below. It also explains why certain interval pairs (like M3 and m6) share similar harmonic properties.

# Examples
- M6 (C up to A) inverts to m3 (A up to C): 6+3=9, M becomes m
- P5 (C up to G) inverts to P4 (G up to C): 5+4=9, P stays P
- +4 (C up to F#) inverts to o5 (F# up to C): 4+5=9, + becomes o
- m2 inverts to M7; M2 inverts to m7

# Common Errors
- Adding to 8 instead of 9 (the shared note is counted twice)
- Forgetting that perfect stays perfect when inverted

# Common Confusions
- **Numbers add to 9, not 8**: The shared note is counted in both intervals
- **Perfect stays perfect**: P4 inverts to P5, not to something else
- **Tritone inverts to itself**: +4/o5 both equal 6 half steps, so the tritone is its own inversion
