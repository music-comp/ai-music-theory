# Quick Definition
Process music is composition where the musical structure is determined by a gradual, audible process, making the compositional procedure perceptible to listeners.

# Core Definition
Process music refers to compositions structured by systematic procedures that are audible to the listener. Unlike serial procedures (which may be inaudible), minimalist processes (additive, phase shifting) are designed to be heard. The outcome is determined by initial conditions and the process runs its course once set in motion. Both additive process and phase shifting are examples of perceptible musical processes (synthesized from source context, Ch. 35).

# Prerequisites
- **Minimalism** -- Process music is a concept within the minimalist aesthetic

# Key Properties
1. Systematic procedure generates the music
2. The process is AUDIBLE (not hidden)
3. Change occurs gradually enough to be perceived
4. Once started, the process runs to completion
5. Outcome determined by initial conditions and process
6. Distinguishes from serial music (inaudible processes) and aleatoric music (indeterminate)

# Construction / Recognition
- Identify the process (additive, phase shifting, rotation)
- Verify it is audible to the listener
- Observe systematic, gradual change

# Context & Application
Steve Reich articulated this aesthetic. Both *Piano Phase* (phase shifting) and Glass's *Two Pages* (additive process) are process music. The concept connects to algorithmic composition.

# Examples
- Reich, *Piano Phase*: Phase shifting process
- Reich, *Clapping Music*: Rotation process
- Glass, *Two Pages*: Additive process
- Riley, *In C*: Cell-based process (indeterminate repetition counts)

- **Serialism** -- Serial processes are often inaudible; process music's procedures must be perceptible

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Assuming all minimalist music is process music
  **Correction**: Process music is a subset; some minimalist works use processes that are less strictly systematic

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: Thinking process music is improvised
  **Clarification**: The process determines outcomes systematically; it is not improvisation
