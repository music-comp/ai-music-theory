# Quick Definition
An appoggiatura is a non-chord tone approached by leap and left by step, usually accented (on the beat), making it a particularly expressive embellishment.

# Core Definition
The appoggiatura is approached by leap and left by step. It is usually accented (on the beat), which makes it a particularly expressive non-chord tone. Both the leap to and step away from any appoggiatura can be from any direction -- the leap can be ascending or descending, and the resolution can be upward or downward.

# Prerequisites
- **Non-chord tones overview**: Understanding the classification system

# Key Properties
1. Approached by leap, left by step
2. Usually accented (on the beat)
3. Leap can be from any direction (up or down)
4. Resolution step can be in any direction (up or down)
5. Particularly expressive due to accented dissonance
6. Creates strong tension because the dissonance is metrically stressed

# Construction / Recognition
1. Look for a non-chord tone approached by leap
2. Confirm it resolves by step
3. Note that it is typically on a strong beat
4. Label as App. or APP

# Context & Application
Appoggiaturas create strong melodic tension because they are accented dissonances that resolve by step. They are common in expressive, lyrical passages in both classical and popular music. The accented nature distinguishes them from most other NCTs.

# Examples
- "My Heart Will Go On" (Horner/Jennings, 1997) -- appoggiatura creating emotional expression
- Bruce Springsteen, "Born to Run" (1975) -- appoggiatura
- Mozart, Symphony No. 40, K. 550, II -- appoggiaturas resolving upward
- "Want to Want Me" (2015) -- appoggiatura in bar 2

# Common Errors
- Confusing with suspensions (appoggiaturas are approached by leap, not same note)
- Assuming appoggiaturas only resolve downward (they can resolve up or down)

# Common Confusions
- **Not a suspension**: Suspensions are prepared by the same note; appoggiaturas arrive by leap
- **Can resolve up or down**: Both the leap and the step can be in any direction
- **Inverse of escape tone**: Appoggiatura = leap then step; escape tone = step then leap
