# Quick Definition
Eleventh chords include all members up to the 11th (root through 11th); the 11th is the 4th an octave higher and comes from the major scale unless specified otherwise.

# Core Definition
The 11th in a jazz chord is the 4th scale degree an octave higher, coming from the major scale unless otherwise specified. When "11" appears in a chord symbol, all members up to the 11th are assumed present. In practice, the unaltered 11th clashes with the major 3rd, so experienced jazz musicians omit the 11th when playing major-3rd chords. The textbook uses specific notation like C9(13) to clarify which extensions are included (Hutchinson, Ch. 31, p. 447).

# Key Properties
1. "11" is "4" an octave higher
2. 11th comes from the major scale unless otherwise specified
3. All numbers up to the highest are assumed (C11 = root through 11th)
4. Unaltered 11th clashes with major 3rd; often omitted in practice
5. #11 avoids the clash and is common with major and dominant chords
6. "11 vs. sus" rule: label as "sus" if 3rd is absent; "11" if 3rd is present

# Construction / Recognition
**11 vs. sus convention (Section 31.2):**
- C7sus = C-F-G-Bb (no 3rd; the 4th replaces it)
- C11 = C-E-G-Bb-D-F (3rd present alongside 11th)
- C7(#11) = C-E-G-Bb-F# (raised 11th avoids clash)

# Context & Application
Eleventh chords provide additional color in jazz harmony. The #11 is particularly common in Lydian-flavored voicings. The 11 vs. sus distinction is an important notational convention that signals whether the 3rd is present or absent.

# Examples
- C11 = C-E-G-Bb-D-F (p. 447)
- C7sus = C-F-G-Bb (p. 448)
- CΔ7(#11) = C-E-G-B-F# (Lydian sound)
- Cm11 = C-Eb-G-Bb-D-F (no clash because minor 3rd)

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Including both unaltered 11th and major 3rd in a voicing
  **Correction**: The unaltered 11th clashes with the major 3rd; omit one or use #11

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: Confusing "sus" with "11"
  **Clarification**: "Sus" means the 4th replaces the 3rd; "11" means both 3rd and 11th are present
- **Confusion**: Thinking C13 includes only root, 3rd, 5th, 7th, and 13th
  **Clarification**: C13 implies all members through 13th; the 11th is often omitted in practice
