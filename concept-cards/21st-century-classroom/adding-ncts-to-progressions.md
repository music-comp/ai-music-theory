# Quick Definition
Different melodic motions in the framework (repeated notes, steps, leaps) suggest different types of non-chord tone embellishments, from neighbor tones for repeated notes to passing tones for thirds.

# Formal Definition
Adding non-chord tones systematically depends on the underlying melodic motion in the voice being embellished:
- **Repeated notes**: Add upper or lower neighbors
- **Stepwise descent**: Add suspensions, escape tones, double neighbors, chromatic passing tones, or anticipations
- **Stepwise ascent**: Add chromatic passing tones, anticipations, appoggiaturas, double neighbors, or retardations
- **Leap of a 3rd**: Fill with passing tone, or add appoggiatura
- **Leaps of 4th or larger**: Generally do not embellish

# Rules/Procedures
1. **Repeated note**: Add upper or lower neighbor
2. **Step down**: Options include:
   - Suspension (delay resolution)
   - Escape tone (step opposite direction, then leap)
   - Double neighbor
   - Chromatic passing tone
   - Anticipation
3. **Step up**: Options include:
   - Chromatic passing tone
   - Anticipation
   - Appoggiatura (leap beyond, place on beat, resolve after)
   - Double neighbor
   - Retardation (rarely used - upward suspension)
4. **Leap of 3rd**: Options include:
   - Fill with diatonic passing tone
   - Add appoggiatura
5. **Leap of 4th or larger**: Do not embellish (for now)
6. When adding NCTs to multiple voices on same beat, form consonant intervals (3rds, 5ths, 6ths, 8ves)
7. If consonances cannot be formed, stagger NCTs rhythmically

# Musical Context
These embellishment strategies create the florid melodic lines found in Bach chorales and other Baroque music. The systematic approach ensures stylistically appropriate ornamentation.

# Examples
- Repeated C to C: Add D as upper neighbor or B as lower neighbor
- E descending to D: Add suspension (E held, resolves to D) or escape tone (E-F-D)
- C ascending to D: Add C# as chromatic passing tone
- C to E (3rd): Add D as passing tone

# Common Confusions
- Trying to embellish leaps larger than a 3rd (not recommended)
- Not considering multiple embellishment options for the same motion
- Adding NCTs without checking for parallels with other voices
- Placing simultaneous NCTs in dissonant intervals
