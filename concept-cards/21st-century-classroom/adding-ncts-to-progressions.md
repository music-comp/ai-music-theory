# Quick Definition

Different melodic motions in the framework (repeated notes, steps, leaps) suggest different embellishment types: neighbors for repeated notes, suspensions/escape tones for stepwise descent, passing tones for thirds, and no embellishment for leaps of a 4th or larger.

# Core Definition

Adding non-chord tones systematically depends on the underlying melodic motion (Hutchinson, Ch. 28, pp. 412-415):
1. **Repeated note**: upper or lower neighbor
2. **Step down**: suspension, escape tone, double neighbor, chromatic passing tone, anticipation
3. **Step up**: chromatic passing tone, anticipation, appoggiatura, double neighbor, retardation (rare)
4. **Leap of 3rd**: diatonic passing tone, or appoggiatura
5. **Leap of 4th or larger**: do not embellish

# Key Properties

1. Match the NCT type to the underlying motion
2. Repeated note: neighbor tones
3. Stepwise motion: many options (suspensions, escape tones, anticipations, etc.)
4. Leap of 3rd: fill with passing tone or add appoggiatura
5. Large leaps (4th+): leave unembellished
6. When NCTs occur in multiple voices on same beat, form consonant intervals (3rds, 5ths, 6ths, 8ves)
7. If consonance impossible, stagger NCTs rhythmically

# Construction / Recognition

## Embellishment Guide:
- **Repeated note**: Add upper or lower neighbor
- **Step down**: Suspension (delay), escape tone (step opposite then leap), double neighbor, chromatic PT, anticipation
- **Step up**: Chromatic PT, anticipation, appoggiatura (leap beyond, resolve), double neighbor, retardation
- **3rd**: Diatonic passing tone or appoggiatura
- **4th+**: Do not embellish

# Context & Application

- **Typical contexts**: Enriching chorale-style voice leading with melodic interest

# Common Errors

- **Error**: Embellishing leaps larger than a 3rd
  **Correction**: Leaps of 4th or larger should not be embellished (for now)

- **Error**: Placing simultaneous NCTs in dissonant intervals
  **Correction**: Simultaneous NCTs should form 3rds, 5ths, 6ths, or 8ves; stagger if necessary

# Common Confusions

- **Confusion**: There is only one embellishment option per motion type
  **Clarification**: Most motion types offer multiple embellishment choices
