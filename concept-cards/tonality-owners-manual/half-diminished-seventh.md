# Quick Definition

A seventh chord built on a diminished triad with a minor seventh (e.g., B-D-F-A), which gains increased usage in the nineteenth century as part of the expanding functional harmonic vocabulary.

# Core Definition

The half-diminished seventh chord (ii-half-dim-7) is a diatonic chord that becomes increasingly prominent in later functional practice. Tymoczko notes that V-vii-half-dim-7 can support melodic scale-degrees 7-6-5 against bass 5-4-3 as parallel tenths, often decorated by octave displacement (p. 315). The half-diminished seventh is listed alongside the common-tone diminished seventh (ct-dim-7) as a characteristic feature of the expanding harmonic penumbra in the nineteenth century (Fig. 7.1.7). Its increased use reflects the general loosening of harmonic restrictions after the classical-era bottleneck described by the "hourglass model" of functional history, where Haydn, Mozart, and Beethoven represent the point of maximal harmonic regimentation (p. 315).

# Key Properties

1. Built on a diminished triad with a minor seventh (e.g., B-D-F-A)
2. Typically functions as ii-half-dim-7 (pre-dominant) in minor keys
3. V-vii-half-dim-7 supports melodic 7-6-5 against bass 5-4-3 in parallel tenths
4. Increases in prominence during the nineteenth century (Fig. 7.1.7)
5. Part of the expanding functional "penumbra" after the classical-era bottleneck

# Construction / Recognition

## To Identify:
1. Look for a diminished triad (root, minor third, diminished fifth) with a minor seventh above the root
2. In minor keys, check for ii-half-dim-7 functioning as a pre-dominant
3. In the progression V-vii-half-dim-7, listen for parallel tenths between outer voices (7-6-5 over 5-4-3)

# Context & Application

The half-diminished seventh's historical trajectory reflects the broader "hourglass" pattern of functional harmony. During the classical era, harmonic vocabulary contracted to a core set of progressions; in the nineteenth century, the vocabulary expanded again, with the half-diminished seventh and common-tone diminished seventh becoming increasingly important. The chord also appears in earlier music within specific contrapuntal schemas, particularly the V-vii-half-dim-7 parallel-tenths pattern.

# Common Errors

- **Error**: Confusing the half-diminished seventh with the fully diminished seventh
  **Correction**: The half-diminished has a minor seventh interval; the fully diminished has a diminished seventh. They serve different functional roles

# Common Confusions

- **Confusion**: Thinking ii-half-dim-7 and vii-half-dim-7 serve the same function despite sharing chord quality
  **Clarification**: ii-half-dim-7 is pre-dominant while vii-half-dim-7 is dominant-function; same quality but different harmonic roles
