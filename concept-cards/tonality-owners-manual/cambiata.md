# Quick Definition

The sole surviving incomplete neighbor in the Palestrina style -- a dissonant note approached by step and left by leap (typically a descending third), which then resolves by step.

# Core Definition

The cambiata (nota cambiata or "changing tone") is the only incomplete neighbor retained in the strict Palestrina style after the elimination of the various incomplete neighbors found in earlier polyphony (p. 212). It typically involves a stepwise approach to a dissonance followed by a leap of a third in the opposite direction, with subsequent stepwise resolution. Jeppesen notes that accented third-species dissonances are acceptable for Palestrina only when they form this specific figure (fn. 14).

# Key Properties

1. Stepwise approach to a dissonance
2. Leap away (typically descending third) in the opposite direction
3. Subsequent stepwise resolution
4. The sole incomplete neighbor surviving in strict Palestrina style
5. Accented third-species dissonances are acceptable only in this figure (Jeppesen)

# Construction / Recognition

## To Identify a Cambiata:
1. Locate a dissonant note in the third-species (quarter note) context
2. Verify it is approached by step
3. Verify it is left by leap (typically a third in the opposite direction)
4. Check for subsequent stepwise resolution

# Context & Application

The cambiata is characteristic of the strict Renaissance style and represents the one exception to the SNAP framework's otherwise complete coverage of Palestrina's nonharmonic tones. Earlier polyphony (Josquin, Clemens) features a wider array of incomplete neighbors, upward-resolving suspensions, and other nonstandard dissonances that the Palestrina style eliminates.

# Common Errors

- **Error**: Treating any incomplete neighbor as a cambiata
  **Correction**: The cambiata is a specific figure with stepwise-approach/leap-away/stepwise-resolution profile

# Common Confusions

- **Confusion**: Thinking the cambiata is a generic incomplete neighbor
  **Clarification**: In the strict Palestrina style, the cambiata is the only form of incomplete neighbor permitted, with a specific melodic profile
