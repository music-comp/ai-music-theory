# Quick Definition
The smallest level of segmentation analysis -- short grouping units (often two measures) that contain the motivic material for a work, combining together to create subphrases or phrases.

# Core Definition
**Ideas** are "short grouping units that contain the motivic material for the work. They are often two measures long, but they may be longer or shorter. Ideas may group together to create subphrases or phrases." The idea level is the smallest level of a segmentation analysis, shown with square brackets above the staff (Ch. 1).

# Prerequisites
- **Formal hierarchy** -- Ideas are one level in the hierarchy

# Key Properties
1. Smallest level of segmentation analysis
2. Often two measures long (but variable)
3. Contain motivic material
4. Group together into subphrases or phrases
5. Shown with square brackets in analysis

# Context & Application
Ideas serve as the building blocks of larger structures. In a sentence, the basic idea is a specific type of idea. In a period, both the basic idea and contrasting idea are at the idea level.
