# Quick Definition
A four-stage opening schema with melody do-sol-do-do over bass do-ti-la-mi, alternating between 5/3 and 6 chords (I-V-vi-I), featuring a characteristic descending bass from tonic through leading tone to submediant.

# Core Definition
The **Romanesca** is an opening gambit with four stages: melody do(1)-sol(5)-do(1)-do(1) over bass do(1)-ti(7)-la(6)-mi(3). Meter: S-W-S-W. Figured bass: 5-6-5-6. Roman numerals: I-V-vi-I. Its descending bass through the upper tetrachord and alternating 5/3-6 pattern is characteristic (Ch. 12).
