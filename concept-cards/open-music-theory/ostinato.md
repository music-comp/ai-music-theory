# Quick Definition
An ostinato is a repeated rhythmic or pitched musical idea, typically one or more measures long, that persists throughout a section or piece, providing a structural foundation and creating atmosphere. The repetition can involve pitch, rhythm, or both.

# Core Definition
An ostinato is a persistently repeated musical pattern. It can be rhythmic only, melodic, or both. The repetition creates a structural backbone over which other musical elements develop. Ostinato is fundamental to many musical traditions: ground bass in Baroque music, riff-based structures in rock and pop, loop-based electronic music, and minimalist composition. In 20th-century orchestral music, ostinato patterns often create atmospheric or dramatic effects.

# Key Properties
1. Persistent repetition of a musical pattern
2. Can be rhythmic, melodic, or both
3. Typically one or more measures long
4. Provides structural foundation
5. Can create foreboding, militaristic, or hypnotic effects
6. Found across virtually all musical traditions

# Context & Application
Ostinato is one of the most universal musical devices, from Baroque ground bass to 20th-century rhythmic techniques to minimalism and popular music.
