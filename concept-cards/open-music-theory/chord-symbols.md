# Chord Symbols

## Quick Definition

A shorthand notation system using letters, numbers, and symbols to indicate chord root, quality, and sometimes inversion, commonly used in jazz, pop, and lead sheet notation.

## Formal Definition

**Chord symbols** (also called **lead sheet symbols** or **jazz notation**) are alphanumeric labels that specify:
1. **Root**: Capital letter (with accidental if needed): C, D♭, F♯, etc.
2. **Quality**: Abbreviation or symbol indicating chord type
3. **Bass note** (optional): Slash notation for inversions or alternate bass

**Standard quality indicators**:

**Triads**:
- **Major**: Capital letter alone (C, F, A♭)
- **Minor**: "mi" or "m" or "-" (Cmi, Cm, C-)
- **Diminished**: superscript circle "°" or "dim" (C°, Cdim)
- **Augmented**: "+" or "aug" (C+, Caug)

**Seventh chords**:
- **Dominant seventh**: "7" (C7 = C-E-G-B♭)
- **Major seventh**: "maj7", "ma7", or "M7" or "△7" (Cmaj7 = C-E-G-B)
- **Minor seventh**: "mi7", "m7", or "-7" (Cmi7 = C-E♭-G-B♭)
- **Half-diminished seventh**: "ø7" or "mi7(♭5)" (Cø7 = C-E♭-G♭-B♭)
- **Fully diminished seventh**: "°7" or "dim7" (C°7 = C-E♭-G♭-B𝄫)

**Slash notation** (inversions/alternate bass):
- Cmi/E♭ = C minor triad with E♭ in bass (first inversion)
- C/G = C major triad with G in bass (second inversion)
- C/B♭ = C major triad over B♭ bass (not part of triad)

**Extensions and alterations**:
- Add: Cadd9 (C major with added ninth, no seventh)
- Sus: Csus4 (suspended fourth replaces third)
- Altered: C7(♭9) (dominant seventh with lowered ninth)

## Musical Context

Chord symbols serve practical functions:
- **Lead sheets**: Provide harmonic framework without full notation
- **Jazz**: Standard notation for improvisation and comping
- **Pop/rock**: Guitar chord charts and vocal lead sheets
- **Flexibility**: Allow performers to choose voicing and rhythm
- **Transposition**: Easy to read in any key

Chord symbols assume performer knowledge of voice-leading and stylistic conventions.

## Examples

### Basic

**Triads**:
- C = C major (C-E-G)
- Fmi = F minor (F-A♭-C)
- B° = B diminished (B-D-F)
- G+ = G augmented (G-B-D♯)

**Seventh chords**:
- G7 = G dominant seventh (G-B-D-F)
- Dmaj7 = D major seventh (D-F♯-A-C♯)
- Ami7 = A minor seventh (A-C-E-G)
- Bø7 = B half-diminished seventh (B-D-F-A)

**Inversions**:
- C/E = C major with E in bass (first inversion)
- Cmi/G = C minor with G in bass (second inversion)
- C7/B♭ = C dominant seventh with B♭ in bass (third inversion)

### From Repertoire

Jazz fake books: "Autumn Leaves" (Cmi7, F7, B♭maj7, E♭maj7, Aø7, D7, Gmi7). Pop songs: "Let It Be" (C, G, Ami, F). Guitar songbooks use chord symbols exclusively. Real Book standard notation for jazz standards.

## Common Confusions

- Multiple systems exist: "Cmi" vs. "Cm" vs. "C-" (all mean C minor)
- "C7" = dominant seventh (C-E-G-B♭), NOT major seventh (which is "Cmaj7")
- Triangle symbol (△) = major seventh, not just major triad
- Slash doesn't always mean inversion: C/B♭ has non-chord tone in bass
- "Add" vs. extensions: Cadd9 = no seventh; C9 = includes seventh
- Chord symbols ≠ figured bass (different systems with different purposes)
- Quality symbol placement: lowercase "mi" AFTER root letter (not before)
- Circle (°) = diminished; half-circle (ø) = half-diminished
