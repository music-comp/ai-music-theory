# Quick Definition
A three-stage continuation schema (described by Riepel) that sustains the dominant throughout -- melody sol-ti-re over static bass sol-sol-sol -- functioning as a "bridge" prolonging dominant harmony.

# Core Definition
The **Ponte** (Italian: "bridge," described by Riepel) has three stages: melody sol(5)-ti(7)-re(2) over bass sol(5)-sol(5)-sol(5). Meter: S-W-S. Figured bass: 5-7-7. Roman numerals: V-V-V. Unlike Fonte (descending) and Monte (ascending), the Ponte is static, prolonging dominant harmony as a bridge (Ch. 12).
