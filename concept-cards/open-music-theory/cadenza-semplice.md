# Quick Definition
A four-stage cadential schema with melody do-re-re-do over bass mi-fa-sol-do, harmonized I-ii-V-I, representing the simplest complete cadential formula in the galant tradition.

# Core Definition
The **Cadenza Semplice** ("simple cadence") has four stages: melody do(1)-re(2)-re(2)-do(1) over bass mi(3)-fa(4)-sol(5)-do(1). Meter: W-S-W-S. Figured bass: 6-6/5-5-5. Roman numerals: I-ii-V-I. It is the simplest of the cadential schemas, moving through predominant (ii) and dominant (V) to tonic (I) (Ch. 12).

# Key Properties

3. I-ii-V-I harmonic progression
4. Simplest complete cadential formula
5. Cadential function
