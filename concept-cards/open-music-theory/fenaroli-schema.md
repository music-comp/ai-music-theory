# Quick Definition
A four-stage pre-cadential schema with melody fa-mi-ti-do over bass ti-do-re-mi, alternating between V-I through 6/5-5-6/5-6 figured bass, serving as a pre-cadential or incomplete cadence.

# Core Definition
The **Fenaroli** schema has four stages: melody fa(4)-mi(3)-ti(7)-do(1) over bass ti(7)-do(1)-re(2)-mi(3). Meter: S-W-S-W. Figured bass: 6/5-5-6/5-6. Roman numerals: V-I-V-I. It functions as a pre-cadential or incomplete cadence, alternating between dominant and tonic resolutions (Ch. 12).
