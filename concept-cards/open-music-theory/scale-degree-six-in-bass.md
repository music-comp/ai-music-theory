# Quick Definition

Scale degree 6 (la) in the bass is commonly harmonized by vi or IV6, and its function depends on its position in the phrase: at beginnings it prolongs tonic, at middles it connects tonic to predominant areas or creates deceptive motion, and at endings it appears in phrygian half cadences or as a predominant stand-in.

# Core Definition

La (^6) in the bass is commonly harmonized by two chords: **vi** or **IV6** (OMT, Ch. IV.11). Its usage depends on position within the phrase:

**At phrase beginnings**: prolongs tonic in two ways:
- IV6 as predominant: I-IV6-V6/5-I
- Plagal (IV6): I-(IV6)-I6

**At phrase middles**: two functions:
- Deceptive motion: V(7)-vi (avoids cadence)
- Connecting T to strong PD: do-la-fa (^1-^6-^4), harmonized by vi

**At phrase endings**: two functions:
- Phrygian half cadence: iv6-V in minor (^b6-^5)
- Stand-in for strong PD fa (^4) before cadential dominant

# Prerequisites

- **Harmonic function** — La's function depends on its position in the phrase model
- **Tonic prolongation** — La at beginnings serves prolongational purposes
- **Strong predominant** — La at endings can substitute for the strong PD

# Key Properties

1. Harmonized by vi or IV6
2. Function determined by phrase position (beginning, middle, ending)
3. At beginnings: prolongs tonic (two progressions)
4. At middles: deceptive motion or T-to-PD connection
5. At endings: PHC (minor only) or PD stand-in
6. I-IV6-V6/5-I does not work well in minor (augmented second ^b6-^#7)

# Construction / Recognition

## To Determine Function:
1. Identify where la (^6) appears in the phrase
2. Beginning: check if it prolongs tonic (followed by return to I)
3. Middle: check if after V (deceptive motion) or connecting T to PD
4. Ending: check if PHC (minor, iv6-V) or PD substitute

# Examples

**Example 1** (OMT Ch. IV.11, Example 1): I-IV6-V6/5-I in Josephine Lang, Arie.

**Example 2** (OMT Ch. IV.11, Example 5): Deceptive motion in Crussell, Clarinet Quartet Op. 7, II.

**Example 3** (OMT Ch. IV.11, Example 9): PHC in Schubert, "Die Mainacht."
