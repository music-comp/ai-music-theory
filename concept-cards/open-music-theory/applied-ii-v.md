# Applied ii-V

## Quick Definition

A ii-V-I progression transposed to tonicize a chord other than the tonic, treating that chord as a temporary "I" chord.

## Formal Definition

An **applied ii-V** (also called "secondary ii-V") uses the ii-V-I schema to tonicize any chord in a progression. Because the ii-V-I schema is so deeply ingrained in jazz, the entire three-chord pattern (not just the V) can be applied to non-tonic chords.

For example, to tonicize F major in the key of C:
- ii of F = Gm7
- V of F = C7
- I of F = Fmaj7
- Complete applied ii-V-I: Gm7 - C7 - Fmaj7

The association between the chord qualities (m7 - 7 - maj7) and the root motion (down by fifth, down by fifth) is so strong that even an incomplete ii-V (without the I) creates the expectation of the target chord.

## Musical Context

Applied ii-Vs allow jazz composers to move through multiple key centers while maintaining the familiar ii-V-I sound. A tune like "Afternoon in Paris" uses sequential ii-V-Is in different keys to create harmonic motion and variety.

The ii-V space model (McClimon, 2017) visualizes how transforming a Imaj7 chord into a m7 chord changes its function: it becomes the ii7 of a new key, forcing a modulation.

## Examples

### Basic

In C major, tonicizing the IV chord (F):
- Gm7 (ii/IV) -> C7 (V/IV) -> Fmaj7 (IV)

In C major, tonicizing the ii chord (Dm):
- Em7 (ii/ii) -> A7 (V/ii) -> Dm7 (ii)

### From Repertoire

"Afternoon in Paris" (John Lewis):
- Opens with Cm7 - F7 - Bbmaj7 (ii-V-I of Bb, which is bVII)
- Then Bbm7 - Eb7 - Abmaj7 (ii-V-I of Ab, which is bVI)
- Finally Dm7 - G7 - Cmaj7 (ii-V-I of C, the home key)

"Ceora" (Lee Morgan): Entirely composed of connected ii-V-I progressions in various keys.

## Common Confusions

- Applied ii-V is different from applied V alone; the ii chord is included
- The target chord doesn't have to be diatonic; any chord can be tonicized
- An incomplete applied ii-V (no I) still creates expectation of the target
- The "key" doesn't truly change; these are temporary tonicizations
- Applied ii-Vs can chain together, creating sequences of key centers
