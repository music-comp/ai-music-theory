# Quick Definition
Jazz performers embellish existing chord progressions by adding new harmonies. Two main techniques: (1) applied chords — preceding a dominant-quality chord with its ii chord creates a ii-V effect; preceding any chord with its applied V7 intensifies motion; (2) common-tone diminished seventh chords (CTo7) — neighbor-tone embellishment sharing a common tone with the chord being prolonged. These additions may be written, improvised, or shown in parentheses.

# Core Definition
Applied V7: any chord can be embellished by inserting its applied dominant before it (rhythmically splitting the chord's duration). Applied ii: a dominant-quality chord can be embellished by adding the mi7 or half-dim7 a fifth above, creating a ii-V effect. Both techniques transform a static harmony into a more dynamic progression. CTo7 in jazz: the same voice-leading chord as in classical contexts — root of the embellished chord held as common tone, other voices move by step to diminished seventh chord. CTo7 can appear as complete neighbor (chord-CTo7-chord) or incomplete (CTo7 displaces beginning of chord). In lead sheets, embellishments may be unwritten (improvised), built into chord symbols, or shown in parentheses as optional.

# Prerequisites
- ii-V-I progression
- Applied (secondary) dominant chords
- Common-tone diminished seventh chord concept

# Key Properties
1. Applied V7: precede any chord with its secondary dominant
2. Applied ii: precede a dominant chord with its ii to create ii-V effect
3. CTo7: neighbor-tone chord sharing common tone with embellished chord
4. Rhythmically, embellishment splits the duration of the original chord
5. Three notation approaches: unwritten/improvised, built-in, or parenthetical
6. CTo7 chords often not given chord symbols in lead sheets

# Context & Application
"Mood Indigo" (Duke Ellington, 1930): used as backdrop for both techniques. Adding Gmi7 before C7 creates a ii-V in F. Adding Db7 before Gb7 creates an applied V. CTo7 embellishes the opening Bb chord (complete neighbor) and displaces the C7 (incomplete neighbor). These embellishments add richness to an already beautiful progression.

# Examples
**Example 1** ("Mood Indigo"): Applied ii — Gmi7 inserted before C7 creates ii-V in F.
**Example 2** ("Mood Indigo"): CTo7 embellishes opening Bb, inserted on beat 3.
