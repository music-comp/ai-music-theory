# Quick Definition
A five-stage cadential schema that elaborates the Cadenza Semplice by inserting a cadential 6/4 between the predominant and dominant: melody do-re-mi-re-do over bass mi-fa-sol-sol-do, with harmonization I-ii-Cad.6/4-V-I.

# Core Definition
The **Cadenza Composta** ("compound cadence") has five stages: melody do(1)-re(2)-mi(3)-re(2)-do(1) over bass mi(3)-fa(4)-sol(5)-sol(5)-do(1). Meter: W-S-S-W-S. Figured bass: 6-6/5-6/4-7-5. Roman numerals: I-ii-Cad.6/4-V-I. It elaborates the Cadenza Semplice by inserting a cadential 6/4 that resolves to V before the final I (Ch. 12).
