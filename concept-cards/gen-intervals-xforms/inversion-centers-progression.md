# Quick Definition
The progression of pitch-class inversion centers G -> F#/G -> F# in Prokofieff's Melodies op. 35, no. 1, developing the chromatic G/F# relationship established melodically in the opening measure to generate harmonic substitutions throughout the passage.

# Core Definition
The inversional centers progress from I_G (relating expected Eb major to heard E minor at m. 2.5), through I_{F#/G} (relating expected Eb major to heard Eb minor at m. 5.5), to I_{F#} (relating Eb minor to D major and C major to F minor at m. 6). This progression develops the chromatic G/F# pitch-class relationship from the opening melody. The network of these inversion operations (Figure 10.9a) with T_{11} arrows is isographic to the accompaniment figure G-F#-F in m. 1 (Lewin, Section 10.3, pp. 264-265).

# Prerequisites
- **Inversion operation** — the primary analytical tool

# Key Properties
1. G -> F#/G -> F#: three progressively sharpened inversion centers
2. Each center generates a specific harmonic substitution
3. The progression develops the melodic G/F# chromaticism
4. The inversion-center network is isographic to the m. 1 accompaniment
5. T_{11} connects successive centers

# Construction / Recognition
## To Construct:
1. Identify unexpected harmonic substitutions
2. Find the inversion center relating expected and heard harmonies
3. Trace the progression of centers through the passage
## To Recognize:
1. Progressive chromatic shift in inversion centers
2. Motivic source for the centers in the opening melody

# Context & Application
The inversion centers progression shows how a chromatic melodic detail (G-F# in m. 1) can generate an entire passage's harmonic structure. The isography between the center-progression network and the opening accompaniment figure confirms this motivic connection.

# Examples
**Example 1** (Figures 10.8b-10.9, pp. 264-265): I_G -> I_{F#/G} -> I_{F#} with T_{11} arrows. Isographic to G-F#-F in m. 1 accompaniment. I_{F#} simultaneously relates two pairs of triads in m. 6.

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Treating the inversion centers as arbitrary
  **Correction**: Each center has specific motivic justification (G and F# from the opening melody)

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: Thinking the progression must be heard as a "modulation of inversion axis"
  **Clarification**: The centers are analytical explanations for harmonic substitutions, not necessarily perceived as axis shifts
