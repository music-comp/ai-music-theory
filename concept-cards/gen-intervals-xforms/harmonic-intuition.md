# Quick Definition

Harmonic intuition, as Lewin discusses it, refers to our culturally conditioned perception of harmonic relationships between pitches -- we intuit chains of basic harmonic moves rather than directly perceiving complex frequency ratios.

# Core Definition

In the discussion of Example 2.1.5, Lewin argues that "our intuitions are highly conditioned by cultural factors" and that the acoustics of harmonically vibrating bodies do not in themselves provide an adequate basis for grounding those intuitions (p. 48). We do not directly intuit a ratio like 45/32; rather, we intuit a chain of basic harmonic relationships (octave, fifth, third), and the mathematical ratio arises as a valid measurement of that intuited chain. "Our belief in the validity of mathematics carries us the rest of the way" (p. 50).

# Key Properties

1. Harmonic intuitions are culturally conditioned, not purely acoustic
2. We intuit basic harmonic moves: octave (2:1), major third (5:4), dominant fourth-below (3:4)
3. Complex ratios arise as measurements of chains of basic moves
4. The "natural" mathematical factorization may differ from the "intuited" factorization
5. The methodology is significant for validating GIS structures

# Construction / Recognition

## To Construct:
1. Identify basic harmonic relationships with accepted measurements
2. Trace a chain of intuited moves between pitches
3. Compute the composite ratio from the chain

## To Recognize:
1. A ratio is understood as measuring an intuited chain, not as a direct perception
2. The cultural context determines which chain is intuited

# Context & Application

Lewin's discussion challenges the naive view that harmonic intervals are directly perceived through physics. Instead, our sense of harmonic relationship arises from culturally learned chains of basic moves. This has implications for the epistemological status of GIS structures: they model intuitions, not raw acoustics.

# Examples

**Example 1** (pp. 48-50, Figure 2.1): The succession C4-F#4 is heard as "F#4 is the leading tone to the dominant of C." The chain: C4 -> G3 (dominant, 3/4) -> D3 (dominant of dominant, 3/4) -> F#3 (mediant, 5/4) -> F#4 (octave, 2). Composite: 2 * (5/4) * (3/4) * (3/4) = 45/32.

**Example 2** (p. 48): Lewin argues we do NOT intuit C4 and F#4 as sharing a common partial (32nd partial of one, 45th of the other). The ratio 45/32 arises from 2 * (5/4) * (3/4)^2, not from its "natural" factorization 3^2 * 5 / 2^5.

# Common Errors

- **Error**: Treating frequency ratios as direct perceptions.
  **Correction**: Ratios measure intuited chains of basic harmonic moves; they are not themselves the content of intuition.

# Common Confusions

- **Confusion**: Thinking harmonic intuitions are universal and culture-independent.
  **Clarification**: Lewin emphasizes that harmonic intuitions are "highly conditioned by cultural factors."
