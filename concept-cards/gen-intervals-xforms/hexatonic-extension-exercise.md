# Quick Definition
An exercise posed by Lewin asking the reader to develop analogous dual simply transitive groups on the hexatonic collection S' = {C, C#, E, F, G#, A}, extending the octatonic GIS framework to a six-note symmetric collection.

# Core Definition
"As an exercise, the reader may consider a new family of pitch classes, S' = (C, C#, E, F, G#, A), and develop on the new S' two analogous simply transitive groups of operations" (Lewin, Appendix B, p. 252). This hexatonic collection (two interlocking augmented triads) supports a parallel development of dual simply transitive groups and dual GIS structures.

# Prerequisites
- **Octatonic pitch-class set** — The model for the hexatonic extension
- **STRANS1 group** — The template for one simply transitive group
- **STRANS2 group** — The template for the other
- **Dual simply transitive groups** — The theoretical framework to apply

# Key Properties
1. S' = {C, C#, E, F, G#, A} (hexatonic collection)
2. Two interlocking augmented triads: {C, E, G#} and {C#, F, A}
3. |S'| = 6 pitch classes
4. Should support two simply transitive groups of order 6
5. Expected to parallel the octatonic dual-GIS construction

# Construction / Recognition
## To Construct:
1. Identify standard atonal operations preserving S'
2. These form one simply transitive group (analogous to STRANS1)
3. Find all operations on S' commuting with every member of this group
4. These form the dual group (analogous to STRANS2)
## To Recognize:
1. A six-element symmetric collection with two interlocking augmented triads

# Context & Application
This exercise invites the reader to verify that the octatonic GIS construction generalizes to other symmetric pitch-class collections, suggesting a broader theory of non-commutative GIS structures on symmetric sets.

# Examples
**Example 1** (p. 252): S' = {C, C#, E, F, G#, A}. The reader is to develop dual simply transitive groups analogous to STRANS1 and STRANS2.
