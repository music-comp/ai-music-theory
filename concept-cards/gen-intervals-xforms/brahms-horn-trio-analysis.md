# Quick Definition
An extended analysis demonstrating how the "nuclear gesture" (interval 4 subarticulated into 2+2) and "complementary gesture" (interval 8 subarticulated into 10+10) govern intervallic structures throughout the last movement of Brahms's Horn Trio op. 40.

# Formal Definition
Two primary intervallic networks:
1. Nuclear gesture with pickup: interval 4 = 2 + 2, approached by pickup interval 5
2. Complementary gesture: interval 8 = 10 + 10 (mod 12)

Figure 7.6 displays the nuclear gesture; Figure 7.7 shows multiple examples of how these gestures govern the movement.

# Mathematical Formulation
Nuclear gesture (pitch-class intervals):
- Overall: 4
- Subarticulation: 2 + 2
- Pickup: 5

Complementary gesture:
- Overall: 8
- Subarticulation: 10 + 10

Complementary gesture times 2 (Figure 9.6):
- Overall: 4 (= 2 x 8 mod 12)
- Subarticulation: 8 + 8 (= 2 x 10 + 2 x 10 mod 12)

# Musical Context/Application
The analysis progresses from passages where intervallic structures derive from pitch motives (Examples a-e) to passages where intervallic networks become autonomous structures determining musical content (Examples f-h). The analysis uses pitch-class intervals throughout despite the piece's strong diatonic and registral organization.

# Examples
From Figure 7.7:
- (a) Basic motive: nuclear gesture + complementary gesture
- (b) Bass line of opening phrase: nuclear gesture, each stage with pickup
- (c) Melody descending from first climax: complementary gesture with pickups
- (f) Development section sequential passage: complementary gesture at multiple levels
- (g-h) Horn solo (mm. 137-56): complementary gesture leads to "complementary gesture times 2"

The Bb octave-leap idea in (a) and (b) finally interacts with the gestural system in (g), generating Gb through the complementary gesture.

# Common Confusions
- Pitch-class intervals are used despite the diatonic context for specific analytical reasons
- Early passages (a-d) could be analyzed more fully through Schenkerian methods
- Later passages (f-h) show intervallic structures gaining autonomy from pitch-motive derivation
- The "times 2" augmentation relates to both rhythmic and intervallic augmentation
