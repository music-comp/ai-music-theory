# Quick Definition
A motivic fragment from Die Walkure's Todesverkuendigung scene with intervallic network involving intervals 3 and 2, projecting the essential gesture A-to-B with F# pickup, defining an ambitus of F#-to-B that connects to both the FATE and FATE' motives.

# Core Definition
The LOVE motive fragment projects "an overall sense of 'A to B'; as it repeats, it inflects A by a pickup F# and thereby defines a total ambitus of F#-to-B" (Lewin, 8.2.6, p. 185). Figure 8.7(a) shows the LOVE intervallic network. LOVE and FATE share intervals 3 and 2, and both embed the essential A-to-B gesture. The harmonization of LOVE (A-to-B in melody over C-to-B in bass) projects a pitch-class form of FATE (pp. 185-186).

# Key Properties
1. Essential gesture: A-to-B (interval 2)
2. Pickup: F# (interval 3 below A)
3. Ambitus: F#-to-B
4. Intervallic network shares intervals 3 and 2 with FATE
5. Harmonized LOVE (melody + bass) embeds a serial form of FATE: A-C-B

# Construction / Recognition
## To Construct:
1. Identify the A-to-B melodic gesture with F# approach
## To Recognize:
1. Repeated melodic fragment approaching B from A, inflected by F#

# Context & Application
The LOVE fragment appears repeatedly before the scene change. During Siegmund's stichomythy with Brunnhilde, the keys of his questions project LOVE's pitch classes and intervals (Figure 8.9), even as he asks about Sieglinde's fate.

**Example 2** (Figure 8.9, p. 187): Siegmund's question keys (f#, a, b, A, F#, b) project LOVE motive pitch classes.

# Relationships
## Related
- **FATE motive** — Shares intervals 3 and 2; FATE can be embedded in harmonized LOVE
- **FATE' motive** — Recalls F# and A from LOVE

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Treating LOVE and FATE as unrelated
  **Correction**: They share intervals and the A-to-B gesture; FATE is embedded in harmonized LOVE

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: Thinking the LOVE-FATE relationship is about shared pitch content
  **Clarification**: The primary relationship is intervallic, though pitch classes also play a role
