# Quick Definition
The melodic fragment preceding the FATE motive in the Todesverkuendigung, projecting an essential A-to-B gesture elaborated by an F# pickup, with total ambitus F#-to-B related intervallically to FATE.

# Formal Definition
LOVE (Figure 8.7a) as intervallic network:
- Essential span: A-to-B (interval 2)
- Pickup: F# (interval 3 below A)
- Total ambitus: F#-to-B (interval 5)
- The A-B gesture connects LOVE to FATE

# Mathematical Formulation
LOVE network (Figure 8.7a):
- Four nodes: F#, A, B (with octave)
- Arrows: 3 (F# to A), 2 (A to B), 3 (B to lower octave context)
- Compare to FATE (Figure 8.7b): shared 3-arrow and 2-arrow

Alternative interpretation:
- LOVE: essential A-to-B with F# pickup 3 below A
- FATE: essential A-to-B with G# pickup 3 below B
- This attributes priority to pitch relations and hierarchies

# Musical Context/Application
The repeated LOVE fragment before scene 4:
1. Projects overall sense of "A to B" through repetition
2. Inflects A with pickup F#, defining ambitus F#-to-B
3. The harmonization (A minor over C-to-B bass) projects a pitch-class FATE form
4. Dramatically represents Siegmund calming Sieglinde (who sleeps in his arms throughout)

# Examples
From the Todesverkuendigung:
- LOVE repeats over and over in the melody preceding measure 1
- The harmonization places C-B in bass under A-B in melody
- This matrix forms the serial FATE A-C-B
- The A-minor harmony provides fundamental bass A "before" C

The LOVE network's F# and A pitches return in FATE' (mm. 56ff), connecting Siegmund's concern for Sieglinde to the Valhalla confrontation.

# Common Confusions
- LOVE and FATE share intervallic content but are distinct musical entities
- The 3-arrow in LOVE (pickup) differs functionally from the 3-arrow in FATE (melodic)
- The relationship is intervallic, not about pitch-class prolongation
- The A-C-B FATE form emerges from LOVE's harmonization, not from LOVE's melody
