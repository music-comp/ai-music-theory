# Quick Definition
A compositional phenomenon where large-scale passages are related by transposition through RI-chaining (repeated RICH operations), even when their foreground musical events are entirely different.

# Core Definition
Structural sequencing occurs when repeated RI-chaining (RICH operations) on a serial motive generates passages that are transpositionally related at the deep structural level. "When this technique is applied to any given serial motive over and over, alternate forms of the motive-chain will be transposed forms of each other, the interval of transposition depending upon the serial structure of the motive" (Lewin, 7.2, p. 164). The foreground content of these structurally sequenced passages need not be related.

# Prerequisites
- **RI-chaining** — The technique that generates structural sequences
- **TCH transformation** — TCH = RICH composed with RICH, producing the transposition

# Key Properties
1. Generated by repeated application of RICH
2. Alternate forms (every other) are related by the TCH transposition
3. The transposition interval depends on the series' internal structure
4. Foreground events of sequenced passages may differ completely
5. The sequence relationship exists at the level of organizing serial forms, not melodic content

# Construction / Recognition
## To Construct:
1. Begin with a serial motive s
2. Apply RICH repeatedly to generate a chain of forms
3. Compute TCH interval: i = int(s_1, s_N) + int(s_2, s_{N-1})
4. Observe that s, RICH(RICH(s)), RICH^4(s), ... are all T_i-related
## To Recognize:
1. Large passages whose underlying serial organization follows TCH transposition
2. The passage surfaces may appear unrelated

# Context & Application
In the Parsifal transformation music, "Measures 1096-1140 (with Ab) 'sequence' measures 1074-1100 structurally. (The musical foregrounds of the two passages are not related sequentially.)" (p. 164). The TCH interval of 10 is dispersive for Zauber, meaning T_{10}(Z) shares no common notes with Z, so the Z-forms "fill up chromatic pitch-class space very diligently" (p. 164).

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Expecting foreground musical content to be sequentially related
  **Correction**: Structural sequencing operates at the level of serial form organization; surface events may differ entirely

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: Equating structural sequencing with audible sequential repetition
  **Clarification**: This is a deep-structural relationship that may not be perceptible as traditional sequential repetition
