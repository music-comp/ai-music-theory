# Quick Definition
A variant of the FATE motive appearing at measure 56 of the Todesverkuendigung ("Siegmund looks into the eyes of the Valkyrie"), with intervallic structure 3 = (-1) + 4, showing transformation of the original FATE network.

# Formal Definition
FATE' (Figure 8.8) has intervallic structure:
- Overall interval: 3 (vs. FATE's 2)
- Subarticulation: (-1) + 4 (vs. FATE's (-1) + 3)
- The subinterval (-1) remains from FATE
- The pitch class A of FATE' is "bereft of its FATE-partner B"

# Mathematical Formulation
FATE' network:
- Overall: 3 = (-1) + 4
- TCH interval: int(F#, A) = 3 (from first to last element)
- The F# and A recall the LOVE motive's F# and A

Structural relationship:
- FATE: 2 = (-1) + 3, spans A-B
- FATE': 3 = (-1) + 4, spans F#-A
- The shared (-1) preserves the semitone inflection

# Musical Context/Application
FATE' appears when Siegmund confronts his fate - looking into Brunnhilde's eyes and learning he will be separated from Sieglinde in Valhalla. The absence of pitch class B from FATE' represents this dramatic separation: A is "bereft of its FATE-partner B, just as Siegmund will be lonely in Valhalla."

# Common Confusions
- FATE' is not a transposition of FATE; it has different intervallic structure
- The isography with FATE bassline is about transformation structure, not interval content
- TCH intervals differ (3 vs. 2) but the network structure is parallel
- The dramatic interpretation (loneliness) connects to the musical structure (missing B)
