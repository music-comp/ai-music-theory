# Quick Definition
A variant of the FATE motive with network 3 = (-1) + 4, appearing at Siegmund's vision of Valhalla (mm. 56ff.), where the subinterval 3 of FATE becomes the overall interval of FATE', and the TCH interval 3 = int(F#, A) connects to the LOVE motive.

# Core Definition
"The subinterval 3 of the FATE network 2 = (-1) + 3 now becomes the overall interval of the FATE' network 3 = (-1) + 4; (-1) remains a subinterval of FATE'" (Lewin, 8.2.6, p. 186). FATE' recalls the F# and A of the LOVE motive, and its TCH interval is 3 = int(F#, A). FATE' goes through four TCH-sequences, making its course isographic to the FATE bassline chain of Figure 8.6.

# Prerequisites
- **FATE motive** — FATE' is a variant of FATE
- **RICH transformation** — FATE' forms are linked by RICH

# Key Properties
1. Network: 3 = (-1) + 4
2. TCH interval = 3 = int(F#, A)
3. Recalls F# and A of LOVE motive
4. A is "bereft of its FATE-partner B" (dramatic significance: Siegmund alone in Valhalla)
5. Four TCH-sequences, isographic to FATE bassline chain
6. (-1) is preserved from FATE; 3 from FATE becomes 4 in FATE'

# Construction / Recognition
## To Construct:
1. Identify the 3-note cell with intervals (-1) and (+4)
2. Verify overall span of 3 semitones
## To Recognize:
1. Expanded version of FATE with minor-third overall span instead of whole-tone

# Context & Application
FATE' appears "where Siegmund 'looks into the eyes of the Valkyrie'" (p. 186). The pitch class A without its partner B symbolizes Siegmund's loneliness in Valhalla without Sieglinde. The isography between FATE' and FATE bassline chains demonstrates that "RICH and TCH enable us to hear a way in which the bass line of figure 8.6 and all of figure 8.8 project the same overall transformational gesture. Obviously we could not hear such a relation using T_2 in one case and T_3 in the other" (p. 186).

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Using T_3 instead of TCH when comparing FATE' to FATE
  **Correction**: TCH preserves isography; T_3 and T_2 are different transformations

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: Thinking FATE' is unrelated to FATE
  **Clarification**: FATE' expands FATE systematically: (-1) preserved, subinterval 3 becomes overall interval, 3 becomes 4
