# Quick Definition
An extended analysis showing how the intervallic structure of the Zauber (Magic) motive governs the plan of modulations in Parsifal, demonstrating relationships between motivic intervals and large-scale tonal organization through RI-chaining technique.

# Formal Definition
The Zauber motive (Figure 7.2) is a four-note pitch-class series: Ab-Cb-Ebb-Eb (as it appears during the kiss in Act 2). The motive's intervallic structure generates modulatory schemes through RI-chaining, where each new form uses the final two notes of the previous form as its opening two notes.

# Mathematical Formulation
Zauber as intervallic network (Figure 7.5):
- Overall progression: interval 7
- Subarticulated into two subprogressions of interval 3

When RI-chained repeatedly:
- Z_3 = T_10(Z_1) and Z_4 = T_10(Z_2)
- The TCH interval is 10 (dispersive for Z as unordered set)
- INJ(Z, Z)(T_10) = 0; T_10(Z) has no common notes with Z

# Musical Context/Application
Figure 7.3: The phrase introducing the Motive of Faith in the Act 1 Prelude is governed by Z's intervallic structure long before Z appears in the foreground. The local keys Ab-Cb-Ebb-Eb match Z's pitch classes.

Figure 7.4: The transformation music of Act 1 uses four forms of Zauber (Z_1 through Z_4) organized by RI-chaining. Each form is the retrograde inversion of its predecessor, beginning with the final two notes of that predecessor.

# Examples
From Figure 7.4 (transformation music, Act 1):
- Principal local tonics (open noteheads) are organized by Z-forms up to measure 1140
- The RI-chaining creates "structural sequencing": mm. 1096-1140 sequence mm. 1074-1100
- The ten-note series of open noteheads through m. 1140 fills chromatic space (F# and B missing)

The Bell motive (measures 1140-50) relates to Zauber:
- Bell: overall progression -3, elaborated by two subprogressions of 7
- Zauber: overall progression 7, elaborated by two subprogressions of 3

# Common Confusions
- "Modulations" here does not imply Schenkerian prolongation; Wagner's technique is not prolongational in this passage
- The Z-forms govern structural tonics, not foreground events (which differ between sequenced passages)
- The relationship between Bell and Zauber motives is intervallic, not pitch-class prolongational
