# Quick Definition
Motive Y = Ab4-F4-Eb4, derived from X by "folding" the T-relation into T^{-1}, containing the crucial F-Ab span (3 semitones) that proliferates throughout the piece as a major structural element.

# Core Definition
Y follows X in the music and is derived from it: Ab and Eb (connected by T' in X) remain; the T-relation is "folded in both temporally and in registral space" to become T^{-1} (F to Eb). The F-Ab span (3 semitones) starts as secondary but proliferates to control large-scale chromatic events including the structural Cb in the bass and the Tristan-chord sequence (Lewin, Section 10.4, pp. 267-273).

# Key Properties
1. Y = Ab4-F4-Eb4
2. F-Ab span = 3 semitones (minor third), a structurally proliferating interval

4. J(Y) = T3I(Y) = Ab-Cb-Db appears in bass

# Context & Application
