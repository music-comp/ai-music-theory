# Quick Definition
Symmetry realized abstractly among pitch classes rather than specific pitches, where the axis consists of two pitch classes (or two pairs) a tritone apart, called the poles of the axis.

# Formal Definition
Inversional symmetry in pitch-class space occurs when balanced pairs of notes relate through octave equivalence. Because pitch-class space is circular (the twelve pitch classes arranged around a clockface), the axis of symmetry is not a single point but a line passing through two poles a tritone apart. On a pitch-class clockface, the axis passes through n/2 and n/2 + 6 (where n is the index sum of balanced pairs). If the sum is odd, the axis passes between two pairs of pitch classes.

# Mathematical Formulation/Recognition
For balanced pitch classes summing to n:
- Axis poles: n/2 and n/2 + 6

Examples:
- Sum = 8: axis is 4-10 (E-Bb)
- Sum = 0: axis is 0-6 (C-F#)
- Sum = 7 (odd): axis is 3/4-9/10 (Eb/E-A/Bb), passing between pitch classes

The axis notation:
- Even sum: pc-pc (e.g., C/Db-F#/G means axis between C/Db and between F#/G)
- The axis can be visualized as a diameter on the pitch-class clockface

# Musical Context/Application
Pitch-class space symmetry:
1. Allows octave displacement while maintaining symmetry
2. Both poles of the axis may function as centric
3. T6-related passages share the same axis
4. Enables analysis of passages with octave displacements

# Examples
Example 5-7: Bartók's String Quartet No. 5 - The dyad C-Db expands in a symmetrical wedge. Due to octave displacements, the symmetry is clearer in pitch-class space. The axis is C/Db-F#/G.

Example 5-8: Bartók creates axis A-Eb. A is the pitch axis, but A-Eb is the pitch-class axis. When the music returns transposed at T6, the pitch-class axis (A-Eb) remains the same but now Eb is emphasized.

Example 5-9: Finney's Fantasy for Solo Violin - The axis D-Ab remains constant even when the central pitch shifts from D4 to Ab4.

# Common Confusions
- The axis in pc space is two points (poles), not one
- Both poles can function as centric (Eb and A are both relevant in Example 5-8)
- Axes with odd sums pass between pitch classes
- The same pc axis can be realized around different pitch-space axes
