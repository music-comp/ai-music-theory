# Quick Definition
An unordered pitch-class interval (upci), also called interval class, is the shortest distance between two pitch classes, ranging from 0 to 6 semitones.

# Formal Definition
An unordered pitch-class interval (abbreviated upci) measures the shortest distance between two pitch classes, regardless of the order in which they occur. Simply count from one pitch class to the other by the shortest available route, either clockwise or counterclockwise on the pitch-class clockface. Since you never have to travel farther than six semitones to get from any pitch class to another, 6 is the largest unordered pitch-class interval.

# Mathematical Formulation/Recognition
Formula for unordered pitch-class interval:
- upci = (x - y) mod 12 OR (y - x) mod 12, whichever is smaller

Equivalently:
- Calculate both directions
- Take the smaller value
- Result is always 0-6

Example calculations:
| Between | Calculation | upci |
|---------|-------------|------|
| C# and Eb | 3-1=2 or 1-3=10; 2 is smaller | 2 |
| Eb and C# | Same as above | 2 |
| B and F | 5-11=6 or 11-5=6 | 6 |
| D and Bb | 10-2=8 or 2-10=4; 4 is smaller | 4 |

# Musical Context/Application
Unordered pitch-class intervals (interval classes) are the most abstract way to describe intervallic relationships. They identify a "type" of interval without regard to direction or specific registral placement. Interval class 1 includes all semitones, major sevenths, and their compounds; interval class 5 includes all perfect fourths and fifths.

# Examples
**Example 1-14**: Demonstration that the unordered pitch-class interval between C# and A equals the unordered pitch-class interval between A and C#: both are 4, because the order no longer matters and the shortest route from A to C# is the same as from C# to A.

**Example 1-15b** (Schoenberg, String Quartet No. 3): Two statements of opci 4 are balanced by a concluding opci 8; all three represent unordered pitch-class interval 4 (interval class 4).

# Common Confusions
Unordered pitch-class interval and interval class are the same thing. The maximum value is 6 (the tritone) because any larger interval can be expressed as a smaller one in the opposite direction. For example, what might seem like an interval of 8 is actually interval class 4 (since 8 and 4 are complements mod 12).
