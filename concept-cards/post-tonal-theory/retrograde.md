# Quick Definition
The retrograde (R) of a series is the prime ordering played backward, reversing the order of pitch classes and producing a reversed interval succession with each interval replaced by its complement mod 12.

# Formal Definition
The *retrograde* simply reverses the prime ordering—the series is played backward. When the series is retrograded, the interval succession is reversed, and each interval is replaced by its complement mod 12 (1 becomes 11, 2 becomes 10, etc.). There are twelve different forms of the retrograde ordering: R0, R1, R2, ... R11. Importantly, R_n is the retrograde of P_n, so R0 is the retrograde of P0, R1 the retrograde of P1, and so on. This means R_n *ends* rather than *begins* on pitch-class n.

# Mathematical Formulation/Recognition
- R_n = retrograde of P_n (reverses order of P_n)
- R_n ends on pitch-class n (not begins)
- Interval transformation: each interval i becomes (12 - i) mod 12
- Interval order: reversed from P
- Example: If P has intervals <11, 8, 1, 7, 10, 1, 8, 8, 11, 11, 5>, R has intervals <7, 1, 1, 4, 4, 11, 2, 5, 11, 4, 1>

# Musical Context/Application
- One of the four basic orderings (P, R, I, RI) of the twelve-tone series
- Series that are retrograde-related (P and R, I and RI) have complementary intervals in reverse order
- R-forms share pitch-class content with all other series forms
- The retrograde operation can be applied to any P-form to generate the corresponding R-form
- Often used for palindromic or arch-form structures

# Examples
- Schoenberg, String Quartet No. 4: R2 is P2 (D-C#-A-Bb-F-Eb-E-C-Ab-G-F#-B) played backward: B-F#-G-Ab-C-E-Eb-F-Bb-A-C#-D
- Webern, "Wie bin ich froh!": R7 is used alongside P7, I7, and RI7
- The interval 11 in P becomes 1 in R; interval 8 becomes 4

# Common Confusions
- Assuming R_n begins on pitch-class n—it actually *ends* on pitch-class n (because it's P_n backward)
- Expecting intervals to remain the same—they are complemented as well as reversed
- Confusing retrograde (order reversal) with inversion (pitch-class inversion)
