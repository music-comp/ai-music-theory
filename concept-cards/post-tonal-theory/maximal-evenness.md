# Quick Definition

A property of pitch-class sets that are distributed as evenly as possible around the pitch-class circle, with the interval cycles generating the maximally even sets at each cardinality.

# Formal Definition

A pitch-class set is **maximally even** when its pitch classes are positioned as widely dispersed as possible around the twelve-position pitch-class clockface. For any given cardinality (number of pitch classes), there is a unique maximally even set class. These sets arise naturally from the interval cycles and possess special structural and perceptual properties.

The maximally even set at each cardinality corresponds to:
- Cardinality 2: Tritone, sc(06) - from C6
- Cardinality 3: Augmented triad, sc(048) - from C4
- Cardinality 4: Diminished seventh, sc(0369) - from C3
- Cardinality 5: Pentatonic, sc(02479) - 5-note segment of C5
- Cardinality 6: Whole-tone, sc(02468T) - from C2
- Cardinality 7: Diatonic, sc(013568T) - 7-note segment of C5

Complements of maximally even sets are also maximally even.

# Mathematical Formulation/Recognition

**Maximally even set classes by cardinality:**

| Cardinality | Set Class | Name | Cyclic Origin |
|-------------|-----------|------|---------------|
| 2 | 2-6 (06) | Tritone | C6 |
| 3 | 3-12 (048) | Augmented triad | C4 |
| 4 | 4-28 (0369) | Diminished seventh | C3 |
| 5 | 5-35 (02479) | Pentatonic | 5-note C5 segment |
| 6 | 6-35 (02468T) | Whole-tone | C2 |
| 7 | 7-35 (013568T) | Diatonic | 7-note C5 segment |
| 8 | 8-28 (0134679T) | Octatonic | Two C3s |
| 9 | 9-12 (01245689T) | Enneatonic | Three C4s |
| 10 | 10-6 (012346789T) | --- | Five C6s |

**Recognition criteria:**
- All intervals between adjacent pitch classes (around the circle) are as equal as possible
- For cardinalities that divide 12 evenly (2, 3, 4, 6), the intervals are exactly equal
- For other cardinalities (5, 7), intervals alternate between two adjacent values

# Musical Context/Application

Maximal evenness relates to:
- **Harmonic stability**: Maximally even sets are often perceived as stable reference points
- **Referential collections**: The major scale, pentatonic, whole-tone, and octatonic are all maximally even
- **Cyclic derivation**: Understanding maximally even sets reveals their cyclic origins
- **Historical significance**: Many of these sets have been important throughout music history

Compositional implications:
- Maximally even sets provide balance and stability
- Moving toward or away from maximal evenness can create tension/resolution
- The interval cycles generate these harmonies naturally
- Complements of maximally even sets retain the property

# Examples

**From Example 4-10 (Maximally even set classes table):**

**Dyad (cardinality 2):**
- Maximally even: sc(06) - tritone
- To position 2 notes as far apart as possible, place them a tritone apart
- Cyclic origin: C6

**Trichord (cardinality 3):**
- Maximally even: sc(048) - augmented triad
- Three notes positioned at equal distances (4 semitones apart)
- Cyclic origin: C4

**Tetrachord (cardinality 4):**
- Maximally even: sc(0369) - diminished seventh chord
- Four notes positioned at equal distances (3 semitones apart)
- Cyclic origin: C3

**Pentachord (cardinality 5):**
- Maximally even: sc(02479) - pentatonic collection
- Five notes cannot be spaced perfectly evenly; this is the best approximation
- Cyclic origin: 5-note segment of C5

**Hexachord (cardinality 6):**
- Maximally even: sc(02468T) - whole-tone collection
- Six notes positioned at equal distances (2 semitones apart)
- Cyclic origin: C2

**Heptachord (cardinality 7):**
- Maximally even: sc(013568T) - diatonic collection
- The major/minor scale is the most evenly distributed 7-note set
- Cyclic origin: 7-note segment of C5

# Common Confusions

- **Maximally even vs. symmetrical**: All maximally even sets are symmetrical, but not all symmetrical sets are maximally even
- **Perfect vs. approximate evenness**: Only cardinalities 2, 3, 4, and 6 allow for perfectly equal spacing in 12-tet
- **Maximal evenness vs. consonance**: While some maximally even sets (pentatonic, diatonic) are traditionally consonant, others (tritone, whole-tone) are not
- **Single property**: Maximal evenness is a property of the abstract set class, not a particular voicing or registration
