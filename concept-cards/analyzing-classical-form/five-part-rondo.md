## Definition

The five-part rondo consists of five principal sections: three appearances of the refrain alternate with two couplets, yielding an ABACA pattern.

## Structure

| Section | Rondo Term | Formal Function | Tonal Region |
|---------|------------|-----------------|--------------|
| 1 | Refrain 1 (A) | Main theme | I |
| 2 | Couplet 1 (B) | Subordinate-theme complex OR Interior theme | V or minore, (vi) |
| 3 | Refrain 2 (A) | First return of main theme | I |
| 4 | Couplet 2 (C) | Interior theme | minore, IV, (vi) |
| 5 | Refrain 3 (A) | Final return of main theme | I |
| (6) | (Coda) | Optional | I |

## Usage

- Most often for **slow movements** within instrumental cycle
- Sometimes as fast finale
- Rarely as fast opening movement

## The Refrain

- Always appears in the **home key**
- Almost always a conventional, **tight-knit theme** closing with PAC
- Small ternary is commonly used
- Returns usually bring complete structure, sometimes with ornamental changes

## First Couplet Options

### Option 1: Subordinate-Theme Complex
- Modulating transition
- Subordinate theme (group)
- Closing section
- Retransition

### Option 2: Interior Theme
- Set in opposite mode (minore) or submediant (vi)
- Similar to interior theme in large ternary

## Second Couplet

- Usually an interior theme
- Differs from first couplet in melodic-motivic material, tonal region, and formal plan
