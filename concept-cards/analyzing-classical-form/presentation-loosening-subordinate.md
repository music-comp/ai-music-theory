# Quick Definition
A subordinate theme's presentation can be loosened by: (1) additional repetition of the basic idea (third statement creates asymmetry and redundancy); (2) repeating the entire presentation itself; (3) weakening the tonic prolongation through a dominant pedal. These loosening techniques create the theme's characteristically expanded dimensions.

# Core Definition
Presentation function can be loosened in subordinate themes through three techniques: (1) **Additional repetition of the basic idea** -- a third statement renders the grouping structure asymmetrical and promotes functional redundancy, two criteria of loose organization. (2) **Repeating the entire presentation** -- creates redundancy. (3) **Weakening the tonic prolongation** -- typically through a dominant pedal that undermines but does not obliterate the surface-level tonic prolongation implied by the melody (Caplin, Ch. 12, pp. 381-383).
