## Subordinate Theme Terminology

Various terms have traditionally been used for what is here called the subordinate theme.

### Traditional Terms

- "Second theme"
- "Secondary theme"
- "Second subject"

### Problems with Traditional Terms

These terms suggest a numbering scheme starting from the "first theme" (main theme) that often cannot be sustained in analysis: a given subordinate theme is not necessarily the literal "second" theme of a movement.

### Advantage of "Subordinate Theme"

The term **subordinate theme**:
- Does not imply a theme of inferior aesthetic value
- Relates directly to the expression "subordinate key"
- Accurately describes the tonal region confirmed by this theme
