# Quick Definition
Occasionally, the B section of a small ternary takes a distinctly sentential design -- with a presentation-like opening, continuation with fragmentation, and closing HC -- but with loosened characteristics (harmonic instability, asymmetry) appropriate for a contrasting middle. The period form never appears in B sections.

# Core Definition
The B section may display a presentation-like opening (repeated idea), continuation with fragmentation, and closing with an HC, resembling a sentence but rendered looser through specific devices: harmonic instability (presentation may prolong V7 or I6 rather than root-position I), more prominent sequential progressions, asymmetrical grouping, and possible extensions. The period form never appears in B sections because periods are the most tightly knit theme type, and B sections require loose organization; a periodic B section would undermine the necessary contrast with A. The sentence's forward-driving character is compatible with the transitional quality of B sections, the preparation for recapitulation, and looser formal expression.

# Prerequisites
- **Continuation phrase (contrasting middle)** -- The simpler version of continuation-based B
- **Sentence** -- The conventional type being loosened

# Key Properties
1. Resembles sentence: presentation-like + continuation + HC
2. Harmonic loosening: presentation may prolong V7 or I6 (not root-position I)
3. Sequential progressions more prominent than in tight-knit sentence
4. Asymmetrical grouping possible
5. Period form never appears in B sections (too tight-knit)
6. Sentence's forward drive suits B-section function

# Construction / Recognition
## To Identify:
1. Look for repeated idea at opening (presentation-like)
2. Check for continuation characteristics (fragmentation, sequence)
3. Locate closing HC
4. Note loosening: unstable harmony in presentation, asymmetry, extensions
5. Confirm it is not a genuine tight-knit sentence (harmonic requirements not fully met)

# Context & Application
The looser sentential B section represents a more elaborated contrasting middle than either the simple standing on the dominant or the continuation phrase. Its sentential character creates strong forward momentum toward the recapitulation. The prohibition against periodic B sections is a significant generalization: the period's balanced, closed quality is fundamentally incompatible with the B section's transitional, destabilizing function.

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Labeling a sentential B section as a genuine sentence
  **Correction**: The loosened harmonic requirements (no root-position tonic prolongation in presentation) prevent it from being a genuine sentence; "sentential" or "sentence-like" is more appropriate
