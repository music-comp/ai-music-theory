# Looser Sentential Structure (Contrasting Middle)

Occasionally, the B section of a small ternary takes a distinctly **sentential** design, but with loosened characteristics appropriate for a contrasting middle.

## The Pattern

The B section may display:
- Presentation-like opening (repeated idea)
- Continuation with fragmentation
- Closing with HC

This resembles a sentence, but looser.

## Loosening Devices

What makes it looser than a regular sentence:

1. **Harmonic instability**
   - Presentation may prolong V7 or I6 (not root-position I)
   - Sequential progressions more prominent

2. **Asymmetry**
   - Grouping may be less symmetrical
   - Extensions may occur

## Why Period Never Appears

The **period** form never appears in B sections because:
- Periods are the most tightly knit theme type
- B sections require loose organization
- A periodic B section would undermine contrast with A

## Sentence Compatibility

The sentence's forward-driving character is compatible with:
- The transitional quality of B sections
- Preparation for recapitulation
- Looser formal expression

## Cadential Closure

The looser sentential B section typically ends with **HC**, preparing for the recapitulation's tonic return.
