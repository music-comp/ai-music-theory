# Quick Definition
Caplin notes that a theme beginning with a presentation and ending with a consequent is conspicuously absent from the repertory, because the arrangement produces a threefold statement of the basic idea within an excessive tonic prolongation, creating undesirable redundancy.

# Core Definition
Of the logically possible ways to combine sentence and period phrase functions, one pattern is absent: presentation (b.i. + b.i.) followed by consequent (b.i. + c.i.). This would produce three statements of the basic idea: twice in the presentation and once at the return in the consequent. The resulting redundancy within an excessive tonic prolongation (which would span almost the entire theme) likely explains why this potential hybrid rarely occurs.

# Key Properties
1. Would produce three statements of basic idea (b.i., b.i., b.i.)
2. Excessive tonic prolongation
3. Conspicuously absent from the repertory
4. The redundancy makes it compositionally unappealing
