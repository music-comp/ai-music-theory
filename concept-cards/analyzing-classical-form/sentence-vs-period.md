# Sentence vs. Period Comparison

The sentence and period are the two fundamental 8-measure theme types. Understanding their similarities and differences is essential for formal analysis.

## Similarities

Both themes share:
- **8-measure length** divided into two 4-measure phrases
- **End with a cadence**
- **Begin with a 2-measure basic idea** that is restated later

## Differences

| Feature | Sentence | Period |
|---------|----------|--------|
| Number of cadences | One | Two |
| Possible ending cadences | PAC, IAC, or HC | PAC or IAC (never HC) |
| Basic idea restatement | **Repeated** within presentation | **Returns** at start of consequent |
| Location of restatement | Measures 3-4 | Measures 5-6 |

## Aesthetic Qualities

**Sentence**:
- Character of mobility and forward drive
- Open-ended presentation creates momentum
- Destabilizations of continuation provide energy

**Period**:
- Character of balance and repose
- Similar construction of antecedent and consequent
- Both phrases bring cadential closure

## Key Distinction

The fundamental difference lies in how the basic idea is restated:
- **Sentence**: Immediate repetition (no intervening material)
- **Period**: Return after intervening contrasting idea
