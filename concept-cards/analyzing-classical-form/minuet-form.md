## Definition

Minuet form is the formal organization of an individual minuet (either the minuet proper or the trio) within minuet/trio form. Most individual minuets are built in a way that strongly resembles the small ternary theme type (about 90%), with a small number resembling small binary (about 10%).

## Structure

| Section | Function | Typical Ending |
|---------|----------|---------------|
| A | Exposition | PAC (home or subordinate key) |
| B | Contrasting Middle | HC in home key |
| A' | Recapitulation | PAC in home key |

## Functional Labels

**Phrase functions** (from small ternary):
- Presentation, antecedent, continuation, cadential, standing on dominant

**Thematic functions** to identify:
- Main theme
- Transition
- Subordinate theme
- Developmental core elements

## Key Analytical Questions

1. Is a subordinate key confirmed?
2. If so, where and how does that confirmation take place?

### If A Section Modulates:
- Main theme, transition, and subordinate theme functions expressed
- B section free to explore other tonal regions

### If A Section Does Not Modulate:
- Tonal contrast transferred to B section
- Transition and subordinate theme functions appear in B section

## Differences from Small Ternary

- B section usually more complex and longer than in small ternary
- A' section seldom shorter than A section (unlike small ternary)
- A' section often significantly expanded
- Closing section more common in recapitulation
