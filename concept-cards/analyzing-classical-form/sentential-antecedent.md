# Sentential Antecedent (Mini-Sentence)

A **sentential antecedent** (or mini-sentence) occurs when an antecedent phrase is internally structured like a miniature sentence.

## Structure

The basic idea itself contains:
- A 1-measure motive that is immediately repeated (like a "small presentation")
- This creates a 2-measure basic idea with internal statement-response character

The contrasting idea features:
- Fragmentation
- Harmonic acceleration
- Cadential closure (weak cadence)

## Result

The entire 4-measure antecedent resembles a sentence:
- Mm. 1-2: Small presentation (1m + 1m)
- Mm. 3-4: Continuation with cadence

## Terminology

It is preferable to speak of:
- **Mini-sentence** or
- **Sentential antecedent**

rather than calling it a genuine 4-measure sentence, to avoid confusion with true 4-measure sentences (which occur under R = ½N conditions).

## Paired Occurrence

Such miniature sentences rarely appear on their own. They tend to come in pairs as antecedent and consequent:
- Antecedent: Mini-sentence ending with weak cadence
- Consequent: Similar structure ending with strong cadence

## Context

The sentential character of the antecedent supports the notion that the contrasting idea often displays continuational characteristics.
