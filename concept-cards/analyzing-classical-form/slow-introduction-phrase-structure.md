# Slow Introduction Phrase Structure

The formal organization of slow introductions.

## General Character

As befits their expression of uncertainty and instability, slow introductions are usually loose in formal organization.

## Overall Form

Many introductions articulate cadences suggesting:
- Bipartite organization (two parts)
- Tripartite organization (three parts)
- Four or more distinct parts (larger introductions)

Conventional theme types appear only now and then.

## Typical Features

- Prominent melodic-motivic material
- Supported by variety of harmonic progressions
- Multiple cadential articulations
- Possible modulations between parts

## Truncated Small Ternary

Sometimes the formal situation resembles a small ternary:
- Opening thematic material
- Standing on dominant (B-like section)
- But implied A' section is truncated when resolution initiates exposition
