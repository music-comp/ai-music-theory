# Antecedent + Cadential (Hybrid Type 2)

**Antecedent + cadential** is a hybrid type where the second phrase displays minimal characteristics of continuation function and is supported entirely by an expanded cadential progression.

## Structure

- **First phrase (mm. 1-4)**: Standard **antecedent** (basic idea + contrasting idea → weak cadence)
- **Second phrase (mm. 5-8)**: Exclusively **cadential** (supported by ECP)

## Why "Cadential" Rather Than "Continuation"?

In the context of a theme starting like a period:
- We do not necessarily expect a continuation
- The phrase supported by ECP often displays few continuational characteristics
- No fragmentation
- No harmonic acceleration (may even slow down)

## Characteristics of Cadential Phrase

The second phrase:
- Brings new melodic material
- Continues 2-measure grouping structure (no fragmentation)
- Is supported entirely by an expanded cadential progression
- Projects exclusively cadential function

## Modulation Rare

Hybrids of this type rarely modulate because:
- The weak cadence ends the antecedent in the home key
- The ECP immediately follows
- There is no place for a modulating progression

## Relation to Period

This hybrid is somewhat more periodic than "antecedent + continuation" since continuational traits are missing.
