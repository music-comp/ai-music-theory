# Textural Dissolution

**Textural dissolution** is the systematic reduction of musical texture, often used within a standing on the dominant to prepare for a recapitulation.

## Function

Textural dissolution serves to:
- "Clear the stage" before the recapitulation
- Make the return of the basic idea sound fresh
- Create contrast with prior denser textures

## Process

Typical dissolution:
- Multi-voice texture gradually thins
- Voices drop out progressively
- May conclude with single melodic line
- Minimal activity before the return

## Effect

Against the background of reduced texture:
- The recapitulation sounds more prominent
- The return of the basic idea is highlighted
- Fresh, new character is achieved

## Context

Textural dissolution typically occurs:
- Within postcadential standing on the dominant
- Near the end of the B section
- Just before A' begins

## Related Technique

Often combined with:
- **Motivic anticipation**: Preparing motives from the basic idea
- Creates both textural and motivic preparation for return
