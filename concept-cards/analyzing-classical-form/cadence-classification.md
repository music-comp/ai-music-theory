# Cadence Classification

The three basic cadence types can be classified according to their harmonic and melodic completeness.

## The Three Types

### Perfect Authentic Cadence (PAC)
- **Harmonic**: Complete (V → I in root position)
- **Melodic**: Complete (ends on 1 in soprano)
- **Strength**: Strongest

### Imperfect Authentic Cadence (IAC)
- **Harmonic**: Complete (V → I)
- **Melodic**: Incomplete (ends on 3 in soprano)
- **Strength**: Moderate

### Half Cadence (HC)
- **Harmonic**: Incomplete (ends on V)
- **Melodic**: Incomplete (not on 1)
- **Strength**: Weakest

## Classification Table

| Type | Harmonic | Melodic | Overall |
|------|----------|---------|---------|
| PAC | Complete | Complete | Complete |
| IAC | Complete | Incomplete | Incomplete |
| HC | Incomplete | Incomplete | Incomplete |

## No Plagal Cadence

The IV → I motion does NOT constitute a genuine cadence in classical style. Such passages are typically postcadential codettas, not true cadences.

## Syntactical Strength

All cadences of the same type have equivalent **syntactical** strength, regardless of:
- Metrical position
- Dynamic level
- Texture
- Rhetorical emphasis
