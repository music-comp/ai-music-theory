# Contrasting Middle + Continuation (Small Binary)

In Part 2 of a small binary, a contrasting middle may be followed by a **continuation phrase** that leads to the closing PAC.

## Structure

- 4-measure **contrasting middle** (B section) → HC
- 4-measure **continuation** → PAC

## Continuation Characteristics

The continuation phrase may feature:
- Material related to Part 1's continuation
- Fragmentation
- Sequential progressions
- New material leading to cadence

## Two Continuations

If the contrasting middle itself is built as a continuation:
- Part 2 consists of two continuation phrases
- First closes with HC
- Second closes with PAC

## Relation to Part 1

The melodic-motivic content of the final continuation may:
- Relate to continuation material from Part 1
- Bring entirely new material
- Combine elements of both

## Ending Requirement

Regardless of structure, the continuation must close with **PAC in home key** to complete the theme.
