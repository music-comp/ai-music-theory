# Quick Definition
In Part 2 of a small binary, a contrasting middle (ending with HC) may be followed by a continuation phrase (ending with PAC), whose content may relate to Part 1's continuation or bring new material. If the B section itself is a continuation, Part 2 consists of two continuation phrases (HC then PAC).

# Core Definition
Following a contrasting middle, a passage with continuational characteristics brings the second part to a close with a PAC. If the contrasting middle itself is built as a continuation (frequent in small ternaries too), then Part 2 consists of two continuation phrases: the first closing with HC, the second with PAC. The melodic-motivic content of the final continuation may relate back to Part 1's continuation or bring new material.

# Key Properties
1. B section (4 mm.) -> HC; continuation (4 mm.) -> PAC
2. If B is itself a continuation: two continuation phrases total
3. Content may relate to Part 1 or be new
4. Always ends with PAC in home key

# Examples
**Example 1** (p. 269): Haydn, Op. 33/5, iv, mm. 1-16 -- two model-sequence passages as continuations; first closes with HC (contrasting middle), second closes with PAC.
