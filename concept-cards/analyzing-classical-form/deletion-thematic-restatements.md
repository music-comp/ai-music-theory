# Deletion of Thematic Restatements

The removal of repeated material from the recapitulation that was present in the exposition.

## Rationale

Since the main theme's melodic-motivic material is well known by the recapitulation, restatements appropriate in the exposition (where material is heard for the first time) are often eliminated.

## Especially Likely When

- Ideas were prominently exploited in the development section
- Entire main theme was repeated in exposition
- Main theme was constructed as small ternary

## Typical Deletions

### Repeated Main Theme
If entire main theme was repeated in exposition, recapitulation usually states theme just once.

### Small Ternary Main Theme
The A' section alone is likely to be used in the recapitulation.

### Continuation Phrase Repeats
Repeated continuation phrases may be eliminated.

## Effect

Creates formal compression while maintaining essential thematic content.
