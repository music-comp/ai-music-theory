# Crux

The point in the recapitulation where the music diverges from the exposition to effect tonal adjustment.

## Definition

The crux is the moment in the recapitulation's transition where the composer must alter the music to remain in (or return to) the home key rather than modulating to the subordinate key as in the exposition.

## Location

Can occur at any place in the transition:
- Early (near beginning)
- Middle (during continuation)
- Late (near end before subordinate theme)

## Typical Methods

At the crux, the music typically:
- Shifts toward flat-side subdominant region
- Uses this as pivot to return to home-key dominant
- May introduce new sequential passage
- May compress or delete surrounding material

## Analytical Importance

Identifying the crux helps understand:
- How the composer manages tonal adjustment
- What material is retained vs. altered
- The relationship between exposition and recapitulation transitions
