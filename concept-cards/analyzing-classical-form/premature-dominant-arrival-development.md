# Premature Dominant Arrival in Development

When the dominant of a development key or home key arrives before the expected structural close.

## Definition

A dominant arrival in the core that occurs before a genuine cadence materializes - the harmonic goal arrives "too early" in relation to the phrase-structural processes.

## Historical Evolution

- Earlier 18th century: Development units typically closed with PAC in development key
- High-classical: Units end with dominant harmony, usually HC
- Later classical (especially Beethoven): Dominant arrival replaces HC

## Recognition

In retrospect, we understand that the dominant at a certain point is the harmonic goal of the core, marking a premature dominant arrival. This harmony is then typically extended as a standing on the dominant.

## Compositional Effect

- Creates sense of urgency or truncation
- Standing on dominant following premature arrival is often extended
- May include anticipatory motives from main theme
