# Durchfuhrung

The German term for "development," literally meaning "leading through."

## Dual Meaning of "Development"

The term "development" has at least two meanings:

### 1. Formal Section
The central section of a sonata form (and some other forms), standing between exposition and recapitulation.

### 2. Motivic Process
The systematic manipulation and transformation of musical ideas - "developing" motivic material.

## Related Terms

Other terms emphasizing the motivic-process aspect:
- **Working out**: Emphasizes systematic treatment
- **Elaboration**: Schoenberg's preferred term
- **Free fantasia**: Suggests improvisatory quality
- **Durchfuhrung**: German term, literally "leading through"

## Significance

While motivic development can occur anywhere in a form, writers have noted that such motivic work is particularly associated with the central section of sonata form, especially in Beethoven.
