# Multiple Pre-core

A pre-core constructed from two thematic units.

## Characteristics

- Found in a small number of relatively large pre-cores
- Consists of two thematic units
- One or both units is usually incomplete

## Typical Organization

### First Part
May be complete (ending with premature dominant arrival and standing on dominant) or incomplete

### Second Part
- Often consists only of a presentation phrase
- Functions to set up the core
- Groups more intimately with the core than with first pre-core part

## Relationship to Core

When the second part is a presentation:
- The core that follows functions as magnified continuation
- Creates a sentence-like structure at a higher level
- The opening two measures of core model may derive from the same theme as the presentation

## Example Pattern

1. First pre-core part: Main theme material, ends with dominant arrival
2. Second pre-core part: Subordinate theme presentation
3. Core: Continues the subordinate theme material sequentially
