## Aesthetic Effects of Cadential Extension

The techniques of cadential extension give rise to very different aesthetic effects.

### IAC

- Provides a **temporary goal** to the theme
- Insufficiently conclusive
- We know more must happen
- Some sense of closure has occurred

### Deceptive Cadence

- Also provides temporary goal
- **Less** sense of closure than IAC
- Feels more interrupted
- More must happen

### Evaded Cadence

- Makes a considerably more **dramatic** effect
- Imminent closure is **thwarted at the last second**
- Quickly attempted again
- Lack of any event representing formal closure
- Combined with breaking off highly goal-directed process
- Arouses **powerful expectation** for further cadential action

### Why Evaded Cadence Suits Subordinate Theme

The evaded cadence is particularly well suited to subordinate themes:
- Dramatizing the subordinate key is a principal aesthetic objective
- Establishing SK as foil to HK is more effective if the struggle to gain cadential confirmation is **hard-won**

### Abandoned Cadence

- Much **less dramatic**
- Cadential progression loses sense of direction
- Music tends to wander off
- But formal loosening is pronounced
- Appropriate for subordinate themes
