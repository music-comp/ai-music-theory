## Historical Background of Sonata Form Theory

The basic elements of sonata form were first described by theorists in the last decade of the 18th century.

### Early Accounts (1790s)
- H. C. Koch
- Francesco Galeazzi
- August Kollmann

### 19th Century Developments

**Antoine Reicha** (early 19th century): First fully developed description of the form

**A. B. Marx** (mid-19th century): Even more comprehensive theories that had enormous influence throughout the rest of that century

### 20th Century Treatises

**Arnold Schoenberg and Erwin Ratz**: Highly influential on the Caplin textbook approach

**D. F. Tovey**: Exerted powerful impact on many historians and critics

**Charles Rosen**: Work dominated North American thinking in the latter decades of the 20th century

### Contemporary Theory

**James Hepokoski and Warren Darcy**: Recent important treatise offering a theory that differs in a number of respects from the Caplin approach, particularly regarding the medial caesura and the distinction between two-part and continuous expositions.
