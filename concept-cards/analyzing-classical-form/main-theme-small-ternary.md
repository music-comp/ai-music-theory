## Main Theme as Small Ternary

A main theme can take the form of a **small ternary**, though this is relatively uncommon for opening movements.

### Structure

Small ternary main theme consists of:
- **A section**: Initial theme (period, sentence, or hybrid)
- **B section**: Contrasting middle
- **A' section**: Return and recapitulation of A

### Why Relatively Uncommon

Small ternary is less common for main themes because:
- It is a relatively "complete" form in itself
- The return of A' can feel like a recapitulation before the form has really developed
- The form's internal closure can reduce forward momentum

### When It Does Occur

Small ternary main themes are more likely in:
- Slow movements
- Finales with lighter character
- Movements emphasizing formal completeness at the outset

### Small Binary (Rare)

A small **binary** form rarely begins a sonata-form movement:
- Even less common than small ternary
- Lacks the return that provides formal closure
