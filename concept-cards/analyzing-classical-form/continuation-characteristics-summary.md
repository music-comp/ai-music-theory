# Continuation Characteristics Summary

Four distinct compositional devices characterize continuation function. None is strictly required, but at least one is typically present.

## The Four Characteristics

### 1. Fragmentation
- Reduction in grouping unit size
- Example: 2m → 1m → ½m units
- Most typical indicator of continuation

### 2. Harmonic Acceleration
- Faster rate of harmonic change
- Compared to presentation's harmonic rhythm
- May be subtle or dramatic

### 3. Surface Rhythm Acceleration
- Increased rhythmic activity
- Shorter note values
- Greater density of attacks

### 4. Sequential Progressions
- Harmonic sequences
- May support model-sequence technique
- Creates harmonic instability

## Independence of Characteristics

These are **distinct and independent**:
- Any combination may occur
- None is necessary
- One is usually sufficient
- Multiple characteristics reinforce the function

## Recognizing Continuation

When analyzing, look for ANY of these characteristics to identify continuation function. The most obvious indicator is usually fragmentation.

## Compensation

When one characteristic is absent, others often compensate:
- No fragmentation? Look for harmonic acceleration
- No harmonic acceleration? Look for surface rhythm increase
- Sequential progressions especially helpful when others are weak
