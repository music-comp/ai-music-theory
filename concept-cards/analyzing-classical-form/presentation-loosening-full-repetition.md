# Quick Definition
Presentation function can be loosened by repeating the entire phrase before the continuation begins, resulting in four statements of the basic idea (instead of the standard two). This creates a distinctly looser and less integrated expression than a compound sentence, and the final measure of the repeated presentation often elides with the continuation.

# Core Definition
Another way of loosening the presentation is to repeat the entire phrase before the continuation begins. With full phrase repetition, the basic idea appears four times (compared to the standard two in a simple presentation). This could seem to create an 8-measure presentation suggesting a compound sentence, but there is a significant difference: a compound sentence features a repeated compound basic idea (two statements), whereas a repeated presentation features a repeated simple presentation (four statements of a basic idea). The latter has a distinctly looser and less integrated expression than the former. This looser organization is especially appropriate for subordinate themes and is seldom found with main themes. The final measure of the repeated presentation often elides with the beginning of the continuation -- a type of boundary process rarely found in tight-knit sentences (Caplin, Ch. 12, pp. 383-384).

# Key Properties
1. Entire presentation phrase repeated before continuation begins
2. Basic idea appears 4 times (instead of standard 2)
3. Distinctly looser than compound sentence
4. Compound sentence: 2 statements of compound basic idea
5. Repeated presentation: 4 statements of simple basic idea
6. Final measure often elides with continuation beginning
7. Appropriate for subordinate themes; seldom in main themes

# Construction / Recognition
## To Identify:
1. Count statements of the basic idea: if 4, check for repeated presentation
2. Distinguish from compound sentence (compound b.i. vs. simple b.i.)
3. Check for elision between repeated presentation and continuation
4. Note the looser, less integrated expression compared to compound sentence

# Context & Application
Full phrase repetition is one of three methods of loosening the presentation function in subordinate themes (alongside additional repetition of the basic idea and weakened tonic prolongation). The technique creates functional redundancy (more repetition than necessary to establish the basic idea) and, when combined with elision at the continuation boundary, produces a phrase structure distinctly looser than tight-knit norms.

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Labeling a repeated presentation as a compound sentence
