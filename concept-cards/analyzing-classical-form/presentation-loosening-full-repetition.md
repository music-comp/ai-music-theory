## Presentation Loosening - Full Phrase Repetition

Another way of loosening the presentation is to **repeat the entire phrase** before the continuation begins.

### Four-Statement Result

With full phrase repetition:
- Standard presentation: basic idea appears 2 times
- Repeated presentation: basic idea appears **4 times**

### Distinction from Compound Sentence

A repeated 4-measure presentation could seem to create an 8-measure presentation (suggesting compound sentence). But there is a significant difference:

**Compound sentence**: Repeated **compound basic idea** (2 statements)

**Repeated presentation**: Repeated **simple presentation** (basic idea appears 4 times)

The latter has a distinctly **looser** and less integrated expression than the former.

### Association with Subordinate Theme

This looser organization is especially appropriate for subordinate themes and is seldom found with main themes.

### Elision with Continuation

The final measure of the repeated presentation often **elides** with the beginning of the continuation - a type of boundary process rarely found in tight-knit sentences.
