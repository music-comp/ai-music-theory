# Reference to Development Section in Coda

Recalling developmental material that could not appear in the recapitulation.

## The Rationale

The recapitulation requires reworking the exposition, so opportunity to treat development section material may not arise. The logical place to refer to development ideas is the coda, especially at its start.

## Analogous Position

The opening of the coda lends itself to recalling the opening of the development because both follow similar material (closing sections of exposition and recapitulation).

## Appropriate Treatment

Early part of coda is appropriate place to destabilize home-key tonic emphasis through model-sequence technique, which may resemble development passages.

## "Terminal Development"

Earlier theorists viewed the coda as a "terminal" development, especially in Beethoven. This view has fallen into disrepute - it fails to differentiate fundamentally different tonal and formal organizations of development and coda.

To say the coda refers to development material is not to claim it functions or structures itself like a development.
