# Modulating Subordinate Theme in Recapitulation

Handling tonal adjustment when the exposition's subordinate theme was modulatory.

## The Challenge

When the exposition's subordinate theme modulates, the tonal adjustment in recapitulation is more complicated than usual.

## Solution 1: Impression of Modulation

The subordinate theme can still give impression of modulating by:
- Beginning in some other tonal region (of the home key)
- Eventually returning to emphasize the tonic region

This preserves the modulatory character within the home key framework.

## Solution 2: Direct Tonic Opening

Alternatively:
- Theme begins directly on home-key tonic
- Loses some of its original modulatory character
- Simpler but less faithful to original character

## Example

Theme might begin "off tonic" in flat-VI region and eventually "modulate" back to tonic by end of first phrase.
