# Codetta

A **codetta** is a brief postcadential unit that follows a PAC, either singly or as part of a closing section.

## Definition

The codetta:
- Follows immediately after a cadence
- Is brief (typically 1-2 measures)
- Confirms the cadential closure
- May be repeated (often with fragmentation)

## Single Codetta vs. Closing Section

- **Single codetta**: One postcadential unit
- **Closing section**: Multiple codettas in succession

## Content

Codettas typically contain:
- Tonic-confirming material
- Simplified melodic content (often conventional/formulaic)
- Accompanimental figuration
- Repeated patterns

## Harmonic Support

Codettas are usually supported by:
- Tonic prolongation
- Simple I-V-I progressions
- Pedal point on tonic

## Relation to Theme

Codettas are:
- External to the theme proper
- Postcadential (after the structural end)
- Not counted in the theme's measure structure

## Distinction from Coda

- **Codetta**: Brief, follows themes
- **Coda**: Extended, follows entire movements or large sections
