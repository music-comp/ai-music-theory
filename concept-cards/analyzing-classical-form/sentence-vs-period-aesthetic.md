## Sentence vs. Period - Aesthetic Effects

Due to the nature of their internal organization, the sentence and period theme types project quite differing aesthetic effects.

### Period's Character

The period has a **symmetrical structure** that has a certain "repose in itself" due to the balance of its two halves, which are more or less equal.

### Sentence's Character

The 8-measure sentence contains a certain **forward-striving character** due to the increased activity and compression in its continuation phrase, making it fundamentally different from the symmetrical organization of the period.

### Implications for Sonata Form

Since sonata form is inherently the most **dynamic** of the various full-movement forms, the sentence is especially well suited to initiate that formal type.

### Movement Position and Theme Choice

| Movement Type | Favored Theme Type | Reason |
|---------------|-------------------|--------|
| Fast first movement | Sentence (or sentential hybrids) | Dynamic momentum |
| Finale | Period | Lighter, more relaxed affect |
| Slow movement | No particular preference | Varies |
