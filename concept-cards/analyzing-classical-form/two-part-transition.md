## Two-Part Transition

The process of tonal destabilization sometimes takes place in **two distinct stages**, yielding a two-part transition.

### Structure

**First Part** (nonmodulating):
- Follows a main theme ending with PAC
- Leads to an HC (or dominant arrival) **in the home key**
- Remains in home key throughout

**Second Part** (modulating):
- Begins in the home key (though not necessarily on tonic)
- Modulates to the subordinate key
- Ends with an HC in the subordinate key

### Combination

The two-part transition essentially combines:
- A nonmodulating transition (first part)
- A modulating transition (second part)

### Second Part Characteristics

The second part frequently:
- Alludes to opening material of the main theme
- May begin on V7/vi, which pivots to become ii of subordinate key
- Often omits initiating function, starting with continuation (signaled by model-sequence technique)
