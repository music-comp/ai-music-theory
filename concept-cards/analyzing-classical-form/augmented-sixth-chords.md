# Quick Definition
A group of three chromatic pre-dominant harmonies (Italian, German, and French sixths) that resolve to the dominant, featuring the raised fourth scale degree and usually built over the lowered sixth degree.

# Formal Definition
The three varieties of augmented-sixth chords - the so-called Italian, German, and French sixths - are an important subclass of pre-dominant harmonies derived from the dominant of the dominant. Though they all contain the raised fourth scale degree (the leading tone of V), they are usually built over the sixth degree of the natural minor scale (lowered 6). On occasion, these harmonies may be repositioned so that the raised fourth is placed in the bass voice.

# Musical/Theoretical Formulation
All three augmented-sixth chords:
- Contain raised 4 (leading tone of V)
- Are usually built over lowered 6 in bass
- Create the characteristic augmented-sixth interval between lowered 6 and raised 4
- Function as pre-dominants, resolving to V

The three types differ in their additional pitches:
- **Italian sixth**: Only the augmented sixth interval plus the tonic pitch
- **German sixth**: Adds the lowered third (creating a dominant-seventh sonority)
- **French sixth**: Adds the second scale degree (creating a whole-tone subset)

The raised fourth degree's significance is highlighted by its normally being placed in the bass voice when repositioned, enhancing motion to the following dominant root.

# Musical Context/Application
Augmented-sixth chords:
- Function as pre-dominants within cadential progressions
- Are related to secondary (applied) dominants of V
- Create intense chromatic approach to dominant
- May substitute for other pre-dominant harmonies (II6, IV)

Analytical notation: Indicated by "national" labels (It+6, Gr+6, Fr+6).

# Common Confusions
- Treating augmented sixths as independent chord types rather than functional pre-dominants
- Not recognizing their relationship to secondary dominants of V
- Confusing the three types based on additional pitches
- Missing repositioned versions with raised 4 in bass
