# Development Section Analysis Challenges

The difficulties inherent in formal analysis of development sections.

## The Challenge

Formal analysis of development sections can be extremely difficult because:
- Variety of techniques employed is enormous and highly diverse
- The "generalities" offered only begin to scratch the surface
- Individual works often feature unique solutions

## Analytical Approach

Be prepared to encounter situations that do not entirely conform to what is described in textbooks.

Be flexible when using the analytical tools provided.

## When Standard Analysis Fails

In many cases, you may have to adopt an ad hoc approach:
- Describe the general harmonic/tonal plan as best you can
- Account for the grouping structure
- A clear "form-functional analysis" may not automatically arise

## Goal

You should be able to account for the general ways in which the fundamental units of the development present themselves in time, even when conventional labels don't perfectly fit.
