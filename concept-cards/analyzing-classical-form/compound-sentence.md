# Compound Sentence

The **compound sentence** is a 16-measure theme type built from an 8-measure compound presentation followed by an 8-measure continuation ending with a cadence.

## Structure

- 8-measure **compound presentation** (c.b.i. + repetition, no cadence)
- 8-measure **continuation** (fragmentation, acceleration → cadence)

## Compound Presentation

The compound presentation consists of:
- 4-measure **compound basic idea**
- 4-measure **repetition** (exact, statement-response, or sequential)

The entire presentation is supported by tonic prolongational harmony.

## Continuation

The continuation features standard characteristics:
- **Fragmentation** (typically to 2-measure units initially)
- **Harmonic acceleration**
- **Increased surface rhythms**
- **Harmonic sequence**

## Cadential Closure

The compound sentence can end with:
- PAC (most common)
- HC (sometimes)
- IAC (rarely)

## Genre Association

The compound sentence is often used to begin large, public genres:
- Symphony
- Concerto

It is less frequently found opening smaller, private genres like solo sonatas.

## Compressed Continuation

Many compound sentences compress their continuation into 4 measures, restoring balance through repetition or postcadential extension.
