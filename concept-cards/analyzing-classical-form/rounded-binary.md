# Quick Definition
The small ternary form with repeat signs (||: A :||: B A' :||), creating a two-reprise structure. From a functional point of view, rounded binary and small ternary are identical forms; the difference lies only in the repetition scheme.

# Core Definition
Rounded binary features two reprises: the first reprise is the A section repeated, and the second reprise is B + A' repeated together. The debate over whether this form is "binary" or "ternary" is one of the most vigorous in music theory. The binary view emphasizes the two repeated parts and the bipartite tonal shape; the ternary view emphasizes the contrasting middle and recapitulation. Both views are valid. Caplin uses "rounded binary" for the repeated version while emphasizing the ternary functional interpretation.

# Key Properties
1. Structure: ||: A :||: B A' :||
2. Functionally identical to small ternary
3. Binary/ternary debate is long-standing
4. Two-reprise structure often creates parts of similar length
5. The form name acknowledges both perspectives
