# Rounded Binary

**Rounded binary** is the small ternary form with repeat signs, creating a two-reprise structure where each section is repeated.

## Structure with Repeats

||: A :||: B A' :||

- First reprise: A section repeated
- Second reprise: B + A' repeated together

## Relation to Small Ternary

From a **functional point of view**, rounded binary and small ternary are identical forms:
- Same formal functions (exposition, contrasting middle, recapitulation)
- Same tonal organization
- Same structural relationships

The difference lies only in the repetition scheme.

## Two Parts or Three Parts?

This is one of the most vigorous debates in music theory:

**Binary view argues**:
- Two repeated parts are often similar in length
- B section depends structurally on A'
- Modulating A creates bipartite tonal shape

**Ternary view argues**:
- There is a truly contrasting middle
- The opening material is recapitulated

Both views are valid and say something important about the form.

## Terminology

This study uses "rounded binary" to distinguish cases with repetition from the broader "small ternary" concept, while emphasizing the ternary approach due to its compatibility with formal functionality.
