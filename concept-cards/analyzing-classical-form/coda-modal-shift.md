# Modal Shift in Coda

When the coda features a shift to minor mode within its thematic content.

## Occurrence

A second coda theme may bring a modal shift to minor, creating contrast with previous material and the predominantly major-mode character of the movement.

## Effect

The modal shift creates:
- Dramatic contrast
- Sense of uncertainty or tension before final resolution
- Expressive intensification before concluding PAC

## Loosening Device

Modal shift to minor functions as a loosening device typical of subordinate-theme-like organization, contributing to the loose character of many coda themes.

## Resolution

Despite the modal shift, the coda ultimately concludes with:
- Return to major mode
- Powerful PAC in home key
- Closing section confirming tonic

## Example

First coda theme ends with PAC, second coda theme begins with shift to minor, eventually returning to major for expanded cadential progression and final PAC.
