# Pseudo-core

A core substitute that has the character of a core without its structural organization.

## Definition

A unit whose dynamics, rhythm, texture, and emotional character strongly resemble those of a core but whose material is not organized by model-sequence technique.

## Characteristics Resembling Core

- Restlessness, instability, Sturm und Drang
- Relatively loud dynamic
- Thicker textures
- Continuous rhythmic activity ("chugging" effect)

## What's Missing

- No large-scale model (4-8 measures) for sequencing
- No systematic sequential repetition of the model
- No fragmentation process

## Possible Organizations

1. Prominent sequential organization of harmonies (but not supporting model-sequence)
2. Improvisatory "fantasia" or "toccata" style
3. Linear bass progression providing coherence amid harmonic rambling

## Context

- Usually preceded by pre-core-like unit
- Generally closes with HC or dominant arrival plus standing on dominant
