## Standing on the Dominant

A postcadential **standing on the dominant** serves to prolong the final harmony of the HC (or dominant arrival), often with dynamic effect.

### Functions

Two possible dynamic effects:
1. **Sustaining** (and even reinforcing) the dominant arrival
2. **Dissipating** the energy, clearing the stage for subordinate theme

### Methods of Prolongation

The dominant can be prolonged through:
- **Literal extension** of the harmony (relatively rare)
- **Pedal-point prolongation** with neighboring chords
- More extensive subordinate harmonies with bass change
- **Half-cadential progressions** repeated over the dominant

### Typical Structure

Most typically brings:
- A new 2-measure idea (may be larger or smaller)
- The idea is repeated one or more times
- Then fragmented or otherwise liquidated

### Labeling Challenge

No convenient term exists for the opening idea of a standing on the dominant:
- Cannot use "codetta" (traditionally reserved for post-PAC contexts)
- Cannot use "basic idea" (standing on dominant isn't a complete theme; basic ideas normally use tonic)
- Generally speak of a "new idea"
