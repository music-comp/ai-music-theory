# Coda Theme

The thematic unit(s) appearing within a coda, distinguished from codettas.

## Definition

A major distinguishing feature of a coda (as opposed to a closing section of codettas) is the presence of at least one thematic unit that concludes with a PAC - the coda theme.

## Typical Organization

Frequently organized along lines of a subordinate theme, featuring:
- Standard loosening devices
- Highly expanded cadential area
- Extensions and expansions

## Variations in Structure

- Occasionally tight-knit types (sentence, period, hybrid)
- Often based on main theme material when tight-knit
- Some units resemble transitions (HC goal, model-sequence)

## Functionally Neutral Label

Unlike "main theme" or "subordinate theme," coda theme does not imply particular formal characteristics - only that the thematic unit occurs within the coda's scope.

## Closing the Coda Theme

Most close with PAC in home key. Some conclude with HC (resembling transition or development structures).
