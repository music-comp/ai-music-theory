## Hierarchy of Formal Functions

Formal functions operate at multiple levels of structure in classical form.

### Levels of Formal Function

**Theme Level**: The phrase functions (presentation, antecedent, continuation, cadential) are used to construct themes.

**Thematic Level**: The same phrase functions are also used to construct the larger thematic functions of sonata form (transition, subordinate theme, development core), though typically in a looser formal expression.

### The Caplin Approach

What distinguishes this approach to analyzing classical form is the emphasis on identifying constituent formal functions at **all levels of structure** throughout the entirety of the full-movement form.

### Application

The phrase functions appear differently at each level:

| Context | Expression |
|---------|------------|
| Main Theme | Tight-knit, clearly defined |
| Transition | Looser, extended |
| Subordinate Theme | Loosest, expanded |
| Development Core | Very loose, sequential |
