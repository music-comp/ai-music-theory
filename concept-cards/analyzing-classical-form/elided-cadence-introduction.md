# Elided Cadence at End of Introduction

When the final cadence of the slow introduction overlaps with the beginning of the exposition.

## The Technique

Often enough, introductions close with an authentic cadence that elides with beginning of exposition:
- Dominant at end of introduction
- Resolution to tonic simultaneously begins main theme
- Single moment serves dual function

## Alternative Readings

Some such cadences could be heard as evaded rather than elided, in which case introduction would end without genuine cadential closure.

## Analytical Consideration

The distinction between elided PAC and evaded cadence at end of introduction may be ambiguous. Consider:
- Whether discontinuity suggests evasion
- Whether smooth connection suggests elision
- Whether subsequent material suggests "one more try"

## Extended Overlap

In exceptional cases, the structural end of the introduction may occur somewhat after the exposition has begun, with introduction material continuing into the faster tempo.
