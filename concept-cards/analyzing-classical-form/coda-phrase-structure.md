# Phrase-structural Organization of Coda

How codas are typically structured in terms of phrase and thematic units.

## Coda Theme: Subordinate-like

Coda themes usually resemble loose organization of subordinate themes:
- Weak tonic prolongation
- Expanded cadential progression
- Internal dominant arrival
- Modal shift to minor
- Omitted initiating function

## Coda Theme: Tight-knit

Occasionally conventional tight-knit organization appears:
- Usually based on main theme of exposition
- May duplicate its form
- Can be derived from other material

## Transitional or Developmental Passages

Some units conclude with HC (not PAC):
- Resemble transition or development structures
- HC usually appears early in coda
- Especially when section starts without obvious formal beginning

## Multiple Coda Themes

Lengthy codas may contain two or more coda themes, each with its own PAC.

## Closing Section

Most codas end with closing section (codettas) following final PAC. May be new or based on earlier material. Sometimes restored from deleted recapitulation material.
