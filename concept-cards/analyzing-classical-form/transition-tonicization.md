## Tonicization in Transition

**Tonicization of remote regions** is another loosening device used in transitions to destabilize the home key.

### Function

Brief tonicizations in the transition:
- Create tonal instability
- Disorient the listener's sense of key
- Smooth the path toward the subordinate key

### Common Regions Tonicized

In major-mode movements:
- **vi** (submediant) - very common, often pivots to ii of subordinate key
- **ii** - may be tonicized en route to V
- **IV** - occasionally tonicized

### The Submediant as Pivot

The appearance of vi is so common in transitions that it serves as a "flag" signaling modulation:
- vi of home key = ii of subordinate key (in major)
- This pivot chord relationship facilitates smooth modulation

### Distinction from Development

Unlike the development section:
- Transition tonicizations are **brief**
- They do not establish genuine keys
- They serve the goal of reaching the subordinate key dominant
