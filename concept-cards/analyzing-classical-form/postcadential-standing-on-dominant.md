# Postcadential Standing on the Dominant

A **postcadential standing on the dominant** follows the concluding HC of a contrasting middle, reinforcing dominant harmony before the recapitulation.

## Context

This function appears:
- After the B section closes with an HC
- Before the A' section begins
- May elide with the HC or follow directly after

## Preparation Techniques

Two devices typically appear:

1. **Textural dissolution**
   - Systematic reduction of texture
   - May end with single voice
   - "Clears the stage" for fresh return

2. **Motivic anticipation**
   - Motives derived from upcoming basic idea
   - Prepares listener for recapitulation
   - Creates smooth connection to A'

## Elision Option

The standing on the dominant may:
- **Follow** the HC (in next measure)
- **Elide** with HC (begin at same moment as cadential arrival)

## Distinction from B Section Proper

When standing on dominant is postcadential:
- It **extends** the B section's final harmony
- The HC marks the structural end of B
- Standing on dominant is an afterthought/extension

When standing on dominant IS the B section:
- It follows a PAC (from A section)
- It does not extend any cadence
