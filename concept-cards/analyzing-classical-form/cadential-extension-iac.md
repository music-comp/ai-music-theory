## Cadential Extension - Imperfect Authentic Cadence

Most cadential extensions occur when a promised PAC fails to materialize, motivating additional cadential units.

### IAC as Extension Trigger

Since a subordinate theme is **required** to close with a PAC in the subordinate key:
- The appearance of an IAC signals the theme has not yet reached its true end
- The preceding phrase is typically repeated
- The repetition then leads to a PAC

### Antecedent-Consequent Quality

When a phrase ending with IAC is repeated to close with PAC:
- A certain sense of antecedent-consequent functionality is expressed
- But this should **not** normally be considered a period:
  - The passage does not function as self-contained, tight-knit theme
  - Rarely contains other characteristics of true antecedent/consequent
  - The phrase ending with IAC sometimes elides with the following phrase
