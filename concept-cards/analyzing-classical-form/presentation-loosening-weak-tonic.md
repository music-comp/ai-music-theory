## Presentation Loosening - Weakened Tonic Prolongation

Presentation function can acquire a **loose expression** if its supporting tonic prolongation is weakened or destabilized.

### The Strongest Prolongation

The strongest and stablest prolongation features:
- Tonic in **root position**
- Subordinate harmonies on **weak metrical positions**

This is typical of tight-knit sentences.

### Methods of Weakening

**1. Inverting the prolonged harmony**
- Opening prolongation of I6 instead of I
- Less stable foundation

**2. Metrically accenting subordinate harmonies**
- Placing dominant on metrically strong positions
- Weakens the sense of tonic stability

**3. Undermining with dominant pedal**
- All constituent harmonies placed over a dominant pedal (in bass)
- Dominant pedal continues from end of transition
- Creates two levels of prolongation:
  - Lower level: tonic prolongation (satisfies presentation requirement)
  - Higher level: dominant prolongation (undermines but doesn't obliterate)
