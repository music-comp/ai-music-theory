# Standing on the Dominant (Development)

The postcadential prolongation of dominant harmony following the half cadence or dominant arrival that closes a core.

## Characteristics

- Follows the HC or dominant arrival closing the core
- Often highly extended
- May consist of several distinct sections
- Each section may have its own melodic-motivic content

## Preparatory Function

When the standing on the dominant occurs at the end of the development:
- Anticipatory motives derived from the basic idea of the main theme often appear
- Helps prepare for the beginning of the recapitulation
- Creates strong expectation for the return of the main theme

## Terminological Note

Traditional theories call this a "retransition," but by this point the home key has already been achieved by the half cadence. The term retransition is better applied to passages whose primary function is actually returning the music home.
