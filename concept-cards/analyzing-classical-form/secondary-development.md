# Secondary Development

A newly composed passage using model-sequence technique that appears in the recapitulation.

## Definition

Term used by Charles Rosen for new model-sequence passages added to the recapitulation that "develop" the material from earlier sections.

## Typical Locations

- Main theme of recapitulation
- Transition of recapitulation

## Differences from True Development Core

Unlike in a core:
- The model is generally short
- The sequential activity is rarely modulatory
- Harmonies remain closely bound to home key

## Purpose

- Throws new light on old ideas
- Especially treats ideas not featured in the actual development section
- Compensates for lack of core in brief developments
- Provides variety within the recapitulation
