# Small Binary vs. Compound Period

When Part 1 of a small binary ends with an HC, distinguishing from a compound period can be challenging.

## The Confusion

An 8-measure unit ending with HC might be:
- **Compound antecedent** (of a compound period)
- **Part 1** (of a small binary)

## The Key Test

Observe the **repetition scheme**:

**Compound period**:
- Antecedent is NOT repeated before consequent
- The repeat brings stronger cadential closure

**Small binary**:
- Part 1 IS repeated (ends again with HC)
- Part 2 is then repeated (also ends again with HC at its first ending)

## The Principle

If a presumed compound antecedent is **fully repeated** before the "consequent":
- Reinterpret as Part 1 of small binary
- The period option is no longer viable

## Why?

A period's effect depends on:
- First repetition bringing stronger closure
- Antecedent NOT being repeated before consequent

When the "antecedent" is fully repeated and ends again with HC, the period relationship is broken.
