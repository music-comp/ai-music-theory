## Development Keys (DK)

**Development keys** are the tonal regions explored in the development section, typically confirmed by an HC (less often by a PAC).

### Typical Development Keys

**In Major-Mode Movements**:
- vi (submediant minor)
- iii (mediant minor)
- ii (supertonic minor)

**In Minor-Mode Movements**:
- iv (subdominant minor)
- v (dominant minor)

### Character

In contrast to the exposition (which emphasizes major modality), the development tends to emphasize **minor modalities**.

### Confirmation

Development keys are typically confirmed by:
- Half cadence (most common)
- PAC (less often)

### Example
If home key is C major:
- Development keys might include A minor (vi), E minor (iii), D minor (ii)
