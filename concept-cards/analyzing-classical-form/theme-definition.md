# Quick Definition
In Caplin's system, a theme is a complete formal unit encompassing melodic-motivic content, accompanimental texture, and harmonic progressions, normally closed by a genuine cadence. Opening themes typically fill eight measures.

# Core Definition
The term theme has an entirely different meaning from popular usage. It refers to a complete formal unit, which includes its particular melodic-motivic content, its accompanimental texture, and its supporting harmonic progressions. A theme is normally brought to a close by a genuine cadence of some kind. Themes that begin a movement typically fill eight measures of music, but many themes, especially ones later in the movement, are much longer (p. 55).

# Prerequisites
This is a foundational concept with no prerequisites within this source.

# Key Properties
1. A complete formal unit, not merely a melody or tune
2. Includes melodic-motivic content, texture, and harmonic progressions
3. Normally closed by a genuine cadence
4. Opening themes typically 8 measures
5. Later themes may be much longer
6. Major theme types: sentence, period, hybrid, compound, small ternary, small binary

# Construction / Recognition
## To Identify a Theme:
1. Look for a complete formal unit with cadential closure
2. Identify its melodic-motivic content, texture, and harmonic support
3. Determine the theme type (sentence, period, hybrid, etc.)
4. Note its position within the larger form

# Context & Application
Traditional theories number themes as "first theme," "second theme." Caplin's usage is fundamentally different: a theme is a structural unit, not a recognizable melody. This distinction is crucial for formal analysis.

# Examples
**Example 1** (p. 56, Ex. 2.1): Beethoven, Piano Sonata Op. 2, No. 1, i, mm. 1-8 -- an 8-m. sentence theme.

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Identifying a theme by its melody alone
  **Correction**: A theme includes melody, harmony, texture, and cadential closure

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: Using "theme" in the popular sense of a recognizable tune
  **Clarification**: In this system, theme is a complete formal unit with cadential closure
