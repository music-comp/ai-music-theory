# Two-Part Subordinate Theme in Recapitulation

Structural changes necessitated by a two-part subordinate theme from the exposition.

## The Problem

In exposition, internal HC and standing on dominant emphasize dominant harmony of subordinate key, especially with nonmodulating transition.

If left intact in recapitulation, two cadential articulations of home-key dominant would result:
1. HC of transition
2. Internal HC of two-part subordinate theme

This creates potential redundancy.

## Solution

Either:
- First part of subordinate theme is deleted
- Portions of first part are assimilated into preceding transition

Then:
- Second part follows as actual subordinate theme in recapitulation

## Example

Material functioning as internal HC and standing on dominant may conclude the transition in recapitulation (rather than beginning subordinate theme), with deleted first part and retained second part.
