# Upbeat (Anacrusis)

An **upbeat** (anacrusis) is material that precedes the first downbeat of a formal unit, creating rhythmic momentum into the main idea.

## Relation to Basic Idea

The basic idea often begins with an upbeat:
- The upbeat leads into the first downbeat
- It belongs to the basic idea
- It does not constitute a separate formal unit

## Phrase Boundaries

When determining phrase boundaries:
- Count the upbeat as part of the phrase it leads into
- The phrase begins with its upbeat, not its first downbeat
- Cadential arrivals typically occur on downbeats

## Measuring Phrases

A 4-measure phrase with upbeat:
- The upbeat is the first sound
- Four complete measures follow
- The fourth downbeat brings the cadence

## Important Distinctions

The upbeat is:
- Part of the melodic gesture
- Not a separate introduction
- Integral to the basic idea's character

## Lead-in vs. Upbeat

- **Upbeat**: Part of the phrase it leads into
- **Lead-in**: Connects end of one phrase to beginning of next (between phrases)
