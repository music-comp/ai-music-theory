# Intensification of Recapitulation Cadence

Ornamental changes that create greater intensity for cadential arrivals in the recapitulation.

## Purpose

The most important cadence in the form (the essential sonata closure) should receive the most powerful treatment.

## Methods

### Harmonic Embellishment
- Pre-dominant prolonged by additional harmonies (e.g., vii diminished seventh / V)
- More chromatic pre-dominant preparation
- Extended dominant prolongation

### Rhythmic Intensification
- Added sixteenth notes where quarters appeared
- Faster rhythmic motion approaching cadence
- More elaborate trills or ornaments

### Textural Changes
- Fuller texture at cadential arrival
- Additional voices or doublings
- Increased dynamic level

## Effect

Creates sense of arrival and climax appropriate to the form's tonal resolution.
