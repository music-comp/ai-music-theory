# Expanded Cadential Area in Recapitulation

When the subordinate theme's cadential area is more extensively expanded in the recapitulation than in the exposition.

## Purpose

The most rhetorically powerful cadential emphasis in the entire form should confirm the home key (not the subordinate key).

## Methods

Expansion may involve:
- Longer expanded cadential progression
- Additional deceptive or evaded cadences
- More elaborate pre-cadential preparation
- Interpolated passages before final PAC

## Contrast with Exposition

In the exposition, cadential expansion serves to establish the subordinate key with sufficient weight.

In the recapitulation, even greater expansion can be given to ensure the home key receives the most powerful cadential treatment.

## Effect

Creates climactic sense of arrival at the movement's fundamental tonal goal - the essential sonata closure.
