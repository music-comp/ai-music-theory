# Compound Consequent

A **compound consequent** is the closing 8-measure unit of a compound period, structured as a complete simple theme that ends with a strong cadence (PAC).

## Function

The compound consequent:
- Repeats the compound antecedent
- Closes with a PAC to complete the entire theme
- Brings stronger cadential emphasis than the antecedent

## Return of Basic Idea

The return of the opening basic idea at m. 9-10:
- Signals the start of the compound consequent
- Creates the sense that the antecedent is being "repeated"
- Should sound fresh (not anticipated earlier)

## Cadential Emphasis

The final phrase of the compound consequent often features:
- **Expanded cadential progression** (ECP)
- Greater temporal weight for cadential function
- More emphatic closure than the antecedent

## Structural Modeling

The compound consequent is modeled on the antecedent, but:
- The second phrase may differ from the antecedent's
- Changes serve to achieve stronger closure
- A continuation⇒cadential phrase may become purely cadential

## Continuation vs. Cadential

When supported by an ECP, the compound consequent's final phrase:
- May resemble continuation (with fragmention, acceleration)
- May be purely cadential (with few continuational traits)
- Often labeled continuation⇒cadential
