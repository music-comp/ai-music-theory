# Quick Definition
The closing 8-m. unit of a compound period, modeled on the compound antecedent but closing with a PAC. The return of the basic idea at mm. 9-10 signals consequent function, and the final phrase often features an expanded cadential progression for greater cadential emphasis.

# Core Definition
The compound consequent repeats the compound antecedent but achieves stronger closure. The return of the initial basic idea at mm. 9-10 must sound fresh. The second phrase of the consequent often differs from the antecedent's second phrase, typically featuring an ECP to throw greater weight onto the final PAC. When supported by an ECP, the final phrase may resemble the hybrid antecedent + cadential. The two continuation phrases in a compound period may exhibit diverse melodic-motivic content.

# Key Properties
1. Modeled on compound antecedent
2. Closes with PAC (instead of HC)
3. Return of basic idea at m. 9 must sound fresh
4. Final phrase often has ECP for emphasis
5. May change from continuation to cadential function in final phrase
