# Unusual Development Endings

Exceptional situations where the development ends on something other than home-key dominant.

## Standard Ending

The final harmony of the development is usually the dominant of the home key.

## Exception 1: Recapitulation Not Starting on Tonic

If the recapitulation does not begin with home-key tonic, the development's final harmony leads most naturally into the recapitulation's opening harmony.

Example: If recapitulation begins with dominant seventh, development may close with pre-dominant (e.g., V/V).

## Exception 2: Dominant Substitutes

Harmonies that can substitute for the normal home-key dominant:
- V/vi (contains leading tone)
- V/iii (contains leading tone)

Both contain the leading tone - a necessary condition for dominant functionality.

## Cross-Relation Mitigation

To avoid abrupt resolution (due to cross-relation):
- Texture at very end of development reduced to single voice
- Part of broader liquidation process common to developments
