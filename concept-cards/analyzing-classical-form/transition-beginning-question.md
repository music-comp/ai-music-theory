## Where Does a Transition Begin?

Determining where the transition begins can be challenging, especially when it uses main-theme material.

### Common Mistake

Students may locate the beginning at the point where:
- The music departs from the expected path
- The modulation takes place

### Better Understanding

That moment marks the end of main-theme **material**, but from a strictly formal point of view it represents neither:
- An **end** (of the main theme)
- Nor a **beginning** (of the transition)

Rather, it stands **in the middle** of a formal process.

### The True Beginning

The transition's formal beginning is typically at:
- The start of a new presentation phrase
- Even if that phrase initially seems like it might be a consequent to the main theme

### Retrospective Understanding

The identification often happens **retrospectively**:
- Initial impression: consequent of compound period beginning
- Later realization: this was the beginning of the transition
- Prior HC truly closed the main theme
