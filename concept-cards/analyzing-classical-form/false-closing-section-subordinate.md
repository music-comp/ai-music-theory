## False Closing Section in Subordinate Theme

Within a subordinate-theme group, a true closing section appears only after the final theme. However, a PAC of any prior theme may be followed by material with codetta characteristics, which is then retrospectively understood as beginning a new subordinate theme.

### How It Works

1. A subordinate theme closes with PAC
2. Material with **codetta characteristics** follows:
   - Tonic pedal
   - Tonic scale degree prolonged in soprano
   - Recessive dynamic
3. This material is developed beyond typical codetta scope
4. Retrospectively understood as a **basic idea** whose repetition creates a presentation

### "False" Because

The closing section proves to be "false" - it "becomes" the initiating unit of the next subordinate theme.

### When Exposition Lacks True Closing Section

Occasionally, an exposition contains no true closing section:
- Instead, a false closing section appears
- It is extended into a genuine subordinate theme (the last of the group)
- Continuation and cadential functions are added
- The resulting theme tends to be relatively tight-knit
