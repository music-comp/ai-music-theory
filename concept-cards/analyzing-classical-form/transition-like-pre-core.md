# Transition-like Pre-core

A pre-core formed in a way that resembles a transition.

## Characteristics

- Phrase structure usually sentential
- Modulatory character
- Harmonic goal is dominant of a development key

## Sequential Organization

Like most transitions:
- Sequential progressions often found
- Model must be sufficiently small (usually no larger than one measure)
- Must not suggest beginning of core proper

## Distinguishing from Core

The model within a transition-like pre-core is small enough that it does not give the impression of a genuine core, which requires models of 4-8 measures.

## Example Features

Might include:
- Basic idea extended to several measures
- Polyphonic texture
- Continuation featuring model-sequence technique
- HC and standing on dominant of development key
