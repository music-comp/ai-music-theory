# Tonal Adjustment

The process of modifying the recapitulation's music to remain in the home key.

## Basic Requirement

A modulating transition in the exposition must be adjusted in the recapitulation to:
- Remain in the home key
- End there with dominant harmony
- Allow subordinate theme to enter with home-key tonic

## Typical Method

Music shifts toward flat-side subdominant region so that:
- Tonicized IV harmony eventually takes role of pre-dominant
- This leads to final home-key dominant of transition
- Flat-side shift to IV is balanced by "sharp-side" shift back to home key

## Analogy to Exposition

The sharp-side shift back to home key in recapitulation is analogous to the sharp-side shift from home key to subordinate key in exposition.

## Result

The goal dominant is achieved by sharp-side shift in both sections:
- Exposition: Shift yields new key
- Recapitulation: Shift yields original key, but sounding "fresh"
