# Core Concluding Function

The harmonic goal and cadential articulation that closes a development core.

## Primary Goal

The great majority of cores have as their harmonic goal a **dominant** of either:
- The home key
- A development key

A core thus tends to end along the lines of a transition.

## Types of Closure

### Most Common
- Half cadence (leads to fragmentation)

### Alternative
- Dominant arrival (premature dominant arrival is frequent)

### Rare
- Authentic cadential progression in a development key
- May be evaded or abandoned (cadential closure may not be achieved)

## Historical Evolution

- Baroque: Most tonal regions received PAC confirmation
- Pre-classical/Galant: Development units close with PAC in development key
- High Classical: Units end with dominant harmony (HC)
- Later Classical (especially Beethoven): Dominant arrival replaces HC
