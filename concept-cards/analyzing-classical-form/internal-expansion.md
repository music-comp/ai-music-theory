# Quick Definition
Internal expansion inserts additional material within a phrase before it reaches its point of functional completion, stretching the phrase from within rather than adding to its end.

# Core Definition
Caplin distinguishes expansion (inserting material within a phrase before its completion) from extension (adding material after a function is expressed). Expansion is especially associated with the presentation function, where additional repetitions of the basic idea create an "expanded presentation" (e.g., 6 mm. instead of 4). The expanded material could be removed to reconstruct a normative version. At the theme level, when phrases are expanded or extended, the entire theme becomes "expanded" beyond its normative length.

# Prerequisites
- **Phrase deviations** — Must understand the broader framework of deviation techniques

# Key Properties
1. Material inserted BEFORE phrase reaches functional completion
2. Especially associated with presentation function (extra repetitions of basic idea)
3. An "expanded presentation" commonly results from a third statement of the basic idea
4. The expansion can theoretically be removed to reveal a normative version
5. The relation between extension and expansion can be complicated

# Construction / Recognition
## To Identify:
1. Determine where the phrase would normatively reach its goal
2. Locate inserted material that delays arrival at that goal
3. Check if the material is repeated or varied content from within the same function
4. Try reconstructing a normative version by removing the expansion

# Context & Application
Internal expansion commonly affects the presentation phrase, creating expanded presentations of 5 or 6 measures. At the theme level, individual phrase expansions and extensions combine to create an overall theme expansion.

# Examples
**Example 1** (p. 147): Example 5.2 -- expanded presentation with additional statements of the basic idea creating a 6-m. presentation instead of the normative 4 mm.

# Relationships
## Builds Upon

## Enables
- **Theme expansion** — Multiple expanded phrases create expanded themes
## Related
- **Phrase extension** — Both add material, but at different points
## Contrasts With
- **Phrase extension** — Extension adds after completion; expansion adds before completion
- **Phrase compression** — Opposite process

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Conflating expansion with extension
  **Correction**: Expansion inserts within a function before its completion; extension adds after the function is expressed

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: Whether a third statement of the basic idea is "extension of presentation" or "expansion"
  **Clarification**: When the additional repetition occurs before the continuation begins, it is expansion of the presentation; the relation can be complicated and sometimes both terms apply
