## Retransition in Exposition

The final codetta of the closing section is sometimes followed by a **retransition**, a passage that functions to bring the music back to the home key and lead smoothly to a repeat of the exposition.

### Position

- Follows the final codetta of the closing section
- Precedes the repeat of the exposition
- Bridges the subordinate key back to the home key

### Material

The material of the retransition may:
- Grow directly out of the final codetta
- Be quite different from the closing section
- Anticipate main-theme motives to prepare for the repeated exposition

### Function

The retransition helps effect a smooth return to the home key for the repeat of the exposition by:
- Transitioning harmonically from SK to HK
- Preparing the listener for the return of main-theme material
- Creating formal continuity across the repeat sign
