## Definition

Melodic overlap is a boundary process in which the goal of a unit's melody concludes on the downbeat of the first measure of the next unit.

## Mechanism

- The melodic line of one phrase reaches its goal note
- This goal note lands on the **downbeat** of the next phrase
- The melody bridges the boundary between units

## Distinction from Elision

| Melodic Overlap | Elision |
|-----------------|---------|
| Melody's goal on next downbeat | Same moment = end and beginning |
| Melodic connection | Structural overlap |
| Goal note spans boundary | Single point serves dual function |

## Effect

- Creates smooth melodic continuity
- Connects phrases without a gap
- The melody "reaches over" into the next unit
- Softens formal articulation while preserving it

## Common Contexts

- Between phrases within a theme
- At points of formal connection
- When smooth melodic flow is desired
- In lyrical, cantabile passages

## Analytical Recognition

Look for:
- A phrase's final melodic note
- Landing on the downbeat of the next phrase
- While the new phrase's content begins simultaneously
