# Development Tonal Organization

The tonal plan of a development section follows relatively conventional patterns.

## Typical Tonal Plan

1. Begins in the subordinate key (from the end of the exposition)
2. Explores minor-mode tonal regions of the home key
3. Returns to the home key
4. Ends on dominant harmony of the home key

## Minor-Mode Regions in Major Keys

In order of importance:
- Submediant (vi)
- Mediant (iii)
- Supertonic (ii)

## Minor-Mode Regions in Minor Keys

- Subdominant (iv)
- Dominant (v) - minor because based on natural minor scale

## Significance

The emphasis on minor-mode regions creates marked contrast with the exposition, which resides largely in a major-mode subordinate key.
