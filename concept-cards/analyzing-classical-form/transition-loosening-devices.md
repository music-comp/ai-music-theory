## Transition Loosening Devices

Compared to the main theme, the transition acquires a **looser** formal organization through various devices.

### Loosening Devices in Transitions

- **Extension of continuation function** - longer than typical
- **Expansion of cadential function** - elaborated HC approach
- **Omission of initiating function** - starting with continuation
- **Asymmetrical grouping structure** - irregular phrase lengths
- **Modal shift** - moving to minor or introducing chromaticism
- **Tonicization of remote regions** - brief moves away from home/subordinate keys
- **Nonconventional formal structures** - not fitting standard patterns

### Phrase Functions in Transitions

- **Sentential functions** (presentation, continuation, cadential) are most commonly employed
- **Periodic functions** (antecedent) occur now and then
- True consequent (requiring authentic cadence) is seldom found

### Appropriate Loosening

Loosening techniques are entirely appropriate to the transition's fundamental functions:
- Destabilizing the home key
- Effecting modulation
- Motivating the subordinate theme

Structural loosening does **not** reflect compositional inadequacy; deft handling of loosening devices is a sure sign of compositional skill.
