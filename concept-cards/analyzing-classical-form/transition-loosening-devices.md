# Quick Definition
Compared to the main theme, the transition acquires a looser formal organization through seven devices: extension of continuation function, expansion of cadential function, omission of initiating function, asymmetrical grouping structure, modal shift, tonicization of remote regions, and nonconventional formal structures. Sentential functions (presentation, continuation, cadential) are most commonly employed.

# Core Definition
In comparison to the main theme, the transition acquires a looser formal organization. Caplin lists seven loosening devices: (1) extension of continuation function (longer than typical); (2) expansion of cadential function (elaborated HC approach); (3) omission of an initiating function (starting directly with continuation); (4) asymmetrical grouping structure (irregular phrase lengths); (5) modal shift (moving to minor or introducing chromaticism); (6) tonicization of remote regions (brief moves away from home/subordinate keys); and (7) nonconventional formal structures (not fitting standard theme types). Transitions most commonly employ sentential phrase functions (presentation, continuation, cadential). Periodic function (antecedent) occurs now and then, though a true consequent (which by its nature must lead to an authentic cadence) is seldom found. Structural loosening does not reflect compositional inadequacy; deft handling of loosening devices is a sure sign of compositional skill (Caplin, Ch. 11, pp. 331, 346-350).

# Construction / Recognition
## To Identify:
1. Compare the transition's formal organization with the main theme's
2. Check for each of the seven loosening devices
3. Identify which sentential functions are present
4. Note any periodic functions (antecedent occurs; consequent is rare)
5. Assess the overall degree of loosening relative to the main theme

# Context & Application
The loosening of the transition serves its three fundamental functions: destabilizing the home key, loosening the formal organization, and liquidating characteristic motivic material. Each loosening device contributes differently: extension and expansion add temporal weight; omission of initiation removes formal grounding; asymmetry disrupts balance; modal shift and remote tonicization create tonal instability; nonconventional structures resist categorization.
