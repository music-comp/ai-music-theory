# Small Ternary Structure Overview

The small ternary (rounded binary) is a three-section form based on contrast and return.

## Basic Structure

||: A :||: B + A' :||

- **A (Exposition)**: Tight-knit 8m theme → PAC
- **B (Contrasting Middle)**: Loose, dominant emphasis → usually no cadence or HC
- **A' (Recapitulation)**: Return + PAC in home key

## Functional Characteristics

| Section | Organization | Tonal Emphasis | Cadence |
|---------|-------------|----------------|---------|
| A | Tight-knit | Tonic | PAC |
| B | Loose | Dominant | HC or none |
| A' | Variable | Tonic | PAC |

## A Section Options

- Nonmodulating: Stays in home key
- Modulating: Goes to subordinate key (V or III)

## B Section Options

1. Standing on the dominant
2. Continuation phrase → HC
3. Modulating B with retransition

## A' Section Options

1. Same as A (nonmodulating A only)
2. Compressed to single consequent
3. Expanded with development
4. Tonally adjusted (if A modulated)

## Essential Requirements

A' must:
1. Return the basic idea
2. Close with PAC in home key
