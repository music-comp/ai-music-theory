# Quick Definition
The small ternary (rounded binary) is a three-section form (A-B-A') based on contrast and return: a relatively closed exposition (A) is juxtaposed with a structurally open contrasting middle (B), and the original material returns in a recapitulation (A') that ensures complete closure.

# Core Definition
The small ternary consists of three main sections: A (exposition), B (contrasting middle), and A' (recapitulation). Two basic notions lie at its heart: (1) a relatively closed thematic unit is juxtaposed with a structurally open unit of contrasting content and formal organization, and (2) the original unit is brought back in a manner that ensures complete closure of the theme. The typical repeat structure is ||: A :||: B + A' :||, resembling the "rounded binary" arrangement. The functional terms exposition, contrasting middle, and recapitulation relate to those used in sonata form, though the meanings differ in some respects.

# Prerequisites
- **Sentence** -- One of the conventional types used for the A section
- **Period** -- One of the conventional types used for the A section
- **Cadential closure** -- Required for both A and A' sections

# Key Properties
1. Three sections: A (exposition), B (contrasting middle), A' (recapitulation)
2. A: tight-knit, 8-measure theme, closes with PAC
3. B: loose, emphasizes dominant, ends with HC or no cadence
4. A': returns basic idea, closes with PAC in home key
5. Repeat structure: ||: A :||: B + A' :||
6. A may be nonmodulating or modulating (to V or III)
7. A' must close with PAC in home key regardless

# Construction / Recognition
## Section Options:
| Section | Organization | Tonal Emphasis | Cadence | Options |
|---------|-------------|----------------|---------|---------|
| A | Tight-knit | Tonic | PAC | Nonmodulating or modulating |
| B | Loose | Dominant | HC or none | Standing on V, continuation, modulating |
| A' | Variable | Tonic | PAC | Same as A, compressed, expanded, tonally adjusted |

## Essential Requirements for A':
1. Must return the basic idea
2. Must close with PAC in home key

# Context & Application
The small ternary is the third fundamental theme type after the sentence and period. It introduces the crucial distinction between tight-knit and loose formal organization, which becomes the most important analytical concept for studying sonata form. The functional terms (exposition, contrasting middle, recapitulation) deliberately anticipate sonata-form terminology, reflecting the structural parallels between the two forms. The small ternary provides an ideal context for learning comparative analysis of exposition and recapitulation.

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Placing an HC label at the end of a standing-on-the-dominant B section
  **Correction**: A standing on the dominant prolongs a single harmony; no cadential progression occurs, so no cadence (including HC) can be labeled
