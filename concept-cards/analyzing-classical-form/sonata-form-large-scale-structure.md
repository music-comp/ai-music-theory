## Sonata Form Large-Scale Structure

Sonata form consists of three required sections, each with a unique section function:

1. **Exposition** - presents thematic material and establishes tonal conflict
2. **Development** - explores and intensifies material and tonal areas
3. **Recapitulation** - resolves tonal conflict by returning material to home key

### Repetition Structure

- The exposition is almost always repeated (with repetition signs, though sometimes written out)
- The development and recapitulation together are sometimes repeated
- Resulting structure: ||: Exposition :|| ||: Development-Recapitulation :||

### Relationship to Small Ternary

This structure resembles the "rounded binary" version of the small ternary, with the three sections corresponding to the A, B, and A' sections of that form.

### Optional Framing Functions

Two optional functions may frame the form:
- A **slow introduction** may precede the exposition
- A **coda** may follow the recapitulation
