# Ascending Stepwise Sequence in Coda

The most popular sequential pattern used within codas.

## Characteristics

- Ascending stepwise pattern (model rises by step with each repetition)
- Naturally projects increasingly intense emotional excitement
- Used to build energy and momentum
- Common especially early in the coda

## Function

Provides harmonic contrast between:
- End of recapitulation (emphasizing home-key tonic)
- End of coda (also emphasizing home-key tonic)

The sequential passage creates variety within the home-key framework.

## Context

Appears especially when:
- Coda starts with model-sequence passage
- Building toward climactic moment
- Creating progressive dynamic toward final cadence

## Relationship to Core

Model-sequence technique in coda may suggest development core, but:
- Sequencing is nonmodulatory
- Harmonies remain closely bound to home key
- Does not achieve the tonal instability of true development
