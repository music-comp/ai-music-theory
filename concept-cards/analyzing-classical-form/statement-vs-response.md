# Statement vs. Response (Compound Presentation)

When a tonic compound basic idea is followed by a dominant version, distinguishing statement-response (presentation) from antecedent-consequent (period) requires careful analysis.

## The Confusion

When compound basic idea moves from I to V (m. 4) and V to I (m. 8):
- It may seem like antecedent ending with HC (m. 4)
- Followed by consequent ending with authentic cadence (m. 8)

This suggests a period might be forming.

## The Key Test

Examine whether there is a **cadential progression** or only **prolongational** harmony:

**Statement-response (presentation)**:
- Single harmonic progression per phrase
- No distinct cadential progression "ending" a prior prolongation
- The situation is exclusively prolongational

**Antecedent-consequent (period)**:
- Distinct cadential progression at end of each phrase
- Prolongational harmony followed by cadential

## Practical Application

Listen for:
- Does the harmony at m. 4 sound like a genuine cadential goal?
- Or does it sound like part of a broader tonic prolongation?

## Result

True statement-response creates an **open-ended presentation**, not a closed periodic structure.
