# Suffix

A **suffix** is postcadential material that follows a PAC but is more elaborate than a simple codetta, often introducing new melodic material while confirming closure.

## Definition

The suffix:
- Follows a PAC
- Is postcadential (after the structural end)
- May introduce new melodic ideas
- Still functions to confirm closure

## Distinction from Codetta

- **Codetta**: Brief, simple, formulaic material
- **Suffix**: More elaborate, may have new melodic content

Both are postcadential and function to confirm closure.

## Content

Suffixes may contain:
- New melodic ideas (not heard before)
- More elaborate figuration than codettas
- Still predominantly tonic-confirming harmony

## Relation to Theme

Like codettas, suffixes are:
- External to the theme proper
- Postcadential
- Not counted in the theme's measure structure

## Function

Despite more elaborate content, the suffix still:
- Confirms the preceding cadence
- Projects some degree of recessive dynamic
- Extends the sense of closure
