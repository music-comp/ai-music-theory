# Melodic-Motivic Material of Coda

The sources and treatment of melodic content in codas.

## Sources

The melodic-motivic content may draw on any prior material:
- Main theme material (most common for recollection function)
- Subordinate theme material
- Development section material
- Closing section material

## New Material

New material may be encountered, but with important constraints:
- Tends to be highly conventionalized "passage work"
- Often in connection with cadential or postcadential areas
- Avoids new, salient material requiring development
- Avoids initiating new processes that cannot be completed

## Aesthetic Ideal

Classical aesthetic: once music finally stops, listener should have no desire to hear anything more in the movement.

## Exception

Rare insertion of new, highly characteristic material (like funereal music in Beethoven's Ninth Symphony) suggests extramusical or programmatic intentions beyond internal relationships.
