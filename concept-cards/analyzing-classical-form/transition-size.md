## The Size of a Transition

Knowing that the transition constitutes one of three principal formal functions of the exposition, students may believe it should occupy about one-third of the exposition. This is rarely the case.

### Typical Size

The transition is frequently quite **short**:
- Often less than eight measures
- Sometimes only four measures
- Rarely approaches one-third of the exposition

### Common Misconception

Many passages that traditional approaches identify as belonging to the transition are better understood, from the form-functional perspective, as belonging to the **opening part of the subordinate-theme group**.

### Why So Short?

The transition's functions can often be accomplished efficiently:
- Destabilize home key
- Effect modulation (or prepare for it)
- Lead to dominant

These don't require extensive length.
