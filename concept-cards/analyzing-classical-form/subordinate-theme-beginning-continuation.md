## Beginning with Continuation Function

The most effective way of expressing **continuation function** at the start of a subordinate theme is by means of **sequential harmonic progressions**, usually in connection with model-sequence technique.

### Why Sequential Progressions Work

Other continuational characteristics (fragmentation, harmonic acceleration, increased surface rhythm) create their most palpable effect in relation to an immediately preceding initiating function.

By contrast, sequences are **self-evidently continuational** without needing prior initiation.

### Bold Opening

When the **first** subordinate theme of a group begins with continuation:
- The effect is especially bold
- The theme group as a whole lacks a sense of structural initiation
- A more tightly knit second subordinate theme may compensate
- The second theme can emphasize its own presentation (by being repeated)

### Example Pattern

- 3-measure model
- Sequenced twice (e.g., ascending minor thirds)
- New 2-measure model follows
- Sequenced by ascending steps
- All this instability indicates continuation function
