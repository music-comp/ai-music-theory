# Retransition to Subdominant (Coda)

When the recapitulation's closing section leads to subdominant for the coda.

## The Situation

If the exposition closes with a retransition leading back to home key, the recapitulation may bring a similar passage, this time leading to the subdominant region for the beginning of the coda.

## Functional Change

This "retransition" no longer fulfills its nominal function:
- Does NOT return to home key
- Instead modulates to subdominant (or occasionally supertonic)
- Functions more as "transition" to the coda

## Determining Coda Start

The coda typically starts somewhere in this passage - at the point where music departs from exposition's path.

## Alternative Destination

Occasionally the passage moves toward supertonic rather than subdominant, redirecting in the measure following the initial subdominant approach.
