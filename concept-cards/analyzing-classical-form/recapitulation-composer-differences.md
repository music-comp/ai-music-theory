# Recapitulation Composer Differences

How the three major classical composers differ in their approach to recapitulations.

## Mozart and Beethoven

Regularly follow practice of maintaining same structure in recapitulation's subordinate theme as in exposition.
- Closely adhere to sonata principle
- Only occasionally exhibit major structural changes
- Value formal symmetry

## Haydn

Typically alters subordinate-theme area in recapitulation:
- Partly due to tendency toward "monothematic" expositions
- But even with contrasting themes, usually restructures
- Responds to particular aesthetic goals
- Clearly differs from younger contemporaries

## Underlying Aesthetics

Mozart and Beethoven: More wedded to formal symmetry and sonata principle

Haydn: Different aesthetic goals, greater flexibility with recapitulation structure, willing to sacrifice symmetry for other compositional purposes

## Analytical Implication

When analyzing Haydn, expect significant alterations. When analyzing Mozart and Beethoven, expect closer adherence to exposition structure.
