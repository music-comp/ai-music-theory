## Nonmodulating Transition

A **nonmodulating transition** remains in the home key, ending with dominant harmony of the home key rather than the subordinate key.

### Characteristics

- A significant minority of transitions use this technique
- Ends with an HC (or dominant arrival) **in the home key**
- Home key is destabilized by transition ending on dominant (in contrast to main theme's tonic ending)

### Implications

With a nonmodulating transition:
- The subordinate theme must begin with a **direct modulation** to the subordinate key
- Or the subordinate key emerges gradually at the start of the subordinate theme

### Relationship to Main Theme

For a nonmodulating transition to create tonal destabilization:
- The main theme typically ends with a **PAC** (establishing strong tonic)
- The transition then ends with **HC** (creating dominant emphasis)
