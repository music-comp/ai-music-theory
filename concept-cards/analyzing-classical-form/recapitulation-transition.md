## Recapitulation Transition

The most important structural change required of the **transition in the recapitulation** is that it no longer modulate to a new key.

### Required Adjustment

The transition must be **adjusted** to remain in the home key. If the exposition's transition was nonmodulating, then it need not necessarily be altered, but for variety's sake it often is.

### Subdominant Emphasis

Typically, the transition emphasizes the **subdominant region** so that the return to the tonic for the beginning of the subordinate theme sounds fresh.

### Pre-Dominant Function

The tonicized subdominant may then easily function as the pre-dominant leading to the home-key dominant that marks the end of the transition.

### Analytical Approach

The principal analytical method involves **comparison**:
- Work through the section bar by bar
- Compare how the various parts relate to the exposition
- Note all structural and ornamental changes
- Ask the "why" question to suggest compositional rationales for changes
