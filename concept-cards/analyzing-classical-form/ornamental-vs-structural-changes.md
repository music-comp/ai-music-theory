# Ornamental vs. Structural Changes

When comparing exposition and recapitulation, it is essential to distinguish between **ornamental** and **structural** changes.

## Ornamental Changes

Changes in:
- **Melody** (embellishments, ornaments)
- **Texture** (added voices, reduced texture)
- **Dynamics** (louder, softer)
- **Instrumentation** (different instruments)
- **Accompanimental patterning**

These do not affect fundamental organization.

## Structural Changes

Changes in:
- **Tonality** (tonal adjustment)
- **Harmony** (different progression)
- **Basic melodic patterning**
- **Grouping structure** (different phrase lengths)
- **Formal functions** (different phrase types)

These alter the fundamental organization.

## Analysis Procedure

A simple but effective procedure:
1. **Compare** exposition and recapitulation bar by bar
2. **Observe** every change from one to the other
3. **Classify** each change as ornamental or structural
4. **Ask why**: What musical purpose does the change serve?

## Identical Sections

If there are no changes, ask:
- Why has the composer maintained identity?
- What purposes are served by exact repetition?

## Key Insight

You may not always find clear answers, but the effort to understand changes almost always yields interesting observations.
