# Transition-like Core Substitute

A thematic unit in place of a core whose organization resembles a transition.

## Characteristics

- Follows upon a distinct pre-core
- Begins in one key with standard initiating function (presentation, compound basic idea)
- Modulates to another key (development or home)
- Closes with HC or dominant arrival

## Model-Sequence Technique

- May appear somewhere in the unit (usually in continuation function)
- Not in a way that gives rise to a genuine core
- Models are too small or sequences too brief

## Formal Function

Functions as the central section of the development when no true core is present. Provides modulation and dominant preparation similar to exposition transition.

## Typical in Haydn

Haydn frequently writes transition-like units in place of cores, reflecting his general approach to development construction.
