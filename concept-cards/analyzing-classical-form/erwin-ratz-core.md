# Quick Definition
The term "core" (Kern) was first introduced by Erwin Ratz; Caplin's treatment is inspired by Ratz but includes refinements, notably extending the core to include the standing on the dominant.

# Core Definition
"The term 'core' (Kern) was first introduced by Erwin Ratz" (p. 451), the Viennese music theorist and Schoenberg student. Caplin's discussion is "highly inspired by Ratz, though many refinements, as well as some differences, are introduced." Notably, "Ratz does not consider the standing on the dominant to belong to the core proper." Schoenberg's treatment (calling it "elaboration") focused more on tonal flux and motivic play. Earlier theories focused almost exclusively on motivic transformation. Hepokoski and Darcy's recent treatment focuses on "rotational" form.

# Key Properties

3. Schoenberg called it "elaboration," focused on tonal flux and motivic play
4. Earlier theories focused only on motivic transformation, not phrase structure
5. Hepokoski and Darcy emphasize "rotational" form as an alternative framework
