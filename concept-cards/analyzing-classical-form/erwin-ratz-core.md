# Erwin Ratz and the Core Concept

The theoretical origins of the "core" concept in form analysis.

## Origin

The term "core" (German: Kern) was first introduced by Erwin Ratz, the influential Viennese music theorist and student of Schoenberg.

## Ratz's Conception

Ratz identified the core as the central section of the development featuring:
- Model-sequence technique
- Fragmentation
- Characteristic developmental character

## Difference from Caplin

Ratz did not consider the standing on the dominant to belong to the core proper. Caplin's treatment includes it as part of the core's concluding function.

## Broader Context

Schoenberg's treatment of development (which he called "elaboration") attended more to issues of tonal flux and motivic play than to conventional phrase-structural techniques.

Earlier theories focused almost exclusively on "motivic transformation" and said practically nothing about the section's phrase-structural organization.

Hepokoski and Darcy's recent treatment focuses largely on "rotational" form.
