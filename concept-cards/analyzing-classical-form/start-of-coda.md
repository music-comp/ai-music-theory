# Start of the Coda

Determining where the coda begins, which is often not clearly perceived.

## The Rule

For purposes of analytical consistency: **the coda starts at that point where the music of the recapitulation stops corresponding to the exposition**.

## Why It's Difficult

- Onset often not readily perceived
- Coda often under way before expressing sense of formal initiation
- May start with medial or concluding function
- True structural beginning may not appear until later

## Common Beginnings

- New model-sequence passage
- Sequential repetition of recapitulation's last idea
- Material extending the closing section
- "Retransition" leading to subdominant (if exposition had retransition)

## Clear Cases (Rare)

Sometimes unambiguous:
- Closing section clearly over
- Rhythmic continuity broken
- Texture changes
- New initiating unit begins

## Notational Indicators

Double-bar lines with repeat signs may indicate coda start (especially Mozart), but be careful - Haydn often includes coda within repeat scheme.
