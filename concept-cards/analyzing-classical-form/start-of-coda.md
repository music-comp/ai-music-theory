# Quick Definition
The coda starts where the recapitulation stops corresponding to the exposition, even though this moment often does not sound like a "beginning" and may feature medial or concluding function rather than initiation.

# Core Definition
"The coda starts at that point where the music of the recapitulation stops corresponding to the exposition" (p. 542). "Frequently, this moment does not sound like a 'beginning' and may indeed occur as part of an ongoing continuation function." The coda may start with model-sequence passages, sequential repetition of the recapitulation's last idea, or "retransition" material leading to the subdominant. A true structural beginning may not appear until later. "On a few occasions, the start of the coda is unambiguous" — closing section clearly over, texture changes, new initiating unit begins.

# Prerequisites
- **Coda section** — The section whose start is being identified
- **Closing section** — Often continues into the coda without clear break
- **Exposition** — The reference point for determining correspondence

# Key Properties
1. Rule: coda starts where recapitulation stops corresponding to exposition
2. Often does not sound like a beginning
3. May start with continuation or concluding function
4. True initiating function may appear later
5. Clear starts are rare (closing section over, texture change, new unit)
6. Notation (double-bar/repeat signs) may indicate start (especially Mozart)
7. Haydn may include coda within repeat scheme

# Construction / Recognition
## To Identify:
1. Compare recapitulation's closing section with exposition's closing section
2. The point of divergence is the coda's technical start
3. Look for eventual initiating function later in the coda for perceived beginning

# Examples
**Example 15.3** (p. 550): Mozart, Piano Sonata in B-flat, K. 333 — recapitulation ends with retransition at m. 231; coda starts m. 232 by continuing retransition material; functional beginning not sensed until m. 235 with main-theme return.

**Example 15.5** (p. 551): Haydn, Symphony No. 97, i — coda starts m. 246 by sequencing up a third the cadential idea; no true structural beginning until very late.

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Looking for a clear initiating gesture to mark the coda's start
  **Correction**: The coda's technical start is determined by comparison with the exposition, not by perceived initiation; the coda is often already under way before any initiating function appears
