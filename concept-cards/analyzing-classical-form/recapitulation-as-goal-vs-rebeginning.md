# Recapitulation as Goal vs. Rebeginning

The dual perception of the recapitulation's formal function.

## As "End" of Sonata Form

From overall form perspective:
- Recapitulation functions as the "end" of sonata form
- Quality of ending emerges fully toward section's conclusion
- Especially in connection with final PAC of subordinate-theme area

## As "Rebeginning"

At its very beginning:
- Normally gives impression of "rebeginning" the form
- Usually opens just as at start of exposition (especially Haydn and Mozart)
- Reinforces character of "starting over again"

## Beethoven's Approach

- Start of recapitulation often given different dynamic
- Usually louder, more forceful, dramatically charged
- Effect of apotheosis at beginning
- May give impression that recapitulation is structural goal from its start

## Analytical Caution

The dramatic quality of Beethoven's recapitulation openings can create a misguided interpretation that the recapitulation begins with an authentic cadence (it does not - the V-I is not cadential).
