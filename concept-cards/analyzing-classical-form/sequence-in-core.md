# Sequence in Core

The sequential repetition of the model within a development core.

## Characteristics

- Model is sequenced one or more times after initial statement
- A single sequential pattern may be used throughout (descending fifth, ascending second)
- The pattern often changes in the course of the core

## Alterations to Model

- Structure of model normally retained in sequenced version
- Sometimes sequence alters the model, especially with multi-phrase models
- First phrase usually conforms to original model
- Subsequent phrases may be changed or eliminated
- Large models may have incomplete sequences even on first repetition

## Common Sequential Patterns

- Descending fifths (very common)
- Ascending stepwise
- Descending thirds

## Relationship to Harmonic Progression

Sequential repetitions are supported by underlying harmonic progressions that may continue even when the pattern of sequential repetition changes.
