# Fusion of Main Theme and Transition

When main theme and transition combine into a single thematic structure in the recapitulation.

## Normal Situation

Main theme and transition usually remain discrete units in recapitulation, as in exposition.

## Fusion Process

Occasionally they fuse into single thematic structure by:
- Eliminating end of main theme
- Eliminating beginning of transition
- Attaching close of transition to what remains of main theme

## Extreme Case

All transition material may be eliminated:
- Main theme material ends with HC (instead of original authentic cadence)
- This HC serves as transition's dominant goal

## Accompanying Alterations

Often accompanied by techniques used in both main themes and transitions:
- Deleting unnecessary repetitions
- Adding new model-sequence technique
- Emphasizing flat-side tonal regions

## Functional Result

Two formal functions (main theme and transition) are fused into single grouping unit.
