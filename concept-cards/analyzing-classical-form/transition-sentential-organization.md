## Transition Sentential Organization

Most transitions employ the phrase functions associated with the sentence theme type, though in a **loose** manner.

### Typical Functions

- **Presentation** (or compound basic idea)
- **Continuation**
- **Cadential** (half-cadential)

### Loosened Expression

These functions typically appear loosely through:

**Phrase-structural means**:
- Extension
- Expansion
- Compression
- Redundant repetitions

**Harmonic-tonal destabilizations**:
- Modal shift
- Chromaticism
- Tonicization

### "Sentence" vs. "Sentential"

Should we label all such structures as "sentences"?

- In some cases, loosening techniques don't exceed normal deviations
- But often the length and complexity vastly differ from an 8-measure sentence
- In those cases, speak of **sentential** or **sentence-like** organization
- Reserve the term **sentence** for more tightly knit manifestations
