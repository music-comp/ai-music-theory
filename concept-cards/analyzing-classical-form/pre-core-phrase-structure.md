# Pre-core Phrase Structure

Pre-cores can be structured in various ways, employing many kinds of formal functions.

## Complete vs. Incomplete

### Complete Thematic Unit
Contains full complement of:
- Initiating function
- Medial function
- Concluding function

### Incomplete Thematic Unit
- Made up of one or more phrase functions
- Usually lacks concluding function
- Sometimes lacks initiation
- Tends to be relatively short

## Types of Complete Pre-cores

### Tight-knit Pre-core
- Resembles a new main theme
- May take form of sentence, period, or hybrid
- Especially common in Mozart
- Often based on material from actual main theme
- Typically set in subordinate key or development key

### Transition-like Pre-core
- Usually sentential phrase structure
- Modulatory character
- Harmonic goal is dominant of a development key
- Sequential progressions may appear (but model must be small)

## Multiple Thematic Units

A small number of large pre-cores are constructed out of two thematic units, with one or both typically incomplete.
