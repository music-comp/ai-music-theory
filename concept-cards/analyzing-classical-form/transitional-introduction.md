# Transitional Introduction

A passage at the beginning of the pre-core that functions both as introduction and transition.

## Characteristics

- Pre-core begins immediately in a new key or region
- Opening harmony is either dominant or leads to dominant
- Takes on character of extended upbeat to next unit
- "Before-the-beginning" character with transitional harmonic basis

## Terminological Explanation

The expression "transitional introduction" captures the dual function:
- **Introduction**: Appears "before the beginning" with introductory character
- **Transition**: Harmonic basis emphasizes dominant of new region, effecting tonal change

## Resolution

The dominant prolongation within the transitional introduction typically resolves to:
- A new initiating function (beginning of pre-core proper or core)
- Sometimes a cadential function

## Common Contexts

- Beginning of development directly with dominant harmony of development key
- Passage picking up motive from end of exposition's closing section
- Enharmonic or chromatic pivot leading to new key area
