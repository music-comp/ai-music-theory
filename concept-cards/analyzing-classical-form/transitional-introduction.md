# Quick Definition
A passage at the beginning of the pre-core that functions as both introduction (before-the-beginning character) and transition (emphasizing dominant harmony of a new region to effect a tonal change).

# Core Definition
"Sometimes the pre-core begins immediately in a new key (or region)" (p. 481). The opening harmony is either a dominant or a progression leading to dominant, and the music takes on "the character of an extended upbeat to the start of the next unit." The expression "transitional introduction" captures both aspects: introductory (before-the-beginning character) and transitional (dominant-based harmonic emphasis effecting tonal change from the subordinate key to a development key).

# Prerequisites
- **Pre-core** — The formal unit the transitional introduction begins
- **Development section** — The broader context

# Key Properties
1. Pre-core begins immediately in a new key or region
2. Opening harmony is dominant or leads to dominant
3. Character of extended upbeat to the next unit
4. Before-the-beginning quality (introductory)
5. Dominant emphasis effects tonal change (transitional)
6. Resolution leads to new initiating function or cadential function

# Construction / Recognition
## To Identify:
1. Look at the very opening of the development
2. Check whether the harmony is immediately in a new key (not the subordinate key)
3. Note whether the passage has an upbeat/introductory character emphasizing dominant

# Examples
**Example 13.5** (p. 481): Mozart, K. 614 — pre-core begins at m. 87 directly with dominant of C minor (development key); strong before-the-beginning character; core begins when dominant resolves deceptively at m. 90.

**Example 13.16** (p. 481): Haydn, H. 41 — initial material picks up motive from exposition closing section, supported by bVI of subordinate key; shifts to dominant of E-flat (development key) on repetition; pre-core proper begins when dominant resolves.

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: Treating the transitional introduction as either purely an introduction or purely a transition
  **Clarification**: It functions as both simultaneously — the introductory character and the transitional harmonic basis are equally important aspects
