# Closing Section in Recapitulation

How the closing section is handled in the recapitulation.

## Typical Treatment

The closing section following the final PAC is normally retained in the recapitulation as it appeared in the exposition.

## Without Coda

If no coda follows, the closing section may be extended to provide a greater sense of "after the end" for the movement as a whole.

## Alterations for Coda

If recapitulation is followed by coda:
- Final codettas may be altered or eliminated
- Material may be "saved" for coda's closing section
- May lead to different key area (subdominant) for coda start

## Retransition Function

If exposition closes with retransition to home key, recapitulation may bring similar passage leading to subdominant for coda (no longer fulfilling true retransition function).
