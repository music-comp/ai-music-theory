# Content vs. Function in Recapitulation

The crucial distinction between a unit's musical content and its formal function.

## The Principle

We must distinguish between:
- **Musical content**: The melodic-motivic material, textures, rhythms
- **Formal function**: What role the passage plays in the form

## Example: "Reversed" Recapitulation

In a so-called reversed recapitulation:
- Melodic-motivic material may be reordered (subordinate before main)
- But the passage containing main-theme material does not **function** as a main theme
- Rather, it is refashioned to function as a subordinate theme

## Evidence of Functional Change

The passage exhibits loosening devices associated with subordinate-theme function:
- Extended continuations
- Expanded cadential progressions
- Evaded or deceptive cadences

## Analytical Caution

Terminology sometimes confuses this distinction. Be careful not to assume that the presence of certain material determines function - function must be assessed independently.
