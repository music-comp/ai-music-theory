# Quick Definition
The final phrase of a small binary's Part 2 may be built as a consequent (2-m. idea + contrasting idea -> PAC) using a NEW idea rather than the original basic idea. This prevents recapitulation and maintains the small binary character.

# Core Definition
If the final phrase used the original basic idea, a recapitulation would result and the form would be a small ternary. By using a new 2-m. idea, the phrase retains consequent-like internal characteristics (idea + c.i. -> PAC) without creating recapitulation. Although a corresponding "antecedent" is not present, the phrase can still be considered a consequent since its internal characteristics match that function. The new idea may derive from material in the contrasting middle, creating a lower-level periodic relation within Part 2.

# Key Properties
1. Final phrase: new 2-m. idea + contrasting idea -> PAC
2. Using original b.i. would create recapitulation (= small ternary)
3. New idea prevents this, maintaining small binary
4. No fragmentation or ECP (unlike continuation or cadential patterns)
5. New idea may relate to B-section material

# Examples
**Example 1** (p. 266): Beethoven, Op. 30/1, iii -- standing on dominant followed by new consequent with 2-m. idea derived from B-section material.
