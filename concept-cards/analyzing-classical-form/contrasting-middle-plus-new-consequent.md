# Contrasting Middle + New Consequent (Small Binary)

Part 2 of a small binary may close with a phrase resembling a consequent, but built from a **new** 2-measure idea rather than the original basic idea.

## Structure

- 4-measure **contrasting middle** → HC
- 4-measure **"new" consequent** (new 2-m. idea + c.i. → PAC)

## Why "New" Consequent?

If the final phrase used the **original** basic idea:
- A recapitulation would result
- The form would be rounded binary/small ternary

Using a **new** 2-measure idea:
- Avoids recapitulation
- Maintains small binary character
- Yet the phrase functions like a consequent

## Internal Structure

The "new" consequent:
- 2-measure **new idea** (NOT original basic idea)
- 2-measure **contrasting idea** → PAC

## No Continuational Features

Unlike continuation phrases, the new consequent:
- Does not feature fragmentation
- Is not supported by ECP
- Resembles a standard consequent in structure

## Relation to B Section

The new idea may derive from material in the contrasting middle, creating a "lower-level" periodic relation within Part 2.
