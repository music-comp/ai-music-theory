# Closing Section (Small Ternary A Section)

Since the A section of a small ternary closes with a PAC, a **closing section** or codettas may follow before the B section begins.

## Context

After the A section's PAC:
- Optional closing material may appear
- One or more codettas confirm closure
- This material is postcadential (outside the theme proper)

## Structure

A typical closing section:
- Multiple codettas
- Progressive fragmentation (2m → 1m → 1m)
- Recessive dynamic (dying away)

## Single Codetta

A simpler alternative:
- Just one postcadential unit
- Brief confirmation of closure
- Leads directly into B section

## Harmonic Content

Closing sections typically:
- Prolong tonic harmony
- May include simple I-V-I motion
- Confirm the key established by the PAC

## Relation to Theme

Closing material is:
- External to the A section proper
- Not counted in the 8-measure structure
- A framing function
