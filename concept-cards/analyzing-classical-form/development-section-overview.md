## Development Section Overview

The **development section** brings about a high degree of tonal and phrase-structural instability along with an intensity of emotional expression, motivating a restoration of stability accomplished by the recapitulation.

### Functions

1. **Manipulate and recombine** melodic-motivic material from the exposition
2. **Intensify tonal conflict** by exploring remote minor-mode regions
3. **Prepare for recapitulation** through restoration of home key

### Tonal Characteristics

- Explores more remote minor-mode tonal regions (especially vi and iii)
- Uses sequential harmonic progressions
- In long developments, confirms one or more development keys (usually by HC, sometimes PAC)
- Toward the end, the home key returns, confirmed by an HC (or dominant arrival)
- Final standing on the dominant marks the literal end

### Less Conventional Organization

Development sections are considerably less conventional in their phrase-structural organization than expositions. Unlike an exposition, we cannot predict in advance which component parts a given development may contain.
