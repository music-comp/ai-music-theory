# Rotational Form

The concept of recurrent recycling of musical materials in a given order.

## Definition

A formal principle where thematic materials are presented in a consistent order across different sections of the form.

## In Development Sections

Particularly associated with Haydn, who sometimes brings back expositional material in the development in an order similar to that of the exposition:
- Main theme material first
- Followed by transition material
- Followed by subordinate theme material

## Theoretical Source

This concept is stressed by Hepokoski and Darcy in their theory of sonata form as a central organizational principle.

## Broader Application

Beyond development sections, rotational form applies to the entire sonata:
- Exposition presents materials
- Development "rotates" through them
- Recapitulation provides another rotation

## Relationship to Development Analysis

Understanding rotational tendencies can help identify the organization of developments that lack clear pre-core/core structure.
