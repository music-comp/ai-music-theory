## Observation

The small binary form achieves unusual prominence in themes for variation - in most other formal contexts, it is used much less often than the small ternary.

## Rationale

The small binary is favored because it avoids the **internal return of the basic idea** that characterizes the small ternary:

| Form | Basic Idea Returns? | Effect in Variations |
|------|---------------------|---------------------|
| Small Ternary | Yes, in A' section | Basic idea heard 3x per variation cycle |
| Small Binary | No | Basic idea heard 1x per variation cycle |

## Practical Advantage

Since theme and variations form brings **multiple restatements** of the initial basic idea (once per variation), using small binary:
- Avoids overexposing the basic idea
- The return of the basic idea functions exclusively to mark the beginning of each variation
- Maintains freshness throughout the movement

## Statistics

In variation themes:
- Small ternary appears only **somewhat more frequently** than small binary
- This is unusual compared to most other formal contexts where small ternary dominates

## Example Context

When analyzing a variations theme, the choice between small ternary and small binary directly affects how often the listener encounters the primary melodic material, making this structural decision a key compositional consideration.
