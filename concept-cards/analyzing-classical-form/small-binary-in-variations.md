# Quick Definition
The small binary achieves unusual prominence in themes for variation because its absence of an internal return of the basic idea prevents overexposure of that idea across multiple variations.

# Core Definition
The small binary achieves unusual prominence in variation themes -- in most other formal contexts, it is used much less often than the small ternary. Since theme and variations form brings multiple restatements of the initial basic idea (once per variation), the composer can avoid overexposing it by constructing the theme as a small binary, in which the return of the basic idea then functions exclusively to mark the beginning of each variation (Caplin, p. 592).

# Key Properties
1. Small binary appears with unusual frequency in variation themes
2. Avoids the internal return of the basic idea found in small ternary
3. In small ternary: basic idea heard 3 times per cycle (A, A' beginning, and each variation start)
4. In small binary: basic idea heard only at the start of each variation
5. Maintains freshness throughout the movement

# Construction / Recognition
## To Identify:
1. Examine the variation theme's form type
2. If small binary, note the absence of an A' section returning the basic idea
3. Observe how this affects the frequency of the basic idea across variations

# Context & Application
This observation reveals a practical compositional consideration: the choice of theme form has implications for the entire variations movement, not just for the theme itself. It explains an otherwise puzzling statistical anomaly in classical form.

# Examples
**Example 1** (p. 592): The small ternary appears only somewhat more frequently than the small binary in variation themes -- unusual compared to most other contexts.
