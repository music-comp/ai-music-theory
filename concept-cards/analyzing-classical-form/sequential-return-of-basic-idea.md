# Sequential Return of Basic Idea

A **sequential return** of the basic idea occurs when the basic idea returns at the start of the consequent transposed to another scale degree.

## The Pattern

At measure 5, the basic idea returns:
- Transposed (typically up a step to the supertonic region)
- Creating a less stable expression than exact return
- Still functioning as consequent

## Effect on Stability

Sequential return:
- Delays completion of tonic prolongation
- Creates forward momentum
- May suggest continuation-like destabilization

## Harmonic Implications

The transposed return may be supported by:
- Supertonic harmony (ii)
- Other non-tonic harmonies
- Eventual return to tonic for cadence

## Analysis Challenge

When the basic idea returns sequentially:
- The sense of "consequent" may be weakened
- Continuation characteristics may emerge
- Ambiguity between consequent and continuation is possible

## Comparison

| Return Type | Stability | Harmonic Support |
|-------------|-----------|------------------|
| Exact | Most stable | Tonic (I) |
| Dominant version | Moderate | Dominant (V) |
| Sequential | Least stable | ii or other |
