## Expansion of Initial Tonic in ECP

The **initial tonic** of an expanded cadential progression is usually placed in first inversion. The classical composers often use the prominent arrival on I6 as a **cue for the onset of an ECP**.

### Typical Prolongation Method

When expanded, the initial tonic is frequently embellished by a **neighboring dominant seventh in third inversion (V4/3)**:
- V4/3 works especially well
- Voice-leading rule demands its resolution to I6
- Creates elegant neighbor-note motion

### Subordinate Theme Beginning with Expanded I6

When a subordinate theme is supported by a single ECP:
- The entire theme may begin with expanded I6
- Functional label: **cadential** can be assigned to whole theme
- But the expanded I6 projects a degree of harmonic stability
- Opening measures project sense of structural beginning despite overall cadential function

### Effect

The prominent arrival on I6 (whether at start of theme or after continuation):
- Signals beginning of cadential function
- Prepares listener for move through pre-dominant to dominant
