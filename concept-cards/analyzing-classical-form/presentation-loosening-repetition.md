# Quick Definition
Presentation function can be loosened by an additional (third) statement of the basic idea, extending the presentation to six measures instead of the standard four. This renders the grouping structure asymmetrical and creates functional redundancy -- both criteria of loose organization.

# Core Definition
A presentation phrase can be extended by repeating the opening basic idea once again, yielding three statements instead of the standard two. The third statement renders the grouping structure asymmetrical (six measures instead of four) and creates functional redundancy -- more repetition than necessary to express the function. Both asymmetry and redundancy are criteria of loose organization. The third statement often occurs when the second statement moves to a harmony that cannot easily return to tonic, requiring an additional statement to complete the tonic prolongation. For example, a sequence to ii may require a third statement to return the harmony to tonic and properly close the presentation's tonic prolongation (Caplin, Ch. 12, pp. 380-381).

# Key Properties
1. Basic idea stated 3 times instead of standard 2
2. Creates 6-measure presentation instead of standard 4
3. Asymmetrical grouping structure (criterion of loose organization)
4. Functional redundancy (criterion of loose organization)
5. Often motivated by harmonic context (second statement goes to non-tonic harmony)
6. Third statement needed to complete tonic prolongation

# Construction / Recognition
## To Identify:
1. Count statements of the basic idea in the presentation
2. Three statements = extended/loosened presentation
3. Check whether harmonic context motivates the third statement
4. Note the resulting 6-measure (asymmetrical) phrase length

# Context & Application
Additional repetition is one of three methods of loosening the presentation function in subordinate themes (alongside full phrase repetition and weakened tonic prolongation). It is the simplest form of presentation loosening, adding just one extra statement, but it effectively creates the asymmetry and redundancy that signal loose organization.
