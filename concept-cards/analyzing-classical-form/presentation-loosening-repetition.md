## Presentation Loosening - Additional Repetition

Presentation function can be loosened by an **additional repeat** of the basic idea.

### Three-Statement Presentation

A presentation phrase can be extended by repeating the opening basic idea once again:
- Standard presentation: 2 statements of basic idea
- Extended presentation: 3 statements of basic idea

### Why This Loosens

The third statement renders:
- **Grouping structure asymmetrical** (6 mm. instead of 4 mm.)
- **Functional redundancy** - more than necessary to express the function

Both are criteria of loose organization.

### Harmonic Context

The third statement often occurs when:
- The second statement moves to a harmony that cannot easily return to I
- An additional statement is needed to complete the tonic prolongation
- Example: sequence to ii requires third statement to return to tonic
