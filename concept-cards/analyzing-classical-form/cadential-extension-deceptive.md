## Cadential Extension - Deceptive Cadence

Now and then, a subordinate theme brings a **deceptive cadence** in place of an expected authentic cadence.

### How It Works

1. Cadential progression approaches expected PAC
2. Instead: deceptive cadence (V - vi or similar)
3. Ongoing phrase is usually taken up again
4. Eventually leads to final PAC

### Effect

Like the IAC:
- The deceptive cadence provides a **temporary goal** to the theme
- But one that is insufficiently conclusive
- We know more must happen

The deceptive cadence provides **less** sense of closure than an IAC - it feels more interrupted.

### Subsequent Material

Following a deceptive cadence:
- The phrase is usually repeated
- May lead to ECP with expanded pre-dominant
- Ultimately reaches the required PAC
