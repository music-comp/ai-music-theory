# Failed Consequent

A **failed consequent** is a phrase that resembles a consequent (returns basic idea, leads to cadence) but "fails" because it closes with a weak cadence instead of the expected PAC.

## The Situation

In certain Part 1 structures:
- An antecedent or compound basic idea opens
- The second phrase returns the basic idea (suggesting consequent)
- But the phrase modulates or closes with HC

## Why "Failed"?

The phrase fails to achieve consequent function because:
- Consequents require **stronger** cadence than antecedents
- An HC is weaker than any preceding weak cadence
- The expected PAC does not materialize

## Not Simply "Second Antecedent"

Although the internal organization (b.i. + c.i. → HC) resembles an antecedent:
- In context, we expect a consequent
- The sense of "failure" reflects thwarted expectations
- Labeling as "failed consequent" captures this

## Result

The structure becomes nonconventional:
- Compound basic idea + failed consequent
- Antecedent + failed consequent

## Small Binary Connection

Failed consequents are especially associated with Part 1 of small binary, contributing to the form's distinctive character.
