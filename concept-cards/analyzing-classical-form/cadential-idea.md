# Cadential Idea

The **cadential idea** is the final 2-measure unit of a sentence, supported by a cadential progression and bringing closure to the entire theme.

## Characteristics

- Supported by a **cadential progression**
- Melodic content is often **conventional** and formulaic
- Rarely resembles the basic idea
- General melodic contour is **descending** (reflecting the Latin origin of "cadence" meaning "to fall")

## Melodic Content

The cadential idea typically features **liquidation** - the systematic elimination of characteristic melodic and rhythmic motives introduced in the basic idea. The result:
- Fairly generic, interchangeable melodic material
- Conventional closing gestures
- Less distinctive character than the basic idea

## Formal Function

The cadential idea can be identified by its unique formal function as expressing cadential function. It is labeled for this function rather than for its grouping structure.

## Relation to Continuation

The cadential idea often grows naturally out of the preceding continuational materials:
- Fragmentation may continue into the cadential idea
- Harmonic acceleration may persist
- The two functions (continuation and cadential) are **fused**

## Distinct vs. Emergent

In some cases, the cadential idea stands apart from preceding continuational materials. In other cases, the cadence emerges directly from the melodic-motivic content of the continuation.
