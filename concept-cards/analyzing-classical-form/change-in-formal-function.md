# Change in Formal Function (Recapitulation)

When a recapitulation is compressed or expanded, the internal formal functions often change relative to the exposition.

## Compression Changes

When A' is compressed:
- Functions may be eliminated (e.g., antecedent function)
- The recapitulation becomes a single consequent phrase
- Redundant functions are omitted

## Expansion Changes

When A' is expanded:
- New continuation material may appear
- Development of motives may extend phrases
- Additional functions may be inserted

## Same Size, Different Functions

Even when A' has the same length as A:
- Different formal functions may be used
- Example: Period (A) becomes sentential (A')
- The basic idea may be followed by different phrase types

## Example Pattern

Exposition (period):
- Antecedent (b.i. + c.i. → HC)
- Consequent (b.i. + c.i. → PAC)

Recapitulation (sentential):
- Basic idea (2 m.)
- Continuation with fragmentation (6 m.) → PAC

Same length, different functions.

## Analytical Importance

Identifying changes in formal function reveals:
- Compositional choices
- How the piece evolves
- The relationship between exposition and recapitulation
