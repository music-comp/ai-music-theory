# Contrasting Idea Characteristics

The **contrasting idea** exhibits several characteristics that distinguish it from the basic idea and often resemble continuation function.

## Primary Characteristics

1. **Different melodic content** from basic idea
2. **Supported by cadential progression** (not prolongational)
3. **Leads to cadence** (weak, for antecedent)

## Continuational Traits

The contrasting idea often displays characteristics of continuation function:
- **Fragmentation**: Shorter grouping units
- **Acceleration**: Faster harmonic or surface rhythm
- **Sequential hints**: Possible sequential patterns

## Why These Traits?

These traits arise because:
- The phrase must move from opening stability to cadential closure
- This requires some degree of formal destabilization
- Continuational processes achieve this movement

## Harmonic Distinction

Critical distinction from basic idea:
- **Basic idea**: Tonic prolongation (no cadence possible)
- **Contrasting idea**: Cadential progression (cadence results)

## Similarity Permitted

The contrasting idea may actually resemble the basic idea:
- What matters is: it is NOT a **repetition**
- Even minimal contrast is sufficient
- The return at m. 5 clarifies mm. 3-4 as "contrasting"
