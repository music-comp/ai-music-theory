## Tonic Pedal and Fanfare Themes

Some main themes begin with a long **tonic pedal**, over which may be found gestures that evoke a heraldic, **fanfare style**.

### Character and Context

Such powerful passages are ideal for projecting a strong opening, especially in:
- Public genres of symphony
- Overtures

### Formal Ambiguity

These openings often obscure a clear sense of:
- Basic idea and its repetition
- Juxtaposition of basic idea with contrasting idea
- The lack of conventional initiating function makes classification as a standard type difficult

### Analysis

Although such themes have a general **sentential quality**:
- The cadential function is usually obvious
- There is general acceleration of rhythmic activity
- It is difficult to distinguish clearly between presentation and continuation functions

### Result

The theme is best regarded as **nonconventional** in organization while still projecting strength and stability appropriate for a main theme.
