# Quick Definition
In sonata-rondo, one of the four refrains may be omitted without significantly impairing the rondo effect. Mozart frequently eliminates refrain 3 (beginning of recapitulation) or the basic structure of refrain 4 (within the coda).

# Core Definition
The sonata-rondo normally includes four appearances of the refrain, which may feel redundant. One standard deviation, adopted frequently by Mozart, eliminates refrain 3 at the beginning of the recapitulation. Another option eliminates the basic structure of the refrain within the coda, retaining only initial motives. Where Mozart retains refrain 3, he usually eliminates refrain 4, and vice versa (Caplin, pp. 683-684).

# Prerequisites
- **Sonata-rondo** -- The form in which this deviation occurs

# Key Properties
1. Omitting one refrain does not significantly impair rondo effect
2. Option 1: eliminate refrain 3 (recapitulation start); couplet 2 moves directly to couplet 3
3. Option 2: eliminate refrain 4 within the coda (retain only opening motives)
4. Mozart tends to retain one and eliminate the other
5. When refrain 3 is omitted, transition material from couplet 1 bridges the gap
