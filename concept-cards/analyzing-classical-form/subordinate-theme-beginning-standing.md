## Beginning with Standing on the Dominant

The boundary between transition and subordinate theme can become somewhat blurred when the dominant from the end of the transition is **held over** to provide harmonic support for a standing on the dominant at the beginning of the subordinate theme.

### Structure

The standing on the dominant at the structural beginning of the theme:
- Does **not** lead to a standard initiating function (presentation, CBI)
- If it did, we would hear the standing as part of the transition
- Instead, yields directly to **continuation or cadential** functions

### Articulating the Boundary

In most cases when a subordinate theme begins with standing on the dominant:
- A marked **change of melodic-motivic material** helps indicate where transition ends
- Sometimes the transition has its own standing on dominant that **differs** from the one beginning subordinate theme
- This change of material signals that a new theme is underway despite retained dominant harmony

### Distinction from Weak Tonic Prolongation

Important to distinguish from cases where dominant pedal undermines a tonic prolongation (presentation):
- If material implies **tonic prolongation**: presentation with weakened tonic (dominant pedal undermining)
- If material implies **dominant prolongation**: theme begins with standing on the dominant
