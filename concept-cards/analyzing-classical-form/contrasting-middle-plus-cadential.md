# Contrasting Middle + Cadential (Small Binary)

In Part 2 of a small binary, the contrasting middle may be followed by a phrase supported entirely by an **expanded cadential progression** (ECP).

## Structure

- 4-measure **contrasting middle** (B section) → HC
- 4-measure **cadential phrase** (supported by ECP) → PAC

## Distinguishing from Continuation

When supported by ECP, the final phrase may be:
- **Continuation⇒cadential** (if continuational characteristics present)
- **Exclusively cadential** (if few or no continuational traits)

## Standing on the Dominant Aftermath

If the contrasting middle is a standing on the dominant:
- Subsequent ECP may create sense of harmonic acceleration
- Some continuational characteristics may result
- Context determines whether continuation or cadential label is appropriate

## Exclusively Cadential

The final phrase may be purely cadential when:
- Continuational traits are minimal or absent
- The ECP dominates phrase character
- No fragmentation or acceleration beyond what ECP provides

## Key Principle

Even without recapitulation, Part 2 achieves closure through the emphatic cadential function of an ECP-supported phrase.
