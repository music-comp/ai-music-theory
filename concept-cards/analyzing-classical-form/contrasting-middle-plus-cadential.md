# Quick Definition
In Part 2 of a small binary, the contrasting middle may be followed by a phrase supported solely by an ECP, projecting exclusively cadential function (if few continuational traits) or continuation => cadential (if continuational characteristics are present).

# Core Definition
Following a contrasting middle, the final phrase may be supported entirely by an ECP. If the phrase has some continuational characteristics (e.g., harmonic acceleration following a standing on the dominant), it may be labeled continuation => cadential. If few or no continuational traits are present, the phrase is exclusively cadential. The ECP provides emphatic closure without recapitulation.

# Key Properties
1. B section -> HC; cadential phrase on ECP -> PAC
2. May be purely cadential or continuation => cadential
3. Context determines the label

# Examples
**Example 1** (p. 265): Beethoven, Op. 35, mm. 66-81 -- standing on dominant in B section, followed by ECP with some harmonic acceleration.
**Example 2** (p. 271): Mozart, K. 421, iv, mm. 1-24 -- elaborate B section with transition, retransition, and final ECP emphasizing Neapolitan.
