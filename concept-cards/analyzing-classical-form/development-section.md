# Development Section

The second large-scale section of sonata form, standing between the exposition and the recapitulation.

## Definition

The development functions to generate the greatest degree of tonal and phrase-structural instability within the movement, thus motivating the restoration of stability accomplished by the recapitulation.

## Key Characteristics

- Features looser organization than the exposition
- Emphasizes sequential progressions
- Avoids authentic cadential closure in the home key
- Typically ends on dominant harmony of the home key
- Can sometimes exceed the size of the entire exposition
- Usually contains multiple thematic units

## Relationship to Contrasting Middle

The development is a higher-level analogue to the contrasting middle of small ternary form, but distinguished by greater length and complexity.
