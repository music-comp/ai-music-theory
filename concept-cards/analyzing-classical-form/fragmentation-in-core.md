# Fragmentation in Core

The breaking down of the grouping structure within the core, leading toward formal closure.

## Process

- Breaks down the grouping structure defined by the model and its sequences
- Eventually motivates a formal close to the core
- Progressively smaller units emerge

## Content Options

1. May continue to employ ideas from the model
2. May bring about a change in musical content
3. The fragmentation itself may establish a new model for sequential repetition

## Harmonic Support

- Sequential process may become exhausted at onset of fragmentation
- Rest of core may be supported by prolongational progressions
- Similar to regular continuation phrase harmonies

## Example Pattern

A typical pattern might progress:
- 4-measure model
- 4-measure sequence
- 2-measure fragments
- 1-measure fragments
- Half cadence
