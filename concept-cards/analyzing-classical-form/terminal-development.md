# Terminal Development

A now-discredited view of the coda as a second development section.

## Historical Concept

Earlier theorists viewed the coda as a "terminal" development, particularly in Beethoven, because:
- Analogous formal positions (both follow closing sections)
- Model-sequence technique appears in both
- Developmental character of some coda passages

## Why Discredited

The view fails to differentiate fundamentally different organizations:

### Development
- Modulatory sequential activity
- Exploration of remote keys
- Tonal instability as primary character
- Ends on home-key dominant

### Coda
- Nonmodulatory sequences
- Harmonies remain bound to home key
- Tonal stability as primary character
- Ends with home-key PAC

## Better Understanding

To say the coda *refers* to material from development is not to claim the former *functions* or *structures* itself like the latter.
