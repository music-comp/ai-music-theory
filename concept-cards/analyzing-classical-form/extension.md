## Extension

**Extension** occurs when a formal function is expressed over a longer span than would normally be expected.

### Definition

A formal unit is extended when:
- It occupies more measures than standard
- It takes longer to achieve its function
- Elements are repeated or elaborated beyond the minimum

### Common Contexts

**Continuation extension**:
- Model-sequence technique extends continuation
- Fragments are repeated more than necessary
- Harmonic sequences prolong the motion

**Cadential extension**:
- Evaded cadences require further attempts
- "One more time" technique extends cadential function
- ECPs expand the cadential span

### Criterion of Loosening

Extension is a primary criterion of loose organization:
- Creates functional redundancy
- Produces asymmetrical grouping (when other units remain standard)
- Appropriate for transitions and subordinate themes

### Effect

Extension creates:
- Rhetorical emphasis on the function being extended
- Greater dramatic weight
- Delay of the expected goal
- Looser formal expression
