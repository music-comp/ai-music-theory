# Evaded Cadence

An **evaded cadence** (also called "one more time" technique) occurs when the expected cadential goal is replaced by a new formal beginning - the music starts over rather than achieving closure.

## Definition

At the moment of expected cadential arrival:
- Expected: Tonic in stable configuration (PAC)
- Actual: New formal beginning (often return of opening material)

## Mechanism

Instead of the goal tonic harmony resolving the dominant:
- A fresh statement of earlier material begins
- The phrase "evades" closure by starting over
- The cadential process is denied

## Result

The evaded cadence:
- Creates a sense of "one more time"
- Generates phrase extension
- Delays genuine closure
- Motivates additional attempts at cadence

## Distinction from Deceptive Cadence

- **Deceptive**: Dominant resolves to unexpected harmony (vi)
- **Evaded**: Expected cadential goal replaced by new beginning

## Common Contexts

Evaded cadences occur:
- At the end of continuation phrases
- When returning to opening material
- To create large-scale repetitions
- Before major structural arrivals
