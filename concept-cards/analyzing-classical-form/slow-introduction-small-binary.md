# Small Binary Form in Slow Introduction

When a slow introduction takes on a binary formal organization.

## The Structure

Introduction may emerge as highly deviated small binary:
- Part 1: Opens with fanfare, modulates to related key (PAC)
- Part 2: Returns to home key (PAC or elided into exposition)
- Followed by possible closing section

## Unusual Features

Several unusual features may arise:
- Two parts elided (not normally found in small binary)
- Closing section brings stronger closure than end of Part 2
- Parts may be asymmetrical

## Irregularities Expected

Such irregularities are not surprising given the nonconventional form normally taken by a slow introduction. The binary framework provides organization without strict adherence to conventional patterns.

## Effect

The binary organization creates:
- Sense of formal completeness
- Tonal journey (away and back)
- Preparation for main theme
