## Expanded Repetition of Subordinate Theme

If a complete subordinate theme first appears as relatively tight-knit, the theme will sometimes begin to be **repeated and then undergo significant expansion** to render it considerably looser.

### When This Occurs

- Original theme itself omits initiating function
- Or even a tight-knit theme beginning with normal initiation
- The abruptness of closure motivates repetition

### Expansion Techniques in Repetition

The repeated theme becomes looser through:
- **Internal repetition** extending the model and sequences
- **Extension** of cadential idea through evaded cadences
- **"One more time" technique** before final PAC
- **Modal shift** (moving to minor)
- **Tonicization** of remote regions

### Counting Dilemma

Does the expanded repetition constitute a separate "theme" in the subordinate-theme group?

It seems more practical to show the motivic relationship by giving it the **same numerical label**, even though literal number of themes may not be fully reflected.
