# Incomplete Pre-core

A pre-core constructed from phrase functions that do not constitute a complete thematic unit.

## What's Missing

An incomplete pre-core typically lacks:
- A concluding function (most common)
- Or sometimes an initiating function

## Characteristics

- Constructed from one or more phrase functions
- Taken as a whole, does not constitute complete thematic unit
- Tends to be relatively short

## Examples

### Lacking Conclusion
Pre-core begins with presentation or continuation but ends without cadential closure, flowing directly into the core.

### Lacking Initiation
Pre-core begins directly with cadential function (continuing from dominant of new key), lacking any initiating function.

## Multiple Unit Pre-cores

A small number of large pre-cores contain two thematic units, with one or both typically incomplete. For example:
- First part complete (sentence ending with dominant arrival)
- Second part incomplete (presentation only, leading to core)
