## Expanded Cadential Progression Overview

Cadential function can be enhanced, often markedly, by means of an **expanded cadential progression (ECP)**, which supports one or more complete phrases within the theme.

### Characteristics

- Phrases supported by ECP have **distinct melodic ideas and accompanimental textures**
- Often exclusively cadential in function (continuation in preceding phrase)
- Sometimes phrase supported by ECP also contains continuational elements

### Harmonic Structure

The majority of ECPs comprise all four harmonic functions:
1. **Initial tonic** (often I6)
2. **Pre-dominant** (ii, IV, or elaborated)
3. **Dominant** (often with cadential 6/4)
4. **Final tonic** (cadential goal)

Sometimes if continuation concludes on tonic, the cadential phrase begins directly with pre-dominant.

### Simple vs. Elaborated ECPs

**Simple ECPs**: No one harmony given special emphasis over others

**Elaborated ECPs**: One harmony is prominently expanded relative to others. If sufficiently large, the entire progression may support several phrases.
