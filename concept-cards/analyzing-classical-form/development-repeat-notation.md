# Development-Recapitulation Repeat

The notation and practice of repeating the development and recapitulation together.

## The Practice

Double-bar lines with repeat signs instruct the performer to repeat the development and recapitulation together.

## Mozart's Usage

Mozart often separates his codas from the prior sections by means of this notational device - the double-bar occurs before the coda begins.

## Haydn's Variation

Haydn frequently includes the coda itself within the repetition scheme. The presence of double-bar and repeat signs at the very end of movement does not exclude the possibility of a genuine coda.

## Analytical Caution

We cannot assume:
- That material after double-bar is necessarily coda
- That coda (if present) is necessarily outside the repeat
- The notation alone determines formal structure
