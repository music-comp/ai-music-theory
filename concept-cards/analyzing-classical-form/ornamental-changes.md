# Ornamental Changes in Recapitulation

Surface-level modifications that provide variety without altering fundamental structure.

## Parameters Involved

- Dynamics
- Instrumentation
- Register
- Texture
- Accompanimental figuration
- Melodic embellishments

## Motivation

Ornamental changes are motivated by:
- General aesthetic concerns for variety
- Particular expressive values the composer wishes to convey

## Distinction from Structural Changes

Ornamental changes do NOT involve:
- Harmonic-tonal organization
- Melodic-motivic material (at deep level)
- Grouping structure
- Formal functions

## Focus of Analysis

Most analysis focuses on structural changes since they most directly affect formal organization. Ornamental changes are observed and explained as they arise.

## Example

Cadential phrase in recapitulation may have ornamental changes in harmony and rhythm to create greater intensity for cadential arrival, even while retaining underlying structure.
