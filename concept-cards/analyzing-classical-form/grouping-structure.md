# Grouping Structure

**Grouping structure** refers to how music is organized into units of various sizes at multiple hierarchical levels.

## Hierarchical Levels

From smallest to largest:
- **Motive**: Smallest distinctive unit
- **Basic idea**: 2 measures
- **Phrase**: 4 measures
- **Theme**: 8 measures (or more)

## Symmetry and Asymmetry

- **Symmetrical**: Equal divisions (2+2, 4+4, 8+8)
- **Asymmetrical**: Unequal divisions (3+2, 5+3)

Symmetrical grouping is more **tight-knit**; asymmetrical is **looser**.

## Exponentials of Two

Groupings based on powers of 2 are most tight-knit:
- 2 + 2 = 4
- 4 + 4 = 8
- 8 + 8 = 16

Other patterns (3+3, 5+5, 6+6) create looser organization.

## Distinction from Melodic Content

Grouping structure is INDEPENDENT of melodic content:
- Same grouping can contain different melodies
- Same melody can appear in different groupings

Think of the cereal box analogy: box size (grouping) differs from contents (melody).

## Formal Function

Grouping structure contributes to formal function:
- Fragmentation changes grouping structure
- Extensions alter normative groupings
- Compression shortens expected groupings
