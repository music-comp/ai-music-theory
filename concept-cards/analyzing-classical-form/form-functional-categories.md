# Form-Functional Categories

Formal functions in classical music divide into three **temporal categories** that correspond to general notions of time.

## The Three Categories

### Initiating Functions (Beginning)
Express the sense of **beginning**:
- Presentation
- Antecedent
- Compound basic idea
- Basic idea (at idea level)

### Medial Functions (Middle)
Express the sense of **being in the middle**:
- Continuation

### Concluding Functions (Ending)
Express the sense of **ending**:
- Cadential
- Consequent
- Cadential idea (at idea level)

## Hierarchical Application

These categories apply at multiple structural levels:

**Idea level** (2 measures):
- Basic idea (initiating)
- Cadential idea (concluding)

**Phrase level** (4 measures):
- Presentation, antecedent, c.b.i. (initiating)
- Continuation (medial)
- Cadential, consequent (concluding)

**Theme level** (8+ measures):
- Exposition/A section (initiating)
- Contrasting middle/B section (medial)
- Recapitulation/A' section (concluding)

## Syntactical Logic

Conventional themes arrange functions in temporally logical order:
- Initiating → Medial → Concluding
- Beginning → Middle → End

This order reflects our general experience of temporal processes.
