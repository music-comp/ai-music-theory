# Continuation Phrase

The **continuation phrase** is the second 4-measure unit of a sentence, combining continuation and cadential functions.

## Formal Function

Continuation function **destabilizes** the harmonic, tonal, and rhythmic processes established in the presentation. It expresses the sense of "being in the middle" of various melodic, harmonic, and rhythmic processes.

## Four Characteristic Processes

Continuation function is characterized by:

1. **Fragmentation** - reduction in the length of constituent units (e.g., from 2-measure to 1-measure units)
2. **Harmonic acceleration** - faster rate of harmonic change compared to the presentation
3. **Increased surface rhythmic activity** - use of shorter durational values
4. **Sequential harmonic progressions** (with possible model-sequence technique)

At least one, but often several, of these characteristics will be found in a continuation phrase.

## Fusion with Cadential Function

The continuation phrase fuses continuation and cadential functions:
- Continuation function typically appears at the beginning of the phrase
- Cadential function appears toward the end
- The processes of fragmentation and acceleration often extend into the cadential material

## Cadential Closure

A continuation phrase can end with any of the three basic cadences:
- Most often a **perfect authentic cadence** (PAC)
- Sometimes a **half cadence** (HC)
- Rarely an **imperfect authentic cadence** (IAC)

## Why "Continuation"?

The phrase is labeled "continuation" rather than "cadential" because continuation function is usually more salient throughout the entire phrase than cadential function, which appears later.
