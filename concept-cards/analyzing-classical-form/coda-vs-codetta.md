# Coda vs. Codetta

Distinguishing these similar terms by their hierarchical location and formal organization.

## Codetta

- Follows a PAC
- Resides on hierarchical level comparable to basic, contrasting, and cadential ideas
- Relatively small unit (rarely more than four bars)
- Prolongs root-position tonic
- Circles melodically around first scale degree

## Coda

- Follows a recapitulation
- Resides on hierarchical level comparable to exposition, development, recapitulation
- Relatively large unit
- Contains one or more coda themes

## Key Distinction

Despite similar terms:
- A codetta is NOT a "little coda"
- A coda is NOT a large codetta
- The two structures are essentially different in both hierarchical location and formal organization

## Relationship

The coda itself typically ends with a closing section comprising a series of codettas.
