## Periodic Organization in Subordinate Theme

A periodic form may sometimes arise in subordinate themes, though it is considerably **less common** than sentential organization.

### Typical Structure

When periodic organization appears:
- Usually with an **extended consequent**
- Antecedent retains conventional size (4 or 8 measures)
- Consequent is expanded to create looser organization

### Why Less Common

Periodic organization is less suited to subordinate themes because:
- Periods tend to be relatively **tight-knit**
- Subordinate themes require **loose** organization
- The symmetrical balance of a period works against dramatic buildup

### Small Ternary Rarely Occurs

Structures resembling a small ternary are even rarer:
- When a subordinate-theme idea yields to new material
- The idea is **not** normally brought back within the theme itself
- This distinguishes subordinate theme from small ternary structure

### Tight-Knit Theme Within Group

One theme in a subordinate-theme group may take the form of a tight-knit period:
- Creates contrast with looser themes
- Less common than sentential structures
