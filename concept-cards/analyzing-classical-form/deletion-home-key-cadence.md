# Deletion of Home-key Cadence

The removal of the main theme's closing cadence in the recapitulation.

## Rationale

Since there is ample opportunity to confirm the home key later in the recapitulation (in the subordinate theme), a cadence to end the main theme is dispensable.

## Consequences

- Main theme may flow directly into transition
- Subsequent closing section may also be omitted
- Home key hardly requires reinforcement at this point

## Functional Logic

In the exposition, the main theme's cadence:
- Establishes and confirms the home key
- Provides clear articulation before transition

In the recapitulation:
- Home key was already reestablished at end of development
- Will receive ultimate confirmation in subordinate theme
- Cadential articulation is therefore unnecessary

## Effect on Form

Creates greater continuity between main theme and transition, potentially leading to fusion of these units.
