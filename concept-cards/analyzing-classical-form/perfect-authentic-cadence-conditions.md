# Perfect Authentic Cadence Conditions

A genuine **perfect authentic cadence** (PAC) requires specific conditions for complete closure.

## Requirements for True PAC

1. **Root-position V**: The dominant must be in root position
2. **Root-position I**: The tonic must be in root position
3. **Soprano on 1**: The melody must end on the tonic scale degree
4. **Proper progression**: A cadential progression must precede the dominant

## Harmonic Completeness

The V → I progression achieves harmonic completeness:
- Dominant resolves to tonic
- Both chords in root position
- Maximum harmonic stability at goal

## Melodic Completeness

The soprano on 1 achieves melodic completeness:
- Scale degree 1 is the primary melodic goal
- Often approached by step (2 → 1)
- Creates maximum melodic closure

## What Creates IAC Instead

If all conditions are met except soprano:
- Soprano ends on 3 instead of 1
- Result is IAC (imperfect authentic cadence)
- Harmonically complete, melodically incomplete

## Analysis Tip

Always verify soprano position when labeling authentic cadences. The distinction between PAC and IAC has formal consequences.
