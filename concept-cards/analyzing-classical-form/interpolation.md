# Quick Definition
Interpolation inserts completely unrelated material into a theme, creating a parenthetical disruption of the formal process that does not belong functionally to its immediately preceding or following material.

# Core Definition
Unlike extension, where added material continues to express the ongoing function, interpolation inserts material that does not participate in the ongoing formal syntax. The interpolated passage creates a feeling that the formal process has been momentarily suspended, only to resume when the interpolation concludes. Caplin emphasizes that interpolation is much rarer than extension in the classical repertory, and advises: "When in doubt, opt for the former interpretation [extension], unless you are completely sure that the newly inserted material does not belong functionally to its surrounding material."

# Prerequisites
- **Phrase deviations** — Understanding the broader framework of deviation techniques

# Key Properties
1. Inserted material does NOT belong to the ongoing formal function
2. Creates a parenthetical, disruptive effect
3. Much rarer than extension in classical music
4. Material is clearly separable from surrounding context
5. When in doubt, prefer the extension interpretation

# Construction / Recognition
## To Identify:
1. Check if the inserted material continues the ongoing function (if so, it is extension, not interpolation)
2. Verify that the material seems foreign to both its preceding and following contexts
3. Confirm the formal process resumes after the insertion as if interrupted

# Context & Application
Interpolation is the rarest of the four phrase deviation techniques. Its use creates a dramatic parenthetical effect. Analysts should be cautious about applying this label too freely.

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Labeling any unexpected material as interpolation
  **Correction**: Most unexpected material in classical themes is better understood as extension; true interpolation is rare

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: Not distinguishing extension from interpolation
  **Clarification**: The key difference is whether the inserted material continues the ongoing function (extension) or suspends it entirely (interpolation)
