## Comparing Recapitulation to Exposition

The principal analytical method for the recapitulation involves **comparison** with the exposition.

### Analytical Approach

Work through the recapitulation bar by bar:
- Compare how each part relates to the exposition
- Note all **structural changes** (required alterations)
- Note all **ornamental changes** (optional variations)
- Ask the "why" question to suggest compositional rationales

### Structural Changes

**Required alterations** in harmonic-tonal organization and phrase structure:
- Transition must avoid modulation
- Subordinate theme must be transposed to home key
- Main theme's cadence may be altered

### Ornamental Changes

**Optional variations** that do not affect essential form:
- Different instrumentation
- Different dynamics
- Different accompanimental patterning
- Different texture

### Composer Differences

**Mozart and Beethoven**: Normally bring subordinate-theme groups largely unaltered (except transposition)

**Haydn**: Almost as a rule effects major structural changes to the entire recapitulation
