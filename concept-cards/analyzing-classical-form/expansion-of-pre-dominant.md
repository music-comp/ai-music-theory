# Quick Definition
The pre-dominant may achieve prominence in an expanded cadential progression through the employment of a variety of pre-dominant harmonies in a single progression (such as ii6, IV, German augmented sixth), creating sustained preparation for the cadential dominant that may span several measures.

# Core Definition
On occasion, the pre-dominant achieves prominence in an expanded cadential progression. The composer typically employs a variety of pre-dominant harmonies in a single progression -- ii6, IV, German augmented sixth (embellishing ii6), and other chromatic pre-dominants. When the pre-dominant is expanded, it may span several measures (six or more), creating sustained preparation for the cadential dominant and heightening tension before resolution. Pre-dominant expansion often occurs after a deceptive cadence, when the return to cadential function features an elaborated pre-dominant area, or following the repeat of a continuation phrase after a deceptive cadence (Caplin, Ch. 12, p. 397).

# Key Properties
1. Variety of pre-dominant harmonies in single progression
2. Common harmonies: ii6, IV, German augmented sixth
3. May span several measures (six or more)
4. Creates sustained preparation for cadential dominant
5. Heightens tension before dominant resolution
6. Often follows a deceptive cadence

# Construction / Recognition
## To Identify:
1. Locate the pre-dominant area within an ECP
2. Check whether multiple different pre-dominant harmonies appear in succession
3. Measure the temporal span of the pre-dominant area
4. Note if the expansion follows a deceptive cadence
5. Listen for the augmented sixth as a chromatic intensification of the pre-dominant

# Context & Application
Expansion of the pre-dominant is one of three principal ways to expand an ECP (alongside expansion of the initial tonic and the dominant). It is particularly effective when it follows a deceptive cadence, as the return to cadential function after such an interruption warrants intensified preparation for the dominant. The variety of pre-dominant harmonies creates a rich chromatic palette appropriate for subordinate-theme loosening.

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Treating each pre-dominant harmony as initiating a new cadential progression
  **Correction**: Multiple pre-dominant harmonies within a single expanded progression all serve the same pre-dominant function
