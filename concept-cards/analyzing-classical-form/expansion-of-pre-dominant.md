## Expansion of the Pre-Dominant in ECP

The **pre-dominant** may, on occasion, achieve prominence in an expanded cadential progression.

### Typical Method

The composer employs a **variety of pre-dominant harmonies** in a single progression:
- ii6
- IV
- German augmented sixth (embellishing ii6)
- Other chromatic pre-dominants

### Extended Pre-Dominant Span

When pre-dominant is expanded:
- May span several measures (6 or more)
- Creates sustained preparation for the cadential dominant
- Heightens tension before resolution

### Common Context

Often occurs after:
- A deceptive cadence (returning to cadential function)
- The repeat of a continuation phrase following deceptive cadence
