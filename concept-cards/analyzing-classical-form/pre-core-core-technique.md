## Pre-Core/Core Technique

The **pre-core/core** technique is a frequently appearing mode of organization in development sections.

### The Core

The **core** of a development:
- Establishes a relatively large-sized **model** (4-8 measures)
- The model is **sequenced** one or more times
- Subsequent **fragmentation** leads to an HC (or dominant arrival)
- Concludes with a **standing on the dominant**
- Projects an emotional quality of instability, restlessness, and dramatic conflict

### The Pre-Core

A **pre-core** usually precedes the core:
- A passage of lesser emotional intensity than the core
- Difficult to generalize its formal organization
- Various specific techniques are possible

### Landmarks of Development

The most prominent landmarks:
1. **Beginning of core**: Establishment of a relatively large-sized model that is then sequenced
2. **End of core**: The HC (or dominant arrival) and final standing on the dominant

### Formal Outcome

- If the HC is in the home key, the recapitulation normally appears next
- If the cadence is in a development key, more developmental material usually follows
