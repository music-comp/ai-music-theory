## False Closing Section

A **false closing section** occurs when codettas following a main theme PAC are retrospectively understood to function as the beginning of the transition.

### How It Works

1. Main theme closes with a PAC
2. New codetta-like material appears (tonic pedal, descending melody from tonic scale degree)
3. The idea is repeated, suggesting a closing section
4. But as music develops, it becomes clear a more substantial thematic unit is emerging
5. The supposed codettas are retrospectively understood as a **presentation phrase** for the transition

### Characteristics of Initial Material

The codettas initially suggest:
- Postcadential function
- Tonic pedal
- Recessive character

### Reinterpretation

The material is reinterpreted as:
- A basic idea
- Whose repetition creates a presentation
- Leading to continuation function for the transition

### Also Used with Subordinate Theme

The false closing section technique can also begin a subordinate theme within a subordinate-theme group.
