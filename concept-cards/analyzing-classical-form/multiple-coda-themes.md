# Multiple Coda Themes

When a coda contains more than one thematic unit.

## Occurrence

A coda normally consists of at least one coda theme, but lengthy codas may contain multiple coda themes, each ending with its own PAC.

## Organization

### First Coda Theme
- May end with HC or PAC
- If HC, followed by standing on dominant
- Often sets up the second theme

### Second Coda Theme
- May feature contrasting character (e.g., modal shift to minor)
- May omit initiating function
- Usually closes with powerful PAC
- May have expanded cadential progression

## Content

Second coda theme normally contains melodic-motivic material differing from first, though both may draw on earlier material from the movement.

## Effect

Multiple coda themes allow for:
- Greater formal weight to the coda
- Multiple climactic moments
- More elaborate closure of the movement
