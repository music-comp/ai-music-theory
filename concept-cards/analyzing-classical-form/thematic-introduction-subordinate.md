## Thematic Introduction to Subordinate Theme

The structural beginning of a subordinate theme may also be preceded by a brief **thematic introduction**.

### Two Types

**1. Dominant prolongation from transition**:
- Harmony emphasizes dominant
- Prolonged from end of transition
- Resolved to tonic at beginning of subordinate theme
- The transition's final harmony serves as introduction

**2. Anacrusis-like introduction**:
- Tonic harmony (of SK) and new accompanimental pattern start on downbeat of measure following transition end
- Basic idea begins **later in that measure**
- So downbeat of **next** measure is the actual "first" bar of the theme

### Rarity with Main Themes

This second type (anacrusis-like introduction) is rarely encountered with main themes, though notable exceptions exist (e.g., Mozart's Symphony No. 40 in G minor).
