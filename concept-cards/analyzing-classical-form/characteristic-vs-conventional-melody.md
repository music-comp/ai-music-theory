# Quick Definition
Characteristic melody uniquely defines a theme as an individual (used in b.i., ascending, distinctive). Conventional melody is formulaic and interchangeable from piece to piece (used in cadential material, descending, generic). Themes progress from characteristic to conventional through liquidation.

# Core Definition
A characteristic melody uniquely defines a theme as an individual, one different from other themes. A conventional melody is relatively interchangeable from piece to piece. A characteristic melody normally appears at the very beginning of a thematic unit, while a conventional melody is typically used for interior passage work or cadential closure (p. 61). The general melodic contour of a cadence is descending, reflecting the original sense of cadence as "to fall" (Latin, cadere). Cadential material arises when the composer systematically liquidates the characteristic melodic and rhythmic motives introduced in the basic idea (p. 78).

# Key Properties
1. Characteristic: unique, distinctive, idiosyncratic; found in b.i.; typically ascending
2. Conventional: formulaic, generic, interchangeable; found in cadential material; typically descending
3. Theme progresses: characteristic -> (liquidation) -> conventional
4. Liquidation is the bridge between the two
5. The melodic contour of the b.i. typically "opens up" space; cadence "closes down"

# Construction / Recognition
## To Identify:
1. Opening material (b.i.): Is it distinctive, memorable? = Characteristic
2. Closing material (cadential idea): Is it formulaic, interchangeable? = Conventional
3. Note the trajectory from one to the other

# Context & Application
This contrast is fundamental to the dramatic shape of classical themes. Openings should be distinctive (to capture attention and establish identity); endings should be generic (to signal closure and allow formal punctuation).

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Expecting all material in a theme to be equally distinctive
  **Correction**: Classical themes intentionally progress from characteristic to conventional

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: Confusing melodic content type with grouping structure
  **Clarification**: Characteristic vs. conventional concerns melodic content; fragmentation concerns unit length
