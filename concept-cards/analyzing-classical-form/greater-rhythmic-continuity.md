# Greater Rhythmic Continuity in Recapitulation

The tendency to reduce rhythmic interruptions in the recapitulation compared to the exposition.

## The Pattern

The exposition often contains prominent interruptions in rhythmic activity:
- Rests
- Fermatas
- Particularly in main theme and at transition end (medial caesura)

## The Change

The recapitulation often removes such discontinuities to foster greater rhythmic momentum.

## Rationale

Following an emotionally charged development section:
- Flow of events may seem too inhibited
- Too many stops and starts could interrupt the momentum
- Progress needs to feel more continuous

## Methods

- Filling in rests with newly added material
- Eliminating pauses between phrases
- Reducing fermata emphasis
- Compressing two-part transitions to avoid double caesura

## Effect

Creates sense of inevitability and forward drive toward the final cadential goal.
