# Pre-core

The passage at the beginning of the development section that precedes the core.

## Definition

A passage of variable tonal and phrase-structural organization arising at the very beginning of the development and stretching up to the onset of the core.

## Characteristics

- May consist of one or two thematic units
- One or both units may be incomplete (lacking concluding function)
- More relaxed, hesitant, and anticipatory than the core
- Generally softer dynamic level
- Discontinuous rhythmic motion (less active than core)

## Melodic-Motivic Sources

The beginning of the pre-core is typically drawn from:
1. The main theme's basic idea
2. Codettas from the exposition's closing section
3. New material (not found in the exposition)

Melodic ideas from the transition and subordinate-theme group are rarely brought back in the pre-core, at least toward its beginning.

## Analogy

If the core often bursts out with Sturm und Drang, the pre-core can be likened to the calm before the storm.
