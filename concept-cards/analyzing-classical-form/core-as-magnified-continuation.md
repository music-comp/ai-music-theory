# Core as Magnified Continuation

Understanding the core's relationship to continuation function.

## Sequential Organization

With its emphasis on sequential organization, a core strongly projects the sense of "being in the middle" - comparable to continuation function within a phrase.

## Dual Nature of Core

The core functions on two levels:

### As Continuation
- Opening part with pervasive model-sequence technique
- Resembles a magnified continuation
- Often follows directly after a presentational unit

### As Complete Formal Construct
- Consists of more than model-sequence passages
- Also embraces functional conclusion (HC or dominant arrival)
- Includes postcadential standing on the dominant
- Comparable to other thematic units (main theme, subordinate theme, transition)

## Core Following Presentation

When the core is preceded by a presentation (as incomplete second part of pre-core), the relationship is especially clear:
- Presentation in pre-core provides initiation
- Core functions as magnified continuation
- The whole creates a sentence-like structure at a higher level
