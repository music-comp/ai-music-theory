# Quick Definition
The core functions as a magnified continuation due to its pervasive sequential organization, especially when preceded by a presentation phrase in the pre-core, creating a sentence-like structure at a higher hierarchical level.

# Core Definition
"With its emphasis on sequential organization, a core strongly projects the sense of 'being in the middle'" (p. 451), comparable to continuation function. The opening part of a core, with pervasive model-sequence technique, "often appears as a magnified continuation." But the core as a complete formal construct also embraces a functional conclusion (HC or dominant arrival) and standing on the dominant, making it comparable to complete thematic units like the transition. The relationship is especially clear when the core follows a presentation phrase in the pre-core, creating a sentence-like structure.

# Prerequisites
- **Core** — The formal unit understood as a magnified continuation
- **Continuation function** — The phrase function being magnified
- **Sentence** — The theme type whose structure is replicated at a higher level

# Key Properties
1. Sequential organization projects medial function ("being in the middle")
2. Opening part of core = magnified continuation
3. Core also contains concluding function, making it a complete construct
4. When preceded by presentation in pre-core, creates higher-level sentence
5. This higher-level sentence: presentation (pre-core) + continuation (core) + conclusion (HC + standing on V)

# Construction / Recognition
## To Identify:
1. Note whether the core follows a presentation phrase (in the pre-core)
2. If so, the core functions as a magnified continuation of that presentation
3. The overall structure replicates sentence form at the development level

# Examples
**Example 13.9** (p. 468): Haydn, H. 49 — presentation from subordinate theme appears as the second part of the pre-core (mm. 81-84); the core (beginning m. 85) functions as magnified continuation of this presentation, since the model's opening derives from the continuation of the same theme.
