# Quick Definition
Antecedent and consequent functions occur less frequently in subordinate themes than sentential functions. When they appear, the antecedent usually retains conventional size (4 or 8 measures), but the consequent is often expanded (typically through ECP) to create looser organization. Occasionally, one theme in a subordinate-theme group takes the form of a tight-knit period.

# Core Definition
Antecedent and consequent functions occur less frequently in subordinate themes than do the sentential functions of presentation, continuation, and cadential. When periodic functions appear, the antecedent usually retains its conventional size (4 or 8 measures), but the consequent is often **expanded** to create a looser organization. In some cases, one of the themes in a subordinate-theme group may take the form of a tight-knit period (Caplin, Ch. 12, pp. 417-418).
