# Quick Definition
The third fundamental theme type (after sentence and period), consisting of three main sections -- exposition (A), contrasting middle (B), and recapitulation (A') -- based on two principles: (1) a closed thematic unit is juxtaposed with a structurally open contrasting unit, and (2) the original unit returns to ensure complete closure.

# Core Definition
The small ternary's three sections have specific formal functions: A = exposition (exposes fundamental ideas, closes with PAC), B = contrasting middle (looser organization, emphasizes dominant, usually ends with HC or dominant arrival), A' = recapitulation (returns basic idea harmonized by tonic of home key, closes with PAC). The A section is the most tightly knit, the B section is loose, and the A' section varies. The A section may remain in the home key or modulate to a subordinate key (V in major, III or v in minor). The B section always starts in the measure following A (they never elide). The A' must fulfill two requirements: return of the basic idea in the home key, and PAC in the home key.

# Prerequisites
- **Sentence** and **Period** — The A section is usually built as one of these
- **Hybrid themes** — May also serve as A section structure

# Key Properties
1. Three sections: A (exposition), B (contrasting middle), A' (recapitulation)
2. A section: tight-knit, closes with PAC (may modulate)
3. B section: loose, emphasizes dominant, ends with HC or dominant arrival
4. A' section: returns basic idea in home key, closes with PAC
5. A and B never elide
6. If repeated (||: A :||: B A' :||), called "rounded binary"
7. Small ternary and rounded binary are functionally identical

# Examples
**Example 1** (p. 220): Haydn, Piano Sonata H. 40, ii -- A section as modulating period; B as continuation with model-sequence ending HC; A' as tonally adjusted consequent.

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Labeling the end of a standing on the dominant as an HC
  **Correction**: A standing on the dominant has no harmonic change, hence no cadence

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: Thinking small ternary and rounded binary are different forms
  **Clarification**: They are functionally identical; the difference is only the repetition scheme
