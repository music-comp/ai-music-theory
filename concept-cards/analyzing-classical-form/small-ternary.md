# Small Ternary

The **small ternary** is a fundamental theme type consisting of three main sections (A-B-A') based on two principles: contrast and return.

## Core Principles

1. A relatively closed thematic unit (A) is juxtaposed with a structurally open unit of contrasting content (B)
2. The original unit is brought back (A') to ensure complete closure

## Structure

- **A section** (Exposition): Tight-knit 8-measure theme, closes with PAC
- **B section** (Contrasting middle): Loose organization, emphasizes dominant
- **A' section** (Recapitulation): Returns basic idea, closes with PAC in home key

## Functional Labels

The sections have specific formal functions:
- A = **Exposition** (exposes fundamental ideas)
- B = **Contrasting middle** (contrasts through harmony and phrase structure)
- A' = **Recapitulation** (recapitulates opening material)

## Relation to Rounded Binary

If the A section is repeated and if B + A' are repeated together, the form is called **rounded binary**. From a functional point of view, small ternary and rounded binary are identical forms.

## Key Relationships

- Home key (major): Subordinate key typically V
- Home key (minor): Subordinate key typically III or v

The A section emphasizes tonic; the B section emphasizes dominant.
