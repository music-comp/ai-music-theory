# Expanded Recapitulation

The recapitulation may be **expanded** relative to the exposition through motivic development or various phrase deviations.

## Motivic Development in A'

The A' section can "develop" motives from prior sections:
- Stepwise sequences elaborating basic idea material
- New continuational passages
- Extended exploration of motives

## Expansion Techniques

The A' section may be lengthened through:
- **Internal expansion** (material inserted within phrases)
- **Phrase extension** (material added after phrases)
- **Cadential deviation** (deceptive cadence requiring extension)

## Developmental Character

Though motivic development is typically associated with B sections:
- Development can occur in A' as well
- Creates expanded, developmental recapitulation
- May significantly alter character relative to exposition

## Loosening Effect

Expanded recapitulations are typically **looser** than expositions:
- Asymmetrical phrase structure
- Sequential activity
- Chromatic enrichment

## Comparison with B Section

When A' is significantly expanded:
- Both B and A' may appear loosely organized
- Comparison between them becomes complex
- Different loosening criteria may apply to each
