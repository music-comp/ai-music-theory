# Quick Definition
A comparative overview of framing functions: thematic introduction (before-the-beginning, progressive dynamic), closing section (after PAC, series of codettas, usually recessive), and standing on the dominant (after HC, dominant prolongation). All are external to the theme proper.

# Core Definition
Before-the-beginning: thematic introduction (short, minimal motivic content, tonic prolongation, progressive dynamic). After-the-end following PAC: closing section (series of codettas, tonic prolongation, typically recessive dynamic, fragmentation). After-the-end following HC: standing on the dominant (dominant prolongation, may feature textural dissolution and motivic anticipation). All framing functions are external to the theme and could theoretically be removed without destroying its fundamental organization.

# Key Properties
1. Introduction: before the theme, progressive, minimal motivic content
2. Closing section: after PAC, codettas, typically recessive
3. Standing on dominant: after HC, dominant prolongation, no cadence at end
4. All are external to the theme proper
5. Introduction typically prolongs tonic; closing section prolongs tonic; standing prolongs dominant
