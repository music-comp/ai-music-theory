# Framing Function Summary

Framing functions appear before or after themes, "framing" the main thematic content.

## Before-the-Beginning Functions

### Introduction
- Prepares theme's arrival
- Establishes tempo, key, character
- May be brief or extended
- Not counted in theme's measure structure

## After-the-End Functions

### Closing Section
- Multiple codettas following PAC
- Shows fragmentation (progressively shorter units)
- Projects recessive dynamic
- Confirms tonic closure

### Codetta
- Single brief postcadential unit
- 1-2 measures typically
- Confirms cadential closure
- May appear singly or in groups

### Standing on the Dominant (postcadential)
- Follows HC at end of B section
- Reinforces dominant before recapitulation
- May feature textural dissolution

### Suffix
- More elaborate than codetta
- May introduce new melodic ideas
- Still postcadential, confirming closure

## Key Principle

All framing functions are **external** to the theme proper. They could theoretically be removed without destroying the theme's fundamental organization.
