# French Overture Style

The Baroque antecedent of the classical slow introduction.

## Characteristics

Associated with introductory movement (or part of movement):
- Fanfare gestures
- Double-dotted rhythms (later written as double-dotted)
- Overall courtly sense of pomp and circumstance
- Stately, heraldic quality
- Forte dynamic

## What's Missing

Generally lacking in French overture:
- The anticipatory, hesitant gestures
- Uncertainty and instability
- Marked discontinuities
- Premonitory quality

## Historical Evolution

The classical period retains the stately qualities from French overture but adds:
- Anticipatory character
- Hesitant gestures
- Instability
- Discontinuities

This dramatizes the sense of introduction by making it a more premonitory event, rather than purely ceremonial opening.
