## Transition Basics

The **transition** is the second thematic function of the sonata-form exposition. Also called "bridge," it leads from the main theme to the subordinate theme, functioning as a **medial** thematic function.

### Three Primary Functions

1. **To destabilize the home key** - so the subordinate key can emerge as a competing tonality
2. **To loosen the formal organization** - phrase-structural destabilization
3. **To liquidate characteristic motivic material** - especially toward the end, to "clear the stage" for the subordinate theme

### Harmonic Goal

The harmonic goal of a transition is **dominant harmony**:
- Most often of the subordinate key
- Occasionally of the home key

This dominant is usually articulated by:
- A **half cadence** (most common)
- A noncadential **dominant arrival** (looser effect)
