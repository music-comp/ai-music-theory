# Introduction Ending Not on Dominant

When a slow introduction concludes on a harmony other than the expected dominant.

## Typical Ending

In the majority of cases, slow introductions end on dominant of home key, articulated by HC or dominant arrival.

## Unusual Endings

Some introductions end on other harmonies:
- Subdominant (iv or IV)
- Other pre-dominant sonorities
- Minor harmony that functions as iv in home key

## Analytical Understanding

Ending on minor triad is unusual since it cannot be construed as dominant. The logic becomes clear when we see it functions as pre-dominant for the main theme's opening.

## Anticipatory Technique

Rather than ending on harmony whose resolution brings pre-dominant at exposition start:
- Composer introduces pre-dominant in advance
- Sustains it toward end of introduction
- Main theme then begins with this pre-dominant leading to dominant

## Example

Introduction may end on D minor, understood as iv in A minor home key, which then leads to V at beginning of main theme.
