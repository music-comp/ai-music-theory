# Structural Changes in Recapitulation

The principal structural modifications typically observed in recapitulations.

## Main Theme Changes

- Deletion of thematic restatements (repeated continuations, A' sections)
- Newly added passages with model-sequence technique (secondary development)
- Emphasis on flat-side tonal regions
- Deletion of home-key cadence

## Transition Changes

- Tonal adjustment to remain in home key (most essential change)
- Deletions or compressions of tonic-emphasis passages
- Additional model-sequence technique (secondary development)
- Typical adjustment involves flat-side subdominant region

## Subordinate Theme Changes

Least likely to change except for tonal adjustment into home key. If changed:
- Deleting redundant passages (especially in monothematic expositions)
- Expanding cadential area for powerful home-key confirmation

## Closing Section

Usually retained as in exposition. Without coda, may be extended for greater sense of closure.
