## Closing Section After Main Theme

If the main theme ends with a PAC, a **closing section** (consisting of codettas) may follow.

### When It Occurs

A closing section following the main theme:
- Only occurs after a **PAC** (not after HC or IAC)
- Is optional (not required like the closing section after subordinate theme)
- Prolongs the achieved tonic before transition begins

### Characteristics

Main-theme closing section typically features:
- Tonic prolongation
- Postcadential character
- May be relatively brief

### Relationship to False Closing Section

Material that initially seems to be a closing section may retrospectively be understood as the beginning of the transition (false closing section).

### Contrast with Subordinate-Theme Closing Section

Unlike subordinate themes:
- Main themes only **occasionally** include a closing section
- Less energy is built up in achieving the main-theme cadence
- Less need for postcadential dissipation of energy

### Standing on the Dominant

If main theme ends with HC:
- A closing section (strictly speaking) cannot follow
- Instead, a **standing on the dominant** may occur
