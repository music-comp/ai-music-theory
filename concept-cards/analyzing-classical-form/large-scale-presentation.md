## Large-Scale Presentation

In some expansive works by Beethoven, the main theme acquires an overall sentential form, but the initiating units are **enlarged beyond** what would normally be found in a compound sentence.

### Structure

- Opening unit is **significantly larger** than a typical compound basic idea
- This opening unit is **immediately repeated** (occasionally sequential)
- Together they create a large presentation-like unit
- A continuation follows, often "compressed" in relation to the grouping size proposed by the presentation

### Cadences of Limited Scope

Sometimes the opening unit might be sufficiently large that it appears to close with its own cadence. However:
- Never a PAC in the home key (which would suggest the theme was completed)
- Such a cadence should be seen as one of "limited scope"
- It does not bring real closure for the sentential theme as a whole

### Association with Beethoven

This technique is particularly associated with Beethoven's expansive sonata forms, where the opening gesture may span 9 or more measures before being repeated.
