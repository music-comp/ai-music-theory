## Two-Part Subordinate Theme

At times, material following an internal HC sounds more like the beginning of another subordinate theme than continuation of the ongoing one. This creates a **two-part subordinate theme**.

### When It Arises

The internal HC leads to a **new initiating function**, such as:
- A presentation
- A compound basic idea

### Why "Two-Part" Rather Than "Two Themes"

Rather than speaking of two subordinate themes (one ending with HC, the other with PAC), it is more theoretically sound to maintain the fundamental principle that all subordinate themes must close with an authentic cadence.

The structure is therefore a **single two-part subordinate theme**.

### Structure

**Part 1**: Begins in subordinate key, leads to internal HC and standing on dominant

**Part 2**: New initiating function (presentation or CBI), leads eventually to PAC

### Formal Logic

The first part:
- Has clear beginning, middle, ending functions
- Resides fully in subordinate key
- Conforms to subordinate theme requirements except for not ending with PAC
- The lack of PAC motivates the second part
