## Loosening Devices in Subordinate Theme

A subordinate theme usually employs **loosening devices** that effect structural lengthening and introduce various modes of harmonic destabilization.

### Typical Loosening Techniques

1. **Extending or repeating a presentation function**
2. **Weakening the opening tonic prolongation** of a presentation
3. **Extending the continuation function** with model-sequence technique
4. **Extending the cadential function** through evaded cadences and "one more time" technique
5. **Expanding the cadential function** by means of expanded cadential progressions (ECP)
6. **Omitting an initiating function**

### Harmonic Destabilization

Additional loosening through:
- Modal mixture
- Chromaticism
- Tonicization of remote regions

### Effect on Length

These techniques cause the subordinate theme to be almost always **considerably longer** than the main theme.
