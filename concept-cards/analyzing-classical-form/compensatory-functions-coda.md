# Compensatory Functions of Coda

The various roles a coda plays in addressing compositional matters not treated elsewhere.

## The Primary Function

The coda expresses an after-the-end formal function. But since the recapitulation is highly constrained, the coda offers opportunity to compensate for the inappropriateness of earlier sections to achieve certain goals.

## Five Compensatory Functions

### 1. Recollection of Main-theme Ideas
Imparting circular design to overall form by recalling main-theme ideas at the end.

### 2. Restoration of Deleted Material
Recapitulating material deleted from the recapitulation.

### 3. Reference to Development Section
Referring to ideas from development that couldn't appear in recapitulation.

### 4. Shaping a New Dynamic Curve
Creating a different or more powerful dynamic trajectory than recapitulation allows.

### 5. Realization of Unrealized Implications
Realizing implications generated by compositional processes left unrealized in earlier sections.

## Coda as "Conclusion" vs. "After-the-end"

The coda wraps up loose ends (conclusion) while appearing after fundamental processes are complete (after-the-end). Primary processes end in recapitulation; secondary processes often attain closure only in coda.
