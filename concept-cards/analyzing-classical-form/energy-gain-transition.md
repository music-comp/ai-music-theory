## Energy Gain in Transition

The transition often represents a significant **energy gain** in the movement, projecting forward momentum and dynamic drive.

### Manifestations of Energy Gain

**Rhythmic acceleration**:
- More active surface rhythms
- Introduction of running sixteenth notes
- Continuous accompanimental patterns (Alberti, drum bass)

**Dynamic intensification**:
- Crescendo passages
- Forte markings
- More assertive articulation

**Textural density**:
- Fuller accompaniment
- More voices active
- Greater registral span

### "Getting Under Way"

The beginning of the transition is often when the movement seems to be truly "getting under way":
- Main theme may have been tentative or hesitant
- Transition brings confident forward motion
- Establishes the dynamic character of the movement

### Relationship to Standing on the Dominant

The accumulated energy may be:
1. **Sustained** through the standing on the dominant (intense drive to subordinate theme)
2. **Dissipated** during standing on the dominant (clearing the stage for contrasting subordinate theme)
