## Coda Overview

A **coda** is an optional framing function that may follow the recapitulation in sonata form.

### Position

- Follows the recapitulation's final PAC
- Extends beyond the material of the exposition
- Not required but very common

### Distinguishing from Closing Section

The recapitulation ends when it no longer corresponds with the exposition:
- **Closing section**: Returns from exposition
- **Coda**: New material or extended development beyond exposition's scope

### Function

The coda serves to:
- Provide additional emphasis on the home key
- Create a more decisive sense of conclusion
- Allow for final statements of important thematic material
- Balance large-scale proportions

### Relationship to Closing Section

In the absence of a genuine coda:
- The closing section may be **extended**
- This extension imparts a more decisive sense of conclusion
- The boundary between "extended closing section" and "coda" can be unclear
