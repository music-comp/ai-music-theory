# Continuation Phrase (Contrasting Middle)

The contrasting middle can be built as a **continuation phrase**, featuring sequential harmonic activity and closing with an HC.

## Structure

The B section as continuation typically includes:
- **Model-sequence technique**
- **Sequential harmonic progressions**
- Closure with **half cadence**

## Looser Organization

Sequential activity creates looser organization appropriate for B section:
- Harmonic destabilization
- Contrast with tonic-focused A section
- Forward momentum toward recapitulation

## Ending with HC

When built as continuation, the B section usually closes with HC to:
- Set up strong expectations for tonic resolution
- Prepare return of basic idea in A'
- Create appropriate weak-to-strong cadential relationship

## Postcadential Standing on the Dominant

Following the HC, a **postcadential standing on the dominant** may be added to:
- Reinforce the dominant
- Create stronger expectation for recapitulation
- Allow textural dissolution

## Elision Option

The standing on the dominant may:
- Follow directly after the HC
- **Elide** with the cadence (begin at same moment as cadential arrival)
