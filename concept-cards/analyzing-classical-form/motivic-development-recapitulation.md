# Motivic Development in Recapitulation

Although motivic development is typically associated with contrasting middles, it can also occur significantly in the **recapitulation**.

## Beyond the B Section

Development is not confined to the B section:
- The A' section may extensively develop motives
- New consequences may be drawn from melodic material
- The recapitulation can differ markedly from the exposition

## Techniques

Developmental techniques in A' include:
- **Stepwise sequences** elaborating basic idea material
- **New continuation phrases** exploring motives
- **Fragmentation** of previously unified material
- **Chromatic enrichment** of harmonic language

## Result

Such development typically:
- **Expands** the recapitulation
- Changes the theme's **character**
- Creates **structural changes** (not just ornamental)
- May make A' **looser** than A

## Example Effects

A developmental recapitulation may feature:
- Phrase-structural asymmetry
- Prominent sequential passages
- Extensive chromatic harmonies
- Cadential deviations and extensions

## Comparison with B Section

When A' is heavily developmental:
- Both B and A' may be loose
- Comparison between them becomes complex
- Different criteria contribute to loosening in each
