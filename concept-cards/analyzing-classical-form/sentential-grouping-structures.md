## Sentential Grouping Structures

Many formal units in classical movements present a **grouping structure** that resembles the sentence but do not truly create this theme type.

### Core's Sentential Quality

The grouping structure of a core often strongly resembles an extended (compound) sentence. However, there is one important functional difference:

- The opening model and its sequence together **do not prolong tonic harmony** of a key
- Therefore, they cannot legitimately be identified as forming a genuine presentation
- This prohibits us from regarding a core as a sentence theme type

### Standing on the Dominant

The grouping structure of a standing on the dominant may also resemble a sentence. Yet the idea of a genuine sentence must be completely rejected because:
- There is a lack of any real harmonic activity
- There is no cadential articulation within the standing on the dominant

### Terminological Precision

The term **sentence** is best reserved for (relatively) tight-knit themes featuring clear presentation and continuation phrases. Many passages with sentence-like groupings should not be called sentences if they lack the essential harmonic characteristics.
