# Nonmodulating Transition in Recapitulation

How a nonmodulating transition from the exposition is handled in the recapitulation.

## Basic Principle

If the exposition's transition is nonmodulatory, a tonal adjustment is not necessary. The transition may retain its original structure.

## Typical Alterations

Even when not necessary, alterations may still occur to achieve:
- Greater harmonic-tonal variety
- Fresh effect despite remaining in home key
- Especially if preceding main theme remains unaltered

## When Original is Retained

The nonmodulating transition may be kept intact particularly when:
- The main theme of recapitulation already provides secondary development
- The main theme already emphasizes flat-side regions
- Sufficient variety has been achieved elsewhere

## Example

If the main theme adds sequence and flat-side emphasis, the nonmodulating transition can follow identically to the exposition.
