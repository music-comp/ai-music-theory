# Analyzing a Development Section

A systematic approach to analyzing development sections in sonata form.

## Step-by-Step Method

### Step 1: Identify the Core
- Look first for a large-scale model (4-8 measures) that is sequenced at least once
- This establishes whether pre-core/core technique is used

### Step 2: Find the Core's End
- Locate the HC or dominant arrival marking the structural end of the core
- The final boundary occurs where the standing on the dominant plays out

### Step 3: Analyze the Pre-core
- Return to the beginning of the development
- The pre-core stretches from the beginning up to the start of the core

### Step 4: Check for Additional Sections
- If the core closes in a development key, look for:
  - A second core (ending in home key)
  - A retransition (returning to home key)

### Step 5: If No Core is Present
- Consider other organizational options
- Look for pseudo-core, transition-like, or subordinate themelike units
- Describe harmonic/tonal plan and grouping structure as best you can
