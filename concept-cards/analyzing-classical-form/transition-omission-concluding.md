## Transition Omission of Concluding Function

Now and then, the final dominant of a transition gives **no sense of being an ending harmony** whatsoever.

### Characteristics of Missing Closure

- The dominant does not appear to be the goal of the progression
- It receives no emphasis
- It resolves directly to tonic at beginning of subordinate theme
- Little or no rhythmic break
- Transition is left without any kind of functional closure

### How Subordinate Theme is Articulated

The composer usually finds a clear way to articulate subordinate theme beginning:
- Appearance of a basic idea in the context of a presentation phrase
- Clear establishment of root-position tonic

### Fusion with Subordinate Theme

Sometimes the subordinate theme **fuses** with the transition:
- No clear point where transition ends
- No clear point where subordinate theme begins
- A single unit embraces both functions
