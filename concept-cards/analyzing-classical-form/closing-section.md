# Closing Section

A **closing section** is an after-the-end framing function that follows a PAC, consisting of multiple codettas that confirm and extend the cadential closure.

## Structure

A closing section consists of:
- Multiple **codettas** (brief postcadential units)
- Typically showing **fragmentation** (progressively shorter units)
- Confirming the tonic achieved at the cadence

## Function

The closing section:
- Confirms and reinforces the cadential closure
- Projects **recessive dynamic** (dying away of energy)
- Extends the sense of arrival
- Allows the music to "settle" after climax

## Recessive Dynamic

Following the cadence, energy typically dissipates:
- Dynamics often decrease
- Rhythmic activity may slow
- Texture may thin
- The music "winds down"

## Fragmentation in Closing Sections

Codettas typically show progressive shortening:
- First codetta: 2 measures
- Second codetta: 1 measure
- Third codetta: 1 measure (repeated)

## Harmonic Content

Closing sections usually:
- Prolong tonic harmony
- May include simple I-V-I progressions
- Confirm the home key emphatically
