## Main Theme Overview

The **main theme** introduces the principal melodic-motivic material of the movement in the context of the home key.

### Characteristics

- Relatively **tight-knit** in formal organization
- Often structured as a conventional theme type (sentence, period, hybrid, etc.)
- Confirms the home key through cadential closure
- Usually ends with a PAC, sometimes HC (especially in minor), rarely IAC

### Tight-Knit Organization

The main theme defines a standard of tight-knit organization for the movement as a whole. All other sections can be compared as more or less loosely organized relative to the main theme.

### Main Theme Group

An exposition occasionally contains two distinct main themes, each ending with a PAC, forming a **main-theme group**.

### Expressive Qualities

Tight-knit organization is expressed through:
- Clear establishment of home-key tonic
- Cadential confirmation of that key
- Unified melodic material through motives
- Symmetrical grouping structure
- Compact and efficient presentation of phrase functions
