# Small Binary

The **small binary** is a theme type consisting of two parts, distinguished from the small ternary by its lack of recapitulation.

## Essential Difference

The fundamental distinction from rounded binary/small ternary:
- **Small binary**: Does NOT recapitulate opening materials
- **Rounded binary**: DOES recapitulate opening materials

The initial basic idea (harmonized by tonic) does not return later in the theme.

## Structure

||: Part 1 :||: Part 2 :||

Both parts are typically 8 real measures, creating symmetrical 8 + 8 structure.

## Labeling the Parts

Unlike small ternary (A-B-A'), small binary uses neutral terms:
- **Part 1** and **Part 2**

Letter labels (A-B or A-A') are inappropriate because they carry functional meanings that don't apply.

## Motivic Connections

Part 2 often begins with ideas **related to** the beginning of Part 1:
- Creates bipartite structural sense
- Dampens expectations for recapitulation
- Contrasts with rounded binary (where B begins with NEW material)

## Typical Usage

Small binary is especially appropriate for:
- **Variations** forms (basic idea returns at start of each variation)
- **Rondo** themes (minimizes overexposure of basic idea)
