## Subordinate Key (SK)

The **subordinate key** is the secondary tonal center in a sonata-form exposition, creating a tonal contrast with the home key. It must be confirmed by a PAC in the exposition.

### Standard Key Relationships

| Home Key Mode | Subordinate Key |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Major | V (dominant) |
| Minor | III (relative major) or v (minor dominant) |

### Example
- If HK = C major, SK = G major
- If HK = C minor, SK = E-flat major (or G minor)

### Role in the Exposition

- Emerges as a "rival tonality" to the home key
- Confirmed by a PAC at the end of the subordinate theme
- Temporarily gains "structural superiority" over the home key

### In the Recapitulation

The subordinate theme returns transposed into the home key, resolving the tonal conflict established in the exposition.
