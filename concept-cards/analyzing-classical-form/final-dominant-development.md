# Final Dominant of Development

The dominant harmony that concludes the development and resolves to tonic at the beginning of the recapitulation.

## Definition

The dominant harmony that concludes the development - an "ultimate" dominant - which resolves to tonic to initiate the recapitulation.

## NOT a Cadence

We must avoid the temptation to recognize an authentic cadence when it resolves to tonic to initiate the recapitulation.

## Why the Confusion Arises

Especially when opening tonic appears with great dynamic force, it may give misleading impression that V-I motion has cadential quality.

## Correct Understanding

The resolution of final dominant to tonic:
- Initiates a new formal section (recapitulation)
- Marks a "rebeginning" rather than a cadential close
- Functions differently from cadential dominant-tonic motion

## Beethoven's Effect

When recapitulation opens dramatically (apotheosis effect), the impression of structural goal may reinforce misinterpretation of cadential motion.
