# Contrasting Middle + Further Dominant Prolongation (Small Binary)

In Part 2 of a small binary, the dominant harmony ending the contrasting middle may be **prolonged** into the subsequent phrase before resolving.

## Structure

- 4-measure **contrasting middle** → dominant (HC or arrival)
- 4-measure phrase: V prolonged... → deceptive (VI) → PAC

## Dominant Extension

The goal dominant of the B section:
- Is NOT resolved to tonic immediately
- Is sustained into the following phrase
- Often resolves **deceptively to VI**
- Then concludes with PAC

## Not a Simple Consequent

Because the final phrase:
- Does not begin with tonic support
- Prolongs dominant at its opening
- It cannot be straightforwardly labeled as consequent

## Deceptive Resolution

A common pattern:
- Dominant prolonged through mm. 13-14
- Deceptive resolution to VI (m. 14, beat 3 or m. 15)
- Final cadential motion → PAC (m. 16)

**Note**: This is NOT a deceptive cadence; it is simply a deceptive resolution within the phrase.

## Unique Character

This pattern creates a distinctive phrase that defies conventional labeling while achieving necessary closure.
