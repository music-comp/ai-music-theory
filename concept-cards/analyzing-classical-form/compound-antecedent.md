# Compound Antecedent

A **compound antecedent** is the opening 8-measure unit of a compound period, structured as a complete simple theme that ends with a weak cadence.

## Structure Options

The compound antecedent can be built as:

1. **Sentence** (presentation + continuation)
2. **Hybrid** (compound basic idea + continuation)
3. **Hybrid** (antecedent + continuation)

## Cadential Requirements

The compound antecedent closes with:
- Usually a **half cadence** (regular or reinterpreted)
- Rarely an **imperfect authentic cadence**

## Preserving Return Effect

The basic idea should NOT return prematurely at m. 5 because:
- The return must sound fresh at m. 9
- Premature return spoils the consequent effect
- For this reason, compound antecedents rarely end with simple consequent phrases

## Sentence-Type Antecedent

When built as a sentence:
- The restatement of basic idea in mm. 3-4 is a **repeat**
- This does NOT anticipate the **return** at m. 9
- The repeat and return have different formal meanings

## Reinterpreted Half Cadence

When the compound antecedent modulates to the dominant region and closes with a PAC there, the return to home key at m. 9 retrospectively reinterprets that PAC as a kind of half cadence.
