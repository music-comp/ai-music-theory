# Introduction (Thematic)

A thematic **introduction** is a before-the-beginning framing function that precedes and prepares the onset of a theme.

## Function

The introduction:
- Prepares the listener for the theme's arrival
- May establish tempo, key, texture, or character
- Creates anticipation for the main material

## Types

### Brief Introduction
- Just a few beats or measures
- Often a simple gesture (upbeat figure, chord)
- Prepares immediate theme onset

### Extended Introduction
- Multiple measures
- May have its own internal structure
- Creates more elaborate preparation

## Content

Introductions may contain:
- Accompanimental figuration
- Simple harmonic gestures (often dominant preparation)
- Melodic fragments that may relate to the theme
- Textural establishment

## Distinction from Theme

The introduction is:
- External to the theme proper
- Not counted in the theme's measure structure
- Preparatory rather than substantive

## Not Required

Introductions are optional:
- Many themes begin directly without introduction
- When present, they add formal interest
- Their presence affects the theme's character
