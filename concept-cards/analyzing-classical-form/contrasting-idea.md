# Contrasting Idea

The **contrasting idea** is the 2-measure unit that follows the basic idea in an antecedent phrase, leading to a weak cadence.

## What Constitutes Contrast?

"Contrasting" means "not a repetition." The contrast may be:
- **Striking** - entirely different melodic-motivic content
- **Minimal** - subtle differences in contour or directionality

At all times, the second idea must be sufficiently distinct from the first such that we do not perceive it as a repetition.

## Means of Contrast

The contrasting idea achieves contrast primarily through:
- **Melodic-motivic content** - different motives from the basic idea
- **Melodic contour/directionality** - opposite direction of motion
- **Secondary features** - texture, dynamics, articulation

## Harmonic Distinction

The contrasting idea must close with a **cadential progression**:
- Basic idea: Supported by tonic prolongation
- Contrasting idea: Ends with cadential progression (usually HC)

## Continuational Characteristics

A contrasting idea often contains characteristics of continuation function:
- Fragmentation
- Accelerated harmonic or surface rhythm
- Hint of sequential harmonies

## Cadential Idea

If the contrasting idea is entirely supported by a cadential progression, it effectively resembles a "cadential idea" like that ending a sentence.

## Key Point

When the contrasting idea strongly resembles the basic idea, the **return** of the basic idea at m. 5 makes clear that m. 3-4 functions as contrasting.
