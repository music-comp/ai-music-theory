## Declined PAC

A **declined PAC** occurs when a passage seems to be heading for a perfect authentic cadence but ends with a half cadence instead.

### Context in Transitions

When a transition opens with a presentation-like structure:
- Expectation: consequent phrase leading to PAC
- Reality: phrase ends with HC instead
- The "expected" PAC is "declined"

### Why Decline the PAC?

If the transition achieved a PAC:
- It would create another closed theme
- This would be heard as a second main theme
- Not a transitional function

The declined PAC is therefore intentional and necessary for transition function.

### "Failed" Consequent

The phrase following a presentation-like opening:
- Has characteristics of a consequent phrase
- But "fails" to achieve the expected PAC
- Ends instead with HC
- This "failure" is deliberate formal strategy

### Result

The resulting structure is **nonconventional** and appropriately loose for a transition - neither a true sentence nor a true period.
