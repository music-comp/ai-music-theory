# Recapitulation (A' Section) - Small Ternary

The **recapitulation** (A' section) brings back the fundamental melodic-motivic material of the exposition entirely in the context of the home key.

## Two Main Requirements

The recapitulation must:
1. Begin by restating the initial **basic idea** from the A section
2. Close with a **PAC in the home key**

## Tonal Adjustment

If the A section modulated, the A' section must be **adjusted** to remain in the home key:
- Material that appeared in subordinate key now appears in home key
- Harmonies are modified to avoid modulation

## Structural Options

The A' section may be:
- **Identical** to A (when A is nonmodulating)
- **Compressed** into a single consequent phrase (most common)
- **Extended** to develop motives from prior sections

## Compressed Recapitulation

Compression typically eliminates:
- Redundant HC (dominant already emphasized in B)
- Redundant return of basic idea

Result: A single 4-measure consequent phrase.

## Ornamental vs. Structural Changes

- **Ornamental**: Changes in melody, texture, dynamics, instrumentation
- **Structural**: Changes in harmony, grouping, formal functions

Both may occur, but structural changes alter the fundamental organization.
