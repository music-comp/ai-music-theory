# Part 1 - Small Binary

**Part 1** of a small binary is typically an 8-measure unit built like an A section, but with more cadential flexibility.

## Structure

Part 1 is most often built as:
- Simple 8-measure **sentence**
- Simple 8-measure **period**
- Simple 8-measure **hybrid**

## Cadential Options

Unlike A sections (which require PAC), Part 1 may close with:
- **PAC** (like an A section)
- **HC** (unlike an A section)

## Ending with Half Cadence

When Part 1 ends with HC:
- The phrase structure may be unusual
- A "failed consequent" may result

## Failed Consequent

If Part 1 is built as a period but closes with HC instead of PAC:
- The second phrase resembles a **consequent** (returns basic idea)
- But the weak cadence means it "fails" to achieve consequent function
- Label: "compound basic idea + failed consequent"

## Nonconventional Forms

Part 1 may also take nonconventional forms, including:
- Basic idea + failed consequent
- Antecedent + failed consequent

## Indistinguishable from A Section

In many cases, Part 1 is indistinguishable from an A section until we hear Part 2 (lack of recapitulation reveals small binary).
