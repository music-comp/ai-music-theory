## Recapitulation Main Theme

The **main theme in the recapitulation** most often returns without any significant structural alterations.

### Ornamental Changes

Ornamental changes of some kind are likely to occur, especially in chamber and orchestral genres. These may include:
- More emphatic rhythmical expression
- Greater dynamic intensity
- Different accompanimental placement

### Structural Change: Closure

The most significant structural change involves the main theme's closure:

**In the exposition**: The main theme is required to end with a cadence so the home key can be confirmed prior to being abandoned for the subordinate key.

**In the recapitulation**: The home key will necessarily become confirmed in the course of the subordinate theme, so a cadence at the end of the main theme is no longer required and may sometimes be omitted.
