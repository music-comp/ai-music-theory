## Different Origins

Though related through use of same basic formal functions, classical rondo and classical sonata derive from **completely different sources** in the Baroque era.

## Rondo Origin: French Rondeau

- Originates largely in **France**
- Instrumental genre called *rondeau*
- Features alternation of:
  - Refrains closing with authentic cadences in home key
  - Contrasting couplets

## Sonata Origin: Italian Binary Dance

- Originates mostly in **Italy**
- Develops out of Baroque **binary dance movements**
- Each part of binary is repeated
- First part: closes in related key (or on dominant of home key)
- Second part: closes with PAC in home key

## Key Structural Difference

| Rondo | Sonata |
|-------|--------|
| No binary repetition scheme | Binary repetition required |
| Does not repeat expositional material | Exposition must be repeated |
| Refrains close with PAC in home key | First part closes away from home key |

## Quick Identification Tip

To determine if a movement is sonata or rondo:
- Look for **internal repeat sign** dividing movement into two large blocks
- **Sign present**: Probably sonata form
- **Sign absent**: Likely a rondo
