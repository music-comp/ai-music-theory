# Quick Definition
The rondo originates from the French rondeau (alternating refrains and couplets); the sonata originates from Italian binary dance movements. A quick identification method: look for internal repeat signs -- present suggests sonata, absent suggests rondo.

# Core Definition
The classical rondo originates largely in France from an instrumental genre (rondeau) featuring alternation of refrains closing with authentic cadences and contrasting couplets. The sonata originates (mostly in Italy) from Baroque binary dance movements, each part repeated. The rondo forms have no repetition requirements, while the sonata's exposition is normally required to be repeated (Caplin, p. 667).
