## Expanded Periodic Design

Antecedent and consequent functions occur less frequently in subordinate themes than sentential functions. When periodic functions appear, the consequent is often **expanded** to create looser organization.

### Typical Structure

**Antecedent**: Retains conventional size of 4 or 8 measures

**Consequent**: Expanded through various means:
- Expanded cadential progression (ECP)
- Extended continuation section
- Additional cadential evasions

### Tight-Knit Theme Within Group

One theme of a subordinate-theme group may take the form of a **tight-knit period**:
- Less common than sentential structures
- Creates contrast with looser themes in the group

### Relationship to Main Theme

When subordinate theme material derives from main theme's contrasting idea:
- Pitches and rhythm may be clearly related
- New tonal context changes scale-degree functions
- May obscure direct connection between ideas
