## Definition

**Minore**: A passage, variation, or interior theme set in the minor mode of a major-mode home key.

**Maggiore**: A passage, variation, or interior theme set in the major mode of a minor-mode home key.

## Uses in Classical Form

### In Large Ternary
- The interior theme most commonly shifts to minore
- Provides striking modal contrast with the major-mode main theme
- The Italian expression is often indicated by the composer in the score

### In Theme and Variations
- One variation in a set is typically written in the opposite mode
- Minore variations often change the original harmonic-tonal organization
- May alter internal phrase functions while retaining overall form
- Can take on a loosely organized character resembling an interior theme

## Harmonic Considerations in Minore

When shifting to minor mode:
- Some harmonic progressions may become nonsyntactical and require rewriting
- The modal shift provides opportunity for exploring different tonal realms
- The formal structure (small ternary or binary) may change

## Character

- Creates dramatic contrast within a movement
- Often associated with emotional intensification
- The *Sturm und Drang* affect frequently appears in minore passages
