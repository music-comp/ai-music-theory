# Small Binary Without Contrasting Middle

Part 2 of a small binary may lack a clearly discernible contrasting middle, taking instead a loose sentential or purely continuational form.

## Possible Structures

Without contrasting middle, Part 2 may be:
- A loose **sentence** (with destabilized presentation)
- Exclusively **continuation** material
- Exclusively **cadential** material

## Sentence-like Structure

When organized like a sentence:
- Initial 4 measures may use model-sequence technique
- Lack of tonic prolongation prevents true presentation
- Overall sentential shape emerges despite harmonic looseness

## Suggesting B-Section Function

Even without complete contrasting middle:
- Opening may emphasize dominant harmony
- Model-sequence technique may appear immediately
- These suggest B-section characteristics without full realization

## Continuation Function Throughout

Part 2 may function as a large continuation:
- Similar to compound sentence's continuation
- 8 measures of continuational/cadential material
- Provides contrast through looseness rather than distinct B section

## Ending Requirement

Regardless of structure, Part 2 must close with **PAC in home key**.
