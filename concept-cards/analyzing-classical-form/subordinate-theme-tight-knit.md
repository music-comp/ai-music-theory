# Quick Definition
In many subordinate-theme groups, each individual theme is relatively loose, but different loosening devices are distributed among the themes. Frequently, one theme in the group is significantly more tightly knit, creating formal contrast -- it may appear in first position (especially Mozart), middle position, or final position (imparting a distinctly closing quality).

# Core Definition
In many subordinate-theme groups, each individual theme is relatively loose, though different loosening devices are usually distributed among the themes: one theme may feature a series of cadential evasions, another a prominent ECP, one an extended continuation with model-sequence, another a compressed continuation. Frequently, one of the themes in a group is significantly more tightly knit. This tight-knit theme can occupy various positions: in first position (especially common in Mozart), the tight-knit theme opens the group and following themes effect looser organization; in middle position, the tight-knit theme stands between two looser themes, creating formal contrast; in final position, a tight-knit final theme imparts a distinctly "closing" quality to that theme (Caplin, Ch. 12, pp. 405-408).

1. Loosening devices typically distributed among multiple themes

# Construction / Recognition
## To Identify:
1. Analyze each subordinate theme for its degree of looseness
2. Identify which loosening devices are used in each theme
3. Look for one theme that is notably more tight-knit
4. Note the position of the tight-knit theme within the group
5. Consider how the distribution of loosening creates overall formal variety

# Context & Application
The distribution of tight-knit and loose organization within a subordinate-theme group is a key compositional strategy. Uniform looseness would create monotony; the presence of a tight-knit theme provides a structural anchor within the group. The position of this tight-knit theme has expressive implications: at the beginning it establishes stability before subsequent loosening; in the middle it provides respite; at the end it provides closure.
