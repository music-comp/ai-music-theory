## Tight-Knit vs. Loose in Subordinate-Theme Group

In many subordinate-theme groups, each individual theme is relatively loose, though different loosening devices are usually distributed among the themes.

### Distribution of Loosening Devices

Examples of how loosening can be distributed:
- One theme: series of cadential evasions
- Another theme: prominent expanded cadential progression
- One theme: extended continuation with model-sequence
- Another theme: compressed continuation

### Tight-Knit Theme Within the Group

Frequently, one of the themes in a group is **significantly more tightly knit**. This can occur in various positions:

**First position** (especially Mozart):
- Tight-knit theme opens the group
- Following themes effect looser organization

**Middle position**:
- Tight-knit theme stands between two looser themes
- Creates formal contrast within the group

**Final position**:
- Tight-knit final theme
- Imparts distinctly "closing" quality to that theme
- (Distinction between closing "theme" and closing "section" is addressed separately)
