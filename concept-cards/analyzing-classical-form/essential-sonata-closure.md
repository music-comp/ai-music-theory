# Essential Sonata Closure

The crucial cadential moment in the recapitulation that resolves the fundamental tonal conflict.

## Definition

The PAC that closes the subordinate theme in the recapitulation, confirming the home key and resolving the tonal opposition established in the exposition.

## Significance

This moment represents:
- Resolution of subordinate key material into home key
- Completion of the sonata's fundamental tonal narrative
- The most powerful cadential emphasis in the entire form

## Comparison to Exposition

In exposition, PAC confirms subordinate key (the "rival" tonality).
In recapitulation, PAC confirms home key (resolution of rivalry).

## Hepokoski and Darcy's Term

The concept is central to theories emphasizing teleological structure of sonata form - the movement's entire trajectory aims toward this cadential goal.

## Cadential Expansion

The cadential area of the subordinate theme may undergo greater expansion in recapitulation than exposition, ensuring the most rhetorically powerful cadential emphasis confirms the home key.
