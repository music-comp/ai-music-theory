## Home Key (HK)

The **home key** is the primary tonal center of a sonata-form movement, establishing the fundamental point of reference for all tonal activity.

### Role in Each Section

**Exposition**:
- Main theme begins and ends in the home key
- Confirmed by a cadence (usually PAC, sometimes HC, rarely IAC)
- Establishes stability before the tonal conflict begins

**Development**:
- Typically moves away from home key
- Restored toward the end, usually confirmed with an HC or dominant arrival

**Recapitulation**:
- Begins in the home key
- Remains there throughout
- Receives final confirmation with a PAC
