## Eight-Measure Nonconventional Themes

Some main themes are organized in a nonconventional manner but still occupy **eight measures**, like the simple sentence, period, or hybrid.

### Tight-Knit Despite Irregularity

Because the nonconventional theme retains the standard 8-measure length, it maintains a distinct sense of **tight-knit organization** despite its formal irregularities.

### Asymmetrical Subdivision

Most often, the grouping structure of the eight measures is **asymmetrical**, yet the internal units nonetheless manifest a definite expression of phrase functionality.

### Example Structures

Possible asymmetrical groupings within 8 measures:
- 2 + 3 + 3
- 3 + 3 + 2
- 3 + 2 + 3

### Functional Expression

Even with irregular grouping, the internal units typically express:
- Initiating function (though perhaps not a clear basic idea or presentation)
- Continuational characteristics
- Cadential function for closure
