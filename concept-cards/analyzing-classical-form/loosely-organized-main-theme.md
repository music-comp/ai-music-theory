## Loosely Organized Main Theme

Some main themes are constructed in a manner considerably **less tightly knit** than would ordinarily be expected.

### Loosening Techniques

At times, the loosening techniques resemble those associated with a subordinate theme:
- Redundant repetitions
- Cadential extensions and expansions
- Internal dominant arrivals

### Still Tighter Than Subordinate Theme

Although the theme's form-functional characteristics may be like those of a subordinate theme:
- The melodic, rhythmic, and textural aspects are usually typical of a main theme
- "Opening up" gestures and marked discontinuities of rhythm and texture
- **As a rule**, a loosely organized main theme is still more tightly knit than the subordinate theme appearing later in the exposition

### Comparative Analysis

When encountering a loosely organized main theme, compare it with the subsequent subordinate theme to confirm that:
- The subordinate theme is even looser
- The main theme maintains its relative tight-knit position
