# Abandoned Cadence

An **abandoned cadence** occurs when the cadential process simply stops before reaching any kind of harmonic goal - the progression is left incomplete.

## Definition

The cadential progression:
- Begins normally
- Does NOT reach any harmonic goal
- Is simply "abandoned" mid-process

## Distinction from Other Deviations

- **Deceptive**: Dominant resolves to wrong harmony
- **Evaded**: Cadence replaced by new beginning
- **Abandoned**: Cadence simply stops without resolution

## Characteristics

An abandoned cadence:
- Leaves the dominant unresolved
- Creates maximum suspense
- Often followed by silence or dramatic pause
- May lead to completely new material

## Effect

The abandonment creates:
- Strong sense of incompleteness
- Dramatic tension
- Expectation for eventual resolution
- Need for new cadential attempt

## Common Contexts

Abandoned cadences occur:
- In dramatic passages
- Before unexpected turns
- When maximum suspense is desired
- At moments of formal ambiguity
