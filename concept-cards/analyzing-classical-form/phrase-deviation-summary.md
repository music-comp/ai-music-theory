# Phrase Deviation Summary

Phrase deviations alter normative phrase length. Four main types exist, distinguished by position and effect.

## Types Comparison

| Deviation | Position | Effect | Typical Result |
|-----------|----------|--------|----------------|
| Extension | End of phrase | Adds material | 4m → 5-6m |
| Compression | Within phrase | Removes material | 4m → 3m |
| Internal Expansion | Middle of phrase | Inserts material | 4m → 5-6m |
| Interpolation | Middle of phrase | Disrupts process | Varies |

## Extension

- Material added AFTER phrase completion
- Phrase continues beyond expected end
- Does not disrupt formal continuity

## Compression

- Material removed from phrase
- Arrives at goal earlier than expected
- Creates tighter construction

## Internal Expansion

- Material inserted BEFORE phrase completion
- Phrase is "stretched" from within
- Does not disrupt formal continuity

## Interpolation

- Material inserted that DISRUPTS process
- Parenthetical effect
- Foreign to ongoing function

## Theme-Level Effects

Individual phrase deviations combine to create:
- **Expansion**: Theme longer than normative
- **Compression may balance extension**: Net effect may be minimal
