# Consequent vs. Continuation Ambiguity

The opening of a theme's second phrase can give rise to functional ambiguity when what seems like a return of the basic idea (consequent) is modified in ways suggesting a continuation.

## The Problem

Material in mm. 5-6 may:
- **Suggest consequent**: Same melodic pitches as basic idea
- **Suggest continuation**: Different harmonic support, sequential quality, fragmentation

## Consequent Indicators

- Melody identical or similar to opening basic idea
- Tonic harmonic support (or dominant in statement-response pattern)
- Same grouping structure as antecedent

## Continuation Indicators

- Different harmonic support (creating sequential effect)
- Parallel voice-leading patterns suggesting sequence
- Fragmentation into smaller units
- Modulating harmonic progression

## Analysis Approach

When ambiguity exists:
1. Consider the **melodic** content (favoring consequent?)
2. Consider the **harmonic** context (favoring continuation?)
3. Consider the **grouping structure** (fragmented or not?)

## Both Views Valid

A degree of ambiguity may arise where:
- Interpreting as consequent focuses on melodic identity
- Interpreting as continuation focuses on harmonic destabilization

Both views can be legitimately supported by focusing on selected details.
