# Beginning the Pre-core

The opening of the pre-core follows certain conventional patterns for melodic-motivic material.

## Main Theme's Basic Idea

- Creates effective variation on original link between exposition end and main theme
- Repetition of exposition creates this link; pre-core reforges it in different tonal context
- Common in all three major classical composers

## Closing Section Material

- Promotes continuity of motive, rhythm, and texture from exposition end
- Creates effective link between exposition and development
- Material may be metrically displaced or otherwise varied

## New Material

- Material not obviously related to anything earlier in movement
- Mozart particularly favors this approach
- Rarely used by Haydn and Beethoven
- Though seemingly new, may derive from earlier ideas through motivic transformation

## Unusual Beginnings

- Transition or subordinate-theme material rarely appears at the very beginning
- Such material typically appears later within the pre-core or core
