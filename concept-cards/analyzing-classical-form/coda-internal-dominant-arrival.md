# Internal Dominant Arrival in Coda

A dominant arrival occurring within the coda theme before the final PAC.

## Function

Creates structure similar to subordinate theme's internal articulation:
- Premature dominant arrival
- Standing on the dominant
- New continuation following the standing

## Analytical Recognition

When the music following dominant arrival brings clear continuation function (leading eventually to expanded cadential progression and final PAC), we recognize:
- The coda theme is structured less like a core
- More like a subordinate theme with internal dominant arrival

## Relationship to Core

Model-sequence passages at beginning of coda may suggest core, but:
- Harmonies remain closely allied with home key (unlike true development core)
- Internal dominant arrival and subsequent continuation clarify subordinate-theme-like organization
