# Compound Sentence in Slow Introduction

When a slow introduction takes on a compound sentential organization.

## The Structure

Introduction may be organized as compound sentence:
- Compound presentation (compound basic ideas)
- Continuation with fragmentation
- Half cadence in related key
- Standing on dominant

## Problematic Features

The structure may be problematic in some respects:
- Lack of tonic prolongation at end of compound presentation
- Deviations from expected continuational patterns
- Modal inflections creating ambiguity

## Ending

Such an introduction literally ends on its final sonority (often not home-key dominant), which is then understood as:
- Subdominant in home key
- Or other pre-dominant function
- Leading directly into main theme's opening harmony

## Anticipatory Techniques

The end of such an introduction may anticipate:
- Opening harmony of main theme
- Opening melody of main theme
- Creating smooth transition while maintaining distinct identity
