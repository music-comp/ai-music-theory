# Expansion of Theme

**Expansion** (without the qualifier "internal") refers to the overall lengthening of an entire theme beyond its normative size.

## Definition

When one or more phrases undergo extension or internal expansion, the entire theme becomes **expanded** beyond its normative length.

## Theme-Level vs. Phrase-Level

- **Phrase extension/internal expansion**: Affects individual phrases
- **Theme expansion**: The cumulative result affecting total theme length

## Calculating Expansion

A theme's expansion can be measured:
- Normative 8-measure sentence becomes 10 measures = 2 measures of expansion
- Individual phrase deviations combine to create total expansion

## Compensation

Sometimes compression in one phrase compensates for extension in another:
- Extended continuation (6 m.) + compressed something else
- Net expansion may be less than the extension alone

## Importance

Understanding theme expansion helps analysts:
- See past surface irregularities to underlying structure
- Identify how composers manipulate normative forms
- Recognize the relationship between model and realization

## Notation

Use "exp." to indicate expansion at the theme level in analytical diagrams.
