# Quick Definition
Theme expansion refers to the overall lengthening of an entire theme beyond its normative size (e.g., 8 measures) as the cumulative result of individual phrase extensions and/or internal expansions within its constituent phrases.

# Core Definition
When one or more phrases undergo extension or internal expansion, the entire theme becomes expanded beyond its normative length. Sometimes compression in one phrase compensates for extension in another, resulting in less net expansion. The relation between extension and expansion can be complicated: an "extended" continuation may result in an overall "expansion" of the consequent.

- **Internal expansion** — Another phrase-level mechanism

# Key Properties
1. Theme-level concept (cumulative result of phrase-level deviations)
2. Compression may partially compensate for extension
3. Notation: "exp." in analytical diagrams
4. The relation between extension and expansion can be complicated to describe
