# Reversed Recapitulation

When the order of main and subordinate theme material is switched in the recapitulation.

## Definition

Also called "inverted recapitulation" - when subordinate-theme ideas come before main-theme ones.

## Theoretical Problems

The notion is useful descriptively but suffers from theoretical deficiencies:
- Questions how logical it is simply to shift syntactical units
- Melodic-motivic material may be reordered
- But passage containing main-theme material does not **function** as main theme

## The Key Distinction

The passage refashioned from main-theme material:
- Functions as a subordinate theme
- Exhibits loosening devices associated with subordinate-theme function
- Does not retain main-theme function

## Underlying Principle

We must distinguish between:
- The musical content
- The formal function of that content

Terminology sometimes confuses this distinction.
