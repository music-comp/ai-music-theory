# Compound Presentation

A **compound presentation** is the opening 8-measure unit of a compound sentence, consisting of a 4-measure compound basic idea and its repetition.

## Structure

- 4-measure **compound basic idea** (statement)
- 4-measure **repetition** of the compound basic idea (response)

## Harmonic Support

The entire compound presentation is supported by:
- **Tonic prolongational** harmony
- No cadence at the end
- Creates stable harmonic foundation

## Types of Repetition

The compound basic idea can be repeated as:
1. **Statement-response** (I → V, V → I) - most common
2. **Exact repetition** - less frequent
3. **Sequential repetition** - rare

## Statement-Response Pattern

When statement-response repetition is used:
- The sense of antecedent-consequent might arise
- But there is no distinct cadential progression
- The entire situation remains exclusively prolongational
- The unit is an open-ended presentation, not a closed period

## Continuation Expectations

The open-ended quality of the compound presentation sets up strong expectations for an 8-measure continuation phrase to follow.

## Fragmentation in Continuation

Because the compound presentation consists of two 4-measure phrases, the first stage of fragmentation in the continuation usually results in 2-measure ideas.
