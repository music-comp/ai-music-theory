## Resumption After Internal HC

Following an internal HC, the subordinate theme can proceed by **resuming continuation or cadential function** rather than establishing a new initiating function.

### Why This Works

The idea of a half cadence "internal" to a single subordinate theme is well expressed when:
- The cadential dominant is followed by further **continuation**
- In the absence of any new initiating idea
- There is little sense that another theme is beginning

### Continuation Resumption

After internal HC and standing on the dominant:
- New material has character of **continuation** (not initiation)
- Short groups, active rhythms, harmonic instability
- Sequential activity may further express continuation
- Eventually leads to cadential function for PAC closure

### Cadential Resumption

In some cases, internal HC is followed directly by **cadential function**:
- Bypasses any new continuation
- May begin with embellished I6
- Leads to new ECP bringing the theme to PAC closure
