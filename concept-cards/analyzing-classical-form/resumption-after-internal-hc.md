# Quick Definition
Following an internal HC and standing on the dominant, the subordinate theme can resume with continuation function (short groups, active rhythms, sequential activity) or cadential function (beginning with I6, leading to ECP), rather than establishing a new initiating function. The absence of a new basic idea reinforces the interpretation that the HC is internal to a single subordinate theme.

# Core Definition
Following an internal HC (and typically a standing on the dominant), the subordinate theme proceeds by resuming continuation or cadential function rather than establishing a new initiating function. In continuation resumption, the material following the standing on the dominant has the character of continuation (short groups, active rhythms, harmonic instability, sequential activity) rather than initiation, and eventually leads to cadential function for PAC closure. In cadential resumption, the internal HC is followed directly by cadential function, bypassing any new continuation; this may begin with embellished I6 and lead to a new ECP bringing the theme to PAC closure. The idea that the HC is "internal" to a single subordinate theme is well expressed when the cadential dominant is followed by further continuation in the absence of any new initiating idea -- there is little sense that another theme is beginning (Caplin, Ch. 12, pp. 399-401).

# Prerequisites
- **Internal half cadence** -- The HC that precedes the resumption
- **Standing on the dominant** -- Typically intervenes between HC and resumption

# Key Properties
1. Two types: continuation resumption and cadential resumption
2. Continuation resumption: short groups, active rhythms, sequential activity
3. Cadential resumption: begins with I6, leads to ECP
4. No new initiating function (no new basic idea)
5. Absence of initiation reinforces "internal" interpretation
6. Both types lead eventually to PAC closure

# Construction / Recognition
## To Identify:
1. Locate an internal HC followed by standing on the dominant
2. Check what follows the standing: does it bring a new basic idea (new theme) or continuation/cadential material (resumption)?
3. Continuation resumption: look for short groups, sequences, harmonic acceleration
4. Cadential resumption: look for I6 and ECP
5. The absence of a new initiating function confirms the HC as internal

# Context & Application
The two strategies after an internal HC have different implications for the subordinate theme's structure. Continuation resumption creates a single, large subordinate theme with an internal HC dividing it. Cadential resumption more directly drives the theme to its required PAC. In either case, the internal HC represents a moment of partial key confirmation within the ongoing subordinate-theme process.

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Interpreting the material after the internal HC as a new subordinate theme
