# Continuation Becomes Cadential (Continuation⇒Cadential)

**Continuation⇒cadential** describes a phrase that combines continuation and cadential functions, where cadential function is particularly prominent due to an expanded cadential progression.

## The Symbol ⇒

The symbol ⇒ stands for "becomes" and denotes **retrospective reinterpretation**. "Continuation⇒cadential" means "continuation becomes cadential."

## When It Occurs

This designation is used when a single cadential progression begins in measure 5 of the theme and stretches to support the entire 4-measure phrase. This **expanded cadential progression** (ECP) brings more emphatic expression of cadential function than usual.

## Retrospective Reinterpretation

What we initially expect to be a continuation phrase (following a presentation) is understood **retrospectively** to be a cadential phrase based on an ECP, while still containing continuational characteristics.

## Form-Functional Fusion

Even though cadential function is present from the start of the phrase, characteristics of continuation function are usually found as well:
- Fragmentation
- Harmonic acceleration
- Sequential elements

Both interpretations are valid for their temporal context.

## Analytical Notation

When the abbreviation ECP is placed after the first harmony of the cadential progression, it is unnecessary to use horizontal brackets for the rest of the progression.

## Key Point

The term captures the dynamic process of listening, whereby we change our interpretation of function in light of hearing new musical material.
