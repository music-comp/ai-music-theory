# Slow Introduction vs. Thematic Introduction

Distinguishing large-scale slow introductions from smaller thematic introductions.

## Thematic Introduction

- Resides on hierarchical level comparable to basic idea, contrasting idea, etc.
- Short unit
- Normally supported by tonic prolongation
- Generally has no melodic profile
- Precedes a theme

## Slow Introduction

- Resides on level comparable to exposition, development, recapitulation, coda
- Normally consists of one or more thematic units
- Contains prominent melodic-motivic material
- Supported by variety of harmonic progressions
- Precedes entire sonata movement

## Key Distinction

Both express temporal function of "before-the-beginning" but differ in:
- Location in structural hierarchy
- Complexity of formal organization
- Scope and scale

Similar to distinction between coda and codetta.
