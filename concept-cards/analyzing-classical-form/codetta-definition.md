## Codetta Definition and Structure

A **codetta** is a postcadential unit that follows a PAC, prolonging the achieved tonic and reinforcing the sense of cadential arrival.

### Characteristics

- Prolongs **tonic harmony** achieved by the preceding cadence
- Usually features a **tonic pedal**
- Melody often descends from tonic scale degree
- Generally **recessive** dynamic character
- Contains conventionalized material (scales, arpeggios)

### Structure Within Closing Section

The closing section typically contains several codettas:
1. **First codetta**: Often 2-4 measures, establishes pattern
2. **Repetition**: First codetta is typically repeated
3. **Second codetta**: Usually shorter than first (fragmentation)
4. **Further codettas**: Continue fragmentation process

### Melodic Content

The melodic content of codettas usually:
- **Contrasts** with cadential ideas closing the subordinate theme
- Consists of conventionalized scalar or arpeggiated patterns
- Occasionally features significant motivic references (main-theme ideas)

### Compression vs. Expansion

Thanks to fragmentation:
- General sense of **compression** is expressed in closing sections
- Expansion of ideas is **rare** (though exceptions exist)
