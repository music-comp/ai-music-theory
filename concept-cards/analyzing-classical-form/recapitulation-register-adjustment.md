# Register Adjustment in Recapitulation

How the melodic register is handled when transposing material to the home key.

## The Challenge

When the subordinate theme is transposed from subordinate key to home key:
- Simple transposition may place melody too high or too low
- Accompaniment figures may become awkward
- Overall texture may become unbalanced

## Typical Solutions

### Partial Transposition
- Melody transposed down a fifth initially
- Accompaniment moved up a fourth
- Later adjustments return to appropriate register

### Change of Direction
- At certain points, melody transposed up (instead of expected down)
- Prevents descent too low
- Maintains appropriate tessiture for instruments

## Ornamental vs. Structural

Register adjustment is typically an ornamental change (not affecting underlying structure) though it may be combined with other alterations.
