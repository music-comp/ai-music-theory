# Sturm und Drang

German expression meaning "storm and stress," describing the dramatic character typical of development cores.

## In Development Cores

The core typically projects an emotional quality of:
- Instability
- Restlessness
- Dramatic conflict

## Musical Expression

Sturm und Drang character is achieved through:
- Forte dynamic level
- Marked increase in rhythmic activity
- "Chugging" accompanimental patterns (Alberti bass, murky bass)
- Polyphonic complexity (imitation, canon, fugal entries)
- Minor modality and chromaticism
- Sudden dynamic contrasts

## Contrast with Pre-core

If the core often bursts out with Sturm und Drang, the pre-core can be likened to the calm before the storm - more relaxed, hesitant, anticipatory, with softer dynamics.

## Historical Context

The term derives from a late 18th-century German literary movement emphasizing emotional extremes and individual subjectivity, which influenced musical aesthetics of the period.
