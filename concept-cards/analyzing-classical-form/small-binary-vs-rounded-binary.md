# Small Binary vs. Rounded Binary Comparison

Understanding the differences between small binary and rounded binary is essential for correct formal identification.

## Summary Table

| Feature | Rounded Binary | Small Binary |
|---------|---------------|--------------|
| Recapitulation | Always | Never |
| Contrasting middle | Always | Sometimes |
| Part 1 ending | PAC only | PAC or HC |
| Grouping patterns | Variable | Usually 8 + 8 |
| Part 2 opening | New material | Often relates to Part 1 |

## The Essential Difference

**Rounded binary**: Basic idea returns (recapitulation)
**Small binary**: Basic idea does NOT return

## Contrasting Middle

- **Rounded binary**: Always has distinct B section
- **Small binary**: May or may not have B section

## Part 1 Cadence

- **Rounded binary** (A section): Must end with PAC
- **Small binary** (Part 1): May end with PAC or HC

## Motivic Relationships

- **Rounded binary**: B section usually begins with NEW material
- **Small binary**: Part 2 often begins with material RELATED to Part 1

This difference in motivic approach helps dampen/create expectations for recapitulation.
