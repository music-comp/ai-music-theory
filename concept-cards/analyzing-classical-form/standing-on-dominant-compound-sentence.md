# Standing on the Dominant (Compound Sentence)

When a compound sentence ends with an HC, the arrival on dominant harmony may be reinforced by a postcadential **standing on the dominant**.

## Context

This formal unit:
- Follows the half cadence that ends the compound sentence
- Is analogous to a closing section following a PAC
- Is very rare in simple sentences

## Function

The standing on the dominant serves to:
- Reinforce the dynamic energy of the unstable HC ending
- Restore balance with the 8-measure presentation
- Create strong expectation for tonic resolution

## Contrast with Closing Section

Unlike a closing section (which follows PAC):
- Closing section: Recessive dynamic (dying away)
- Standing on dominant: Reinforces dynamic energy (instability)

## Structure

A typical standing on the dominant consists of:
- Repeated one-bar units
- Similar to codettas of a closing section
- Sustained or arpeggiated dominant harmony

## Effect on Theme Length

With a compressed 4-measure continuation + 4-measure standing on the dominant:
- Theme achieves 16-measure length
- Balance with presentation is restored
- Yet formal function is distinct (continuation + postcadential)
