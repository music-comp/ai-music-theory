# Hesitant Gestures in Slow Introduction

The uncertain, anticipatory quality expressed through specific musical gestures.

## Characteristics

Hesitant gestures create sense of uncertainty and anticipation through:
- Marked discontinuities in melody, rhythm, texture, and dynamics
- Instabilities of harmonic progression
- Minor modality
- Chromaticism (at times extreme)
- Fermatas prohibiting rhythmic continuity
- Lack of harmonic third (doubt about mode)
- "Limping" rhythmic patterns

## Relationship to Stately Character

The slow introduction unites hesitant gestures with stately, heraldic qualities:
- Slow tempo and fanfare gestures suggest ceremony
- Hesitant qualities suggest anticipation and uncertainty
- Both are essential to classical slow introduction character

## Difference from French Overture

The hesitant, premonitory gestures are generally lacking in Baroque French overture style, which emphasizes ceremonial aspects without uncertainty.

## Function

Creates sense that the true beginning (exposition) is being anticipated, setting up powerful effect when fast music begins.
