# Quick Definition
A structural overview of the small binary: Part 1 (sentence, period, or hybrid, closing with PAC or HC) + Part 2 (with or without contrasting middle, always closing with PAC in home key), totaling 16 measures in a symmetrical 8+8 layout.

# Core Definition
Part 1 options: sentence, period, hybrid (8 mm.), ending with PAC or HC. Part 2 with contrasting middle: B + continuation, B + cadential (ECP), B + new consequent, B + further dominant prolongation. Part 2 without contrasting middle: loose sentence, exclusively continuation/cadential material. Part 2 always ends with PAC in home key. The form is distinguished from rounded binary by the absence of recapitulation.

# Key Properties
1. Part 1: PAC or HC; Part 2: always PAC in home key
2. Part 2 may or may not have contrasting middle
3. Four patterns when B section is present
4. Usually symmetrical 8+8
5. Phrase deviations are infrequent
