# Small Binary Structure Overview

The small binary is a two-part form distinguished from rounded binary by the absence of recapitulation.

## Basic Structure

||: Part 1 :||: Part 2 :||

Typically 8 + 8 = 16 measures, symmetrical.

## Essential Difference from Rounded Binary

**Rounded Binary**: Returns basic idea (recapitulation)
**Small Binary**: Does NOT return basic idea

## Part 1 Options

Structure:
- Sentence, period, or hybrid
- 8 measures typically

Cadence:
- PAC (like A section) OR
- HC (unlike A section—distinguishing feature)

## Part 2 Options

**With Contrasting Middle**:
1. B + Continuation → PAC
2. B + Cadential (ECP) → PAC
3. B + New Consequent → PAC
4. B + Further V prolongation → VI → PAC

**Without Contrasting Middle**:
- Loose sentence structure
- Exclusively continuation/cadential material

## Distinguishing Features

| Feature | Small Binary | Rounded Binary |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| Recapitulation | No | Yes |
| Part 1 cadence | PAC or HC | PAC only |
| Part 2 opening | Often relates to Part 1 | New material |

## Usage

Preferred for variations and rondos where basic idea returns at new sections anyway.
