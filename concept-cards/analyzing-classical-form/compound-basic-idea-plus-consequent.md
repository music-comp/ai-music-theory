# Compound Basic Idea + Consequent (Hybrid Type 4)

**Compound basic idea + consequent** is an infrequently occurring hybrid type that strongly resembles the period. The only essential difference is that the contrasting idea in mm. 3-4 does not bring a weak cadence.

## Structure

- **First phrase (mm. 1-4)**: **Compound basic idea** (basic idea + contrasting idea, NO cadence)
- **Second phrase (mm. 5-8)**: **Consequent** (return of basic idea + contrasting idea → PAC)

## Resemblance to Period

This hybrid differs from a period only in m. 4:
- **Period**: Weak cadence closes antecedent
- **This hybrid**: No cadence; tonic prolongation continues

## Return of Basic Idea

Measures 5-6 bring back the opening basic idea in the manner of a consequent phrase. This return may be supported by different harmony (often dominant).

## Tonic Prolongation

More often than with the period, the return of the basic idea (mm. 5-6) is supported by different harmony. This may delay completion of the opening tonic prolongation until the consequent is under way.

A single tonic prolongation may underlie the first seven measures of the theme.

## Continuational Expression

The consequent phrase within this hybrid frequently accelerates the harmonic rhythm compared to the preceding compound basic idea, imparting a degree of continuational expression as well.
