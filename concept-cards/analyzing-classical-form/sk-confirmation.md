## Subordinate Key Confirmation

The **confirmation of the subordinate key** with a perfect authentic cadence is the essential harmonic-formal requirement of the subordinate theme.

### The Requirement

Every subordinate theme must close with a **PAC in the subordinate key**:
- This is non-negotiable from a form-functional perspective
- Any exception to this rule calls for special explanation
- The PAC fully confirms the subordinate key

### Partial vs. Full Confirmation

**Partial confirmation**: HC at end of modulating transition
- Establishes SK dominant as temporary goal
- Does not fully confirm the key

**Full confirmation**: PAC at end of subordinate theme
- Achieves complete cadential closure in SK
- Allows SK to temporarily "reign supreme" over HK

### Dramatization

The typical aesthetic goal is to:
- **Delay** the arrival of the anticipated PAC
- **Dramatize** the struggle to achieve cadential confirmation
- Make the eventual PAC a powerful moment of key confirmation
