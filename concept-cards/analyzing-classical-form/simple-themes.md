# Simple Themes

**Simple themes** are the fundamental 8-measure theme types: sentence, period, and hybrids. They contrast with compound themes, which are 16 measures.

## The Simple Theme Types

1. **Sentence** (presentation + continuation)
2. **Period** (antecedent + consequent)
3. **Hybrid 1** (antecedent + continuation)
4. **Hybrid 2** (antecedent + cadential)
5. **Hybrid 3** (compound basic idea + continuation)
6. **Hybrid 4** (compound basic idea + consequent)

## Normative Length

Simple themes are **defined** as 8 measures in length. However:
- Phrase deviations may alter actual size
- The notational device R = 2N may make themes appear as 16 measures
- Such cases still **sound** like 8-measure themes

## Distinction from Compound

- **Simple**: 8 real measures, simpler internal structure
- **Compound**: 16 real measures, more complex internal structure

## Building Blocks

Simple themes serve as:
- Fundamental units for larger forms
- Internal structure for compound themes
- Components of small ternary/binary forms

## Foundation of Classical Form

Understanding simple themes is essential because compound themes and larger forms are built from their functional components.
