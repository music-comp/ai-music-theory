# Presentation Function

**Presentation function** is an initiating formal function that creates a solid structural beginning for a theme by establishing its melodic-motivic content within a stable harmonic-tonal environment.

## Characteristics

Presentation function achieves its initiating quality through:
- **Initial statement** of the basic idea (setting forth fundamental material)
- **Immediate repetition** (fully "presenting" the material to the listener)
- **Tonic prolongation** (providing harmonic stability)

## Relation to Basic Idea

The basic idea is also an initiating formal function at a lower structural level. When repeated, the sense of formal initiation is made stronger. Presentation function, embracing two statements of the basic idea, **enhances** the overall sense of formal initiation.

## Temporal Meaning

Presentation function articulates a powerful sense of **beginning** the theme. It is one of the three temporal functions of the sentence:
- Presentation = Beginning
- Continuation = Middle
- Cadential = Ending

## Phrase vs. Function

The terms "presentation phrase" and "presentation function" refer to different phenomena:
- **Presentation phrase** = A term of grouping structure (a 4-measure unit)
- **Presentation function** = A term of formal function (expressing temporal initiation)

This distinction between grouping structure and formal function is central to understanding classical form.
