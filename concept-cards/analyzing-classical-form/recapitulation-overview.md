## Recapitulation Overview

The **recapitulation** resolves the tonal conflict of the movement by bringing back most of the material of the exposition firmly in the context of the home key.

### Signaling the Recapitulation

The start of the recapitulation is signaled by the return of the main theme's basic idea in the home key.

### Thematic Units

The basic thematic units return in order:
1. **Main theme** - usually returns without significant structural alterations
2. **Transition** - must be adjusted to remain in the home key
3. **Subordinate theme** - returns transposed into the home key

### Types of Changes

**Structural changes**: Required alterations in harmonic-tonal organization and phrase structure
- Transition must be adjusted to avoid modulation
- Subordinate theme must be transposed

**Ornamental changes**: Variations in instrumentation, dynamics, accompanimental patterning, texture, etc.

### Final Cadential Confirmation

Just as the subordinate theme in the exposition needs to confirm the subordinate key with a PAC, the subordinate theme in the recapitulation must provide perfect authentic closure in the home key.
