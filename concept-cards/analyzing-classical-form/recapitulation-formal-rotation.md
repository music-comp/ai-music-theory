# Formal Rotation in Recapitulation

Understanding the recapitulation as a formal rotation through exposition material.

## The Concept

By restating exposition material in roughly the same order, the recapitulation fulfills what Hepokoski and Darcy emphasize as a formal rotation.

## Conventional Labeling

It is conventional to label recapitulation thematic functions with same terms as exposition:
- Main theme
- Transition
- Subordinate theme (including closing section)

## Terminological Issue

This practice is rooted in traditional notion that formal units are defined primarily by musical content, not necessarily by formal function.

## Functional Differences

Yet the formal functions these units serve in recapitulation differ significantly from those in exposition. The names are the same, but the functions are different.

## Caplin's Approach

For sake of tradition, the conventional labeling is maintained while acknowledging the significantly different formal functions that these units serve.
