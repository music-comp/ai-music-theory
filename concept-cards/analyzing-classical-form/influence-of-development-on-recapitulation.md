# Influence of Development on Recapitulation

How the content and organization of the development section can affect recapitulation changes.

## Causal Connection?

To speak of development "influencing" recapitulation is not to suggest causal determination. The repertory abounds in counterexamples.

Rather, it is a question of **appropriateness of occurrence**:
- When we attend to certain events in development
- We may believe it fitting that some other event does (or does not) occur in recapitulation

## Types of Influence

### Compensatory Sequences
- If development lacks a core, recapitulation may add model-sequence passage
- Compensates for missing sequential development

### Deleted Material
- If an idea is highly exploited in development
- It becomes appropriate (not necessary) to delete it in recapitulation

### Transposed Material
- Material prominent in development may "need" recapitulation in home key
- If not possible in recapitulation proper, may appear in coda

## Analytical Insight

Observing these relationships deepens understanding of the movement as a unified whole.
