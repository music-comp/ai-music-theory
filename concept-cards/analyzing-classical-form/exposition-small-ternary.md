# Quick Definition
The A section is the most tightly knit section of the small ternary, usually built as a simple period, sentence, or hybrid theme, closing with a PAC (either in the home key or a subordinate key), and sometimes followed by a closing section.

# Core Definition
Most small ternary expositions are built as conventional theme types (period most common, sentence, hybrids). They usually occupy 8 measures but may be altered by phrase and cadential deviations. The A section may remain in the home key or modulate to a subordinate key (V in major, III or v in minor). Both options create the same formal type. Whether modulating or nonmodulating, the A section closes with a PAC. Occasionally the A section takes a nonconventional form. Since it closes with a PAC, a closing section may follow.

# Key Properties
1. Most tightly knit section of the small ternary
2. Usually a conventional theme type (period, sentence, hybrid)
3. Closes with PAC (home key or subordinate key)
4. May be nonmodulating or modulating
5. Both options are the same formal type
6. May be followed by a closing section
7. Occasionally nonconventional in form
