# Exposition (A Section) - Small Ternary

The **exposition** (A section) of a small ternary is a relatively tight-knit 8-measure theme that closes with a PAC.

## Structure

The A section is typically built as:
- Simple **period** (most common)
- Simple **sentence**
- **Hybrid** theme
- Less often: compound theme or nonconventional form

## Cadential Requirements

The A section closes with a **PAC**:
- In the **home key** (nonmodulating), or
- In a **subordinate key** (modulating)

## Tonal Organization

The A section emphasizes **tonic** harmony:
- Opens with tonic
- Closes with tonic (even when modulating to new key)
- Creates stable tonal foundation

## Modulating vs. Nonmodulating

The A section may:
- Remain entirely in the home key
- Modulate to a closely related key (V or III)

Both options create the same formal type; the tonal organization has consequences for B and A' but doesn't change the fundamental form.

## Tight-Knit Organization

The exposition is the most tightly knit section:
- Emphasis on tonic
- Closes with PAC
- Symmetrical grouping structure
- Conventional thematic design

## Closing Section Option

Since the A section closes with PAC, it may be followed by codettas or a closing section.
