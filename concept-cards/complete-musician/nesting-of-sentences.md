# Quick Definition
A sentence structure in which the B section itself contains a miniature 1:1:2 structure, creating a hierarchical nesting of proportional patterns.

# Core Definition
Nesting of sentences occurs when the B portion of a larger sentence (A-A'-B) itself unfolds as a miniature sentence (c-c'-d), creating a recursive proportional structure. When both A and B portions contain nested structures, the A's nested elements are labeled a, a', b and B's nested elements are labeled c, c', d (to avoid letter collision). (Laitz, Ch. 21, pp. 479-480)

# Key Properties
1. B section contains its own 1:1:2 pattern
2. Lowercase letters for nested level, uppercase for outer level
3. Creates acceleration and fragmentation
4. Demonstrates the recursive nature of musical proportion

# Examples
**Example 1** (p. 479): Beethoven, Piano Sonata no. 1 in F minor -- mm. 5-6 (c-c') repeat at one-measure intervals, followed by mm. 7-8 (d), creating a 1+1+2 within the 4-measure B of the 2+2+4 sentence.
