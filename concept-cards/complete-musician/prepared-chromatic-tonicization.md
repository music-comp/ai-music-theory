# Quick Definition
Prepared chromatic tonicization occurs when a chromatic tone is preceded by its diatonic version in the same voice, creating a smooth chromatic line (e.g., G-G#-A).

# Core Definition
Chromatic preparation is the practice of preceding a chromatic tone with its diatonic version in the same voice. This softens the harsh aural effect of the chromatic tone and creates a smooth chromatic line. For example, G-G#-A in the same voice creates a prepared chromatic passing tone. Prepared chromaticism is always preferred in part writing. When preparation occurs in a different voice instead, a cross relation results (Laitz, Ch. 24, p. 542).

# Key Properties
1. Diatonic form precedes chromatic form in the same voice
2. Creates smooth chromatic line
3. Softens the harsh aural effect
4. Preferred over unprepared chromaticism
5. When impossible, cross relations may be unavoidable
