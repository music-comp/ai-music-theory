# Quick Definition
A pause or break in the musical flow, often marking the boundary between subphrases, though a caesura alone does not guarantee a subphrase division.

# Core Definition
A caesura is a perceptible pause in the musical continuity, created by rests, rhythmic breaks, or the ending of melodic activity. It can help delineate subphrases within a phrase, but the presence of a caesura does not automatically create subphrases -- a continuous harmonic progression spanning the caesura may override the surface break. (Laitz, Ch. 17, p. 418)

# Key Properties
1. A perceptible break in musical flow
2. Often marks subphrase boundaries but not always
3. Harmonic context determines whether the caesura is structurally meaningful
4. Can occur within phrases, between phrases, and between sections
