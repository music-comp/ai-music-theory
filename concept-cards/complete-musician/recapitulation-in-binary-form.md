# Quick Definition
The recapitulation in rounded binary form is the restatement of the opening material in the tonic key, occurring after the digression and interruption in the second section.

# Core Definition
After the digression leads to an interruption (structural HC), the opening material returns in the tonic. This return is the recapitulation, which provides both melodic and tonal closure. The recapitulation may be a literal or varied restatement of the opening material (Laitz, Ch. 26, p. 589).

- **Rounded Binary Form** -- the formal context for recapitulation

# Key Properties
1. Returns opening material in the tonic
2. Follows the digression and interruption
3. May be literal or varied
4. Provides tonal closure for the entire work
