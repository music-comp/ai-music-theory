# Quick Definition
The technique of supporting the same or similar melody with different harmonies, commonly used in the consequent phrase of a parallel period to change the cadential destination from HC/IAC to PAC.

# Core Definition
Reharmonization occurs when a melody that appeared with one harmonization is presented again with different supporting harmonies. In parallel periods, the consequent phrase begins with similar melodic material but diverges harmonically to reach a stronger cadence (PAC instead of HC/IAC). The point of harmonic divergence is where the reharmonization occurs. (Laitz, Ch. 20, pp. 466-468)

# Key Properties
1. Same or similar melody with different harmonies
2. Common in consequent phrases of parallel periods
3. Point of divergence redirects harmony to stronger cadence
4. A fundamental compositional technique
