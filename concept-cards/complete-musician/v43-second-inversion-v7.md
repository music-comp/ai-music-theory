# Quick Definition
V4/3 places ^2 in the bass; it functions like viio6 as a passing chord between I and I6 (or I6 and I), or as a neighbor chord to I.

# Core Definition
**V4/3** (second inversion of V7) places the chordal fifth (^2) in the bass. Since ^2 also appears in the bass of viio6, V4/3 functions the same way -- primarily as a **passing chord** between I and I6, with the outer voices often moving in parallel tenths. It can also neighbor root-position I. Exception: in the ascending passing progression I-V4/3-I6 (bass ^1-^2-^3), the chordal seventh (^4) may ascend to ^5 instead of resolving down, because the strong parallel-tenth motion between bass and soprano overrides normal seventh resolution (Laitz, Ch. 11, pp. 249-250).

# Prerequisites
- **V7** -- V4/3 is its second inversion
- **Passing chord** -- V4/3 functions as a passing chord

# Key Properties
1. Bass = ^2 (same as viio6)
2. Functions as passing chord (I-V4/3-I6 or I6-V4/3-I)
3. Can also neighbor root-position I
4. Outer voices often in parallel tenths
5. Exception: ^4 may ascend to ^5 when parallel tenths control the outer voices
