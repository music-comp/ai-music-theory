# Quick Definition
An elided resolution occurs when the augmented sixth chord moves directly to V7, and #4 does not resolve literally to 5 but instead moves directly to 4 (and on to 3).

# Core Definition
When the augmented sixth chord moves directly to V7, an elided resolution often occurs: #4 does not resolve to 5 as expected but moves directly to 4 (the seventh of V7), which then resolves to 3. The resolution of #4 to 5 is thus "skipped over" or elided (Laitz, Ch. 30, p. 680).

# Prerequisites
- **Augmented Sixth Chord** -- the chord being resolved

# Key Properties
1. #4 moves to 4 instead of 5
2. Occurs when augmented sixth moves directly to V7
3. The resolution to 5 is implied but not literally stated
4. 4 then resolves to 3 as the seventh of V7
