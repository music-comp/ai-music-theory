# Quick Definition
Triad quality is determined by the intervals between root, third, and fifth: major (M3+m3=P5), minor (m3+M3=P5), diminished (m3+m3=d5), and augmented (M3+M3=A5).

# Core Definition
Four types of triads exist, named by the quality of the third between root and third, and the quality of the fifth between root and fifth:
1. **Major** (M): M3 + m3 = P5 -- consonant
2. **Minor** (m): m3 + M3 = P5 -- consonant
3. **Diminished** (d or deg): m3 + m3 = d5 -- dissonant
4. **Augmented** (A or +): M3 + M3 = A5 -- dissonant

# Prerequisites

# Key Properties
1. Major and minor triads are consonant because they span a perfect fifth
2. Diminished and augmented triads are dissonant because of their imperfect fifth
3. In common practice, only major, minor, and diminished function as independent harmonies
4. Quality is determined by the intervals from root to third and root to fifth
