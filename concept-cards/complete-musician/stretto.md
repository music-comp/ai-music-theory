# Quick Definition
A contrapuntal intensification technique in which imitative entries of a motive overlap more closely than in their original presentation, creating a sense of compression and urgency.

# Core Definition
Stretto (Italian, "tightened") is a type of imitation in which successive voice entries follow one another at shorter time intervals than in the original presentation, so that entries overlap. This compression creates heightened intensity and is typically used at climactic moments. In fugues, stretto often appears near the end as a culminating device. (Laitz, Ch. 16, p. 377)

# Key Properties

2. Creates compression and urgency

4. Common near the end of fugues
