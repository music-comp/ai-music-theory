# Quick Definition
Compound rondo form is a rondo in which individual sections (refrains and episodes) contain nested smaller forms, typically rounded binary or simple binary.

# Core Definition
As with ternary form, rondo form can be a compound form with nested forms within its sections. Refrains and episodes may each be cast in rounded binary form (a-dig-a') or other small forms. This creates multiple levels of formal structure within the larger ABACA or ABACABA plan (Laitz, Ch. 32).

# Prerequisites
- **Rondo Form** -- the outer form
- **Binary Form** -- the typical nested form

# Key Properties
1. Nested binary or other forms within rondo sections
2. Multiple levels of formal hierarchy
3. Each section (refrain, episode) may have its own internal form

# Examples
**Example 1** (p. 735): Clementi Piano Sonatina -- A1, B, A2, and C are all in rounded binary form within the five-part rondo.

**Example 2** (p. 738): Mozart, Viennese Sonatina in C -- Each section contains nested binary forms.
