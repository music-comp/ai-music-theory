# Quick Definition
A ritornello is an instrumental interlude that punctuates formal parts and sections of text in a da capo aria or similar vocal work.

# Core Definition
Ritornellos are instrumental passages that recur throughout a vocal work, especially the da capo aria. They typically introduce the main melodic material before the voice enters, articulate formal boundaries between sections, and may return between subsections of text. The ritornello tune often relates motivically to the vocal melody (Laitz, Ch. 31).

# Prerequisites
- **Da Capo Aria** -- the primary context for ritornellos

# Key Properties
1. Instrumental (non-vocal) passage
2. Recurs at structural points in the form
3. Often introduces melodic material later taken up by the voice
4. Articulates formal divisions

# Examples
**Example 1** (p. 712): Bach, St. Matthew Passion -- Ritornellos at mm. 1-6, 25-30, and 38-41 punctuate the A and B sections, with the opening ritornello tune related to the soprano's melody.
