# Quick Definition
The systematic transposition of a motive to successive pitch levels, creating a directional pattern. When accompanied by harmonic support, a motivic sequence may generate a harmonic sequence.

# Core Definition
A motivic sequence is the systematic repetition of a motive at successive diatonic pitch levels. It is essentially a melodic sequence applied to a specific motive. When the motivic sequence is supported by harmonies that also follow a sequential pattern (model and copy), the result is a full harmonic sequence. When only the motive repeats at different levels without harmonic replication, it remains a melodic/motivic sequence. (Laitz, Ch. 16, pp. 370-371)

# Key Properties
1. Motive repeated at successive pitch levels
2. Can be tonal or real
3. May or may not generate a harmonic sequence
4. Common development technique
