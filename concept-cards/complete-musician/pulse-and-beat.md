# Quick Definition
Pulses are undifferentiated, equally spaced clicks or taps; beats are pulses occurring within a musical context where some feel stronger than others.

# Core Definition
Undifferentiated (same quality, loudness, and length) and equally spaced clicks or taps are called **pulses**. When you nod your head or tap your foot at a steady rate when listening to music, these pulses are called **beats**, because they now occur within a context that differentiates them -- some beats feel stronger than others.

# Prerequisites
- No formal prerequisites

# Key Properties
1. Pulses are abstract, undifferentiated time points
2. Beats are pulses given meaning by musical context
3. Some beats feel stronger (accented) and others weaker (unaccented)
4. The speed of the beat is called the tempo

# Context & Application
The beat is the fundamental temporal unit of music. Listeners naturally respond to beats by tapping, nodding, or moving. All rhythmic and metric organization builds upon the beat.

# Relationships
## Enables
- **Tempo** -- the speed of the beat
- **Meter** -- the grouping of strong and weak beats
- **Rhythm** -- patterns of durations layered over the beat
