# Quick Definition
The two structural components of a sequence: the model presents the basic two-chord contrapuntal/harmonic pattern, and the copies replicate it at successive pitch levels.

# Core Definition
The **model** presents the basic contrapuntal/harmonic pattern of a sequence -- typically two chords with their voice-leading and any melodic figuration. The **copies** replicate the model at different diatonic pitch levels, preserving the voice-leading pattern exactly. A sequence typically contains two or three copies. Any melodic or motivic figuration introduced in the model must be replicated in each copy. (Laitz, Ch. 22, p. 497)

# Key Properties
1. Model: the initial two-chord pattern
2. Copies: exact replications at different pitch levels
3. Typically 2-3 copies
4. Figuration in the model must appear in all copies
