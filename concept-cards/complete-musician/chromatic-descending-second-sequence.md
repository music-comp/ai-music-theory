# Quick Definition
The chromatic D2 (-P4/+m3) sequence descends by whole step through all major triads, based on the 5-6 contrapuntal motion, dividing the octave into six major seconds (whole-tone path).

# Core Definition
The chromatic D2 (-P4/+m3) sequence relates to the diatonic D3 (-4/+2) "descending 5-6" sequence. Both are held together by the underlying 5-6 motion. In the chromatic form, the model and all copies contain major triads, and each repetition descends by a major second. The sequence carves out a whole-tone path (e.g., G-F-Eb-Db-Cb-Ab-G), dividing the octave symmetrically into six major seconds and skipping over the dominant. To reintroduce tonal focus, the sequence must be broken off after the repetition on bVI and led to the pre-dominant (Laitz, Ch. 36).

# Key Properties
1. Based on 5-6 contrapuntal motion
2. All chords are major triads
3. Descends by major second (whole tone)
4. Carves out a whole-tone path
5. Skips over the dominant
6. Barely tonal -- must break off for tonal reentry
7. More common in major mode
