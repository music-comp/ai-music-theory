# Quick Definition
Sequences in minor keys use the natural (descending melodic) minor scale, placing the bass tritone near the end of the sequence (rather than near the beginning as in major). Root-position ii^o triads are acceptable within the sequential pattern.

# Core Definition
In minor-mode sequences, the diatonic pitch collection used is the natural minor (descending melodic minor). The bass tritone leap, which occurs near the beginning in major-mode D2 sequences, occurs near the end in minor. The root-position ii^o chord appears within the pattern and is acceptable because it is part of the overall repeating contrapuntal pattern rather than an independent entity. In D3 sequences in minor, minor v (with lowered ^7) may appear to avoid the augmented second. (Laitz, Ch. 22, pp. 499-500)

# Key Properties
1. Uses natural minor scale
2. Bass tritone near end (not beginning) in D2
3. Root-position ii^o acceptable within pattern
4. Minor v may appear in D3 to avoid augmented second
