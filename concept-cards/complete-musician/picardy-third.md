# Quick Definition
A Picardy third raises the third of the final tonic chord in a minor-key piece to form a major triad, providing a particularly satisfying form of closure.

# Core Definition
The **Picardy third** is an idiom in which the tonic harmony that closes a piece in a minor key contains a raised third, forming a major triad. Late-Renaissance and Baroque composers viewed this as a particularly satisfying form of closure (Laitz, Ch. 8, p. 190).

# Key Properties
1. Occurs at the final tonic chord of a minor-key piece or section
2. Raises ^3 to create a major tonic triad
3. Provides enhanced sense of closure
4. Most common in Renaissance and Baroque music
