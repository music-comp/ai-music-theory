# Quick Definition
An anacrusis (upbeat or pickup) is one or more notes that precede the first full measure of a piece, leading into the downbeat.

# Core Definition
An **anacrusis** (also called an **upbeat** or **pickup**) consists of one or more notes that precede the first downbeat of a piece or phrase. The anacrusis creates forward momentum toward the first strong beat. In notation, an anacrusis appears as an incomplete first measure; the missing beats are typically compensated in the final measure of the piece.

# Prerequisites
- **Meter** -- the anacrusis is defined relative to the metric downbeat

# Key Properties
1. Precedes the first full measure
2. Creates anticipation and forward motion toward the downbeat
3. The first measure is incomplete; missing beats often appear in the final measure
4. Very common in vocal music and dance forms

# Context & Application
Many well-known melodies begin with an anacrusis (e.g., "The Star-Spangled Banner," many Bach chorales). Recognizing an anacrusis is essential for correct metric analysis.
