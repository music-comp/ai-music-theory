# Quick Definition
Beat division is the way a beat splits into smaller equal parts: into two (simple/binary division) or three (compound/ternary division), and this distinction is the fundamental criterion separating simple from compound meters.

# Core Definition
**Beat division** refers to how each beat is subdivided into smaller equal parts. In **simple meters**, the beat divides into two equal parts (binary or duple division). In **compound meters**, the beat divides into three equal parts (ternary or triple division). This distinction is the primary criterion for classifying meters as simple or compound.

# Prerequisites
- **Pulse and Beat** -- beat division subdivides the beat

# Key Properties
1. Binary division (2 parts): characteristic of simple meter
2. Ternary division (3 parts): characteristic of compound meter
3. Further subdivision is possible: binary subdivides into 4, 8, etc.; ternary into 6, 9, etc.
4. Borrowed divisions cross between simple and compound (e.g., triplets in simple meter)

# Context & Application
Beat division creates the rhythmic texture of music. The choice between binary and ternary division profoundly affects the music's character.
