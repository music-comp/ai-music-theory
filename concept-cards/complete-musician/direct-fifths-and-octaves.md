# Quick Definition
Direct (hidden) fifths and octaves occur when soprano and bass approach a P5 or P8 by similar motion; avoid these unless the soprano moves stepwise.

# Core Definition
**Direct fifths and octaves** (also called **hidden fifths/octaves**) occur when the soprano and bass approach a perfect fifth or octave by similar motion (both voices moving in the same direction). This is a guideline (not an absolute rule): avoid similar motion to octaves and fifths between soprano and bass, unless the soprano moves stepwise (Laitz, Ch. 8, p. 198, Guideline 6).

# Prerequisites
- **Parallel fifths and octaves** -- direct fifths are a related but less severe issue

# Key Properties
1. Soprano and bass approach P5 or P8 by similar motion
2. Guideline, not absolute rule
3. Permitted if soprano moves by step
4. Applies only to the soprano-bass pair (outer voices)
