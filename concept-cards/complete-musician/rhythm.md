# Quick Definition
Rhythm refers to the ever-changing combinations of longer and shorter durations and silence that populate the surface of a piece of music.

# Core Definition
**Rhythm** refers to the ever-changing combinations of longer and shorter durations and silence that populate the surface of a piece of music. Rhythm is often patterned, and rhythmic groupings may divide the beat, align with the beat, or extend over several beats.

# Prerequisites
- **Pulse and Beat** -- rhythm occurs relative to the beat

# Key Properties
1. Rhythm operates on the musical surface, providing moment-to-moment temporal variety
2. Rhythmic patterns may subdivide, align with, or span multiple beats
3. Rhythm is distinct from meter (which is the underlying framework of strong/weak beats)

# Context & Application
Rhythm is one of the two fundamental temporal aspects of music (along with meter). It creates the specific temporal profile that gives a melody its identity.
