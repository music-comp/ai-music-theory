# Quick Definition
Tempo is the speed of the beat -- how fast or slowly the music moves.

# Core Definition
**Tempo** is the speed of the beat; that is, how fast or how slowly you nod or tap. Tempo is typically indicated by Italian terms (Allegro, Adagio, etc.) or by metronome markings specifying beats per minute.

# Prerequisites
- **Pulse and Beat** -- tempo measures the speed of the beat

# Key Properties
1. Tempo establishes the overall pace of the music
2. Italian terms provide general tempo ranges (e.g., Allegro = fast, Adagio = slow)
3. Metronome markings provide exact tempo specifications

# Context & Application
Tempo profoundly affects the character and expressiveness of music. The same melody at different tempos can convey entirely different emotions.
