# Quick Definition
Simple binary form is a binary form in which no thematic material from the first section recurs in the second section.

# Core Definition
When the two sections of a binary form share no melodic material, the thematic design is called simple. Both sectional and continuous harmonic structures can combine with a simple thematic design. Simple binary forms may also be balanced if the closing material of the first section recurs transposed at the end of the second (Laitz, Ch. 26, p. 588).

# Prerequisites
- **Binary Form** -- the overarching formal concept

# Key Properties
1. No recurring thematic material between sections
2. Can be sectional or continuous
3. The digression after the double bar may use entirely new material
4. May also be balanced (closing material recurs)
