# Quick Definition
A period with contrasting melodic openings and an interrupted harmonic structure (antecedent ends on V/HC, consequent ends with PAC). Abbreviated CIP.

# Core Definition
A contrasting interrupted period has (1) **contrasting** melodic openings -- the consequent begins with different melodic material than the antecedent, and (2) **interrupted** harmonic structure -- the antecedent ends on V (HC). The contrast in melodic content increases variety while the harmonic interruption maintains the question-answer relationship. (Laitz, Ch. 20, pp. 469-470)

# Key Properties
1. Contrasting melodic openings
2. Antecedent ends with HC
3. Consequent ends with PAC
4. Less common than PIP
