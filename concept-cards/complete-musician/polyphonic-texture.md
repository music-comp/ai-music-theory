# Quick Definition
Polyphonic (contrapuntal) texture combines two or more independent melodies where no single voice is clearly the melody and the others accompaniment.

# Core Definition
**Polyphonic** or **contrapuntal texture** is the combination of two or more melodies such that there is no clear distinction between melody and accompaniment. Although polyphonic texture features greater independence of voices, even transparent textures like the two-voice counterpoint studied in Chapter 4 qualify as polyphonic because two independent melodies are combined.

# Prerequisites
- **Musical Texture** -- polyphonic is one of the three categories
- **Counterpoint** -- polyphonic texture is the product of contrapuntal writing

# Examples
**Example 1** (p. 149): Bach, Fugue in G minor from Well-Tempered Clavier (Example 6.7) illustrates polyphonic texture with multiple independent voice entries.
