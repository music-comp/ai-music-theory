# Quick Definition
A scherzo is a more spirited replacement for the minuet in the minuet-trio form, developed by Haydn and Beethoven, maintaining the same compound ternary structure but with a faster, more energetic character.

# Core Definition
In the hands of Haydn and Beethoven, the Classical minuet was often transformed into a more spirited piece called a scherzo. The scherzo retains the compound ternary structure of minuet-trio form (scherzo-trio-scherzo da capo) but replaces the courtly dance character with a faster, more vigorous style (Laitz, Ch. 31).

# Key Properties
1. Faster, more energetic than the minuet
2. Retains compound ternary structure (scherzo-trio-scherzo)
3. Developed by Haydn and Beethoven
4. Standard inner movement in symphonies and chamber music
