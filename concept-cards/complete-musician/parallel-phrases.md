# Quick Definition
Phrases that begin with similar or identical melodic material, creating a sense of unity within a period. The consequent typically diverges from the antecedent to reach a stronger cadence.

# Core Definition
Parallel phrases share similar melodic openings. In a parallel period, the consequent begins like the antecedent but diverges at some point to achieve a different (stronger) cadence. The parallel relationship creates unity and the divergence creates the necessary contrast for closure. (Laitz, Ch. 20, pp. 466-468)

# Key Properties
1. Similar melodic openings
2. Consequent diverges to reach a stronger cadence
3. Creates unity within the period
