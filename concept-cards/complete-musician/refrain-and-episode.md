# Quick Definition
In rondo form, refrains (A1, A2, A3, etc.) are recurring sections of the same material in the home key, while episodes (B, C, etc.) are sections of contrasting material in other keys.

# Core Definition
Rondo form alternates refrains with episodes. The refrain is the first thing heard and always occurs in the home key. Episodes provide both tonal and thematic contrast. The number and arrangement of refrains and episodes determines the rondo type: five-part (A1-B-A2-C-A3) or seven-part (A1-B1-A2-C-A3-B2-A4). Later refrains may omit repeats or present ornamented versions of the original material (Laitz, Ch. 32).

# Key Properties
1. Refrains always in the home key
2. Episodes in contrasting keys
3. Refrains may be varied or ornamented in later appearances
4. Episodes may contain nested binary forms

# Examples
**Example 1** (p. 731): Couperin, "Air tendre" -- Three refrains in G minor (i), two episodes in Bb major (III) and D minor (v).
