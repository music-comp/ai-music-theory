# Quick Definition
Closely related keys are adjacent on the circle of fifths, differing by only one sharp or flat in their key signatures and sharing most of their pitch content.

# Core Definition
Keys that are adjacent to one another on the circle of fifths -- that is, whose key signatures differ by one sharp or flat -- are called **closely related keys**. For example, Ab major is closely related to both Db major and Eb major. Another method: build a diatonic triad on each scale degree of the home key; all consonant (major and minor) triads form tonics for closely related keys. Diminished triads are excluded (cannot serve as tonics).

# Prerequisites
- **Circle of Fifths** -- closely related keys are adjacent on the circle
- **Key Signatures** -- they differ by one accidental

# Key Properties
1. Closely related keys share six of seven pitch classes
2. They differ by exactly one accidental in their key signatures
3. Five closely related keys exist for any given tonic
4. For C major: D minor (ii), E minor (iii), F major (IV), G major (V), A minor (vi)
5. For C minor (natural minor): Eb major (III), F minor (iv), G minor (v), Ab major (VI), Bb major (VII)
6. Modulation to closely related keys is smooth because of shared pitch content

# Construction / Recognition
## To Construct:
1. Build triads on each scale degree of the home key
2. Identify the major and minor triads (exclude diminished)
3. These are the closely related keys

## To Recognize:
1. Compare key signatures -- if they differ by one or fewer accidentals, the keys are closely related

# Context & Application
Closely related keys are the most common modulation destinations in common-practice music. The most common targets: major keys to V, vi, iii; minor keys to III, v, VI.

# Examples
**Example 1** (p. 13): Ab major is closely related to Db major (one more flat) and Eb major (one fewer flat).

# Relationships
## Builds Upon
- **Circle of Fifths** -- proximity on the circle defines close relationship

## Related
- **Relative Keys** -- always closely related (same key signature)
- **Parallel Keys** -- not closely related (differ by three accidentals)
