# Quick Definition
The interruption in binary form is the structural half cadence that divides the second section, marking the point where the harmonic progression is interrupted and begins again in the tonic.

# Core Definition
The structure of a rounded binary form resembles a parallel interrupted period. The structural HC in the second section is called an interruption (marked with a double slash //), since it is the point where the harmonic progression is interrupted and begins again in the tonic with the recapitulation. This is analogous to the interruption in a PIP but complicated by the repeats (Laitz, Ch. 26, p. 589).

# Prerequisites

# Key Properties
1. A structural half cadence
2. Divides the second section into digression and recapitulation
3. Marked with a double slash (//) in analysis
4. The harmonic progression restarts on the tonic after the interruption
5. Analogous to the interruption in a parallel interrupted period
