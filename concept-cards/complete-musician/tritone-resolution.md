# Quick Definition
The tritone in V7 (^7-^4 as d5 or ^4-^7 as A4) resolves by contrary motion: the d5 contracts to a third; the A4 expands to a sixth.

# Core Definition
The **tritone** within V7 is formed between ^7 and ^4 (the chordal third and seventh). When spelled as a diminished fifth (^7-^4), it contracts by contrary motion to a third (^1-^3). When spelled as an augmented fourth (^4-^7), it expands by contrary motion to a sixth (^3-^1). This resolution by contrary motion is fundamental to the V7-I voice-leading connection and is one of the primary forces driving dominant to tonic (Laitz, Ch. 9, pp. 221-222).

# Prerequisites
- **V7** -- contains the tritone
- **Voice leading** -- the tritone resolves according to voice-leading principles

# Key Properties
1. d5 (^7 above ^4): contracts to M3 or m3
2. A4 (^4 above ^7): expands to m6 or M6
3. Resolution is always by contrary motion
4. Both voices resolve by step
5. Fundamental to V7-I voice leading
