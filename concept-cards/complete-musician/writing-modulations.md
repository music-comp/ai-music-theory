# Quick Definition
Writing modulations requires balancing the duration of two key areas, choosing appropriate pivot chords (preferably pre-dominants in the new key), and closing with a strong cadence.

# Core Definition
Guidelines for writing modulations: (1) Balance the duration of the two tonal areas; place the pivot about halfway through the phrase. (2) Figure out potential pivot chords by lining up diatonic chords for each key; the best pivots act as PD in the new key. (3) Do not crash into a PAC immediately after the pivot; insert a contrapuntal cadence or EPM first. (4) Use a stepwise soprano line moving to a strong PAC with cadential 6/4 in the new key. Always test modulations on the piano (Laitz, Ch. 25, pp. 572-573).

# Key Properties
1. Balance duration of both key areas
2. Best pivots function as PD in the new key
3. Avoid using V of the new key as pivot
4. Insert contrapuntal harmony before the cadence
5. Test on the piano -- some pivots look correct but do not work aurally
