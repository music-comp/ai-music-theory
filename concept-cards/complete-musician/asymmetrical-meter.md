# Quick Definition
Asymmetrical meters have time signatures with top numbers like 5 or 7 that cannot be evenly divided into groups of 2 or 3, resulting in measures with unequal beat groupings.

# Core Definition
**Asymmetrical meters** have beats that are not all the same length, typically combining groups of 2 and 3. Common asymmetrical meters include 5/8 (grouped as 2+3 or 3+2) and 7/8 (grouped as 2+2+3, 2+3+2, or 3+2+2). These meters are common in folk music from Eastern Europe and the Balkans and became more frequent in Western art music during the twentieth century.

# Prerequisites

# Key Properties
1. Beats within the measure are of unequal length
2. 5/8 = 2+3 or 3+2; 7/8 = various combinations of 2s and 3s
3. Common in folk music traditions
4. The specific grouping pattern is usually consistent within a section
