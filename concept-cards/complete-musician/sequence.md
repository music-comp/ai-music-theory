# Quick Definition
A sequence is the immediate restatement of a musical idea on different scale degrees.

# Core Definition
A **sequence** is the immediate restatement of a musical idea on different scale degrees. This technique creates recognizable patterns that help unify musical passages while also advancing the melody to new pitch levels (Laitz, Ch. 7, p. 171).

# Prerequisites
- **Melodic fluency** -- sequences contribute to structural lines

# Key Properties
1. A musical idea is repeated at a different pitch level
2. The repetition is immediate (follows the original statement directly)
3. Creates unity through pattern recognition
4. Advances the melody to new pitch levels

# Examples
**Example 1** (p. 171): In "God Save the King," mm. 9-10 imitate mm. 7-8 by repeating the material down a second -- an immediate restatement on different scale degrees.
