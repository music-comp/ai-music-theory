# Quick Definition
A direct step-descent bass in the minor mode (^1-^7-^6-^5), often associated with melancholy texts and used as a repeating ostinato.

# Core Definition
A lament bass is a direct step-descent bass cast in the minor mode: ^1-lowered ^7-^6-^5. The expressive half step ^6-^5 and the minor-mode coloring make it particularly suited to melancholy texts. Lament basses often function as ostinatos (ground basses), repeated throughout an entire piece or section. (Laitz, Ch. 18, p. 436)

# Prerequisites

# Key Properties
1. Minor mode: ^1-lowered^7-^6-^5
2. Expressive half step ^6-^5
3. Often repeated as ostinato (ground bass)
4. Associated with melancholy texts since 17th century

# Examples
**Example 1**: Purcell, "Dido's Lament" from Dido and Aeneas -- repeated chromatic lament bass.

**Example 2** (p. 436): Traditional, "Erie Canal" -- simple diatonic lament bass pattern.
