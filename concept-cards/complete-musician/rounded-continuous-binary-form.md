# Quick Definition
Rounded continuous binary form combines a continuous harmonic structure (first section closes away from tonic) with a rounded thematic design (opening material returns after a digression), making it the most common two-reprise form.

# Core Definition
The most common two-reprise form features: three-part thematic design (A, digression, recapitulation/A') and two-part harmonic structure (tonic to non-tonic key in section 1, digression through interruption and recapitulation in tonic in section 2). In major, section 1 typically modulates to V; in minor, to III or v. The digression is unstable and sequential, leading to an interruption (structural HC), after which the opening material returns in the tonic (Laitz, Ch. 26, pp. 590-591).

# Prerequisites
- **Rounded Binary Form** -- the thematic design
- **Continuous Binary** -- the harmonic structure

# Key Properties
1. First section closes away from tonic (continuous)
2. Second section begins with digression
3. Structural HC (interruption) divides the second section
4. Opening material returns in tonic after the interruption
5. Prototype for sonata form
