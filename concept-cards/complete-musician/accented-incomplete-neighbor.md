# Quick Definition
An accented incomplete neighbor (AIN) leaps from a chord tone to a dissonant pitch on an accented beat, then resolves by step to a different chord tone -- essentially interchangeable with the appoggiatura.

# Core Definition
The **accented incomplete neighbor** (AIN) is an incomplete neighbor that occurs on a metrically accented beat. It leaps from a chord tone and resolves by step (up or down) to a different chord tone. It is essentially interchangeable with the appoggiatura, and Laitz uses the terms nearly synonymously. The AIN combines the characteristics of an incomplete neighbor (one-directional step resolution) with accented placement, making it dramatically prominent (Laitz, Ch. 13, pp. 309-310).

# Key Properties
1. Enters by leap from a chord tone

3. Resolves by step to a different chord tone
