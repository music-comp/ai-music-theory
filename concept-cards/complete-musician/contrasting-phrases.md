# Quick Definition
Phrases that begin with different melodic material, providing variety within a period while maintaining the antecedent-consequent cadential relationship.

# Core Definition
Contrasting phrases begin with different melodic material. In a contrasting period, the consequent introduces new melodic ideas while still fulfilling the harmonic obligation to provide stronger closure than the antecedent. (Laitz, Ch. 20, pp. 469-470)

# Key Properties
1. Different melodic openings
2. Still maintain antecedent-consequent cadential relationship
3. Provides greater variety than parallel phrases
