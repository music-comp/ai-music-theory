# Quick Definition
An ostinato is a repeated musical idea that provides the backbone of continuous variations, appearing as a repeating bass pattern (ground bass), harmonic pattern (chaconne), or both (passacaglia).

# Core Definition
Ostinati provide the backbone of continuous variations, over which changes in register, texture, and motivic design occur. They generally appear in the lower voices and can take the form of a ground bass, chaconne, or passacaglia. The ostinato creates structural continuity while permitting surface-level variety (Laitz, Ch. 26, p. 598).
