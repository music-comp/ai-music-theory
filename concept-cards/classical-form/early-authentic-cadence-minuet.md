# Quick Definition
A PAC in the home key that occurs before the end of the A section in a modulating minuet, creating the strongest expression of main-theme function.

# Core Definition

# Prerequisites
See front matter.

# Key Properties
See Core Definition.
An early authentic cadence is a PAC in the home key appearing within the A section of a modulating minuet, closing main-theme function before the section proceeds to modulate. It is followed by another thematic unit that modulates or begins directly in the subordinate key. The early PAC may close a cadential phrase, a consequent, or a complete main theme.

# Construction / Recognition
- A PAC in the home key occurs within (not at the end of) the A section
- The section continues with material that modulates
- Main-theme function is strongly articulated

# Context & Application
The early authentic cadence provides the clearest articulation of the main-theme/transition boundary within a minuet exposition.

# Examples
- Beethoven, Violin Sonata in F, Op. 24, iii, m. 4
- Haydn, Symphony No. 98 in B-flat, iii, m. 4
- Mozart, Piano Sonata in A, K. 331/300i, ii, m. 10
