# Quick Definition
A core substitute in the development whose tonal and phrase-structural organization resembles a transition: it begins in one key with a standard initiating function, modulates to another key, and closes with an HC or dominant arrival.

# Core Definition
In place of a core, the central section of the development may be occupied by a thematic unit whose tonal and phrase-structural organization resembles a transition. This transition-like core substitute follows upon a distinct pre-core, begins in one key with a standard initiating function (presentation, compound basic idea), modulates to some other key (development or home), and closes with an HC or dominant arrival. Model-sequence technique of modest scope may appear (usually in a continuation function), but not in a way that gives rise to a genuine core -- the models are too small.

# Key Properties
1. Begins in one key with standard initiating function (presentation or compound basic idea)
2. Modulates to another key (development key or home key)
3. Closes with HC or dominant arrival
4. May include model-sequence technique, but models are small (typically 1 measure)
5. Small model size prevents the impression of a genuine core
6. Follows a distinct pre-core
7. Especially common in Haydn's developments

# Construction / Recognition
- Identify a unit in the development that modulates from one key to another
- Note the initiating function (presentation or compound basic idea)
- Observe the concluding HC or dominant arrival
- Check for model-sequence technique: if models are small (1 measure), it is not a genuine core
- Compare with the exposition's transition for structural parallels

# Context & Application
Transition-like units are one of several core substitutes found when the development lacks a genuine core. They are particularly common in Haydn, who as a general rule does not employ pre-core/core technique. The small scale of any sequential models within the unit is the key distinguishing feature from a genuine core.

# Examples
- Haydn, Piano Trio in F-sharp minor, H. 26, ii, mm. 17-30 (ex. 13.15): transition-like unit begins with 1-m. basic idea, descending-fifth sequential progression, premature dominant arrival at m. 24; model too small for a genuine core

# Common Errors
- Mistaking modest model-sequence technique for a genuine core (the model must be 4+ measures for a core)
- Treating the transition-like unit as a genuine transition (it occurs within the development, not between main and subordinate themes)
- Assuming all developments must have a core

# Common Confusions
- **Transition-like unit vs. transition-like pre-core**: A transition-like pre-core precedes the core; a transition-like unit replaces the core
- **Model size**: 1-measure models within a transition-like unit do not create a core; 4-8 measure models would
