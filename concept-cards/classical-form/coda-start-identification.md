# Quick Definition
The often-difficult task of locating where the coda begins, defined as the point where the recapitulation's music no longer corresponds to the exposition, even if that moment is not perceived as a structural beginning.

# Core Definition
Unlike an exposition, development, or recapitulation, whose beginning is usually articulated by a clear initiating function, the onset of the coda is often not readily perceived. In many cases, the coda is obviously under way before it expresses any sense of formal initiation. The "start" of the coda is best located at that moment when the music of the recapitulation no longer corresponds to that of the exposition, even if that moment is not perceived as a structural beginning. On a few occasions, the start is unambiguous (rhythmic break, texture change, new initiating unit, or notation after double-bar repeat signs). Often, however, the coda begins with a medial or concluding function, and a true structural beginning is not expressed until later, if at all.

# Prerequisites
- **Coda**: The section whose start is being identified
- **Recapitulation**: The section from which the coda emerges

# Key Properties
1. The coda's start is often not readily perceived by the listener
2. Best located at the point where the recapitulation stops corresponding to the exposition
3. This analytical start may differ from the perceived beginning
4. May differ from the notational start (double-bar repeat signs)
5. The coda frequently starts with a medial or concluding function (not initiation)
6. A true structural beginning may not appear until much later in the coda
7. May start with model-sequence passage or sequential repetition of the recapitulation's last idea
8. If the exposition has a retransition, the recapitulation may have a similar passage leading to IV for the coda

# Construction / Recognition
- **Clear start**: rhythmic break, texture change, new initiating unit (basic idea or presentation); double-bar repeat signs
- **Ambiguous start**: coda begins with medial or concluding function (continuation, cadential, sequential)
- **Rule**: locate where the recapitulation's music departs from the exposition's path
- A true structural beginning may not appear until later (with return of main-theme material, for instance)

# Context & Application
The difficulty of locating the coda's start reflects the fact that it is an optional section without a mandatory formal opening. Many codas grow organically out of the recapitulation's closing material. The analyst must distinguish between the analytical start (where correspondence ends), the notational start (repeat signs), and the perceived functional beginning (where a new initiation is felt).

# Examples
- Mozart, String Quintet in G Minor, K. 516, i (ex. 12.1): coda starts at m. 232 notationally (after repeat sign); functional beginning not sensed until m. 235 with main theme's basic idea
- Mozart, Symphony No. 36 ("Linz"), K. 425, i (ex. 12.2): coda starts at m. 269 where music departs from exposition's path
- Haydn, Symphony No. 97 in C, i (ex. 12.3): coda starts with sequential repetition of cadential idea; no true beginning until m. 279 with main-theme material
- Beethoven, Piano Sonata in E-flat, Op. 7, iv (ex. 12.4): coda starts at m. 140 (where music departs from exposition); functional beginning is return of main-theme material at m. 143
- Mozart, Symphony No. 39, K. 543, ii (ex. 12.5): coda starts at m. 137 where the music departs; codettas from recapitulation are reinterpreted as basic ideas, producing a false closing section

# Common Errors
- Expecting every coda to begin with a clear new theme or initiating function
- Equating the notational start with the functional beginning

# Common Confusions
- **Three distinct "starts"**: The notational start (repeat signs), the analytical start (departure from exposition correspondence), and the perceived functional beginning may all differ
- **No initiating function required**: Many codas begin with medial or concluding function
- **Late structural beginning**: A true sense of structural beginning may not appear until very late in the coda, or not at all
