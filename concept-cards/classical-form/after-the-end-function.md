# Quick Definition
The coda's primary temporal function of expressing the quality of "after-the-end" — music that follows the completion of the movement's fundamental formal and tonal processes.

# Formal Definition
Because a movement's structural close is fulfilled by the recapitulation, the primary function of a coda is to express the temporal quality of "after-the-end." This characterization can seem to conflict with the coda's role as the movement's "genuine conclusion" (completing secondary processes). The conflict is reconciled by recognizing that the fundamental tonal, melodic, and phrase-structural processes are completed by the recapitulation (hence "after-the-end"), while secondary processes often attain closure only in the coda (hence "genuine conclusion"). A coda avoids introducing new material that calls for further development or initiating new processes that cannot be completed.

# Construction/Recognition
- The coda does not normally initiate any significant tonal changes
- Tends to remain in the home key with only brief tonicizations
- Avoids introducing genuinely new material requiring further development
- Does not start new processes that cannot be completed within the coda
- Governed by the aesthetic ideal: "once the music finally stops, the listener should have no desire to hear anything more"

# Musical Context
The after-the-end function is analogous at a higher level to the function of a closing section (codettas) that follows a PAC ending a theme. Both express postcadential continuation, but the coda operates at the level of the entire movement rather than a single theme.

# Examples
- All codas embody this function; the concept is illustrated through the fundamental constraint that codas must not create expectations for further music

# Common Confusions
- "After-the-end" and "genuine conclusion" are not contradictory — they address different levels of process
- The coda's after-the-end quality does not make it unimportant; it simply means the primary structural processes are already resolved
- A coda that introduces genuinely new developmental processes would violate its after-the-end function
