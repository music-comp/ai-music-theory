# Quick Definition
The final series of codettas that ends the coda following the last PAC, sometimes using new material, sometimes restoring material deleted from the recapitulation.

# Formal Definition
Most codas end with a closing section (made up of codettas) that follows the final perfect authentic cadence of the home key. The melodic-motivic content of this closing section may be new or based on material from earlier in the movement. Sometimes the closing section is extremely short or omitted altogether. If the recapitulation's closing section was eliminated (because the coda starts immediately after the subordinate-theme group), the coda will likely restore that deleted material as its own closing section. When this occurs, the coda can sometimes give the impression of being interpolated in, rather than following on, the recapitulation.

# Construction/Recognition
- Follows the final PAC of the coda
- Consists of codettas prolonging root-position tonic
- May be new material, or may restore deleted material from the recapitulation
- Sometimes main-theme material is repurposed as a closing section (without cadential closure)
- May be very short or absent

# Musical Context
The closing section of the coda provides the very last sounds of the movement. Its character determines the final impression: triumphant fanfare, quiet dissolution, or simple affirmation of the tonic.

# Examples
- Haydn, Symphony No. 97 in C, i (ex. 12.9): main-theme fanfare material returns at m. 279 but without cadential activity; root-position tonic is retained obsessively, so the material functions as a closing section rather than a theme
- Haydn, Piano Trio in C, Hob. XV:27, iii (ex. 12.8): closing section from the recapitulation was eliminated; the coda restores it as its own closing section

# Common Confusions
- The closing section of the coda is hierarchically nested: it consists of codettas within the coda, just as the exposition's closing section consists of codettas within the subordinate-theme group
- When the coda restores the recapitulation's deleted closing section, it can seem as though the coda was "inserted" between the subordinate theme and the closing section
