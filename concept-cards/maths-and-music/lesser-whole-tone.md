# Quick Definition
The lesser whole tone is the interval with frequency ratio 10/9, approximately 182.40 cents, about 18 cents flat of the equal-tempered whole step.

# Formal Definition
The lesser whole tone is the rational interval corresponding to the ratio 10/9 = 2 * 5 / 3^2 in Q+. It is a 5-limit interval involving the primes 2, 3, and 5. The interval between the lesser and greater whole tones is the comma of Didymus: (9/8) / (10/9) = 81/80 ~ 21.51 cents. Measured in cents: 1200 * log2(10/9) ~ 182.40.

# Mathematical Context
The ratio 10/9 relates to other fundamental intervals: the greater whole tone (9/8) times the lesser whole tone (10/9) equals the just major third (5/4). The tempered whole step (200 cents) lies between the lesser whole tone (~182 cents) and the greater whole tone (~204 cents), closer to the latter. The gap between these two whole tones -- the comma of Didymus at ~21.5 cents -- is a crucial measure of the incompatibility between 3-limit and 5-limit tuning.

# Musical Context
In the just intonation scale, the lesser whole tone appears at the intervals 2-to-3 and 5-to-6, while the greater whole tone appears at 1-to-2, 4-to-5, and 6-to-7. This means that the just intonation scale has two different sizes of whole steps, unlike the Pythagorean scale (which uses only the greater whole tone) or equal temperament (which has only one whole step size).

# Examples
- 1200 * log2(10/9) ~ 182.40 cents (tempered step = 200 cents, difference ~ 17.60 cents)
- Greater whole tone / lesser whole tone = comma of Didymus: (9/8) / (10/9) = 81/80
- Greater whole tone + lesser whole tone = just major third: 9/8 * 10/9 = 5/4
- Scale of comparison in cents: lesser whole tone (182) < tempered step (200) < greater whole tone (204)

# Common Confusions
- Equal temperament hides the distinction between the two whole tones by averaging them into a single 200-cent step
- The lesser whole tone is substantially flatter than the tempered step (~18 cents), much more so than the greater whole tone is sharper (~4 cents)
- The lesser whole tone (10/9) is a 5-limit interval, while the greater whole tone (9/8) is a 3-limit interval
