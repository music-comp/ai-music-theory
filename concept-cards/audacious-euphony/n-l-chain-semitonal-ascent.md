# Quick Definition
An extended **N**/**L** chain in the third paragraph of Dvorak's "New World" Symphony development carries semitonally ascending minor tonics every eight measures, creating an intensive chromatic slide that dominates the passage's upshift trajectory.

# Formal Definition
The **N**/**L** chain generates semitonal (T1) upshift through alternation of the W-group **N** (nebenverwandt) with the H-group **L** (Leittonwechsel). In the Dvorak development, this chain produces a series of semitonally ascending minor tonics: eb minor (m. 233) -> e minor (m. 241) -> f minor (m. 249) -> f# minor (m. 257), each arriving every eight measures. Dominant seventh chords mark four-bar medians between minor tonics. At m. 253, Dvorak synopsizes the prior twenty measures by planing minor triads with under-sevenths in rapid succession.

# Construction/Recognition
The **N**/**L** chain in the development:
- Minor tonic every eight measures, ascending by semitone
- Dominant seventh chord at four-bar median
- One exception: m. 253 dominant arrives two bars early, allowing acceleration
- At m. 255, Dvorak compresses the harmonic motion, planing eb minor, e minor, f minor triads with under-sevenths
- f# minor at m. 257 simultaneously fulfills two trajectories: the slow (8-bar) and fast (1-bar) periodicities

# Musical Context
The **N**/**L** chain is the most common T1 sequence type (more common than **S**/**P** or **H**/**R**). Its intensive use in the Dvorak development creates the passage's relentless upshifting energy. On the Tonnetz, it appears as a southwest slide.

# Examples
- Dvorak, Symphony No. 9, first movement, mm. 233-273: Extended **N**/**L** chain dominating the third and fourth paragraphs
- The fourth paragraph (m. 257) reprises the opening of the third, with additions and deletions
- The synoptic compression at mm. 253-256 functions as a rhythmic diminution of the prior chain

# Common Confusions
- The ascending direction (minor tonic up by semitone) is a T1 upshift, despite the minor mode
- The dominant seventh at each median is part of the **N** transformation, not an interruption
- The simultaneous fulfillment of two periodicities at m. 257 is a key compositional moment
