# Quick Definition
A connected graph representing the 24 consonant triads and 4 augmented triads, where edges connect triads related by single semitonal displacement (minimal voice-leading work).

# Formal Definition
The Cube Dance is a geometric representation whose nodes comprise the 24 consonant triads and the 4 augmented triads (28 nodes total). Edges connect pairs of triads related by single semitonal displacement - that is, triads differing by exactly one semitone in one voice under idealized voice leading. The graph visualizes the voice-leading proximities central to pan-triadic syntax. Its name derives from its visual appearance, which resembles cubes connected in a dancing configuration.

# Construction/Recognition
Structure:
- 28 nodes: 24 consonant triads + 4 augmented triads
- Edges connect triads with voice-leading distance of 1 semitone
- Each consonant triad connects to:
  - 1 augmented triad (by semitone displacement)
  - 2 other consonant triads via P or L transformation
- Each augmented triad connects to 6 consonant triads (its Weitzmann region)

The graph can be viewed as:
- 4 "cubes" (Weitzmann regions) connected through shared triads
- A unified representation of hexatonic and Weitzmann organization
- The minimal voice-leading network for consonant triads

# Musical Context/Application
The Cube Dance enables visualization of:
- Single-semitone voice-leading paths between any two triads
- The relationship between hexatonic cycles and Weitzmann regions
- How augmented triads mediate between consonant triads
- The unified structure of pan-triadic voice-leading space

It serves as a "stage upon which imaginative performances are mounted" - a map for exploring compositional possibilities and analyzing existing passages.

# Examples
- Hexatonic cycles appear as 6-node circuits within the Cube Dance
- Weitzmann regions appear as the 6 consonant triads surrounding each augmented triad
- Any path between triads on the Cube Dance represents a voice-leading connection
- Web animations coordinate with the printed diagrams (companion website)

# Common Confusions
- The Cube Dance includes augmented triads as nodes, unlike some other representations
- Edges represent voice-leading proximity, not all possible transformations
- The graph captures only single-semitone connections; larger voice-leading distances require multiple edges
- The visual "cube" appearance is a consequence of the underlying mathematical structure, not an arbitrary design choice
