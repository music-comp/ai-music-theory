# Quick Definition
The process by which a diatonic collection becomes a bounded microecological space on the Tonnetz, activating the syntactic and semantic forces of classical tonality through identification of syntonic images and closing of the diatonic parallelogram.

# Core Definition
Diatonic encapsulation occurs when the diatonic collection forms a parallelogram whose interior is tiled into six consonant triads. The parallelogram "encapsulates its contents into a microecological hothouse, activating the internal dynamics signified by Rings arrows" (p. 194). Within this space, "the identification of the tonic immediately evokes a hierarchization of the constituent tones and chords, intuitions about their context-free proximity, a syntax that governs component ordering, and the semantics of Rings arrows, which symbolize a rich network of overlapping metaphors: energy, gravity, attraction, magnetism, discharge, orientation, center, departure and arrival, return, passing, neighboring, leading, deception, completion/incompletion, suspension, finality, stability/instability" (p. 195).

# Prerequisites
- **Diatonic Tonnetz**: The geometric structure that gets encapsulated
- **Syntonic image**: Their identification enables the closure
- **Rings arrows**: The semantic forces activated by encapsulation

# Key Properties
1. Encapsulation activates the full suite of tonal expectations and semantic predications
2. Only central triads (sheltered from unstable corners) can serve as tonic
3. The process is dynamic: walls can be erected, dismantled, and moved
4. A "blank ear" provisionally encapsulates any heard triad; confirmation or disconfirmation follows
5. Standard confirmation: T-S-D-T progression (Gottfried Weber's "Attunement of the Ear to a Key")

# Construction / Recognition
Metaphors for encapsulation and its dissolution:
- Entering the four walls of home
- Sprouting landing gear and coming within view of a planet or airport
- Exiting freeway and entering a fortified village or gated community
- The Tonnetz as "hybrid automobile: one engine for the highway of chords, and one for touring the encapsulated neighborhoods" (p. 203)

Forces that erect walls: cadential confirmation, reverse hemiola, accented dissonance, flanking by syntonic images, thematic statements on hypermetric downbeats (Wagner vs. Brahms methods).

Forces that dissolve walls: sequential motion, L/R chain traversal, chromatic drift, hypermetric broadening.

# Context & Application
The convertible Tonnetz treats diatonic space as a "black-box theater, whose walls can be erected, dismantled, and moved without fuss" (p. 203). Every major and minor triad "is capable of diatonic encapsulation. Our filled ears are as subliminally alert to this potential as the blank ear that attunes a triad to a key" (p. 207).

Each locally tonicized triad within a chromatic journey creates a local encapsulation: at mm. 31-35 of Liszt's Consolation, A minor is "a diatonically organized, sealed capsule that bobs on the pan-triadic sea. Its interior is an ecological microsystem within which the fraught tensions and attractions, gravities and magnetisms of tonality are active. On its exterior, it is tethered to the broader universe by different forces and relations" (p. 205).

# Examples
- **Liszt Consolation no. 3**: Each hexatonic station locally encapsulated through tonicization (Figure 8.9, pp. 204-206)
- **Wagner Parsifal Faith Proclamation**: eb minor encapsulated by reverse hemiola, F-half-dim-7, and syntonic image flanking (Figure 8.11, pp. 208-209)
- **Brahms Symphony no. 1 finale**: Thematic statements on hypermetric downbeats erect walls; liquidations dissolve them (Figure 8.13, pp. 210-211)

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Assuming encapsulation is all-or-nothing
  **Correction**: Walls can "ease open" gradually during sequences or "flip closed" at cadences

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: Encapsulation is permanent once established
  **Clarification**: The same triad can be encapsulated in one phrase and released in another

- **Confusion**: Encapsulation merely identifies key membership
  **Clarification**: It activates the full suite of semantic forces -- gravitation, desire, leading -- not just classification
