# Quick Definition
An **S**/**P** chain is a chromatic sequence generated by alternating Slide and Parallel transformations, producing transposition by semitone (T1 or T11), involving the maverick **S** transformation.

# Formal Definition
The **S**/**P** chain combines a W-group transformation (**S**, maverick) with an H-group transformation (**P**, normative), generating a semitonal (T1) chromatic sequence. It is one of three transformation pairs producing semitone transpositions, alongside the more common **N**/**L** chain and the rare **H**/**R** chain. The involvement of the maverick **S** makes it relatively rare.

# Construction/Recognition
Alternate **S** and **P** transformations:
- First lower the third of each major triad (**P** to minor)
- Then lower root and fifth (**S** to next major triad a semitone below)
- Or reverse the order for ascending transposition
- The entire chain executes a complete tour around Cube Dance

# Musical Context
The **S**/**P** chain produces a descending semitonal sequence that, like the **L**/**R** chain, can connect two **PL**-related harmonies that stand directly adjacent in the same hexatonic and Weitzmann region, but by an elliptical circumnavigation of the full triadic universe.

# Examples
- Liszt, *Il Penseroso*, mm. 17-20: Connects Gb major to D major through a series of semitonal descents (figure 5.14), executing a complete tour around Cube Dance

# Common Confusions
- All three T1-generating pairs (**N**/**L**, **S**/**P**, **H**/**R**) produce semitonal transposition but with different voice-leading profiles
- The **S**/**P** chain is much rarer than the **N**/**L** chain
- The complete **S**/**P** tour connects triads that are directly adjacent in the same region, taking the "long way around"
