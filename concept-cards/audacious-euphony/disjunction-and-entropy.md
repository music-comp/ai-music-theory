# Quick Definition
The capacity of the pan-triadic model to represent not only conjunct voice leading but also its opposite (disjunction) and maximum variety along the conjunct/disjunct spectrum (entropy).

# Formal Definition
While the pan-triadic model privileges conjunct voice leading, it also provides tools for modeling disjunction. Maximal disjunction places triads in zone-diametric positions (6 zones apart, achieved by T2 or T6 transposition). Maximum entropy distributes triadic pairs evenly across all interval classes, achieved when voice-leading zones form an all-interval tetrachord.

# Musical/Mathematical Formulation
Maximal disjunction:
- Zone-diametric triads: separated by 6 units (maximum voice-leading distance)
- Achieved by tritone (T6) or whole-step (T2) transposition
- Mahler's "fallen from heaven" effect: C major to D major

Lexical diversity with disjunction:
- Two zone-diametric triads combined with their hexatonic poles
- Results in four triads with no pitch-class overlap (aggregate completion)
- Bach's four-chord complex: rotates through zones 1, 8, 7, 2

Maximum entropy (all-interval triadic quartets):
- Voice-leading zones form all-interval tetrachord [0146] or [0137]
- Pairs distributed across all six interval classes
- Example: {g minor, Eb major, A major, d minor} = zones {7, 8, 2, 4} = [0146]

# Musical Context/Application
The model enables systematic identification of disjunction and entropy without abandoning voice-leading foundations. Romantic aesthetics' fascination with the remote and unattainable can be modeled precisely. Atonal pitch-class theory's all-interval tetrachords find application in triadic contexts.

# Examples
- Bach Prelude (Dahlhaus 1967b): Four-chord disjunction complex
- Wagner Parsifal: H-related pair transposed by tritone
- Beethoven "Tempest" Sonata finale, mm. 8-12: All-interval tetrachord [0146]
- Wagner Gotterdammerung opening: All-interval tetrachord [0137]

# Common Confusions
- Disjunction is the opposite of conjunction, not a different phenomenon
- Entropy maximizes variety, not randomness
- The model enables but does not privilege disjunction analysis
