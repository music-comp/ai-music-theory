# Quick Definition
Cohn's metaphor for locally tonicized triads within a larger chromatic journey: each is "a diatonically organized, sealed capsule that bobs on the pan-triadic sea," with an interior activating tonal tensions and an exterior tethered to the broader universe by voice-leading forces.

# Core Definition
In the analysis of Liszt's Consolation no. 3, each hexatonic station is described as having a dual nature: "A minor, like F major before it and A major after it, is a diatonically organized, sealed capsule that bobs on the pan-triadic sea. Its interior is an ecological microsystem within which the fraught tensions and attractions, gravities and magnetisms of tonality are active. On its exterior, it is tethered to the broader universe by different forces and relations" (p. 205).

# Prerequisites
- **Diatonic encapsulation**: The theoretical concept the metaphor illustrates
- **Convertible Tonnetz**: The model within which capsules bob on the sea

# Key Properties
1. Interior: full diatonic syntax with Rings arrows active
2. Exterior: voice-leading relations to neighboring triads in chromatic space
3. The capsule's internal function is determinate; its external function may be indeterminate
4. Multiple capsules can coexist along a chromatic journey
5. Each capsule is locally coherent but globally mysterious

# Construction / Recognition
At mm. 31-35 of the Consolation: "Every event has a determinate role within the diatonic pitch space of a minor. Within that context, there exists no functional or enharmonic ambiguity. All of the modal degrees of a minor are present" (p. 205). But "What we do not understand is the diatonic function of that tonic itself, in relation to the larger Db major macrocosm: whether it is rooted on its 5-hat or 6-hat; whether it functions as subdominant by virtue of carrying the subdominant agent or as dominant by virtue of carrying the dominant agent" (p. 205).

# Context & Application
The sealed capsule metaphor is the most vivid expression of how double syntax operates in practice. It captures the experience of hearing locally tonal music that participates in a non-tonal larger progression. The metaphor also appears in the convertible Tonnetz's "hybrid automobile" description: "One is free to establish an encapsulated diatonic space... or perambulate more freely, meandering from triad to triad across shared edges" (p. 203).

# Examples
- **Liszt Consolation, A minor** (mm. 31-35): "Fully comfortable as a diatonically determinate microcosm" yet indeterminate within Db major (p. 205)
- **Each hexatonic station**: f minor, F major, a minor, A major -- all sealed capsules
- **Brahms Symphony no. 1**: Eb major and B major thematic statements as capsules within chromatic episode

# Common Errors
- **Error**: The capsule's indeterminate external function is a flaw
  **Correction**: Indeterminacy is a feature of double syntax; the capsule model makes it analytically productive

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: Sealed capsules are isolated from each other
  **Clarification**: They are connected by voice-leading forces on their exterior surface
