# Quick Definition
A maximally entropic ensemble of four triads whose constituent pairs are distributed as evenly as possible among the six interval classes, achieved when the voice-leading zones of the four triads form an all-interval tetrachord.

# Formal Definition
Maximum entropy in a triadic quartet requires that the six pairwise distances between the four triads be distributed as evenly as possible across the six possible interval classes. This is achieved when the voice-leading zones of the four triads correspond to one of the two all-interval tetrachords: prime form [0146] or [0137]. Both are octatonic subsets, and since the eight consonant-triad voice-leading zones have octatonic structure, triadic quartets with maximally distributed intervals exist. Following Robert Morris (1990), inspired by Elliott Carter's compositional practice, any nonintersecting union of a tritone with a minor third creates an all-interval tetrachord.

# Construction/Recognition
A maximally entropic quartet consists of:
- Two zone-diametric (T2- or T6-related) triads of one mode
- Two triads of the opposite mode related by an odd transposition (three zones apart)
- The resulting zone set corresponds to [0146] or [0137]

# Musical Context
The concept bridges atonal pitch-class theory with pan-triadic voice-leading analysis, suggesting that concepts from Carter and post-tonal theory can illuminate nineteenth-century chromatic practice.

# Examples
- Beethoven, "Tempest" Sonata finale, mm. 8-12: iv, bII, V6, i progression in d minor; zones {g = 7, Eb = 8, A = 2, d = 4} = [0146] (figure 5.26a)
- Wagner, *Gotterdammerung* opening: eb minor alternating with Cb major, db minor, D major; zones {eb = 7, Cb = 8, db = 1, D = 5} = [0137] (figure 5.26b)

# Common Confusions
- Maximum entropy is not random; it is the most even distribution of intervals, requiring careful selection
- The two all-interval tetrachord types [0146] and [0137] exhaust the possibilities for maximum entropy
- The octatonic structure of consonant-triad voice-leading zones is what makes these constructions possible
