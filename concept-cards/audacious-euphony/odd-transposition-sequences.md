# Quick Definition
Chromatic sequences that transpose by an odd number of semitones (1, 3, 5, 7, 9, or 11), generated by alternating one H-group and one W-group transformation, each involving three units of voice-leading work and moving between adjacent hexatonic and Weitzmann regions.

# Formal Definition
Odd-transposition sequences are distinguished from even-transposition sequences (which remain within a single region). Transposition by semitone (T1, T11) engages all three voices in parallel motion. The remaining odd transpositions (T3, T5, T7, T9) feature two voices moving in similar motion, one by semitone and the other by whole step. All odd-value transpositions involve three units of semitonal work, ranking them next on the scale of parsimony after the hexatonic and Weitzmann transformations (which involve one or two units).

# Construction/Recognition
- Identify the sequence's transpositional interval
- If odd, it must combine one H-group and one W-group transformation
- Three voice-leading units can be conceived as combining two parsimonious steps
- Even-transposition sequences (T2, T4, T6) stay within regions and require different analysis

# Musical Context
The distinction between odd and even transpositions is structurally fundamental: even transpositions preserve hexatonic or Weitzmann region membership, while odd transpositions necessarily cross between adjacent regions. This crossing is what allows odd-transposition sequences to potentially circumnavigate the entire triadic universe.

# Examples
- T1/T11: Semitone sequences (**N**/**L**, **S**/**P**, **H**/**R**)
- T3/T9: Minor-third sequences (**P**/**R**, **N**/**H**, **S**/**L**)
- T5/T7: Perfect-fifth sequences (**L**/**R**, **N**/**P**, **H**/**S**)

# Common Confusions
- T7 and T5 are inversionally related and produce the same interval class; similarly T9 and T3, T11 and T1
- Three semitones of work =/= a minor third interval; it refers to the total displacement across all three voices
- The threshold from two to three units of work marks the boundary between intra-regional and inter-regional motion
