# Quick Definition
Three different ways of measuring triadic distance -- common-tone count, Tonnetz edge-traversals, and voice-leading work -- each distributing the twenty-three types of triadic pairings differently across categories.

# Core Definition
Figure 4.7 "models the distribution of the twenty-three types of triadic pairings... under three measurements: (a) number of common tones; (b) Tonnetz distance, measured as edge-traversals; and (c) voice-leading work" (p. 84). "Method (c) creates the most categories and most evenly distributes the twenty-three progressions among the available categories" (p. 84). Tymoczko (2009c, 2010) notes that C major to F major is "closer on the Tonnetz than... C major to f minor, even though the latter progression involves less voice-leading work" (p. 84).

# Prerequisites
- **Tonnetz representation**: The space on which distances are measured
- **Voice-leading work**: The preferred distance metric

# Key Properties
1. Common-tone count: 3 categories (0, 1, 2 common tones) -- fewest distinctions
2. Tonnetz distance: Varies by edge-traversals -- intermediate distinctions
3. Voice-leading work: 7 categories (0-6 semitones) -- most and most even distinctions
4. Voice-leading work provides "more intuitively satisfactory results" where methods disagree

# Construction / Recognition
Key discrepancy: C major to a minor (R): 2 common tones but 2 units voice-leading work. C major to f minor (N): 1 common tone but also 2 units voice-leading work. The Tonnetz makes R look closer than N; voice-leading work shows them equal.

# Context & Application
The comparison motivates the expanded Tonnetz representation (Figure 4.6c) where augmented triads have area, aligning Tonnetz distances with voice-leading work.

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Equating common-tone count with voice-leading proximity
  **Correction**: R maximizes common tones but requires more voice-leading work than L and P

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: The three metrics are interchangeable
  **Clarification**: They give different rankings; voice-leading work is preferred for its finer distinctions and intuitive satisfaction
