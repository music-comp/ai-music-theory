# Quick Definition
A form of harmonic dualism grounded in the physical reality of directed voice leading rather than the metaphysical claims of historical dualism, correlating with but not mandating dominant/subdominant functional interpretations, and immune to standard critiques of Riemannian dualism.

# Core Definition
The dualism "merely explores the ramifications of the following aggregation of circumstances: that pitch motion exists on a linear continuum, which our culture metaphorically projects onto a vertical axis, as higher and lower; that pitch-class motion exists on a cyclical continuum; thus, that motion along either continuum can occur in two opposite directions; and that those motions have experiential correlates in the aural perception of sound and the kinetics of its production. This is a dualism that even the most skeptical realist can abide!" (p. 142). It is also "strongly related to the duality of strong and weak harmonic progression" (Schoenberg, developed by Meeus 2000, p. 142n7).

# Prerequisites
- **Upshifting and downshifting**: The directional concepts being elevated to a dualism
- **Voice-leading zones**: The system in which the dualism operates

# Key Properties
1. Grounded in physical voice-leading direction, not metaphysics
2. Immune to dismissal as metaphysical or based on spurious physics
3. Correlates with but does not mandate functional interpretation
4. Strong progressions (roots descending by third/fifth) all upshift under idealized voice leading
5. One complication: major-second root motion (IV -> V) involves six units in either direction (equivocal)

# Construction / Recognition
The dualism manifests whenever voice-leading has a consistent direction:
- Downshifting may be interpreted as dominant/authentic
- Upshifting may be interpreted as subdominant/plagal
- The functional scaffold can be shed if uncomfortable

# Context & Application
This dualism provides the philosophical foundation for the departure-return script and all functional interpretations of voice-leading zones.

# Examples
- Every departure-return scheme instantiates this dualism
- The kinetic experience of performers embodies it physically
