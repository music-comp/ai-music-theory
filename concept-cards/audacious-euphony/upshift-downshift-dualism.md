# Quick Definition
The upshift/downshift dualism provides a form of harmonic dualism grounded in the physical reality of directed voice leading rather than the metaphysical claims of historical dualism, correlating with but not mandating interpretations of dominant/subdominant function.

# Formal Definition
The upshift/downshift dualism aggregates several circumstances: pitch motion exists on a linear continuum projected culturally onto a vertical axis (higher/lower); pitch-class motion exists on a cyclical continuum; motion along either continuum occurs in two opposite directions; those motions have experiential correlates in aural perception and kinetic production. This dualism is "immune to dismissal on the grounds that it is based on metaphysical or spurious physical claims" and represents a dualism "that even the most skeptical realist can abide."

# Construction/Recognition
Correlations with functional harmony:
- Downshifting may be interpreted as dominant/authentic motion
- Upshifting may be interpreted as subdominant/plagal motion
- Strong progressions (roots descending by third/fifth, ascending by second) all upshift under idealized voice leading
- The correlation is suggestive but not mandatory

The one complication: modally matched chords whose roots are a major second apart (IV -> V in major; ii -> iii in minor) involve six units of voice leading in either direction, making their directional status equivocal.

# Musical Context
The upshift/downshift dualism provides a theoretically neutral alternative to Riemannian functional dualism. It allows structural claims about departure-return schemes without requiring commitment to specific functional interpretations.

# Examples
- Schubert, "Auf dem Flusse": Downshifting correlates with dominant motion, upshifting with return to tonic
- Schubert, Bb Sonata: Expositional descent and recapitulatory ascent in voice-leading zones
- The dualism is "strongly related to the duality of strong and weak harmonic progression" (Schoenberg, developed by Meeus 2000)

# Common Confusions
- This is NOT the metaphysical dualism of Riemann; it is grounded in physical voice-leading direction
- The correlation with T/S/D functions is suggestive, not definitional
- The dualism applies to both pitch space (register-specific) and pitch-class space (idealized voice leading)
