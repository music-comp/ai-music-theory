# Quick Definition
The hexatonic and Weitzmann regions stand in a figure-ground relation: for the hexatonic mariner, Weitzmann regions form bridges between hexatonic pools; for the Weitzmann landlubber, hexatonic regions are rivers to cross.

# Core Definition
Figure 5.2 portrays the four augmented triads at cardinal points, each connected to the six triads of its Weitzmann region (minor triads clockwise, major counterclockwise). Intersecting these Weitzmann regions are hexatonic regions, shown as textured pools. The figure suggests that the Weitzmann and hexatonic regions are in a "figure-ground relation" (p. 102). The metaphor is inherently perspectival: the same structure appears differently depending on which region type the analyst takes as primary.

# Prerequisites
- **Hexatonic region**: The four closed systems of six triads connected by L and P
- **Weitzmann region**: The four closed systems of six triads centered on augmented triads

# Key Properties
1. Weitzmann regions appear as "water bugs" with augmented triad bodies and consonant triad legs
2. Hexatonic regions appear as "pools" between adjacent augmented triads
3. Left-to-right ordering in Weitzmann's figure 5.1 corresponds to clockwise ordering in the circular figure 5.2
4. The relation is perspectival: neither system has priority

# Construction / Recognition
1. Place four augmented triads at cardinal points of a circle
2. Connect each to its six Weitzmann-region consonant triads (broken-line "water bugs")
3. Identify hexatonic regions as the pools of triads between adjacent augmented triads
4. Observe that crossing between hexatonic pools requires passing through a Weitzmann bridge (and vice versa)

# Context & Application
The figure-ground metaphor provides the visual and conceptual basis for understanding Cube Dance. It motivates the "amphibious vehicle" needed to circumnavigate the triadic universe and explains why chromatic sequences must alternate between H-group and W-group transformations.

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Treating one perspective (hexatonic or Weitzmann) as the "correct" one
  **Correction**: The figure-ground relation is inherently perspectival; both are equally valid

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: Thinking the water bugs and pools represent different musical phenomena
  **Clarification**: They represent two views of the same underlying voice-leading space
