# Quick Definition
In Cohn's tetrachordal voice-leading system, the minor seventh chord serves as a bridging chord within 4-Cube Trio, occupying even-numbered voice-leading zones and connecting Tristan-genus chords from adjacent Boretz regions.

# Formal Definition
The **minor seventh chord** plays two distinct roles in Cohn's framework:

1. **Combination strategy**: Generated by the union of two R-related triads (e.g., D minor + F major = D-F-A-C). This creates a parallelogram on the Tonnetz bisected by a major-third edge.

2. **Bridging role in 4-Cube Trio**: Minor seventh chords occupy even-numbered voice-leading zones between Boretz regions, providing connections (alongside French sixth chords) that bridge the gaps left by octatonic transformations.

The twelve minor seventh chords appear as triangular nodes at the 12:00, 4:00, and 8:00 positions of 4-Cube Trio.

# Musical/Mathematical Formulation
Structure:
- Minor triad + minor seventh above root (m3 + P5 + m7)
- Or: union of R-related triads sharing major-third edge
- Set class: 4-26, prime form [0358]

Voice-leading position:
- Even-numbered voice-leading zones (0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10)
- Bridges between Tristan-genus chords in odd zones
- Fills gaps created by octatonic transformations (which jump zones)

Generation by combination:
- D minor (D-F-A) + F major (F-A-C) = D-F-A-C (Dm7)
- The two triads share the F-A edge (major third)

# Musical Context/Application
Minor seventh chords in 4-Cube Trio:
- Provide stepwise connections between Tristan-genus chords
- Enable continuous voice-leading paths through even zones
- Can be engaged or bypassed in circumnavigating passages

In Chopin's e minor Prelude:
- Minor seventh chords appear in the chromatic descent
- They bridge gaps between dominant and half-diminished seventh chords
- Enable continuous, incremental downshifting

# Common Confusions
- Minor seventh is not part of Tristan genus (which includes only V7 and ø7)
- Minor seventh provides bridges in 4-Cube Trio, alongside French sixths
- The chord can be understood as R-related triads combined, or as a seventh chord
- Either minor seventh or French sixth can be removed from 4-Cube Trio without disconnecting the graph
