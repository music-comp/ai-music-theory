# Quick Definition
Three overlapping analyses of Schubert's Bb Sonata (D. 960) first movement: hexatonic constituency, Weitzmann-region constituency, and voice-leading zones, with the zone analysis revealing a two-phase descent (5 -> 4 -> 2), nadir at zone 1, and compensatory ascent with recapitulatory overshoot to zone 7.

# Core Definition
Figure 6.9 presents three analytic underlays: (1) hexatonic assigns function by hexatonic constituency (dominant arrives at A major, m. 58); (2) Weitzmann assigns by Weitzmann-region (dominant arrives already at f# minor second theme); (3) voice-leading zones create a hybrid (exposition = two-phase descent 5 -> 4 -> 2, development nadir at zone 1, recapitulation = compensatory ascent through 4 and 5, overshoot to 7). Only one of the eight non-tonic keys (d minor) is diatonic to Bb major.

# Prerequisites
- **Departure-return script**: The framework guiding analysis
- **Voice-leading zones**: The metric for tracking the movement's trajectory

# Key Properties
1. Three compatible but non-identical analytical readings
2. Exposition: two-phase descent (zone 5 -> 4 -> 2)
3. Development: nadir at c# minor (zone 1), reversal at A major (zone 2)
4. Recapitulation: ascent through zones 4, 5; overshoot to b minor (zone 7)
5. Zone analysis is the most neutral; functional interpretations optional

# Construction / Recognition
- Hexatonic analysis: T = Bb hexatonic system; S = Eb hexatonic; D = F hexatonic
- Weitzmann analysis: T = Bb Weitzmann region; D arrives with f# minor (common third with F)
- Zone analysis: Track pc sums for each prolonged harmony; plot trajectory

# Context & Application
The analysis demonstrates that voice-leading zones can organize a full sonata movement without requiring commitment to contested functional interpretations. The three analyses show different levels of analytical commitment, with zones as the most theoretically neutral option.

# Examples
- **Retransitional d minor** (m. 171): tonic under hexatonic analysis; dominant under Weitzmann analysis (FA third affiliates with F major)
- **Recapitulatory b minor**: tonic (hexatonic, shared third with Bb); tonic (Weitzmann); subdominant overshoot (zones, at 7)
- **The movement's thematic materials emphasize major thirds outlining augmented triads** (p. 149)
