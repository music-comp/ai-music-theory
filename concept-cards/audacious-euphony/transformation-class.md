# Quick Definition
Equivalence classes of transformations that can substitute for each other in voice-leading trajectories: H-class (L, P, H), W-class (R, N, S), and E-class (LP, PL, identity).

# Formal Definition
Having established voice-leading zones for triads, analogous equivalence classes apply to transformations. Three transformation classes are defined:
- H-class: {L, P, H} - maps consonant triads between zones X and X +/- 1
- W-class: {R, N, S} - maps consonant triads between zones Y and Y +/- 2
- E-class: {LP, PL, E} - maps triads within their own zone (identity or zone-preserving)

These classes enable systematic description of voice-leading trajectories independent of specific transformation choices.

# Musical/Mathematical Formulation
Class properties:
- H-class: shifts zone by 1, preserves Weitzmann region
- W-class: shifts zone by 2, preserves hexatonic region
- E-class: shifts zone by 0, preserves both regions

Compound classes:
- HW or WH: connects zones 3 apart (odd transposition sequences)
- HWH: connects zones 4 apart
- WHW: connects zones 5 apart
- HWHW or WHWH: connects zones 6 apart (maximum distance)

Generalization:
- HWH covers 27 specific transformation combinations (3 x 3 x 3)
- HWHW covers 81 specific transformation combinations (3^4)

# Musical Context/Application
The transformation class concept consolidates statements about voice-leading that would otherwise require enumerating many specific cases. The consolidation parallels numerical claims like "odd + odd = even" which summarize infinite specific cases in one statement.

# Examples
- 1 <--(H)--> 2 summarizes all H-class connections between zones 1 and 2
- 2 <--(W)--> 4 summarizes all W-class connections between zones 2 and 4
- The generalizing power resembles set-class abstraction in atonal theory

# Common Confusions
- Transformation classes are about equivalence for voice-leading purposes, not general identity
- The classes describe zone-shifting capacity, not other transformation properties
- E-class operations stay within a zone (no net voice-leading shift)
