# Quick Definition
The key sequence of Schumann's *Dichterliebe* executes two complete upshifting rotations of voice-leading space across sixteen songs, with scalar downshift (decrementing key signatures) realized as triadic upshift.

# Core Definition
Lerdahl (2001) plots the key sequence through Weber's regional graph. Cohn extends this: "The continuous downshifting in scalar space through the first eleven songs of *Dichterliebe* is realized in triadic space as a phased upshifting that continues straight through to the end of the cycle" (p. 149). Boundary harmonies trace two complete Cube Dance revolutions, with cyclic renewal at song 9 (the exact halfway point). Table 6.1 restores four withdrawn songs to show the original twenty-song structure.

# Prerequisites
- **Continuous upshift**: The compositional script being instantiated
- **Voice-leading zones**: The metric for tracking the tonal plan

# Key Properties
1. Two complete Cube Dance revolutions across sixteen songs
2. Cyclic renewal at song 9 (halfway, A7 matching C#7 from song 1)
3. Scalar downshift (key signatures: three sharps to zero) coincides with triadic upshift
4. Each half upshifts zones 2 -> 8, introduces unique downshift, then upshifts 7 -> 1
5. Four withdrawn songs (a, b, A, B) strengthen half-to-half parallelism
6. Echoes Op. 24 *Liederkreis* (nine songs completing one rotation)

# Construction / Recognition
Table 6.1 tracks boundary harmonies (beginning/ending triads of songs):
- Songs 1-8 (first rotation): zones 2, 2, 5, 8, 7, 10, 11, 1
- Songs 9-16 (second rotation): zones 2-5, 7, 8, 5, 7, 8, 11, 1-2

# Context & Application
The analysis resolves the long-debated question of whether *Dichterliebe* is "organically unified" by offering a non-conventional metric of coherence: not monotonal organization, but continuous directed voice leading across the entire cycle.

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Scalar downshift equals triadic downshift
  **Correction**: These dimensions are independent; Dichterliebe demonstrates both simultaneously

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: The two rotations are exactly parallel
  **Clarification**: The pace is not precisely matched, but structural parallels exist
