# Quick Definition
An **S**/**L** chain is a chromatic sequence generated by alternating Slide and Leittonwechsel transformations, producing transposition by minor third (T3), involving the maverick **S** transformation.

# Formal Definition
The **S**/**L** chain combines a W-group transformation (**S**, maverick) with an H-group transformation (**L**, normative), generating a T3 chromatic sequence. Along with the **P**/**R** and **N**/**H** chains, it is one of three transformation pairs that produce minor-third transpositions. Its involvement of the maverick **S** transformation makes it less common than the **P**/**R** chain.

# Construction/Recognition
Alternate **S** and **L** transformations:
- **S** maps a major triad to the minor triad with the same third (e.g., C major to c# minor)
- **L** then maps to the relative major (e.g., c# minor to E major)
- The net transposition is a minor third

# Musical Context
The **S**/**L** chain appears in compositions by Liszt and Schubert, typically generating extended downshift sequences offset by upward registral transfers.

# Examples
- Liszt, *Grande fantaisie symphonique* ("Lelio Fantasy"), mm. 195-202: Extended downshift sequence (figure 5.8)
- Schubert, Ab major Mass, Sanctus opening: Similar progression in the upshifting direction with falling bass, though insertion of a back-relating dominant makes voice leading less efficient

# Common Confusions
- All three T3-generating pairs (**P**/**R**, **N**/**H**, **S**/**L**) produce the same transpositional interval but with different voice-leading profiles
- The **S** transformation is the maverick element; **L** is classically normative
- Not to be confused with the **N**/**L** chain, which produces T1 (semitonal) transposition
