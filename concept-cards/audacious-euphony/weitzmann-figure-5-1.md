# Quick Definition
The foundational diagram from Weitzmann's 1853 treatise *Der ubermassige Dreiklang* showing first- and second-order relations of each augmented triad, reproduced as figure 5.1 with analytical overlay revealing latent hexatonic regions and cyclic structure.

# Core Definition
Figure 5.1 reproduces Weitzmann's figure (earlier presented as figure 3.9) with an analytical overlay. Weitzmann created it to portray relations of augmented triads but "has nothing further to say about this figure" (p. 102). Cohn identifies four latent properties: (1) directional consistency (downward-displacing triads left, upward right -- "rightward motion in graphic space is associated with upshifting in pitch space"); (2) redundancy (each cluster appears twice); (3) hexatonic regions (consonant triads between adjacent augmented triads form hexatonic regions); (4) implicit cyclicity (same triads at left and right margins). These observations "imply figure 5.2" -- the circular representation.

# Prerequisites
- **Weitzmann region**: Understanding the six-triad cluster around each augmented triad
- **Augmented triad as hub**: The role of augmented triads as central organizing nodes

# Key Properties
1. Four quadrants, each centered on an augmented triad
2. First tier (highlighted): six consonant triads reachable by one semitone displacement (= Weitzmann region)
3. Second tier: triads reachable by displacing two tones in the same direction
4. Directional consistency: left = downshift, right = upshift
5. Pruning redundancies yields exactly 24 consonant triads

# Construction / Recognition
Reading Weitzmann's figure with Cohn's overlay:
1. Identify the four augmented triads as organizing centers
2. Note the highlighted first-tier triads (Weitzmann regions)
3. Observe the redundant clusters at adjacent boundaries (= hexatonic regions)
4. Recognize the implicit cycle by connecting left and right margins

# Context & Application
This figure is the "point of entry" (p. 102) to the unified model. All subsequent constructions (figure 5.2, Cube Dance, voice-leading zones) derive from the latent properties Cohn identifies in Weitzmann's original.
