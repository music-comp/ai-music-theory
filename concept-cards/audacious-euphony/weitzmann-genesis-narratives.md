# Quick Definition
Weitzmann's 1853 treatise presents three genesis narratives (Entstehung) for the augmented triad: (1) how it emerges locally in composition, (2) its systematic position relative to keys, and (3) how it generates consonant triads - the third narrative inverting the direction of the second.

# Formal Definition
Weitzmann uses the verb entstehen (arise/generate) and noun Entstehung (origin/generation) to describe three different relationships between augmented and consonant triads. The first narrative concerns compositional emergence at specific moments; the second concerns systematic position in the tonal universe; the third inverts the second to show augmented triads as sources from which consonant triads are derived.

# Musical/Mathematical Formulation
The three narratives:

1. Chapter 2 - "Preparation, Origin, and Introduction":
- Compositional/surface level
- How augmented triads "come to life" at particular moments
- Presents 16 ways to connect consonant triads to augmented triads via semitone
- Like saying "Isaac was born of Abraham"

2. Chapter 6 - "Natural Origin of the Augmented Triad Most Important to Each Key":
- Systematic level (augmented derived from consonant)
- Nebenverwandt pentachord F-Ab-C-E-G contains augmented triad at interior: F[Ab-C-E]G
- Each augmented triad is "the most important" to six keys
- The augmented triad serves the consonant context

3. Chapter 7 and Figure 3.9 - Augmented triads generate consonant triads:
- Systematic level (consonant derived from augmented)
- "From the following augmented triads... arise the [consonant] triads"
- Each augmented triad is placed at center of six-triad cluster
- Inverts the direction of the second narrative

# Musical Context/Application
The three narratives represent different levels of explanation and different directions of theoretical priority. Weitzmann maintains the moment-to-moment priority of consonance (narrative 1) while acknowledging the systematic priority of the augmented triad (narrative 3). This dual perspective anticipates modern understanding of the augmented triad's structural role.

# Examples
From the text (pp. 73-76):

On the first narrative:
"The primer presents sixteen ways to connect a consonant triad to an augmented one via semitonal voice leading."

On the second narrative:
"Weitzmann combines f minor and C major triads into a pentachord, FAᅈCEG, from whose interior he extracts the augmented triad: F[AᅈCE]G. 'From the connection of these two nebenverwandt chords arises the augmented triad most important to the two keys represented by them.'"

On the third narrative:
"With this, Weitzmann turns back the flow of his second genesis narrative. At the systematic level, it is the augmented triads that are the sources, and the consonant triads the products."

Relationship between narratives:
"The first genesis narrative nonetheless remains intact. Immediately following the passage just quoted, Weitzmann presents seven full pages of examples, comprehensively enumerating the ways that an augmented triad can resolve. It is always the dissonance that is resolving to the consonance, never the other way around."

# Common Confusions
- "Genesis" (Entstehung) has different meanings in each narrative; context determines which
- The narratives are not contradictory but operate at different levels of explanation
- Compositional emergence (narrative 1) and systematic generation (narrative 3) can coexist
- Weitzmann did not resolve the tension between narratives; he presented both perspectives
