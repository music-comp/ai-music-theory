# Quick Definition
Weitzmann's 1853 treatise presents three genesis narratives (*Entstehung*) for the augmented triad: (1) how it emerges locally in composition, (2) its systematic position relative to keys, and (3) how it generates consonant triads -- the third narrative inverting the direction of the second.

# Core Definition
Weitzmann uses the verb *entstehen* (arise/generate) and noun *Entstehung* (origin/generation) in three distinct senses. The first narrative (Chapter 2 of the treatise) concerns "how this strange chord could come to life, prepared through major and minor triads" -- sixteen ways to connect a consonant triad to an augmented one (p. 73). The second (Chapter 6) derives the augmented triad from the nebenverwandt pentachord: "From the connection of these two nebenverwandt chords arises the augmented triad most important to the two keys represented by them" (p. 74). The third (Chapter 7 and Figure 3.9) inverts the flow: "From the following augmented triads... arise the [consonant] triads indicated by the letters next to them" (p. 76). "With this, Weitzmann turns back the flow of his second genesis narrative. At the systematic level, it is the augmented triads that are the sources, and the consonant triads the products" (p. 76).

# Prerequisites
- **Augmented triad**: The subject of all three genesis narratives
- **Nebenverwandt**: The second narrative derives the augmented triad from paired nebenverwandt chords
- **Weitzmann's treatise**: The narratives are the treatise's core structure

# Key Properties
1. **Narrative 1** (compositional emergence): How augmented triads "come to life" at particular moments; 16 connection methods
2. **Narrative 2** (systematic derivation, consonant -> augmented): Nebenverwandt pentachord F[Ab-C-E]G contains augmented triad at interior
3. **Narrative 3** (systematic generation, augmented -> consonant): Augmented triads generate consonant triads via semitonal displacement
4. Narratives 2 and 3 operate at the systematic level but in opposite directions
5. Narrative 1 operates at the compositional/surface level and remains intact alongside Narrative 3
6. The three relate "as specific to general" (narratives 1 and 2), like "Isaac was born of Abraham" vs. "invention is born of necessity" (p. 74)

# Construction / Recognition
- **Narrative 1**: In a specific composition, how does a consonant triad lead to an augmented one?
- **Narrative 2**: In the tonal system, how does the nebenverwandt pair generate its "most important" augmented triad?
- **Narrative 3**: In the tonal system, how does each augmented triad generate its surrounding consonant triads?

# Context & Application
The three narratives capture the dual perspective central to Cohn's project: at the moment-to-moment level, consonance takes priority (Narrative 1), but at the systematic level, the augmented triad can be understood as primary (Narrative 3). This duality is the essence of consonance/dissonance reciprocity.

# Examples
**Narrative 1** (p. 73): "The primer presents sixteen ways to connect a consonant triad to an augmented one via semitonal voice leading."

**Narrative 2** (p. 74): "Weitzmann combines f minor and C major triads into a pentachord, FAb-CEG, from whose interior he extracts the augmented triad: F[Ab-C-E]G."

**Narrative 3** (pp. 75-76): "From the following augmented triads... arise the [consonant] triads indicated by the letters next to them.... The chords placed immediately next to the augmented triad are attained through the half-step progression of one of their voices."

**Coexistence** (p. 76): "The first genesis narrative nonetheless remains intact. Immediately following the passage just quoted, Weitzmann presents seven full pages of examples... It is always the dissonance that is resolving to the consonance, never the other way around."

# Common Errors
- **Error**: Treating the narratives as contradictory
  **Correction**: They operate at different levels of explanation and coexist; Weitzmann maintained both perspectives

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: "Genesis" (*Entstehung*) means the same thing in each narrative
  **Clarification**: The word shifts meaning: compositional emergence (1), systematic derivation (2), systematic generation (3)
- **Confusion**: Narrative 3 replaces Narrative 2
  **Clarification**: Narrative 3 inverts the direction of Narrative 2 but does not negate it
