# Quick Definition
Liszt's 1879 Kyrie from *Missa pro organo lectarum* exemplifies a departure-return script realized as a palindrome of voice-leading zones, where each individual voice forms a palindrome but the staggered firing order prevents strict triadic palindrome.

# Core Definition
The brief wordless setting has three phrases (Kyrie-Christe-Kyrie), each with three parallel segments plus a cadential fourth (twelve segments total). Ramon Satyendra (1992, pp. 98-101) observed that "each individual voice constitutes a palindrome whose axis is the double bar" where the key signature converts between flats and sharps. Yet the staggered voice entries prevent triadic palindrome. The palindrome is "proper not to the progression of the triads themselves but to that of voice-leading zones that they represent" (p. 146). All voice leading downshifts in the first two phrases and upshifts in the final phrase.

# Prerequisites
- **Departure-return script**: The compositional framework

# Key Properties
1. Individual voices: strict palindromes about central axis
2. Triadic progression: NOT strictly palindromic (deviations in triads 3-4)
3. Voice-leading zones: strict palindrome
4. Deviations: Db major -> f minor mirrored by c# minor -> A major (retrograded transposition down diminished fourth)
5. Entirely triadic; little functional harmony beyond cadences

# Context & Application
This analysis demonstrates that departure-return schemes can operate in chromatic music with virtually no connection to diatonic function. The structure is "evident to the organist's hand" through the kinetic complementarity of downshifting and upshifting.

# Examples
- The Kyrie demonstrates the independence of voice-level palindrome from triadic-level palindrome
- The zonal palindrome reveals structure that would be invisible to conventional harmonic analysis

# Common Errors
- **Error**: The triadic progression is palindromic
  **Correction**: Only the voice-leading ZONES form a strict palindrome; the triads deviate

# Common Confusions
- **Confusion**: A piece needs functional harmony for structural coherence
  **Clarification**: The Kyrie achieves coherence through voice-leading trajectory alone
