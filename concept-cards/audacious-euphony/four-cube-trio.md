# Quick Definition
A connected graph representing the voice-leading relationships among Tristan-genus chords, diminished seventh chords, French sixth chords, and minor seventh chords, analogous to the Cube Dance for triads.

# Formal Definition
The 4-Cube Trio is a geometric representation whose nodes comprise the 24 Tristan-genus chords (12 half-diminished sevenths + 12 dominant sevenths), the 3 diminished seventh chords, 6 French sixth chords, and 12 minor seventh chords. Edges connect chords related by single semitonal displacement. The graph extends the Cube Dance concept to four-note chords, providing a unified representation of voice-leading space for seventh chords.

# Musical/Mathematical Formulation
Structure:
- 24 Tristan-genus nodes (12 half-dim7 + 12 dom7)
- 3 diminished seventh chord nodes (perfectly even)
- 6 French sixth chord nodes
- 12 minor seventh chord nodes
- Total: 45 nodes (varies based on which chord types included)

Edges connect chords differing by one semitone in one voice:
- Each Tristan-genus chord connects to diminished sevenths (its Boretz region center)
- Analogous to how consonant triads connect to augmented triads in Cube Dance

# Musical Context/Application
The 4-Cube Trio enables:
- Visualization of seventh-chord voice-leading networks
- Analysis of chromatic seventh-chord progressions (late Wagner, Liszt, early Schoenberg)
- Extension of pan-triadic principles to dissonant harmonies
- Understanding the "Tristan genus" systematically

The graph shows that voice-leading proximity principles apply broadly, not just to consonant triads.

# Examples
- Tristan chord progressions can be traced on the 4-Cube Trio
- Sequences of dominant and half-diminished sevenths
- Late 19th-century chromatic passages involving seventh chords
- Scriabin's mystic chord explorations

# Common Confusions
- The 4-Cube Trio is for four-note chords; the Cube Dance is for three-note chords
- Not all seventh chord types are included - specifically Tristan-genus and related types
- The diminished seventh chords play the structural role that augmented triads play in Cube Dance
- The graph represents voice-leading proximity, not functional relationships
