# Quick Definition
Cohn's methodological invitation to temporarily suspend overlearned habits of classical diatonic theory -- "forgetting" that triads are acoustic consonances generated from roots in diatonic scales relative to a tonic -- in order to foreground their alternative voice-leading properties.

# Formal Definition
Because classical diatonic theory is typically the first and often the only music theory one encounters, "its status as theory, a way of looking, easily hardens into nature: the way that music is." In response, Cohn occasionally invites readers to "forget" that major and minor triads are acoustic consonances generated from roots occupying a position in a diatonic scale with respect to some tonic. This is a "cultivated and strategic denial, motivated by the desire to prevent our default habits from flooding back in and drowning those properties and relations that I wish to foreground."

# Construction/Recognition
The technique involves bracketing:
- That triads are acoustic **consonances**
- That they are generated from **roots**
- That roots occupy positions in **diatonic** scales
- That scales relate to some **tonic**

Cohn compares this to how physicians bracket the aesthetic qualities of the bodies they examine -- qualities that might interest them in other contexts but are "not relevant to the diagnosis or treatment of some ailment."

# Musical Context
Strategic forgetting is necessary because the properties Cohn wishes to foreground (near evenness, voice-leading proximity, hexatonic organization) are invisible under the default lens of classical theory. As the book progresses through its final chapters, readers "will increasingly find themselves on familiar ground, although their perspective on that ground will have been altered in some meaningful way."

# Examples
- When analyzing hexatonic cycles, set aside the impulse to assign Roman numerals relative to a tonic
- When examining the *Tarnhelm* progression, resist interpreting each triad as "borrowed from" some key
- When tracking voice-leading paths on the Tonnetz, attend to semitonal displacements rather than root relations
- Cohn continues to name triads by their roots (e.g., "C major") as a heuristic convenience while noting that roots have no theoretical status in pan-triadic syntax: "heuristics are not equal to ontology"

# Common Confusions
- Strategic forgetting is not a permanent rejection of classical theory; it is a temporary methodological bracketing
- The technique does not claim that consonance, roots, and keys are unreal; it claims they are not the only relevant properties
- Forgetting is strategic, not naive -- it is performed by someone who knows full well what is being set aside
- The goal is not to replace classical theory but to create space for an alternative perspective to become visible
