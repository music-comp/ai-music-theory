# Quick Definition
A circumnavigation of Cube Dance occurs when a triadic progression traverses all twelve voice-leading zones, completing a full cycle through the four hexatonic and four Weitzmann regions, typically requiring transformational substitutions to avoid the full twenty-four-triad chain.

# Formal Definition
A complete circumnavigation of Cube Dance (one full rotation) traverses all twelve voice-leading zones. For chains generated by T1 or T5 transpositions, a strict alternation of two transformations would require all twenty-four triads to close. Transformational substitutions (file transfers) allow shortcutting this journey while preserving the voice-leading trajectory. Some passages execute multiple rotations, as in the Beethoven Ninth Symphony Scherzo (more than two tours) or Schumann's *Dichterliebe* (two complete rotations across sixteen songs).

# Construction/Recognition
- On Cube Dance: Track clockwise (upshift) or counterclockwise (downshift) motion through the twelve radii
- One rotation = twelve zones traversed
- Shortcutting via file transfers reduces the number of triads needed
- Multiple rotations indicate extended voice-leading trajectories

# Musical Context
Circumnavigation produces a satisfying sense of closure by returning to the starting zone. The technique is characteristic of extended chromatic passages in nineteenth-century music and can operate at various time scales, from single passages to entire song cycles.

# Examples
- Beethoven, Ninth Symphony, Scherzo: C major through nineteen triads to A major, more than two tours (Cohn 1991, 1992, 1997)
- Liszt, *Il Penseroso*: Complete **S**/**P** chain tour connecting Gb major to D major (figure 5.14)
- Brahms, Symphony No. 2, first movement: **R**/**L** chain from f# minor executes a full clockwise rotation (figure 5.11)
- Schumann, *Dichterliebe*: Two complete upshifting rotations across the sixteen-song cycle (figure 6.15)

# Common Confusions
- Circumnavigation does not require visiting all twenty-four triads; it requires traversing all twelve zones
- File transfers allow circumnavigation with fewer triads while maintaining directional consistency
- Multiple circumnavigations are possible and indicate sustained directional commitment
