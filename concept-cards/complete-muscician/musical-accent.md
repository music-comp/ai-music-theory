# Quick Definition
A musical accent is any event that draws the listener's attention through emphasis, whether created by temporal means (duration, metric position) or nontemporal means (dynamics, register, texture, contour, pitch instability).

# Formal Definition
Accents in music fall into two broad categories: temporal and nontemporal. Temporal accents include metrical accents (emphasis from metric position), durational/agogic accents (emphasis from longer note values), and rhythmic accents. Nontemporal accents include registral accents (emphasis from extreme high or low register), articulative/phenomenal accents (dynamics, sforzandi, staccato, slurs), textural accents (changes in musical texture), contour accents (changes in melodic direction), harmonic accents (changes in harmony), and pitch accents (dissonant or unstable pitches that draw attention). When multiple accent types align, the emphasis is particularly strong.

# Musical Context
Composers coordinate and manipulate various accent types to control the listener's perception of emphasis, structure, and expression. Accents that align with the meter reinforce it; accents that contradict the meter create syncopation and tension.

# Examples
- Bach's Violin Partita no. 3, Prelude: E receives metric, durational, and contour accents, establishing it as the tonic.
- Beethoven, "Tempest" Sonata (Example 2.13): sforzandi on weak beats create articulative accents that contrast with the metric accents.
- Chopin, Waltz in A minor (Example 2.12): registral accents from low bass notes on beat 1, then displaced to weak beats.
- Mozart, Piano Sonata in F (Example 2.16): contour accents from changes in melodic direction, textural accents from changes in accompaniment pattern.

# Common Confusions
Students sometimes think accents only come from dynamics (playing louder). In fact, accents arise from many sources. A long note on a weak beat creates an accent through duration; a sudden change in register creates an accent without any change in volume. The interplay of different accent types is central to musical expression.
