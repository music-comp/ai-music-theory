# Quick Definition
Resolution is the motion from a dissonant or unstable interval/chord to a consonant or stable one, discharging the musical tension and energy created by dissonance.

# Formal Definition
Resolution is the process by which dissonant intervals and chords move to consonant ones, discharging the tension and energy created by dissonance. Only through resolution can the energy of dissonance be released. Resolution follows specific directional rules: tendency tones resolve by step in specific directions (^7 up to ^1, ^4 down to ^3, ^2 to ^1 or ^3), the tritone resolves by contrary motion (d5 contracts to a 3rd, A4 expands to a 6th), and chordal sevenths resolve by descending step. The concept of resolution extends hierarchically: a dissonant seventh may be "only relatively resolved" by an imperfect consonance (a sixth), which itself resolves to a more stable perfect consonance (a fifth).

# Musical Context
Resolution is the fundamental dynamic of tonal music -- it creates the tension-release cycles that give music its forward motion and dramatic shape. Without dissonance there would be no resolution; without resolution, dissonance would be uncontrolled noise. The interplay between tension and resolution operates at every level, from individual intervals to phrase-level cadences to the entire tonal trajectory of a piece.

# Examples
In a descending C-major scale over sustained C, the dissonant major seventh (B over C) resolves to the consonant sixth (A over C), which in turn resolves to the more stable perfect fifth (G over C). The B is only "relatively" resolved by A; true stability arrives with G. In V7-I, all four tendency tones resolve simultaneously: ^7 to ^1, ^4 to ^3, ^2 to ^1 or ^3, ^5 to ^1.

# Common Confusions
Students sometimes think resolution means any motion away from dissonance. True resolution requires motion to specific consonances in specific directions. The hierarchical nature of resolution (relative vs. complete stability) is also important -- not all consonances provide equally satisfying resolutions.
