# Competency Questions for Analyzing Classical Form (Caplin)

## Definitional (14 questions)

1. What is a basic idea in Caplin's formal theory?
2. What is a contrasting idea?
3. What is a sentence (as a theme-type)?
4. What is a period (as a theme-type)?
5. What is a hybrid theme?
6. What is a compound theme (compound period, compound sentence)?
7. What is a cadential function?
8. What is a continuation function?
9. What is a presentation function?
10. What is the essential expositional closure (EEC)?
11. What is the essential structural closure (ESC)?
12. What is a subordinate theme in Caplin's theory?
13. What is a standing on the dominant?
14. What is a framing function (before-the-beginning, after-the-end)?

## Relational (12 questions)

15. How does the sentence relate to the period as contrasting theme-types?
16. How do hybrid themes derive from combining sentence and period components?
17. How does tight-knit organization relate to loose organization?
18. How does intrinsic formal function relate to extrinsic formal function?
19. How does the main theme function relate to the subordinate theme function within a sonata exposition?
20. How does the transition mediate between main theme and subordinate theme tonally and formally?
21. How does the recapitulation modify the exposition's tonal plan?
22. How does the development section relate to the exposition thematically and harmonically?
23. How does concerto form relate to standard sonata form (double exposition, cadenza)?
24. How does the small ternary relate to rounded binary form?
25. How does the coda relate to the structural close of a movement?
26. How does the slow introduction function relative to the sonata-allegro proper?

## Procedural (10 questions)

27. How do I identify whether a theme is a sentence, period, or hybrid?
28. How do I locate and label the formal functions (presentation, continuation, cadential) within a theme?
29. How do I distinguish a half cadence from a dominant arrival that is not cadential?
30. How do I identify cadential deviations (evaded cadence, deceptive cadence, abandoned cadence)?
31. How do I analyze the formal sections of a sonata-form exposition?
32. How do I determine the boundaries of a transition section?
33. How do I identify the EEC in a subordinate theme group?
34. How do I analyze a development section's core and pre-core?
35. How do I determine which rondo form (five-part, seven-part, sonata-rondo) a movement uses?
36. How do I identify model-sequence technique in a development or continuation?

## Prerequisite (7 questions)

37. What harmonic concepts (cadence types, tonicization, modulation) must I know before applying Caplin's formal analysis?
38. What must I understand about phrase rhythm before analyzing theme-types?
39. What must I know about conventional cadential progressions (I6-ii6-V7-I) before identifying cadential function?
40. What must I know about sentence and period structure before understanding compound themes?
41. What must I know about basic theme-types before analyzing sonata form?
42. What must I know about exposition structure before understanding recapitulation adjustments?
43. What must I know about small binary and small ternary before understanding large-scale forms?

## Diagnostic (8 questions)

44. What distinguishes tight-knit from loose formal organization?
45. What distinguishes a PAC from an IAC in Caplin's cadential hierarchy?
46. What distinguishes a real continuation from a dissolving continuation?
47. What distinguishes a dependent transition from an independent transition?
48. What distinguishes the main theme's formal rhetoric from the subordinate theme's?
49. What distinguishes a small binary from a small ternary (rounded binary)?
50. What distinguishes a five-part rondo from a sonata-rondo?
51. What distinguishes an evaded cadence from a deceptive cadence?
