# Competency Questions for Music Theory for the 21st-Century Classroom

## Definitional
1. What is a major scale, and how does the pattern of whole and half steps define it?
2. What are the three types of minor scales, and how do they differ?
3. How does a seventh chord differ from a triad?
4. What is a secondary dominant chord, and what key does it tonicize?
5. Define mode mixture and provide an example.
6. What is the Neapolitan chord and its primary voice leading characteristic?
7. What is an augmented sixth chord, and how many common types are there?
8. What does enharmonic modulation mean vs. traditional modulation?

## Relational
9. How does harmonic function depend on both Roman numeral and context?
10. What is the relationship between a motive and a subphrase?
11. How do non-chord tones relate to underlying harmonic structure?
12. Compare binary, ternary, and sonata forms structurally.
13. How does voice leading in seventh chords differ from triads?
14. What is the relationship between set theory and serial composition?
15. How do minimalist techniques relate to traditional form and development?

## Procedural
16. How do you identify intervals using letter-name method and quality?
17. How do you write a proper Roman numeral analysis?
18. What are the rules for realizing figured bass?
19. How do you construct and resolve a secondary dominant in four parts?
20. What are the steps for diatonic modulation?
21. How do you analyze voice leading with augmented sixth chords?
22. What is the procedure for deriving prime form in set theory?
23. How do you apply twelve-tone serialism to compose a melody?

## Prerequisite
24. What knowledge of scales/intervals is needed for chord construction?
25. Why is diatonic harmony a prerequisite for secondary dominants and mixture?
26. What melodic analysis skills are needed before studying large forms?
27. How does functional harmony provide foundation for modulation?

## Diagnostic
28. Can you identify and label all non-chord tones in a score excerpt?
29. Can you determine Roman numerals, functions, and voice leading for a progression?
30. Can you distinguish secondary dominants, mode mixture, and Neapolitan harmony?
