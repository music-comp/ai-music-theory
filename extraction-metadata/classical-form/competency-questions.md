# Competency Questions for Analyzing Classical Form

## Definitional (What is X?)
1. What is a sentence?
2. What is a period?
3. What is a basic idea?
4. What is a cadential function?
5. What is a hybrid theme?
6. What is a compound theme?
7. What is the small ternary form?
8. What is sonata form?
9. What is a transition?
10. What is a subordinate theme?
11. What is a development section?
12. What is a recapitulation?
13. What is a coda?
14. What is a rondo form?
15. What is concerto form?

## Relational (How does X relate to Y?)
1. How does a sentence differ from a period?
2. How does the transition connect main theme to subordinate theme?
3. How does small ternary relate to rounded binary?
4. How does the recapitulation relate to the exposition?
5. How does concerto form relate to sonata form?
6. How do hybrid themes relate to sentences and periods?

## Procedural (How do I do X?)
1. How do I identify a sentence vs. a period?
2. How do I analyze the formal functions within a theme?
3. How do I locate the essential expositional closure?
4. How do I identify cadential deviations?
5. How do I analyze a sonata-form exposition?

## Prerequisite (What before X?)
1. What must I know before analyzing sonata form?
2. What must I know before understanding compound themes?
3. What harmonic concepts are prerequisite for form analysis?

## Diagnostic (What distinguishes X from Y?)
1. What distinguishes tight-knit from loose organization?
2. What distinguishes a PAC from an IAC?
3. What distinguishes main theme from subordinate theme function?
4. What distinguishes a five-part rondo from a seven-part rondo?
5. What distinguishes simple binary from rounded binary?
