# Competency Questions for Twentieth-Century Harmony

## Foundational/Conceptual
1. What is the relationship between interval consonance-dissonance and the harmonic series in 20th-century practice?
2. How do composers use modal systems compared to traditional major/minor tonality?
3. What distinguishes polymodality from traditional modulation?
4. How do equal-interval chords challenge traditional root concepts?
5. What is the difference between tonality and atonality, and where does the boundary lie?
6. How does the tritone function differently in diatonic vs. chromatic contexts?
7. What role do synthetic scales play in 20th-century harmonic expansion?
8. How do spacing and register transform consonant-dissonant quality?

## Construction/Technique
9. How is a polychord constructed vs. a compound chord?
10. What methods build quartal chords while preserving their distinctive sound?
11. How are tone clusters classified and notated?
12. What techniques integrate quartal with tertian harmony?
13. How do added-note chords differ from extended tertian chords?
14. How is mirror harmony constructed?
15. What methods allow smooth transitions between tertian, quartal, and secundal textures?
16. How do composers create harmonic ambiguity through equidistant constructions?

## Voice Leading & Progression
17. What principles govern progression when traditional root relationships don't apply?
18. How do outer voices determine direction without traditional bass-root relationships?
19. What role do common tones play in chromatic progressions?
20. How do composers use harmonic rhythm for formal structure?
21. How is dissonance resolved without traditional tendency tones?
22. How does parallel voice motion affect harmonic clarity?
23. What distinguishes chord connection from chord progression?

## Harmonic Function & Tonality
24. What methods establish tonality without dominant-tonic cadences?
25. How does polytonality function structurally?
26. What are techniques for chromatic vs. diatonic modulation in non-traditional contexts?
27. How do composers maintain harmonic direction in atonal writing?
28. What is the relationship between serial harmony and harmonic organization?
29. How can dissonance become the "consonant norm"?
30. What distinguishes transient modulation from harmonic elision?

## Synthesis & Integration
31. How do composers combine multiple harmonic systems within a single work?
32. What transitional materials allow movement between contrasting textures?
33. How do melodic and rhythmic elements guide harmonic progression?
34. What relationships exist between harmonic choices, orchestration, and form?
35. How does pandiatonic writing relate to traditional diatonic harmony?
