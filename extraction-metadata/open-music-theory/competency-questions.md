# Competency Questions for Open Music Theory

## Definitional (What is X?)
1. What is the difference between pitch and pitch class?
2. Define modal mixture and how it differs from modulation.
3. What constitutes a perfect authentic cadence?
4. What is the difference between a half cadence and an authentic cadence?
5. What is a motive, and how does it differ from a phrase?
6. Define consonance and dissonance in species counterpoint.
7. What is a twelve-tone row?
8. What is pitch-class set theory vs. traditional interval nomenclature?
9. What is orchestration (vertical vs. horizontal approaches)?

## Relational (How does X relate to Y?)
10. How do T/PD/D functions relate to the phrase model?
11. What is the relationship between antecedent and consequent phrases?
12. How do augmented sixth chords function as pre-dominants?
13. How do common-tone chords relate to voice-leading efficiency?
14. How do swing eighths in jazz relate to straight eighths?
15. What is the relationship between tresillo and African/Cuban traditions?
16. How do enharmonic and octave equivalence define pitch-class identity?
17. How does modal mixture in major relate to the parallel minor?
18. How do T, I, R, RI operations maintain serial integrity?
19. How do harmonic function and voice-leading interact in cadences?

## Procedural (How do I do X?)
20. How do you analyze phrase-level structure using segmentation?
21. How do you compose first-species counterpoint?
22. How do you determine if a cadence is perfect or imperfect?
23. How do you notate swing rhythms?
24. How do you transpose a twelve-tone row?
25. How do you apply modal mixture for stronger pre-dominants?
26. How do you distinguish tonicization from modulation?
27. How do you voice a seventh chord in four-part writing?
28. How do you orchestrate a four-part progression?
29. How do you perform retrograde inversion on a tone row?
30. How do you transcribe pop/rock syncopation into standard notation?
31. How do you determine the prime form of a pitch-class set?

## Prerequisite (What before X?)
32. What is needed before studying species counterpoint?
33. What must be mastered before understanding modal mixture?
34. What is necessary before analyzing harmonic function?
35. What prerequisites are needed for twelve-tone composition?
36. What must one understand before tonicization and modulation?
37. What rhythmic concepts are essential before pop/rock syncopation?
38. What interval/triad knowledge is needed for augmented sixth chords?
39. What is required for set-theoretic pitch organization?

## Diagnostic (What distinguishes X from Y?)
40. How do you distinguish PAC, IAC, and HC by ear?
41. How do you identify motivic transformations (augmentation, inversion, retrograde)?
42. How do you identify swing eighths, backbeat, and syncopation in jazz?
43. How do you identify P, R, I, RI forms in a twelve-tone matrix?
