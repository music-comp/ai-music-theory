# Competency Questions for The Complete Musician

## Definitional (What is X?)
1. What is a major scale?
2. What is a minor scale (natural, harmonic, melodic)?
3. What is a key signature?
4. What is a triad?
5. What is a seventh chord?
6. What is a cadence?
7. What is a nonchord tone?
8. What is a sequence?
9. What is a period?
10. What is an applied chord?
11. What is modal mixture?
12. What is a Neapolitan chord?
13. What is an augmented sixth chord?
14. What is sonata form?
15. What is a motive?

## Relational (How does X relate to Y?)
1. How does the dominant relate to the tonic?
2. How do pre-dominant chords lead to the dominant?
3. How does the submediant function as a tonic substitute?
4. How do relative major and minor keys relate?
5. How does tonicization differ from modulation?
6. How do sequences relate to modulation?
7. How does binary form relate to sonata form?
8. How does the mediant relate to the tonic and dominant?
9. How do chromatic sequences extend diatonic ones?
10. How does modal mixture relate to borrowed chords?

## Procedural (How do I do X?)
1. How do I construct a major scale?
2. How do I write a four-voice (SATB) chorale?
3. How do I identify and resolve nonchord tones?
4. How do I analyze a harmonic progression with Roman numerals?
5. How do I write a proper voice-leading connection between chords?
6. How do I identify a sequence type?
7. How do I modulate to a closely related key?
8. How do I resolve an augmented sixth chord?
9. How do I analyze a sonata form exposition?
10. How do I use figured bass to realize a continuo part?

## Prerequisite (What before X?)
1. What must I know before learning seventh chords?
2. What must I know before understanding voice leading?
3. What must I know before studying chromaticism?
4. What must I know before analyzing sonata form?
5. What must I know before understanding modal mixture?

## Diagnostic (What distinguishes X from Y?)
1. What distinguishes major from minor mode?
2. What distinguishes consonance from dissonance?
3. What distinguishes a passing tone from a neighbor tone?
4. What distinguishes a half cadence from a deceptive cadence?
5. What distinguishes a sentence from a period?
6. What distinguishes tonicization from modulation?
7. What distinguishes simple from compound meter?
8. What distinguishes the Italian, German, and French augmented sixth chords?
