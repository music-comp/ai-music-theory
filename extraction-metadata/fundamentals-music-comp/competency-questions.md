# Competency Questions for Fundamentals of Musical Composition

## Definitional (What is X?)
1. What is musical form?
2. What is a phrase in musical composition?
3. What is a motive?
4. What is developing variation?
5. What is liquidation?
6. What is a period?
7. What is a sentence (in compositional structure)?
8. What is a caesura?
9. What is the small ternary form?
10. What is the contrasting middle section?
11. What is a minuet?
12. What is a scherzo?
13. What is theme and variations form?
14. What is the motive of variation?
15. What is a transition in larger forms?
16. What is a rondo?
17. What is sonata-allegro form?
18. What is an elaboration (Durchführung)?
19. What is a coda?
20. What is a retransition?

## Relational (How does X relate to Y?)
1. How does a period differ from a sentence?
2. How does melody relate to theme?
3. How does the accompaniment relate to the theme?
4. How does character relate to mood in composition?
5. How does the motive of variation relate to the original theme?
6. How does the contrasting middle section relate to the A-section?
7. How does a scherzo differ from a minuet?
8. How does the exposition relate to the recapitulation in sonata-allegro?
9. How does a sonata rondo differ from a large rondo?
10. How does centripetal harmony differ from centrifugal harmony?

## Procedural (How do I do X?)
1. How do I construct a phrase from a motive?
2. How do I build a sentence opening (presentation)?
3. How do I construct an antecedent of a period?
4. How do I write a consequent that completes a period?
5. How do I apply liquidation to complete a sentence?
6. How do I write an accompaniment for a theme?
7. How do I create a contrasting middle section?
8. How do I construct variations on a theme?
9. How do I write a transition between themes?
10. How do I organize a variation set?

## Prerequisite (What before X?)
1. What must I know before understanding developing variation?
2. What must I know before constructing a period?
3. What must I know before writing a small ternary form?
4. What must I know before composing a minuet or scherzo?
5. What must I know before understanding sonata-allegro form?
6. What must I know before writing theme and variations?

## Diagnostic (What distinguishes X from Y?)
1. What distinguishes exact repetition from modified repetition of a motive?
2. What distinguishes a half cadence from a full cadence in period construction?
3. What distinguishes common errors from common confusions in motive treatment?
4. What distinguishes formal variations from character variations?
5. What distinguishes a codetta from a coda?
6. What distinguishes a principal theme from a subordinate theme in sonata form?
7. What distinguishes asymmetrical from irregular construction?
8. What distinguishes the trio from the main section in dance forms?
